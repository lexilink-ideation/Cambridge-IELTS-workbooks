📋 Passage Overview

The thylacine (Tasmanian tiger), an extinct dog-like marsupial. The passage describes its appearance, carnivorous diet and hunting, breeding, its mainland decline (linked to dingoes) and its final decline and extinction in Tasmania up to 1936. Question types: note completion (Q1–5) and TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q6–13).

💡 Reading tip: Note-completion answers appear in passage order. For T/F/NG, watch dates and numbers closely — many statements test a specific year or figure, and FALSE means the passage gives a different one.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

In terms of feeding, it was exclusively carnivorous, and its stomach was muscular with an ability to distend so that it could eat large amounts of food at one time.
Main Clauseit was exclusively carnivorous, and its stomach was muscular
Subordinate / Relativeso that it could eat large amounts of food at one time (clause of result/purpose)
Grammar NoteTwo clauses joined by 'and'; 'so that' introduces the result of the muscular, stretchy stomach.
Simple MeaningIt only ate meat, and its stretchy stomach let it eat a lot at once.
中文在进食方面,它完全食肉,胃部肌肉发达且能扩张,因而一次能吃下大量食物。
Its extinction coincided closely with the arrival of wild dogs called dingoes in Australia and a similar predator in New Guinea.
Main ClauseIts extinction coincided closely with the arrival of wild dogs … and a similar predator
Subordinate / Relativecalled dingoes (reduced relative clause = 'which were called dingoes')
Grammar Note'coincided with' takes a noun phrase; 'called dingoes' is a reduced clause.
Simple MeaningIt died out at about the same time dingoes and a similar predator arrived.
中文它的灭绝与被称为丁格犬的野狗在澳大利亚出现,以及新几内亚出现类似捕食者的时间高度吻合。
While this determined campaign undoubtedly played a large part, it is likely that various other factors also contributed to the decline and eventual extinction of the species.
Main Clauseit is likely that various other factors also contributed to the decline
Subordinate / RelativeWhile this determined campaign undoubtedly played a large part (concession clause); that various other factors also contributed (noun clause)
Grammar Note'While' = 'although'; 'it is likely that…' places the real subject at the end.
Simple MeaningAlthough hunting mattered a lot, other things probably also caused the extinction.
中文尽管这场坚决的猎杀运动无疑起了很大作用,但很可能还有多种其他因素也促成了该物种的衰落与最终灭绝。
Official protection of the species by the Tasmanian government was introduced in July 1936, 59 days before the last known individual died in Hobart Zoo on 7th September, 1936.
Main ClauseOfficial protection of the species … was introduced in July 1936
Subordinate / Relative59 days before the last known individual died… (time phrase)
Grammar NotePassive main clause; the appositive time phrase gives the tragic timing.
Simple MeaningThe government protected it in July 1936 — just 59 days before the last one died in September.
中文塔斯马尼亚政府于1936年7月正式立法保护该物种,而仅59天后,已知最后一只个体便于1936年9月7日在霍巴特动物园死去。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause and effect. The mainland extinction is linked (as a probable cause) to the arrival of dingoes; the Tasmanian decline is 'attributed to' hunting plus other factors. Cause language ('due to', 'attributed to', 'contributed to') guides answers 4–5 and Q6.

Chronology. The decline runs 1830s → 1899 (one zoo birth) → 1914 (Flynn's proposal) → 1929 (limited protection) → 1930 (last wild one shot) → 1936 (protection; last individual dies) → 1986 (declared extinct). Dates drive the T/F/NG order.

Claim vs certainty. Words like 'probably', 'it is likely', 'thought to be' show the writer is cautious — useful when a statement claims something the passage does not firmly state (NOT GIVEN).
