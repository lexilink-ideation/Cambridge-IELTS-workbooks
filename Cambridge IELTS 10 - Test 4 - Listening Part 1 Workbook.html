Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Edith Pargetter telephones Thorndyke's Builders because she has several jobs that need painting and fixing in her flat. Mr Thorndyke takes her details, asks about parking, and then makes a table of the work to be done in the kitchen and the garden.

这是“打电话找工人”的场景。前半段是姓名拼写、地址、联系时间、停车位置,后半段是具体工作项目表格。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY for all ten answers — so 'the glass' must be written as glass and 'a fence' as fence. Also watch Q2: Mr Thorndyke says 'West Park Flats' twice before Edith corrects him — 'No, actually it's East Park.'

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The note headings compress what the speakers say. Practise expanding them into full questions — that is the sound you are listening for. 把笔记标题还原成对话中的提问。
Q3 — 'Behind the ___'
Note wording
(Behind the ___)
Audio wording
'Yes, it's at the back of the library.'
Key paraphrase
behind = at the back of
Trap
'quite difficult to get to' comes first and carries no answer.
Your turn
Q4 — 'Best time to contact'
Note wording
Best time to contact customer: during the ___
Audio wording
'I'm out a great deal in the afternoons and evenings.' — 'So would the best time to ring you be in the morning?'
Key paraphrase
contact = ring; the answer is the time NOT rejected
Trap
Two wrong times ('afternoons and evenings') are said before the right one.
Your turn
Q5 — 'Where to park'
Note wording
opposite entrance next to the ___
Audio wording
'park by the postbox on the other side of the road from the entrance'
Key paraphrase
next to = by; opposite = on the other side of the road from
Trap
The note reverses the order of the audio — postbox comes first when spoken.
Your turn
Q6 — 'full quote'
Note wording
Needs full quote showing all the jobs and the ___
Audio wording
'I'll list all the jobs separately with individual prices.'
Key paraphrase
showing all the jobs = list all the jobs separately; the ___ = individual prices
Trap
'itemised' is Edith's word; 'prices' is Mr Thorndyke's. The gap needs the noun.
Your turn
Q9 — 'approximately one week in advance'
Table wording
Strip paint and plaster approximately one ___ in advance
Audio wording
'strip the old paint and plaster it about a week before I paint it'
Key paraphrase
approximately = about; in advance = before
Trap
'a week' → the gap already has 'one', so write only week.
Your turn
