📋 Quick Review

A woman asks her friend Coleman about the guitar group he has joined. He explains who runs it, the level, where and when it meets, a website for buying instruments, and what a typical 45-minute lesson is like. Expect a spelled-out surname, a time, and a table of lesson activities (ONE WORD ONLY).

💡 Listening tip: When a name is spelled out ('M-A-T-H-I-E-S-O-N'), write each letter as you hear it and check afterwards. For the table (Q7–10), read the row labels first so you know whether the gap needs an activity, an object, or a method.
