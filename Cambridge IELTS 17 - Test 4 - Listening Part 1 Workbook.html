📋 Quick Review

A customer arranges a home clean with Easy Life Cleaning Services. The agent explains the basic package, extra weekly and monthly services, other help available, and how the cleaners are checked and managed. Notes, ONE WORD for each answer.

💡 Listening tip: Household vocabulary drives every answer here (floors, fridge, windows, balcony). Predict the room feature or item before each gap, and watch the 'weekly' vs 'monthly' headings so answers land in the right section.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The notes reword the agent's words. Train the swap.
Ironing (Q3)
Question note
ironing clothes – ___ only
What you hear
'we'll iron your shirts, but nothing else'
Key paraphrase
'nothing else' = only shirts
Your turn
Allergy service (Q7)
Question note
for customers who are allergic to ___
What you hear
'a special service for people who react badly to dust'
Key paraphrase
react badly to = allergic to → dust
Your turn
Feedback (Q10)
Question note
customers send a ___ after each visit
What you hear
'you send us a review to tell us how it went'
Key paraphrase
feedback after a visit = a review
Your turn
