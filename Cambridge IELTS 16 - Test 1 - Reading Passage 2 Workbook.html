📋 Passage Overview

The Step Pyramid of Djoser. A history of Egypt's first stone pyramid, designed by the official Imhotep for King Djoser at Saqqara — its construction stages, its huge walled complex, and the mysterious stone vessels found inside. Question types: matching headings (Q14–20), notes completion (Q21–24) and choose TWO (Q25–26).

💡 Reading tip: For matching headings, read the paragraph's topic sentence and overall point, not just one detail. A heading must summarise the whole paragraph.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

For reasons which remain unclear, Djoser's main official, whose name was Imhotep, conceived of building a taller, more impressive tomb for his king by stacking stone slabs on top of one another.
Main ClauseDjoser's main official conceived of building a taller, more impressive tomb
Subordinate / Relativewhich remain unclear (relative); whose name was Imhotep (relative); by stacking stone slabs… (gerund of method)
Grammar NoteTwo relative clauses add information; 'by + -ing' shows the method.
Simple MeaningFor unknown reasons, the official Imhotep thought of a taller tomb made by stacking stone slabs.
中文出于不明原因，朝臣伊姆霍特普想到用层层堆叠石板来为国王建造更高大的陵墓。
The weight of the enormous mass was a challenge for the builders, who placed the stones at an inward incline in order to prevent the monument breaking up.
Main ClauseThe weight of the enormous mass was a challenge for the builders
Subordinate / Relativewho placed the stones at an inward incline (relative); in order to prevent… (purpose)
Grammar NoteRelative clause + 'in order to' purpose clause.
Simple MeaningThe huge weight was hard to manage, so builders angled the stones inward to stop it collapsing.
中文巨大的重量对建造者是个挑战，他们把石头向内倾斜摆放以防止建筑坍塌。
If someone wished to enter, he or she would have needed to know in advance how to find the location of the true opening in the wall.
Main Clausehe or she would have needed to know … how to find the location of the true opening
Subordinate / RelativeIf someone wished to enter (conditional)
Grammar NotePast unreal conditional ('would have needed'); embedded question 'how to find…'.
Simple MeaningTo get in, a person had to already know where the real entrance was.
中文若想进入，必须事先知道墙上真正入口的位置。
There is no agreement among scholars and archaeologists on why the vessels were placed in the tomb of Djoser or what they were supposed to represent.
Main ClauseThere is no agreement among scholars and archaeologists
Subordinate / Relativeon why the vessels were placed… or what they were supposed to represent (embedded questions)
Grammar Note'There is no agreement on + wh-clause' shows uncertainty — a common academic pattern.
Simple MeaningExperts disagree about why the vessels were put there and what they meant.
中文学者们对这些容器为何被放入陵墓、代表什么意义并无共识。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology / development: the passage moves from earlier flat clay tombs → Imhotep's stacked-slab idea → the six-level Step Pyramid. Matching-headings answers often depend on spotting this 'before → change → result' order.

Purpose signals: false doors and the trench were built 'to discourage unwanted visitors'; stones were angled 'in order to prevent the monument breaking up'. Watch for 'to / in order to' — they explain WHY a feature exists.

Certainty vs doubt: some facts are firm ('rose 62 meters'), others disputed ('no agreement… why the vessels were placed'). Heading iv ('a single certainty among less definite facts') rewards readers who notice this mix.
