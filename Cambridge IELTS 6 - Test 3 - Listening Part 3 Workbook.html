Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

Marketing Assignment: Music Preferences · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Jack and Lucy, two students, plan a marketing assignment together. They must investigate one part of the entertainment industry by interviewing people, and they choose to study music preferences, comparing two age groups. Through discussion, they design a survey covering age groups, music genres, listening mediums, purchase sources, and venues for live music.

This is a classic Part 3 academic discussion: two speakers planning a project, negotiating decisions, and building a list together — a good source of practice for sentence completion and note completion.

💡 Part 3 strategy: In academic discussions, one speaker often suggests an idea and the other rejects or improves it. The final agreed answer — not the first suggestion — is usually what you should write down. Watch for phrases like "no, I don't think so" or "maybe you're right" that signal a change of mind.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In academic discussions, watch for one speaker proposing and the other rejecting or accepting — the final agreement is the answer.
Q22 — Research Method (No Real Choice)
Question wordingThe method the students must use to collect data is ______.
Exact script line
LUCY: Can we mail out a questionnaire, or do face-to-face interviews, or maybe even observations?JACK: Well actually, it looks like we don't have a choice. We have to do telephone interviews.
Key paraphrase"we don't have a choice" = the method is fixed by the assignment rules, not chosen among the options Lucy lists
Your practice
Q23 — Total Number of Interviewees (A Calculation)
Question wordingIn total, the students must interview ______ people.
Exact script line
JACK: We have to do two groups, remember, and it looks like we have to interview fifteen for each group.LUCY: That's thirty altogether then.
Key paraphraseThe answer requires simple maths: 15 × 2 groups = 30 — Lucy states the total directly
Your practice
Q24 — Jack's Opinion (Rejecting Lucy's Suggestion)
Question wordingJack thinks the music preferences of ______ listeners are similar.
Exact script line
LUCY: Male and female?JACK: No. Most of my female friends like the same music as me. Different age groups would be much more likely to show up differences, I think.
Key paraphrase⚠️ The question asks what Jack thinks IS similar (male/female), not what he proposes to study (age) — read the question carefully!
Your practice
Q28 — Source of Music (Building a List)
Question wordingSource of music: Music shops · ______ · Internet
Exact script line
LUCY: You mean like, do they buy it in music shops, or department stores?JACK: Yes, or download it from the Internet.
Key paraphraseLucy proposes the category and Jack simply agrees ("Yes") — the answer is Lucy's suggestion, not Jack's
