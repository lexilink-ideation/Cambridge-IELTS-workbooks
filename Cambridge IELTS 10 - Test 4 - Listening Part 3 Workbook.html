Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Tim asks Laura about the work placement she did last summer at a small environmental services company, where she produced a company brochure. They discuss the skills she gained, the benefits to the company, and then the six stages of applying through the government agency STEP.

这是“学长学姐经验分享”场景。后半段是六阶段配对题——每个阶段该找谁获取信息,信息源共七个,顺序与对话一致。

💡 Listening tip: In Q25–30 you must match each stage to the right source of information. Notice how often Laura corrects Tim's suggestion: 'I suppose I should ask my tutor's advice' → 'One of the career officers would be better.' The corrected version is the answer.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The options in this part are single abstract nouns ('communication', 'organisation'); the audio gives concrete behaviour. Practise labelling behaviour with the right abstract noun. 录音是具体行为,选项是抽象名词。
Q21/22 — 'communication'
Option A
communication
Audio wording
'I got better at explaining things and asserting my opinions, because I had to have weekly consultations … and give him a progress report'
Key paraphrase
communication = explaining + asserting opinions + reporting
Trap
The word 'communication' is never spoken.
Your turn
Q21/22 — 'organisation'
Option E
organisation
Audio wording
'Do you think you got any better at managing your time and prioritising things?' — 'Oh, definitely. There was so much pressure to meet the project deadline.'
Key paraphrase
organisation = time management + prioritising
Trap
Tim asks the question; Laura's 'Oh, definitely' is the confirmation. Never mark an answer on the question alone.
Your turn
Q23/24 — 'cost savings'
Option B
cost savings
Audio wording
'if they'd used a professional advertising agency … presumably they'd have paid a lot more?' — 'Oh, yes. I worked it out — it would have been 250 per cent more.'
Key paraphrase
cost savings = they would have paid 250% more otherwise
Trap
The saving is stated as a hypothetical (third conditional), not as a direct 'we saved X'.
Your turn
Q26 — 'discussing options'
Stage
discussing options
Audio wording
'I suppose I should ask my tutor's advice.' — 'One of the career officers would be better, they've got more knowledge about the jobs market'
Key paraphrase
the corrected suggestion is the answer
Trap
'personal tutor' is said first and is genuinely an option in the box — but it belongs to Q30.
Your turn
Q27 — 'getting updates'
Stage
getting updates
Audio wording
'it's best to be proactive, and get updates yourself by checking the website … Your mentor is supposed to keep you informed, but you can't rely on that.'
Key paraphrase
internet = checking the website
Trap
'mentor' is mentioned in the very same sentence — but explicitly as the unreliable option.
