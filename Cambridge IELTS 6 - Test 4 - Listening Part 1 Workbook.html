Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Booking a Computing Conference · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A woman telephones the Conference Centre Reservations desk to ask about a computing conference called "Future Directions in Computing" and to book her place. The clerk explains the registration fee, two accommodation options, and how to travel to the Centre from the station.

This is a classic Part 1 form/note-completion task: two speakers, a transactional phone call, and answers — mostly numbers, prices and short location details — that appear in the same order as the questions.

💡 Part 1 strategy: Before you listen, underline every gap and predict the answer type — a price, a length of time, a street name. Watch for numbers that get explained or qualified (e.g. a "discount" that turns out not to apply to her) — the true, final figure is always the answer.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 6  |  🎧 Test: 4, Section 1  |  ❓ Questions: 1–10

📝 Task type: Note/form completion — short answers (numbers, words)

🗣️ Setting: A phone call to a Conference Centre Reservations desk

👥 Speakers: Woman (caller, wants to attend a conference) · Clerk (reservations desk)

Task breakdown

Q1–2: Registration fee and payment method for the full three-day conference.

Q3–5: Two accommodation options — price per night and location.

Q6: What the clerk will post to her.

Q7–8: The Conference Centre's exact address/location.

Q9–10: Two ways to travel from the station to the Centre.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

IELTS Listening questions never use the exact same words as the audio. Study each contrast below, note the key paraphrase, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — The Registration Fee
Question wordingFull three-day fee: £______
Audio meaningThe clerk explains that attending Friday and Saturday only doesn't save money — the daily rate stays £35 — but if she books all three days together, the total comes to a fixed, lower amount and includes a free dinner.
Key paraphrase"altogether" / "the whole conference" = the combined 3-day price, not 3 × £35
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — Method of Payment
Question wordingPayment method: ______
Audio meaningThe clerk says the Centre cannot process card payments over the phone, so she must either post a cheque in advance or settle the bill in person when she arrives.
Key paraphrase"we don't take credit cards" = no card payment allowed → cheque (or pay on arrival)
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — Location of the Centre
Question wordingAddress: ______ Road, next to the ______
Audio meaningThe clerk gives directions by naming the road and describing a landmark building right beside the Centre, so callers can recognise it easily.
Key paraphrase"right at the end of the road" = at the very last point of South Park Road, beside the library
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — Getting There from the Station
Question wordingTravel options: taxi (£______) or bus number ______
Audio meaningThe clerk offers two alternatives: a short, slightly pricier taxi ride, or a regular, cheaper bus service that goes directly to the Centre without any changes.
Key paraphrase"runs every half hour, straight to the centre" = the bus is frequent and direct — no transfers needed
