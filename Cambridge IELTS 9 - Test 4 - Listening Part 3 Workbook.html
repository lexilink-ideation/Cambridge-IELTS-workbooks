Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Paul, a course tutor, interviews Kira, a former student who went straight into the third year of a Pharmacy degree. She describes the difficulty of writing critically, how approachable the lecturers are, her very heavy practical timetable, and her advice for other overseas students. Question types: multiple choice (Q21–22), sentence completion (Q23–25, ONE WORD ONLY) and short answer (Q26–30).

💡 Listening tip: Q23–25 are sentence completions with a strict ONE WORD ONLY limit, and each needs a specific word class — Q23 needs a verb after 'easier to', Q24 an adjective after 'more', Q25 a verb after 'that'. Predict the word class before you listen and you will not be caught by longer phrases.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The questions use formal wording; Kira speaks naturally. Practise the bridge.
Q22 — Succeeding with assignments
Option C
change her way of thinking
What you hear
'People expect you to have problems with the process of reading and writing but, in fact, it is more a question of altering your viewpoint towards academic study'
Key paraphrase
'altering your viewpoint' = change her way of thinking; 'but in fact' cancels options A and B
Your turn
Q25 — Why students make appointments
Sentence
students want to discuss things that worry them or that ______ them very much
What you hear
'usually to talk about something in the course that's worrying them, but sometimes just about something that might really interest them'
Key paraphrase
the sentence mirrors 'worrying… / interest…' → interest
Your turn
Q27 — Hospital frequency
Question
In the second semester how often did Kira work in a hospital?
What you hear
'every second day we went to one of the big hospitals'
Key paraphrase
'every second day' = every 2 days (write it as a frequency, not a duration)
Your turn
Q30 — What overseas students need
Question
In addition to the language, what must overseas students become familiar with?
What you hear
'the biggest problem for me was a lack of familiarity with the education system here'
Key paraphrase
'a lack of familiarity with X' → they need to become familiar with X = the education system
Your turn
