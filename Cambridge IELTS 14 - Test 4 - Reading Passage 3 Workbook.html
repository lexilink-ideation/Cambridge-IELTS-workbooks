📋 Passage Overview

How dangerous is marine debris really? Ecologist Chelsea Rochman and her colleagues reviewed more than a hundred scientific papers on ocean trash to check how many of the 'perceived threats' were actually proven. The passage examines their findings — including a surprising result about mussels — and what it means for how we study marine debris.

💡 Reading tip: Distinguish 'perceived' (believed) threats from 'proven' ones — the passage turns on this contrast. For True/False/Not Given, match the exact claim, not the general topic.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Plenty of studies have sounded alarm bells about the state of marine debris; Rochman and her colleagues set out to determine how many of those perceived risks are real.
Main ClauseRochman and her colleagues set out to determine how many of those perceived risks are real
Subordinate / Relativehow many of those perceived risks are real (embedded question)
Grammar NoteThe embedded question is the object of 'determine'; a semicolon links two related statements.
Simple MeaningMany studies warn about ocean trash; Rochman's team tried to find out how many warnings are true.
中文许多研究已就海洋垃圾的现状敲响警钟；罗克曼及其同事着手确定这些被认为的风险中有多少是真实的。
A study could show that certain seabirds eat plastic bags, and go on to warn that whole bird populations are at risk of dying out.
Main ClauseA study could show that … seabirds eat plastic bags, and go on to warn that whole bird populations are at risk of dying out
Subordinate / Relativethat certain seabirds eat plastic bags / that whole bird populations are at risk (two noun clauses)
Grammar NoteTwo 'that' clauses report the finding and the warning; 'go on to' shows a next step.
Simple MeaningA study might show birds eat plastic, then warn that whole bird groups could die out.
中文一项研究可能表明某些海鸟会吞食塑料袋，接着警告说整个鸟群都有灭绝的风险。
But the truth was that nobody had yet tested those perceived threats.
Main Clausethe truth was that nobody had yet tested those perceived threats
Subordinate / Relativethat nobody had yet tested those perceived threats (noun clause complement)
Grammar NotePast perfect ('had yet tested') shows the testing had not happened before that point.
Simple MeaningIn fact, no one had actually tested those assumed dangers yet.
中文但事实是，还没有人真正检验过这些被认为的威胁。
In 83 percent of cases, the perceived dangers of ocean trash were proven true.
Main Clausethe perceived dangers of ocean trash were proven true
Subordinate / RelativeIn 83 percent of cases (fronted adverbial)
Grammar NoteThe passive 'were proven true' reports the study's result; the fronted phrase gives the proportion.
Simple MeaningIn 83% of cases, the assumed dangers turned out to be real.
中文在83%的案例中，海洋垃圾被认为的危害被证实是真实的。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Perceived vs proven. The whole passage tests whether widely believed ('perceived') dangers are actually 'proven'. Keep this distinction in mind for every question.

Method and result. The team reviewed 100+ papers and 366 perceived threats, finding 83% proven true. Numbers and proportions are strong anchors for True/False/Not Given.

A surprising case. One study expected mussels to be harmed by eating plastic; the surprise result matters for the True/False items about mussels. Read the exact claim carefully.
