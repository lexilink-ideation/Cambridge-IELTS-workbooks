📋 Passage Overview

Roman tunnels. How the Romans borrowed the Persian qanat method and later the counter-excavation method to build water, road and mining tunnels — including the tools, the challenges of meeting in the middle, and how hard rock was cracked. Question types: diagram labelling (Q1–6), True / False / Not Given (Q7–10) and short-answer (Q11–13).

💡 Reading tip: For diagram labelling, look at what each label points to and find that exact feature in the text. Answers appear in the order the passage describes the process.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

In the early first millennium BCE, they introduced the qanat method, which consisted of placing posts over a hill in a straight line, to ensure that the tunnel kept to its route.
Main Clausethey introduced the qanat method
Subordinate / Relativewhich consisted of placing posts… (relative); to ensure that the tunnel kept to its route (purpose)
Grammar NoteRelative clause defines the method; 'to ensure that…' gives the purpose.
Simple MeaningThey created the qanat method: posts in a line kept the tunnel on course.
中文在公元前一千纪早期，他们引入了坎儿井法：在山上沿直线立桩，以确保隧道不偏离路线。
This method required greater planning and advanced knowledge of surveying, mathematics and geometry, as both ends of a tunnel had to meet correctly at the center of the mountain.
Main ClauseThis method required greater planning and advanced knowledge
Subordinate / Relativeas both ends of a tunnel had to meet correctly at the center of the mountain (reason)
Grammar Note'as' = 'because', giving the reason more skill was needed.
Simple MeaningDigging from both ends needed careful maths so the halves met in the middle.
中文这种方法需要更周密的规划和高深的测量、数学与几何知识，因为隧道两端必须在山体中央准确会合。
When the sole purpose of a tunnel was mineral extraction, construction required less planning, as the tunnel route was determined by the mineral vein.
Main Clauseconstruction required less planning
Subordinate / RelativeWhen the sole purpose… was mineral extraction (time/condition); as the tunnel route was determined by the mineral vein (reason)
Grammar NoteA 'when' condition + an 'as' reason clause frame the main statement.
Simple MeaningMining tunnels needed less planning because the ore itself set the route.
中文当隧道的唯一目的是采矿时，施工所需规划较少，因为隧道走向由矿脉决定。
When the rock was hard, the Romans employed a technique called fire quenching, which consisted of heating the rock with fire and then suddenly cooling it with cold water so that it would crack.
Main Clausethe Romans employed a technique called fire quenching
Subordinate / RelativeWhen the rock was hard (condition); which consisted of heating… and then… cooling it (relative); so that it would crack (result)
Grammar NoteCondition + relative describing the technique + 'so that' result.
Simple MeaningFor hard rock, they heated it then cooled it fast to make it crack.
中文当岩石坚硬时，罗马人采用“火淬法”：先用火加热岩石，再突然用冷水冷却，使其开裂。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Two methods compared: the qanat method (vertical shafts, faster, more straightforward) versus counter-excavation (from both ends, needing surveying and maths). Diagram and TFNG answers depend on keeping the two apart.

Cause → effect (speed): shafts let workers dig from many points → qanat is faster; hard rock → fire quenching → very slow progress (30 cm/day). Follow how each factor changes the timescale.

Evidence type: the Saldae error is recorded in an inscription, not a book — a classic TFNG trap. Always check what kind of source the passage names.
