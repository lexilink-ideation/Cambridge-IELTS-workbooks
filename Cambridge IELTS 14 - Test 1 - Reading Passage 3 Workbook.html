📋 Passage Overview

Motivational factors and the hospitality industry. This academic passage asks how hotels can develop and keep high-performing staff. It reviews Human Resource Management (HRM) research (Pfeifer, Lucas) and a study by Tews, Michel and Stafford on 'fun at work' in a restaurant chain, discussing working conditions, job satisfaction, staff turnover and performance.

💡 Reading tip: Yes/No/Not Given tests the writer's claims/opinions, not just facts. Ask: does the writer actually state this view? If the text is silent, choose NOT GIVEN.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

A critical ingredient in the success of hotels is developing and maintaining superior performance from their employees.
Main ClauseA critical ingredient in the success of hotels is developing and maintaining superior performance
Subordinate / Relativefrom their employees (prepositional phrase)
Grammar NoteA subject-complement sentence: X is Y. The gerunds 'developing and maintaining' name the ingredient.
Simple MeaningHotels succeed largely by building and keeping high staff performance.
中文酒店成功的一个关键因素，是培养并保持员工的卓越表现。
Pfeifer emphasizes that in order to succeed in a global business environment, organizations must make investment in HRM.
Main ClausePfeifer emphasizes that … organizations must make investment in HRM
Subordinate / Relativein order to succeed in a global business environment (purpose adverbial)
Grammar Note'that…' is a noun clause reporting Pfeifer's view; 'in order to' states the purpose.
Simple MeaningPfeifer says firms must invest in HRM to succeed globally.
中文Pfeifer 强调，要在全球商业环境中取得成功，组织必须对人力资源管理进行投资。
By contrast, the service sector, and more specifically hotels, has traditionally not extended these practices to address basic employee needs.
Main Clausethe service sector … has traditionally not extended these practices
Subordinate / Relativeto address basic employee needs (infinitive of purpose)
Grammar Note'By contrast' signals a shift from the previous idea; 'has not extended' is present perfect.
Simple MeaningUnlike other sectors, hotels have usually not used these practices to meet staff's basic needs.
中文相比之下，服务行业，尤其是酒店业，历来没有把这些做法延伸到满足员工的基本需求上。
They discovered that activities designed for staff to have fun improved their performance, and that management involvement led to lower turnover.
Main ClauseThey discovered that … activities … improved their performance … and that management involvement led to lower turnover
Subordinate / Relativedesigned for staff to have fun (reduced relative); two 'that' clauses
Grammar NoteTwo noun clauses ('that…') are the objects of 'discovered', joined by 'and'.
Simple MeaningThey found fun activities raised performance, and manager involvement reduced staff leaving.
中文他们发现，为员工设计的趣味活动提升了其表现，而管理层的参与则降低了员工流动率。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Problem → solution. The passage sets up a problem (how to get and keep great staff) and reviews possible solutions (better working conditions, HRM investment, fun at work). Track which paragraph offers which solution.

Citing sources. Named researchers (Pfeifer 1994, Lucas 2002, Tews/Michel/Stafford) each contribute a claim. Matching-features and Y/N/NG questions often test who said what.

Watch 'turnover'. Here 'turnover' means the rate staff leave, not sales. The summary (Q36-40) reports the restaurant study: research on staff in a chain of restaurants; fun improved performance; management involvement lowered turnover; and it links to goals and personal characteristics.
