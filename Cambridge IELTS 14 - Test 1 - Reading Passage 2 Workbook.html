📋 Passage Overview

The growth of bike-sharing schemes around the world. The passage traces the history of urban bike-sharing from the 1965 Witte Fietsenplan in Amsterdam (created by the activist group Provo and engineer Luud Schimmelpennink) through later attempts and a 1999 scheme, examining why early schemes failed and how the idea eventually spread worldwide.

💡 Reading tip: 'Which paragraph contains…' questions do not follow text order. Scan for the specific detail (a date, a name, an amount) rather than reading each paragraph fully.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

The original idea for an urban bike-sharing scheme dates back to a summer's day in Amsterdam in 1965.
Main ClauseThe original idea … dates back to a summer's day in Amsterdam in 1965
Subordinate / Relativefor an urban bike-sharing scheme (prepositional phrase modifying 'idea')
Grammar Note'dates back to' + a time expression sets the historical starting point.
Simple MeaningThe idea of city bike-sharing began on a summer day in Amsterdam in 1965.
中文城市共享单车最初的想法可以追溯到1965年阿姆斯特丹的一个夏日。
They believed the scheme, which was known as the Witte Fietsenplan, was an answer to the perceived threats of air pollution and consumerism.
Main ClauseThey believed the scheme … was an answer to the perceived threats of air pollution and consumerism
Subordinate / Relativewhich was known as the Witte Fietsenplan (non-defining relative clause)
Grammar NoteThe relative clause adds the scheme's name; the commas mark extra, non-essential information.
Simple MeaningThey thought the scheme (called the Witte Fietsenplan) solved the dangers of pollution and consumerism.
中文他们认为这个被称为“白色自行车计划”的方案，能应对空气污染和消费主义带来的威胁。
The bikes were then left unlocked at various locations around the city, to be used by anyone in need of transport.
Main ClauseThe bikes were then left unlocked at various locations around the city
Subordinate / Relativeto be used by anyone in need of transport (infinitive of purpose)
Grammar Note'to be used…' explains the purpose; the passive keeps focus on the bikes.
Simple MeaningThey left the bikes unlocked around the city so anyone who needed transport could use them.
中文随后这些自行车被不上锁地放在城市各处，供任何需要出行的人使用。
He recalls how the scheme succeeded in attracting a great deal of attention.
Main ClauseHe recalls how the scheme succeeded in attracting a great deal of attention
Subordinate / Relativehow the scheme succeeded… (noun clause, object of 'recalls')
Grammar NoteThe 'how' clause works as the object of the verb 'recalls'.
Simple MeaningHe remembers that the scheme got a lot of attention.
中文他回忆说，这个方案成功地吸引了大量关注。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology. The passage is broadly time-ordered (1965 → later attempts → 1999 → today). Dates and years are strong anchors for locating information questions.

Cause of failure. Look for reasons schemes failed: bikes were misused/stolen, a proposal was turned down by a government department, or a partner withdrew support. These map directly to the 'Choose TWO letters' questions.

Summary of the 1965 scheme. The final summary (Q23-26) retells paragraph A: Provo were activists; the threats were pollution and consumerism; they gave out leaflets; and the police intervened. Track the paraphrase from text to summary.
