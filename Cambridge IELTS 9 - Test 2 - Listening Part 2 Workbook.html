📋 Quick Review

A speaker welcomes new members to a Wildlife Club near London. She describes three local parks and open spaces (Halland Common, Holt Island, Longfield Country Park), then the week's activities at Longfield, and finally labels a map of the new Hinchingbrooke Park. Question types: table completion (Q11–13), multiple choice (Q14–16) and map labelling (Q17–20).

💡 Listening tip: For the map (Q17–20), orient yourself before the recording: find the two gates, the lake, the paths and the compass. Then track the exact position language — in the north west, to the west of it, in the southern part, leading off from the path, the circular area surrounded by paths, between two paths. Prepositions, not nouns, carry the answers.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 2 hides answers inside long descriptive sentences. Practise reducing them to the note word.
Q11 — Holt Island
Table cell
many different ______
What you hear
'Holt Island, which is noted for its great range of trees'
Key paraphrase
'a great range of' = 'many different' → trees
Your turn
Q12 — Opening days
Table cell
between ______ and ______
What you hear
'The island is only open to the public from Friday to Sunday'
Key paraphrase
'from X to Y' = 'between X and Y' → Friday / Sunday
Your turn
Q15 — Wednesday activity
Option B
visitors should book in advance
What you hear
'We keep to a small number of people in the group, so… you'll need to phone the park ranger a few days ahead'
Key paraphrase
'phone a few days ahead' = book in advance → B
Your turn
Q19 — Flower garden
Map task
flower garden = letter ___
What you hear
'you can go to the flower garden: that's the circular area on the map surrounded by paths'
Key paraphrase
shape ('circular') + relation ('surrounded by paths') identify the letter — always use BOTH clues
Your turn
