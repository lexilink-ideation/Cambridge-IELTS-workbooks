Cambridge IELTS 16 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A woman phones Picturerep, a company that copies old photos to digital format, and asks an employee about the service — how to send the photos, the price, payment, folders, improvements and how long it takes. Note completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: Watch for a spoken number that is a price (£195) and for words the speaker says can or can't be done — the answer is often the thing that CAN'T (e.g. 'focus').

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The notes paraphrase the phone call. Train the swap between note and audio.
The price (Q2)
Question note
basic service (300–400 photos): £ ___
What you hear
'We charge £195 for 300 to 400 photos for the basic service'
Key paraphrase
one clear number for the basic service = 195
Your turn
What they can't do (Q8)
Question note
they cannot improve photos that are not in ___
What you hear
'aren't properly in focus… that's one thing we can't do'
Key paraphrase
'that's one thing we can't do' flags the answer = focus
Your turn
The box (Q10)
Question note
best to send photos in a ___ box
What you hear
'a strong cardboard box, that's best. Plastic ones sometimes break in the post'
Key paraphrase
the recommended box is cardboard, not plastic
Your turn
