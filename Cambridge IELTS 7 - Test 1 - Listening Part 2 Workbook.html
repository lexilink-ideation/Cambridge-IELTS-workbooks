📋 Quick Review

Part 2 is a monologue: a single speaker gives a talk to an audience about "PS Camping," a company that organises camping holidays across Europe. The speaker explains the company's history, where its campsites are located, the family-friendly activities on offer, its insurance policy, and the equipment/facilities available in and around the tents.

This is a typical "informative talk" Part 2 — expect a mix of multiple-choice questions about facts (history, location, rules) and a matching task about facilities.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In monologue talks, the speaker usually follows the order of the questions on the page. Watch for numbers that are corrected or qualified (e.g. "twenty-five years... twenty years ago... fifteen years") — these are common Part 2 traps where several similar numbers appear close together.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Q12 (country with most sites)
Question wording"The company has most camping sites in..."
Audio meaning"France is where we have the majority of sites."
Key paraphrase"the majority of" = "most"
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Q15 (insurance)
Question wording"The holiday insurance... must be taken out at the time of booking."
Audio meaning"we require that you arrange this when you make your holiday reservation."
Key paraphrase"make your holiday reservation" = "at the time of booking"
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Q16 (recommend a friend)
Question wording"Customers who recommend PS Camping to friends will receive..."
Audio meaning"In return, we'll send you a thank-you present, which you can choose from a list of high-quality items."
Key paraphrase"a thank-you present" = "a free gift"
