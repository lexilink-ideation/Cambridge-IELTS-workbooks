📋 Quick Review

A seminar talk on the future of management over the next ten years. The speaker moves through five clear headings: business markets, external influences, business structures, management styles and changes in the economy.

这是一篇商科讲座。五个小标题就是路标,每个标题下2–3个答案,顺序完全一致。答案全部是常见商务名词/形容词。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY. The speaker signposts every new heading — 'One area I want to mention is business markets…', 'I think businesses are becoming more open to external influences…', 'the structure of companies is changing…', 'Management styles will almost certainly continue to change…'. If you hear a heading, look at the next bullet on your paper immediately.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Section 4 notes are compressed. The speaker gives a full sentence with examples; the note keeps only the skeleton. Practise moving between the two. 讲座给的是完整句子和例子,笔记只保留骨架。
Q31 — 'greater competition among companies'
Note wording
greater ___ among companies
Audio wording
'a major increase in competition, with companies from all round the world trying to sell similar products'
Key paraphrase
greater = a major increase in; among companies = from all round the world
Trap
'Consumers will have much more choice' follows — a result, not the answer.
Your turn
Q32 — 'large global companies'
Note wording
increase in power of large ___ companies
Audio wording
'governments are actually losing power to major global corporations'
Key paraphrase
increase in power of companies = governments losing power to them; companies = corporations
Trap
The audio states the opposite side (governments lose) — you must flip it.
Your turn
Q34 — 'more discussion with customers'
Note wording
more discussion with ___ before making business decisions
Audio wording
'companies are consulting customers more and more before making their business decisions'
Key paraphrase
discussion with = consulting
Trap
'finding out what they want and providing it' comes next — a description, not the answer word.
Your turn
Q36 — 'a particular project'
Note wording
more teams will be formed to work on a particular ___
Audio wording
'a greater emphasis within companies on teams created with a specific project in mind'
Key paraphrase
a particular = a specific; formed = created
Trap
'disbanded' and 'new ones formed' follow — tempting but not the gap word.
Your turn
Q39 — 'women taking senior roles'
Note wording
changes influenced by ___ taking senior roles
Audio wording
'caused above all by more women moving into senior management positions'
Key paraphrase
influenced by = caused by; taking senior roles = moving into senior management positions
Trap
The previous sentence says senior managers are currently 'mainly men in their fifties and sixties' — do not write 'men'.
Your turn
