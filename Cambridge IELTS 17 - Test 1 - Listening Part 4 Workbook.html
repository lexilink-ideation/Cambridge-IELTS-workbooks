📋 Quick Review

An academic talk on labyrinths — winding spiral paths with a single route to the centre. The lecturer contrasts labyrinths with mazes, traces early examples across cultures, and explains why labyrinths are used today for wellbeing. Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Part 4 is a monologue with no repetition. Use the sub-headings on the note sheet to keep your place, and predict the part of speech for each gap — most answers here are single nouns.
