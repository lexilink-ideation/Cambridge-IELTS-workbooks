Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 15 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Ruth and her partner, discuss a project on birth order — how a child's position in the family may shape their personality. First they match personality traits to family positions (Q21–26), then answer multiple-choice questions about the research and their own experience of sibling rivalry (Q27–30).

💡 Listening tip: In matching, the eight options stay on screen but are said in a different order and reworded. Cross off each letter as it is used. For 'Choose TWO', both speakers must agree — listen for 'yes, definitely' or 'me too'.
