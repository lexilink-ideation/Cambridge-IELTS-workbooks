📋 Passage Overview

'Cork' — a factual passage about the bark of the cork oak (Quercus suber). It explains the material's properties, where the trees grow, the slow patient cycle of harvesting by hand, and why screw caps have taken over from cork stoppers — before arguing that cork's sustainability makes its future look promising again. Question types: TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q1–5) and note completion (Q6–13).

💡 Reading tip: For 'ONE WORD ONLY' notes, copy the word exactly as printed — no plural, no tense change. For T/F/NG, remember that a superlative or absolute claim in the statement ('the thickest of any living tree', 'the only way') must be matched by an equally absolute statement in the text.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Developed most probably as a defence against forest fires, the bark of the cork oak has a particular cellular structure — with about 40 million cells per cubic centimetre — that technology has never succeeded in replicating.
Main Clausethe bark of the cork oak has a particular cellular structure
Subordinate / Relative'Developed most probably as a defence against forest fires' = reduced participle clause (= 'which was probably developed…'); 'that technology has never succeeded in replicating' = relative clause defining the structure; the dashes hold an added statistic.
Grammar NoteFronted past participle + a defining relative clause with the object omitted ('that … replicating' = replicating the structure).
Simple MeaningThe bark probably evolved to protect the tree from fire. Its cell structure is unique and nobody has managed to copy it artificially.
中文软木橡树的树皮很可能是作为抵御森林火灾的防护而形成的,它拥有一种特殊的细胞结构——每立方厘米约四千万个细胞——这是技术至今仍无法复制的。
Like grape vines, the trees thrive in poor soil, putting down deep roots in search of moisture and nutrients.
Main Clausethe trees thrive in poor soil
Subordinate / Relative'Like grape vines' = comparison phrase; 'putting down deep roots in search of moisture and nutrients' = present participle clause showing how/result.
Grammar NoteA simile opener plus an '-ing' clause that explains the manner of thriving.
Simple MeaningLike vines, cork oaks grow well in bad soil because their roots go deep for water and food.
中文与葡萄藤一样,这些树在贫瘠的土壤中也能茁壮生长,它们把根深深扎下去寻找水分和养分。
Southern Portugal's Alentejo region meets all of these requirements, which explains why, by the early 20th century, this region had become the world's largest producer of cork.
Main ClauseSouthern Portugal's Alentejo region meets all of these requirements
Subordinate / Relative'which explains why… this region had become…' = non-defining relative clause referring to the whole first idea; 'by the early 20th century' = time phrase inside it.
Grammar NoteSentential 'which' (refers to the whole clause, not one noun) + past perfect 'had become' for a point completed before that time.
Simple MeaningAlentejo has the right climate and soil, so by about 1900 it was already the world's biggest cork producer.
中文葡萄牙南部的阿连特茹地区满足所有这些条件,这解释了为什么到20世纪初,该地区已成为世界最大的软木产地。
No mechanical means of stripping cork bark has been invented, so the job is done by teams of highly skilled workers.
Main ClauseNo mechanical means of stripping cork bark has been invented / the job is done by teams of highly skilled workers
Subordinate / RelativeTwo independent clauses joined by 'so' (result).
Grammar NoteNegative subject 'No mechanical means' takes a singular verb 'has'; both clauses are passive.
Simple MeaningThere is no machine that can strip cork, so skilled people do it by hand.
中文目前尚未发明剥取软木树皮的机械方法,因此这项工作由技术高超的工人团队手工完成。
The tiniest concentrations — as little as three or four parts to a trillion — can spoil the taste of the product contained in the bottle.
Main ClauseThe tiniest concentrations can spoil the taste of the product
Subordinate / Relative'as little as three or four parts to a trillion' = parenthetical quantity; 'contained in the bottle' = reduced relative clause (= 'which is contained in the bottle').
Grammar NoteSuperlative subject + a reduced passive relative clause modifying 'product'.
Simple MeaningEven a very small amount of TCA can ruin how the drink in the bottle tastes.
中文即使是最微小的浓度——低至万亿分之三或四——也能破坏瓶中产品的味道。
Secondly — and very importantly — cork is a sustainable product that can be recycled without difficulty.
Main Clausecork is a sustainable product
Subordinate / Relative'that can be recycled without difficulty' = defining relative clause.
Grammar NoteListing adverb 'Secondly' + a writer's comment inserted between dashes to signal emphasis.
Simple MeaningCork is environmentally friendly and easy to recycle — and the writer thinks this matters a lot.
中文其次——而且非常重要的是——软木是一种可持续的产品,可以毫不费力地回收利用。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition first. The passage opens by defining cork ('the thick bark of the cork oak tree') and listing its properties. Whenever a passage begins with a definition, expect the following paragraphs to prove or illustrate each listed property — 'buoyant' is later explained by the air-filled cells, 'elastic' by the squash-and-spring-back image.

Cause and effect. Notice the chain: poor soil + 400–800 mm rain → Alentejo suits cork oaks → Alentejo becomes the world's largest producer. Signals to watch: 'which explains why', 'due to', 'The result has been'.

Chronology / process. The harvesting paragraph is a time-ordered process: vertical cuts → lever the bark away → dry on the ground four months → boil in factories → 60% becomes stoppers. Process paragraphs are where 'ONE WORD ONLY' answers hide, so read the sequence markers ('First', 'then', 'before being taken').

Contrast and evaluation. The final two paragraphs set screw caps against cork: screw caps win on price and convenience ('cheaper to manufacture', 'more convenient'), cork wins on image, recyclability and environment ('however', 'Firstly', 'Secondly', 'Moreover'). The writer's own attitude appears in the last clause: 'looks promising'.
