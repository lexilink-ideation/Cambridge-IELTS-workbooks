📋 Passage Overview

Visual Symbols and the Blind describes experiments testing whether blind people understand visual symbols the same way sighted people do. In Part 1, blind subjects interpret lines of motion on drawings of wheels; in Part 2 they match abstract shapes (circle/square) to word pairs such as soft–hard. The results suggest the blind interpret these symbols much like the sighted.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

This fact was drawn to my attention dramatically when a blind woman in one of my investigations decided on her own initiative to draw a wheel as it was spinning.
Main ClauseThis fact was drawn to my attention dramatically
Subordinate / Relativewhen a blind woman … decided on her own initiative to draw a wheel as it was spinning (time clause)
Grammar Notepassive 'was drawn to my attention'; 'on her own initiative' = without being asked; 'as' = while.
Simple MeaningI noticed this strongly when a blind woman chose, by herself, to draw a spinning wheel.
中文当我研究中的一位盲人女性主动决定画出一个正在旋转的轮子时，这一事实戏剧性地引起了我的注意。
So I decided to test whether various lines of motion were apt ways of showing movement or if they were merely idiosyncratic marks.
Main ClauseSo I decided to test
Subordinate / Relativewhether … were apt ways of showing movement or if they were merely idiosyncratic marks (embedded question)
Grammar Note'whether … or if …' presents two alternatives inside the verb 'test'.
Simple MeaningSo I tested whether the motion lines really showed movement, or were just personal marks.
中文于是我决定检验：各种运动线到底是恰当地表现了运动，还是仅仅是个人独有的标记。
Because motion devices are unfamiliar to the blind, the task I gave them involved some problem solving.
Main Clausethe task I gave them involved some problem solving
Subordinate / RelativeBecause motion devices are unfamiliar to the blind (reason clause); (that) I gave them (contact relative)
Grammar Note'Because…' front reason; 'the task I gave them' omits 'that' (a contact clause).
Simple MeaningSince the blind don't know motion symbols, my task made them solve a problem.
中文由于运动符号对盲人来说是陌生的，我给他们的任务需要一定的解决问题的能力。
Thus, we concluded that the blind interpret abstract shapes as sighted people do.
Main Clausewe concluded that the blind interpret abstract shapes as sighted people do
Subordinate / Relativeas sighted people do (clause of comparison / manner)
Grammar Note'as … do' = in the same way as; 'Thus' signals the conclusion.
Simple MeaningSo we decided the blind understand abstract shapes the same way sighted people do.
中文因此，我们得出结论：盲人对抽象形状的理解与视力正常者相同。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Anecdote → hypothesis → experiment: the blind woman's spontaneous drawing (anecdote) leads the writer to design a controlled test with a sighted control group.

Evidence & consensus: results are reported as percentages of agreement; the key finding is that blind and sighted subjects reached the same favoured answers.

Generalisation: from wheels (Part 1) the writer moves to abstract shapes (Part 2) to build a broader claim about visual metaphor.

Author's conclusion: signalled by 'Thus, we concluded…' — the blind interpret abstract shapes as the sighted do (matches Q40).
