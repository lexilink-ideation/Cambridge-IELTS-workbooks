📋 Quick Review

A talk about three concerts planned for a university arts festival on the theme of links between the UK and Australia. The speaker focuses on three Australian composers — Liza Lim, Ross Edwards and Carl Vine — and their music. This is a note-completion task: ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: With three named people, keep track of who is being described. Predict the part of speech for each gap — an instrument, an adjective, or a field of study.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

A synonym or definition usually signals the gap word. Train the swap.
Vine's studies
Question note
Vine first went to university to study ___
What you hear
'he went to university to study physics, before changing to composition'
Key paraphrase
the first subject was physics; 'before changing' shows he switched later
Your turn
Edwards's theme
Question note
His music celebrates the ___ of cultures in Australia
What you hear
'a celebration of the diversity of cultures that Australia can be proud of'
Key paraphrase
the noun is diversity — the range/variety of cultures
Your turn
