Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 19 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Marie and Colin, discuss their food-science projects — Marie is making a vegan egg alternative from chickpeas, and Colin is reusing stale bread with 3-D printing. They then talk about food-label sensors and several food trends, giving opinions on each. Expect two 'choose TWO' tasks (Q21–24) and a matching-opinions task (Q25–30).

💡 Listening tip: In 'choose TWO letters' tasks, the two answers may be far apart and both speakers may raise ideas that are then dropped. In the matching task, listen for the opinion attached to each trend, not just whether the trend is mentioned.
