📋 Quick Review

A guide welcomes visitors to Fiddy Working Heritage Farm, an open-air museum showing English country life at the end of the nineteenth century. First he gives safety advice (Q11–14), then he describes the layout of the site so visitors can find their way (Q15–20 map labelling).

前半段是“注意事项”笔记填空,后半段是地图题——最考验方位词。

💡 Listening tip: For the map, mark where you are first, then follow the speaker's direction words: opposite, beside, just before, on the first bend, branches off to the right, cross the bridge. The answers come in question order, so if you lose one, jump to the next landmark.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

On map questions the paraphrase is usually a direction, not a synonym. Train the direction language.
Q13 — wear shoes
Question note
wear ___
What you hear
'you might slip if you're wearing sandals so I'm glad to see you're all wearing shoes'
Key paraphrase
'sandals' is the negative example; the advice word is shoes
Your turn
Q14 — dogs
Question note
not bring ___ into the farm, with certain exceptions
What you hear
'they'll have to stay in the car park unless they're guide dogs'
Key paraphrase
'unless they're guide dogs' = 'with certain exceptions' → dogs
Your turn
Q16 — the maze
Question note
16 Maze
What you hear
'It's opposite the New Barn, beside the side path that branches off to the right just over there'
Key paraphrase
two clues combined: opposite the New Barn + beside the right-hand side path
Your turn
Q17 — the cafe
Question note
17 Cafe
What you hear
'go towards the bridge and turn right just before it … the cafe's on the first bend you come to'
Key paraphrase
'just before it' means you do not cross the bridge — a very common trap
Your turn
Q19 — covered picnic area
Question note
19 Covered picnic area
What you hear
'just after you cross the bridge there's a covered picnic spot on the right'
Key paraphrase
'picnic spot' = 'picnic area'; note the contrast with picnicking in the field
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

These chunks are all direction language. Write them exactly — the small words carry the meaning.
1.The car park is on your left.
2.It's opposite the New Barn, beside the side path.
3.Go towards the bridge and turn right just before it.
4.The cafe's on the first bend you come to.
5.You'll come to the Black Barn just where the path first bends.
6.Just after you cross the bridge there's a covered picnic spot on the right.
7.Walk along the footpath through the field to the left of the farmyard.
8.Please don't frighten or injure the animals.
