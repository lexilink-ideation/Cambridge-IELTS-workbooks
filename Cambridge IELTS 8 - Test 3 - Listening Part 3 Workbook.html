Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 8 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A tutor interviews Paul about his work experience in South America and helps him choose course modules. Paul explains why he went, the voluntary project he chose, why he switched projects, what he learnt in the village, and his views on his manager and pay. They then discuss four modules and whether he will take each one. It is a two-speaker discussion with multiple-choice and a matching (will / might / won’t) task.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Track reasons and decisions. For the module-matching task (Q27–30) decide, for each one, whether Paul will (A), might (B) or won’t (C) take it — listen past first reactions to his final choice (‘I’ll put that down as a definite’, ‘I’d much sooner do something else’).

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ21: Paul went to South America because he wanted …
What you hear / read"my main idea was to find out more about the way people lived there. My spoken Spanish was already pretty good."
Key paraphrase‘learn about Latin American life’ = the way people lived → answer C (not to improve Spanish).
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptQ23: Why did Paul change project?
What you hear / read"I never really knew whether I’d be needed … I wanted something that had more of a proper structure."
Key paraphrase‘his first job was not well organised’ = lacked structure → answer A (not boring or too hard).
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptQ25: What does Paul say about his manager?
What you hear / read"he gave me lots of advice and guidance."
Key paraphrase‘very supportive’ = gave advice and guidance → answer C.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4
Question / promptQ27: Gender Studies module …
What you hear / read"If it was to do with people in the villages rather than those in the public sphere, I would."
Key paraphraseThe module is about the public sphere, so Paul won’t do it → C.
