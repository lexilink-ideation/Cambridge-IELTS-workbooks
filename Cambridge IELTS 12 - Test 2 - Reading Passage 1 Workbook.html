📋 Passage Overview

'The risks agriculture faces in developing countries' — a synthesis of an online debate. Nine paragraphs (A–I) report what named experts said about the risks smallholder farmers face and how to reduce them: state intervention, private insurance, climate-resilient crops, collective action and direct selling. Question types: matching information (Q1–3), matching people (Q4–9) and two 'choose TWO' tasks.

💡 Reading tip: In a 'matching people' task, first highlight every name in the passage — they are the only anchors that matter. Then read only the sentence attached to each name. The instruction 'NB You may use any letter more than once' means Murphy and others will appear twice.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Two things distinguish food production from all other productive activities: first, every single person needs food each day and has a right to it; and second, it is hugely dependent on nature.
Main ClauseTwo things distinguish food production from all other productive activities
Subordinate / RelativeThe colon introduces two parallel clauses ('first… and second…') that name the two things.
Grammar NoteColon + enumerated parallel structure. The colon means 'here they are'.
Simple MeaningFood production is unlike any other industry for two reasons: everyone needs food daily and has a right to it, and it depends heavily on nature.
中文有两点使粮食生产区别于所有其他生产活动:第一,每个人每天都需要食物并有权获得食物;第二,它极度依赖自然。
However, smallholder farmers in developing countries must in addition deal with adverse environments, both natural, in terms of soil quality, rainfall, etc., and human, in terms of infrastructure, financial systems, markets, knowledge and technology.
Main Clausesmallholder farmers in developing countries must in addition deal with adverse environments
Subordinate / Relative'both natural… and human…' = a correlative pair modifying 'environments'; each half is expanded by 'in terms of…'.
Grammar Note'both … and …' with heavy post-modification; 'in addition' signals an extra burden beyond the risks all farmers face.
Simple MeaningOn top of the usual risks, small farmers in poor countries also face bad natural conditions and bad human systems.
中文然而,发展中国家的小农户还必须应对不利的环境,既包括自然方面(土壤质量、降雨等),也包括人为方面(基础设施、金融体系、市场、知识与技术)。
Sophia Murphy suggested that the procurement and holding of stocks by governments can also help mitigate wild swings in food prices by alleviating uncertainties about market supply.
Main ClauseSophia Murphy suggested …
Subordinate / Relative'that the procurement and holding of stocks by governments can also help mitigate wild swings in food prices' = noun clause; 'by alleviating uncertainties about market supply' = means phrase.
Grammar NoteReporting verb + that-clause + 'by + -ing' showing method.
Simple MeaningMurphy said governments buying and storing food can steady prices, because it removes doubt about supply.
中文索菲亚·墨菲认为,政府采购并储备粮食也有助于缓解粮价的剧烈波动,因为这消除了市场供应方面的不确定性。
However, some commentators responded that cash transfers to poor families do not necessarily translate into increased food security, as these programmes do not always strengthen food production or raise incomes.
Main Clausesome commentators responded …
Subordinate / Relative'that cash transfers… do not necessarily translate into increased food security' = noun clause; 'as these programmes do not always strengthen food production or raise incomes' = reason clause.
Grammar Note'not necessarily' = a hedge (sometimes yes, sometimes no); 'as' here means 'because'.
Simple MeaningSome people replied that giving money to poor families does not always mean more food security, because it may not increase production or income.
中文然而,一些评论者回应说,向贫困家庭发放现金并不一定能转化为粮食安全的提升,因为这些项目未必能加强粮食生产或提高收入。
Kabir warned that financial support schemes often encourage the adoption of high-input agricultural practices, which in the medium term may raise production costs beyond the value of their harvests.
Main ClauseKabir warned …
Subordinate / Relative'that financial support schemes often encourage the adoption of high-input agricultural practices' = noun clause; 'which… may raise production costs beyond the value of their harvests' = non-defining relative clause referring to those practices.
Grammar NoteNested clauses: that-clause containing a which-clause. 'in the medium term' = after a few years.
Simple MeaningKabir warned that financial help pushes farmers into expensive farming methods that may eventually cost more than the crops are worth.
中文卡比尔警告说,资金支持计划往往鼓励采用高投入的农业方式,而这在中期可能使生产成本超过收成的价值。
One commentator, Giel Ton, warned that collective action does not come as a free good.
Main ClauseOne commentator, Giel Ton, warned …
Subordinate / Relative'Giel Ton' = appositive naming 'One commentator'; 'that collective action does not come as a free good' = noun clause.
Grammar NoteAppositive between commas + that-clause. 'a free good' is an economics term for something with no cost.
Simple MeaningGiel Ton warned that working together as a group is not free — it costs time, effort and money.
中文一位评论者希尔·顿警告说,集体行动并非免费之物。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Problem → causes → solutions. Paragraphs A–C set out what makes food production unusual and vulnerable; D–I list proposed remedies. Once you see that D onwards is a solutions list, each paragraph becomes one solution type: state services (D), welfare and subsidies (E), private financial tools (F), climate-resilient crops (G), collective action (H), direct selling (I).

Claim and counter-claim. This is a debate synthesis, so almost every proposal is immediately answered. 'However, some commentators responded that…'; 'Kabir warned that…'; 'Others contended that…'. When you meet 'However' or 'warned', expect the opposite view — and expect a matching-people question there.

Contrast: all farmers vs smallholders. Paragraph B carefully separates risks 'farmers everywhere face' from what smallholders 'must in addition deal with'. That distinction is exactly what Q2 tests ('challenges faced only by farmers in certain parts of the world').

Cause and effect chains. Climate change → lower yields + more extreme weather → greater smallholder vulnerability → the solution of resilient crop varieties. Following the arrows makes the 'choose TWO' answers (D: effects of changing weather patterns) easy to confirm.

Cost qualification. Ton's remark that 'collective action does not come as a free good. It takes time, effort and money to organise, build trust and to experiment' is the passage's one note about the difficulty of co-operation — the exact target of Q3.
