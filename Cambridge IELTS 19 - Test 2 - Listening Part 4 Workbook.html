📋 Quick Review

A student presents a project on tardigrades — microscopic animals also called 'water bears' or 'moss piglets'. The talk covers their names, physical appearance, habitat, their remarkable survival state (cryptobiosis), feeding, and conservation status. Note completion — ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Science talks are packed with detail. Follow the on-paper headings (Physical appearance, Habitat, Cryptobiosis, Feeding) to stay oriented, and don't be distracted by the many numbers — only some are answers.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The notes compress the talk; match the paraphrase to the exact word.
Body fluid (Q34)
Question note
body filled with a liquid that carries both ___ and blood
What you hear
'oxygen and also blood are transported in a fluid that fills the cavity of the body'
Key paraphrase
transported = carries; the missing item is oxygen
Your turn
DNA protection (Q37)
Question note
A type of ___ ensures their DNA is not damaged.
What you hear
'tardigrades produce a protein that protects their DNA'
Key paraphrase
protects = ensures not damaged → protein
Your turn
