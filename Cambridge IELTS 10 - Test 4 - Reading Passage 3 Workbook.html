Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 4 — Reading Passage 3: When Evolution Runs Backwards

Practice Workbook

📋 Passage Overview

For a century biologists accepted Dollo's law — that evolution cannot run backwards. But atavisms (evolutionary throwbacks) keep appearing: a whale with leg bones, salamanders that regained metamorphosis, lizards that re-evolved toes. Raff calculated that 'silent genes' can survive 6–10 million years; Wagner found examples that break that limit, and the answer may lie in embryonic development.

这是一篇进化生物学文章,结构是:旧定律 → 反例 → 遗传学解释 → 解释的时间上限 → 突破上限的新证据 → 新解释(胚胎)。

💡 Reading tip: Q32–36 is sentence-ending matching. Every ending is a noun phrase, so grammar will not separate them — you must match by meaning. Do the easy ones first: Q35 ('shark and killer whale') is found instantly by scanning, and Q33 ('opposing views') must be the option naming two people.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

For the better part of a century, most biologists have been reluctant to use those words, mindful of a principle of evolution that says 'evolution cannot run backwards'.
Main Clausemost biologists have been reluctant to use those words
Subordinate / Relative'For the better part of a century' = time phrase; 'mindful of a principle of evolution' = adjective phrase of reason; 'that says 'evolution cannot run backwards'' = relative clause
Grammar NoteAn adjective phrase ('mindful of…') expresses reason without a conjunction. This sentence is the source of Q32.
Simple MeaningFor almost a hundred years, biologists avoided the term because of the rule that evolution cannot go backwards.
中文近一个世纪以来,多数生物学家都不愿使用这些词,因为他们顾及那条“进化不能倒退”的进化学原则。
In 1890 he proposed that evolution was irreversible: that 'an organism is unable to return, even partially, to a previous stage already realised in the ranks of its ancestors'.
Main Clausehe proposed that evolution was irreversible
Subordinate / Relativeafter the colon, a restating that-clause containing a quotation; 'already realised in the ranks of its ancestors' = reduced relative describing 'a previous stage'
Grammar NoteA colon introduces a precise restatement — free paraphrase practice. 'Even partially' is a strong qualifier: not even a partial return is allowed.
Simple MeaningIn 1890 Dollo said evolution cannot be reversed: an organism cannot go back, even partly, to a stage its ancestors had already passed.
中文1890年,他提出进化是不可逆的:“一个生物体无法回到——哪怕只是部分回到——其祖先谱系中已经实现过的某个先前阶段。”
They reasoned that while some evolutionary changes involve the loss of genes and are therefore irreversible, others may be the result of genes being switched off.
Main ClauseThey reasoned that … others may be the result of genes being switched off
Subordinate / Relative'while some evolutionary changes involve the loss of genes and are therefore irreversible' = concession clause inside the that-clause; 'genes being switched off' = gerund with its own subject
Grammar Note'While' = 'although'. Note the passive gerund 'being switched off' — a compact way of naming a process. This distinction is the heart of Q29.
Simple MeaningThey argued that some changes destroy genes and cannot be undone, but others only switch genes off.
中文他们推断:虽然某些进化变化涉及基因的丢失、因而不可逆转,但另一些变化可能只是基因被“关闭”的结果。
The team calculated that there is a good chance of silent genes surviving for up to 6 million years in at least a few individuals in a population, and that some might survive as long as 10 million years.
Main ClauseThe team calculated that … and that …
Subordinate / Relativetwo parallel that-clauses; 'surviving for up to 6 million years' = gerund phrase; 'in at least a few individuals in a population' = prepositional phrase narrowing the claim
Grammar NoteNote the careful hedging: 'a good chance', 'at least a few individuals', 'might survive'. Scientific caution is everywhere in this passage.
Simple MeaningThey worked out that silent genes probably last up to 6 million years in some individuals, and sometimes as long as 10 million.
中文研究团队计算得出:沉默基因在种群中至少少数个体身上,很有可能存活长达600万年,而有些甚至可能存活长达1000万年。
From a detailed analysis of the salamanders' family tree, however, it is clear that the other lineages evolved from an ancestor that itself had lost the ability to metamorphose.
Main Clauseit is clear that the other lineages evolved from an ancestor
Subordinate / Relative'From a detailed analysis of the salamanders' family tree' = prepositional phrase of source; 'that itself had lost the ability to metamorphose' = relative clause with emphatic 'itself'
Grammar Note'It is clear that…' with a delayed subject clause. The emphatic 'itself' matters: the ancestor had ALREADY lost the ability, so the others must have regained it.
Simple MeaningThe family tree shows the other salamander lines came from an ancestor that had itself already lost metamorphosis — so they got it back.
中文然而,对蝾螈家族谱系的详细分析清楚地表明:其他谱系是从一个自身已经丧失变态能力的祖先演化而来的。
Later in development these features disappear thanks to developmental programs that say 'lose the leg'. If for any reason this does not happen, the ancestral feature may not disappear, leading to an atavism.
Main Clausethese features disappear / the ancestral feature may not disappear
Subordinate / Relative'thanks to developmental programs that say 'lose the leg'' = reason phrase with relative clause; 'If for any reason this does not happen' = conditional clause; 'leading to an atavism' = result participle
Grammar Note'Thanks to' normally marks a positive cause; here it is neutral. The final '-ing' clause expresses result — a very common academic pattern.
Simple MeaningNormally the growth instructions remove these features. If that fails, the ancestral feature stays — producing a throwback.
中文在发育后期,由于“去掉那条腿”之类的发育程序,这些特征会消失。如果因任何原因这一过程没有发生,祖先性状便可能不会消失,从而导致返祖现象。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

1. Law and exception (定律与例外). The spine of the passage. Law: Dollo (1890) — evolution is irreversible. Modification: early 20th-century biologists 'qualified it in terms of probability, stating that there is no reason why evolution cannot run backwards — it is just very unlikely'. Exceptions: the 1919 humpback whale, the mole salamanders, the Bachia lizards. Resolution: silent genes, and then embryonic development. Every question maps onto one link in this chain.

2. Two opposing figures (两位对立人物). The opening paragraphs deliberately pair Lombroso and Dollo: 'While Lombroso was measuring criminals, a Belgian palaeontologist called Louis Dollo was studying fossil records and coming to the opposite conclusion.' Lombroso thought throwbacks were real and visible; Dollo thought they were impossible. This single sentence answers Q33.

3. Quantification as argument (以量化推进论证). Raff's contribution is to turn a philosophical question into a number: 'decided to use genetics to put a number on the probability of evolution going into reverse'. The logic runs: silent genes accumulate mutations → they eventually become useless → therefore there is a maximum survival time → 6 to 10 million years → 'throwbacks are possible, but only to the relatively recent evolutionary past'. That time limit then becomes the thing Wagner's data breaks.

4. Competing explanations (对立解释). For the Bachia lizards the passage gives two possibilities: (a) the traits are simply lost and then independently reappear, like the dorsal fins of sharks and killer whales — i.e. convergent evolution, not true reversal; (b) 'more intriguing', the genetic information survived for tens or hundreds of millions of years and was reactivated. Q35 tests (a) and Q36 tests (b) — read the two sentences together.

5. Attribution and disagreement (归属与分歧). Note how carefully the writer marks who thinks what: 'Roy Chapman Andrews argued'; 'Raff and colleagues reasoned'; 'The simplest explanation is that the toed lineages never lost their toes, but Wagner begs to differ'; 'According to his analysis'. Where the writer reports without endorsing, questions asking whether something is proven will be NOT GIVEN or NO.

6. Ending on an open question (以开放性问题收尾). The passage does not conclude — it proposes. 'The answer may lie in the womb… If for any reason this does not happen, the ancestral feature may not disappear.' Because the final mechanism is offered as a possibility, Q40 ('might be caused by developmental problems in the womb') is YES — the hedge in the question matches the hedge in the text.
