Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two trainee teachers, Seb and Lia, discuss a video of children doing origami as an educational tool. They review the skills it develops, comment on individual children, and plan to use it in their own teaching. Expect a choose TWO task (Q21/22), matching children to comments (Q23–27) and multiple choice (Q28–30).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task (Q23–27), each child is discussed in turn. Listen for the behaviour — settling down, working alone, wanting to be best, finding it easy, looking unsure — and match it to the comment, not to the name.
