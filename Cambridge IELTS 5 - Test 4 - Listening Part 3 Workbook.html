📋 Quick Review

In this business-studies tutorial, students (including Karin) and a tutor discuss a company case study. They talk about sales, competition, interest rates, staff training and the advertising campaign. Expect note completion and multiple-choice where opinions are compared.

💡 Listening tip: In academic discussions, distinguish a fact from an opinion, and note words like 'I disagree' or 'that's where…' which mark a change of view and often the answer.
