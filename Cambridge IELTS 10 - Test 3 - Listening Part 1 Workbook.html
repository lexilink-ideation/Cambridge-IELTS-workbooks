Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 3 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Carol Smith phones (and visits) the Early Learning Childcare Centre to enrol her daughter Kate. The director fills in an enrolment form: personal details, days and times, childcare group, meals, medical conditions, an emergency contact and how the fees will be paid.

这是“报名登记表”场景。表格题的顺序就是对话的顺序,注意年龄、日期、时间、拼写四类高频信息。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. Q1 is a classic age trap — 'She's three now but she turns four next month' → the director says 'I'll put four down because that's how old she'll be when she starts.' Always wait for the speaker who is writing the form.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

An enrolment form uses very compressed labels. Practise turning each label back into the question the director actually asks — that is the sound you are waiting for. 把表格标签还原成对话中的提问。
Q3 — 'Days enrolled for'
Form wording
Days enrolled for: Monday and ___
Audio wording
'have you decided on the days you'd like to bring your daughter here?' … 'I suppose Thursday would be all right'
Key paraphrase
days enrolled for = days you'd like to bring your daughter
Trap
Wednesday (her preference) and Friday (swimming) are both eliminated before the answer.
Your turn
Q4 — 'Start time'
Form wording
Start time: ___ am
Audio wording
'What time would you like your daughter to start?' — 'I'll drop her off at 8:30'
Key paraphrase
start time = drop-off time
Trap
7:30 (centre opens), 9:00 (her work) and 6 o'clock (centre closes) are all said.
Your turn
Q6 — 'Which meal/s are required'
Form wording
Which meal/s are required each day? ___
Audio wording
'she won't need dinner, will you give her breakfast before she comes?' — 'Yes, she'll only need lunch'
Key paraphrase
required = need
Trap
All three meals are named. The word 'only' identifies the answer.
Your turn
Q7 — 'Medical conditions'
Form wording
Medical conditions: needs ___
Audio wording
'No. But she does need to wear glasses.'
Key paraphrase
medical condition = wearing glasses (here)
Trap
The answer follows a 'No' — do not stop listening after a negative.
Your turn
Q10 — 'Will pay each ___'
Form wording
Will pay each ___
Audio wording
'You can choose to pay at the end of each term or … a slightly discounted rate if you pay every month.' — 'I'll do that then.'
Key paraphrase
each = every; 'I'll do that' refers to the second option
Trap
'term' is offered first. You must track what 'that' refers to.
Your turn
