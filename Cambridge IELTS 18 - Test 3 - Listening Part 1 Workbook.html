Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 3 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Dan Green phones the Wayside Camera Club and Breda helps him fill in a membership form. He gives his address and reasons for joining, then describes three photography competitions he has entered, with the instructions and the feedback he received. Expect form completion (Q1–4) and table completion (Q5–10).

💡 Listening tip: When a name is spelled out (M-A-R-R-O-W-F-I-E-L-D), write letters as you hear them and check the double letters. In the competitions table, keep the columns straight: title · instructions · feedback.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The feedback is described in everyday words; compress it to the exact answer.
Feedback on the first photo (Q-area)
Table note
The picture's composition was not good.
What you hear
'it was too busy as a picture… so it was the composition that they criticised'
Key paraphrase
'too busy' = poor composition
Your turn
Feedback on the sunset (Q7)
Table note
The ___ was wrong.
What you hear
'I should have waited a bit longer to get the shot… the timing wasn't right'
Key paraphrase
waited too soon = the timing was wrong → timing
Your turn
