📋 Quick Review

A talk on maple syrup: what it is, the maple tree and its growing needs, how early producers made maple sugar, and modern production from tapping the tree to finishing the syrup. Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: A process description (tapping → collecting → evaporating → finishing) gives you the order of answers. Predict the noun or adjective for each gap and follow the stages in sequence.
