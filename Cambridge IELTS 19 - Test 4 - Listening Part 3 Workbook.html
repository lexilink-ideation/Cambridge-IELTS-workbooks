Cambridge IELTS 19 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Jane and Kieran, talk about looking after books — how to pack and handle them, why old books turn yellow, and the layout of Jane's grandfather's bookshop. Expect multiple choice (Q21–25) and a matching task locating types of books in the shop (Q26–30).

💡 Listening tip: For the shop-layout matching task, build a quick mental map as Jane describes each area. Each type of book has one location — listen for the place phrase ('need a ladder', 'by the front door', 'the far end', 'part of the coffee shop').
