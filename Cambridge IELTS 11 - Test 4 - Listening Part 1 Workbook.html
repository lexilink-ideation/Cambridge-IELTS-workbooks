📋 Quick Review

Melanie phones the Stretton Festival box office and Rob tells her about three events — a jazz band, the duck races and the flower show (table completion, Q1–7) — and then which of three plays suits children, adults, or everyone (Q8–10).

表格填空 + 三项配对。表格题一定要先看栏目标题(Cost / Venue / Notes)。

💡 Listening tip: In a table, read across the row and down the column before you listen. The Venue column needs places (school, hall), the Notes column needs people and prizes. Q6 is a spelled name (B-Y-T-H-W-A-I-T-E), so be ready to write letters.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Table gaps are short, but the sentence that contains the answer is long. Practise the compression.
Q1 — the school
Table gap
The ___ school
What you hear
'Yes, that's right, the secondary school. Make sure you don't go to the primary school by mistake!'
Key paraphrase
the warning about the wrong school confirms the right one → secondary
Your turn
Q2 — the instrument
Table gap
Carolyn Hart (plays the ___)
What you hear
'Doesn't she play the oboe, or flute or something?' — 'Yes, the flute.'
Key paraphrase
the guess comes first, the confirmation second → flute
Your turn
Q4 — the prize
Table gap
Prize: tickets for ___ held at the end of the festival
What you hear
'wins its owner free tickets for the concert on the last night of the festival'
Key paraphrase
'on the last night' = 'held at the end' → concert
Your turn
Q7 — the presenter
Table gap
Prizes presented at 5 pm by a well-known ___
What you hear
'The prizes are being given by a famous actor, Kevin Shaw'
Key paraphrase
'famous' = 'well-known'; you need the job, not the name → actor
Your turn
Q9 — 'Fire and Flood'
Question
Who is 'Fire and Flood' suitable for?
What you hear
'If you'd like something for yourself and your husband, and leave your children with a babysitter … children might find it rather frightening'
Key paraphrase
'leave your children with a babysitter' = adults only → B
Your turn
