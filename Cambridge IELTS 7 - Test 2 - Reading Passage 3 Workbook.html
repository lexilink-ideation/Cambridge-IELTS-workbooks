Practice Workbook — "Makete Integrated Rural Transport Project"

📋 Passage Overview

This passage describes the Makete Integrated Rural Transport Project (MIRTP), a development project launched in 1985 in a remote, mountainous district of south-western Tanzania. Instead of simply building more roads (the conventional approach, which had disappointing results elsewhere in Africa), the project first researched how rural households actually used transport, then tested low-cost, practical solutions — improved paths and footbridges, locally made wheelbarrows, and donkeys — alongside limited road improvements.

The passage traces MIRTP through three phases (research, implementation, and refinement) and reflects on both its successes and its limitations, concluding that the "integrated rural transport" concept is now an accepted model for future projects in Tanzania.

💡 Reading strategy tip: This passage uses labelled sections (A–F) and is tested with heading-matching questions — a task where you must find the main idea of each section rather than individual facts. Read each section once for its overall purpose (what stage of the project it describes) before you try to match headings.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"The disappointing results of many conventional road transport projects in Africa led some experts to rethink the strategy by which rural transport problems were to be tackled at the beginning of the 1980s."
Main ClauseThe disappointing results... led some experts to rethink the strategy
Subordinate / Relative Clause"by which rural transport problems were to be tackled" — relative clause modifying "strategy"
Grammar Note"lead someone to do something" is a causative structure; "by which" introduces a passive relative clause referring back to "strategy".
Simple MeaningBecause earlier African road projects had disappointing results, experts began rethinking their whole approach to rural transport in the early 1980s.
中文非洲许多传统道路交通项目令人失望的结果，促使一些专家在20世纪80年代初开始重新思考解决农村交通问题的策略。
"The underlying assumption was that the time saved would be used instead for activities that would improve the social and economic development of the communities."
Main ClauseThe underlying assumption was [that-clause]
Subordinate / Relative Clause"that the time saved would be used... for activities that would improve..." — noun clause as complement, with a nested relative clause modifying "activities"
Grammar NoteNested clauses: a noun clause (subject complement) contains a further relative clause.
Simple MeaningThe project assumed that time saved on transport would instead go toward activities that help communities develop.
中文其基本假设是：节省下来的时间将被用于那些有助于促进社区社会和经济发展的活动。
"Having determined the main transport needs, possible solutions were identified which might reduce the time and burden."
Main Clausepossible solutions were identified
Subordinate / Relative Clause"Having determined the main transport needs" (perfect participle phrase = after the needs had been determined); "which might reduce the time and burden" (relative clause on "solutions")
Grammar NotePerfect participle shows an action completed before the main clause; passive voice ("were identified") downplays who did the identifying.
Simple MeaningAfter determining what people needed most, the team looked for solutions that could reduce time and effort.
中文在确定了主要的交通需求之后，项目组识别出了可能减轻时间和负担的各种解决方案。
"It would have been easy to criticise the MIRTP for using in the early phases a 'top-down' approach, in which decisions were made by experts and officials before being handed down to communities, but it was necessary to start the process from the level of the governmental authorities of the district."
Main ClauseIt would have been easy to criticise the MIRTP... but it was necessary to start the process from the level of the governmental authorities
Subordinate / Relative Clause"in which decisions were made by experts and officials before being handed down to communities" — relative clause modifying "a 'top-down' approach"
Grammar Note"It would have been easy to..." is a hypothetical/counterfactual structure, contrasted by "but"; "before being handed down" uses a passive gerund after "before".
Simple MeaningAlthough MIRTP could easily be criticised for starting with expert-led decisions, this was actually necessary because district government support had to come first.
中文MIRTP在早期阶段采用了"自上而下"的方式（即由专家和官员先做决定，再下达给社区），这本来很容易受到批评，但从地区政府当局层面开始推进这一进程实属必要。
"Modifications to the original design by local carpenters cut production time and costs."
Main ClauseModifications... cut production time and costs (whole simple sentence)
Subordinate / Relative ClauseNone — a simple sentence useful as a base for combining drills
Grammar Note"Modifications to X by Y" — two prepositional phrases modifying the subject noun "Modifications".
Simple MeaningLocal carpenters changed the original wheelbarrow design, reducing both production time and cost.
中文当地木匠对原始设计做出的改进，缩短了生产时间并降低了成本。
"The concept of integrated rural transport is now well established in Tanzania, where a major program of rural transport is just about to start."
Main ClauseThe concept of integrated rural transport is now well established in Tanzania
Subordinate / Relative Clause"where a major program of rural transport is just about to start" — non-restrictive relative clause modifying "Tanzania"
Grammar Note"where" introduces a relative clause referring to a place; "just about to" expresses near-future action.
Simple MeaningThe idea of integrated rural transport is now widely accepted in Tanzania, where a large new program is about to begin.
中文综合农村交通的理念如今已在坦桑尼亚广泛确立，一项大型农村交通项目即将启动。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & Effect

Bad roads and difficult terrain → households spent an average of 7 hours a day on transport → MIRTP researched the problem before proposing solutions, leading to targeted, practical improvements rather than blanket road-building.

Contrast

The passage contrasts a "top-down" approach (decisions made by experts/officials first) with a bottom-up, request-based approach (paths improved "only at the request of communities"). It also contrasts motorised transport (which mostly failed due to breakdowns) with low-cost solutions (donkeys, wheelbarrows) that succeeded better.

Comparison

Cost comparisons are used throughout: a locally made wheelbarrow (5,000 shillings) costs about a quarter of an imported one; a donkey (20,000 shillings) still costs less than a bicycle, yet equals over half a year's average household income — showing that even "cheap" options remain a real barrier for the rural poor.

Examples

Concrete statistics support the general claims: 95% of transport was on foot, 80% was within the locality, and 70% was related to collecting water/firewood or travelling to grinding mills — evidence used to justify MIRTP's focus on local, everyday transport rather than long-distance roads.

Definitions

Key term: MIRTP = Makete Integrated Rural Transport Project, a Tanzanian pilot project (started 1985) that applied the concept of "integrated rural transport" — addressing all real transport needs together, not just roads.

Chronology

Phase I (Dec 1985 – Dec 1987): research and household survey → Phase II (Jan – Feb 1991): implementation of road improvements, paths, wheelbarrows, and donkey promotion → Phase III (Mar 1991 – Mar 1993): refinement and institutionalisation of successful measures.

Author's Attitude

The tone is balanced and evaluative — acknowledging both successes (paths, road access, donkeys) and failures (motorised vehicle efficiency, high wheelbarrow costs), while ultimately presenting MIRTP as a valuable model ("will act as a reference for future work").
