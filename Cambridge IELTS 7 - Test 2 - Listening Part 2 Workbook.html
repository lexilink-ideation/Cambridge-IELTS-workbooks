📋 Quick Review

This Section 2 recording is an automated message from a city "Tourist Line" phone service. A single speaker (no dialogue) describes different ways visitors can get around the city, focusing first on a river boat trip with four stops (Green Banks, City Bridge, Roman Landing, and Newtown), then on two competing sightseeing bus companies.

This is typical IELTS Listening Part 2 content: one person giving information to a general audience, tested with a map/plan labelling task followed by table/short-answer questions.

💡 Listening strategy tip: For map-labelling questions, follow the direction words carefully (west to east, north to south) and use landmarks already printed on the map (like "Green Banks" or "Roman Landing") as anchor points. For the table questions, notice that the speaker moves stop-by-stop in a fixed order — once you hear the next stop letter, you know the previous answer window has closed.

stopping pointa place where a boat, bus or train pauses to let people on/off停靠点"There are four main stopping points along the river."Often labelled as "Stop A, B, C, D" on maps
booking officea place where tickets are bought or reservations made售票处/预订处"You can find the main booking office at Stop A."Fixed collocation — don't confuse with "ticket office"
formal gardensgardens laid out in a neat, symmetrical, planned style规整式花园"The palace has beautiful formal gardens."Adjective + noun pair — listen for the adjective before "gardens"
refreshmentsfood and drink, especially light snacks茶点，饮食"The restaurant offers a wide range of refreshments."Always used in the plural form
commercial centrethe business/shopping district of a city商业中心"You get good views over the old commercial and banking centre."Paraphrase for "business district" / "downtown"
interactive museuma museum where visitors can touch/use exhibits, not just look互动式博物馆"There's an interactive museum at Roman Landing.""Interactive" is a common IELTS adjective for modern museums
specialise (in)to focus on one particular subject or area专营，专注于"The bookshop specialises in local history."Followed by "in" + noun/gerund
entertainment complexa large building containing several leisure facilities娱乐综合体"The entertainment complex has a cinema and a bowling alley."Often followed by a list — expect several blanks nearby

commentarya spoken description given during a tour or event解说，讲解"The tour runs with a live commentary in English."Listen for the language(s) it's offered in
