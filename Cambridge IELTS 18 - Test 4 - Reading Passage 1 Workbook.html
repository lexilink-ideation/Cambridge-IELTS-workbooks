📋 Passage Overview

Green roofs. Rooftops covered with plants are spreading through cities worldwide. The passage reviews their many benefits (energy savings, flood control, wildlife, food, wellbeing), the role of city authorities, why newer buildings suit them, and new variations such as 'blue' and 'brown' roofs. Question types: paragraph matching, summary completion and two choose TWO tasks.

💡 Reading tip: A letter may be used more than once in paragraph-matching (Q1 and Q5 are both paragraph D). For the 'choose TWO' tasks, both answers must come from the paragraph named in the question (C for Q10/11, E for Q12/13).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

But the involvement of city authorities, businesses and other institutions is crucial to ensuring their success — as is research investigating different options to suit the variety of rooftop spaces found in cities.
Main ClauseThe involvement of city authorities, businesses and other institutions is crucial.
Subordinate / Relative'to ensuring their success' (infinitive/prepositional) + 'as is research investigating…' (inverted addition).
Grammar Note'as is research…' uses inversion to add a second essential factor.
Simple MeaningSuccess needs backing from authorities and businesses — and research into what suits each roof.
中文但城市当局、企业和其他机构的参与，对确保其成功至关重要——研究不同方案以适应城市中各种屋顶空间同样如此。
Being able to keep enough water at roof height and distribute it right across the rooftop is crucial to maintaining the plants on any green roof.
Main ClauseBeing able to keep enough water at roof height and distribute it is crucial to maintaining the plants.
Subordinate / Relative'to maintaining the plants on any green roof' (prepositional purpose).
Grammar NoteA gerund phrase ('Being able to…') is the subject.
Simple MeaningKeeping and spreading enough water on the roof is essential to keep the plants alive.
中文能够在屋顶高度储存足够的水并将其分布到整个屋顶，对于维持任何绿色屋顶上的植物都至关重要。
To convince investors and developers that installing green roofs is worthwhile, economic arguments are still the most important.
Main ClauseEconomic arguments are still the most important.
Subordinate / Relative'To convince investors and developers that installing green roofs is worthwhile' (infinitive of purpose + noun clause).
Grammar NoteThe fronted infinitive states the purpose; 'that…' is its object clause.
Simple MeaningTo persuade investors, money-based arguments still matter most.
中文要说服投资者和开发商相信安装绿色屋顶是值得的，经济方面的论据仍然最为重要。
For example, 'blue roofs' enable buildings to hold water over longer periods of time, rather than draining it away quickly — crucial in times of heavier rainfall.
Main Clause'Blue roofs' enable buildings to hold water over longer periods of time.
Subordinate / Relative'rather than draining it away quickly' (contrast) + 'crucial in times of heavier rainfall' (reduced comment).
Grammar Note'rather than + -ing' contrasts two behaviours; the dash adds a comment.
Simple MeaningBlue roofs store water for longer instead of draining it fast — important when rain is heavy.
中文例如，"蓝色屋顶"能让建筑较长时间地储水，而不是迅速排走——这在暴雨时期至关重要。

Exemplification: San Francisco (requires green roofs) and Toronto (policies since the 1990s) are examples of city leadership (Q2).
