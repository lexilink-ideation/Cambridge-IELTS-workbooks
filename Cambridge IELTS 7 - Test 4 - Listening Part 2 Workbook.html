Cambridge IELTS 7 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Bicentennial Park Walking Tour · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Sally, a tour guide, leads a group on a walking tour of Bicentennial Park. She explains the park's history (it used to be an industrial site of factories and warehouses), why local residents pushed for a public park instead of housing, and then describes the formal gardens, the Tower, the views over the nearby Olympic site, and finally the nature reserve area with its mangroves, Frog Pond and Waterbird Refuge.

This is a typical Part 2 monologue: one speaker, a guided tour format, with a mix of multiple choice, map/plan labelling, and table completion questions. Directions (north/south/east/west) are critical for the map task.

💡 Part 2 strategy: For map-labelling questions, first locate yourself using the "You are here" marker, then track the compass directions the guide gives ("to the east...", "around to the west...") as she describes what's visible from each side of the Tower.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 7  |  🎧 Test: 4, Section 2  |  ❓ Questions: 11–20

📝 Task types: Multiple choice (Q11–14) · Plan/map labelling (Q15–17) · Table completion (Q18–20)

🗣️ Setting: A guided walking tour of a public park built on a former industrial site

👤 Speaker: Sally, the tour guide

Task breakdown

Q11–14: Tour type, the site's industrial past, why residents wanted the park, and the Tower's location.

Q15–17: Labelling features on the park map (car park, rose garden, café).

Q18–20: A table of nature reserve areas — their facilities and visitor activities.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

IELTS Listening questions never use the exact same words as the audio. Study each contrast below, note the key paraphrase, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — Type of Tour
Question wordingWhat kind of tour is Sally leading?
Audio meaningSally hopes everyone is wearing comfortable shoes and can keep up the pace — implying physical movement on foot, not sitting on a vehicle.
Key paraphrase"comfortable shoes...keep up the pace" = a walking tour
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — Original Buildings
Question wordingThe original buildings on the site were ______
Audio meaningSally says there used to be a lot of factories, and that most of the space was originally warehouses and storehouses — all industrial-type buildings.
Key paraphrase"factories...warehouses and storehouses" = industrial buildings
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — What Residents Wanted
Question wordingThe local residents wanted to use the site for ______
Audio meaningA developer proposed high-rise housing, but residents rejected this — they wanted open space for outdoor activities instead of housing or a sports complex.
Key paraphrase"open space for outdoor activities" = leisure
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — Frog Pond Activity
Question wordingFrog Pond — Facility: outdoor classroom — Activity: ______
Audio meaningSally explains the outdoor classroom near the Frog Pond is a favourite spot for school parties, and specifically that primary schools use the area for a particular subject.
Key paraphrase"used by primary schools for biology lessons" = the activity is biology lessons
