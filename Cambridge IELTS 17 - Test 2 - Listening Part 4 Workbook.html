📋 Quick Review

A lecture on the impact of digital technology on the Icelandic language. The speaker describes Icelandic today, how young speakers use English online, why tech companies write little in Icelandic, and the government's response. Note completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: One answer here is a large number (Q31). Practise hearing numbers like 'three hundred and twenty-one thousand' and writing them as digits. All other answers are single words.
