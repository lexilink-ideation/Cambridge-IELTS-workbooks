📋 Quick Review

An academic lecture on four business values — collaboration, industriousness (hard work), creativity and excellence. The speaker's argument is contrarian: each 'good thing' can backfire if leaders ignore the side effects. Note completion, NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS.

💡 Listening tip: Part 4 follows the lecture's own headings, which are printed on your question paper. If you lose your place, jump to the next heading word ('Collaboration', 'Industriousness', 'Creativity', 'Excellence') and rejoin. Two-word answers are allowed here, so don't panic at 'side effects'.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Lecture notes compress long sentences. Practise reducing the speaker's wording to two words maximum.
Q32 — what managers must manage
Question note
Senior managers need to understand and deal with the potential ___ that may result
What you hear
'if those leaders adopt values without understanding and managing the side effects that arise'
Key paraphrase
deal with = managing; that may result = that arise → side effects
Your turn
Q34 — what other teams experienced
Question note
Other teams experienced ___ from trying to collaborate
What you hear
'The other teams tried to collaborate… and descended into confusion'
Key paraphrase
experienced ___ = descended into ___ → confusion
Your turn
Q37 — the meaning of 'lazy'
Question note
'lazy' refers to people who avoid doing tasks that are ___
What you hear
'a rational determination not to carry out unnecessary tasks'
Key paraphrase
avoid doing = determination not to carry out → unnecessary (or 'not necessary')
Your turn
Q40 — pioneers vs followers
Question note
pioneers had a ___ that was far higher than that of followers
What you hear
'the pioneers commanded an average market share of 29 percent, while the followers achieved… only 13 percent'
Key paraphrase
had a ___ far higher = commanded an average ___ of 29% vs 13% → market share
Your turn
