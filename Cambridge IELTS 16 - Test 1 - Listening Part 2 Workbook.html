Cambridge IELTS 16 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Julia Simmons, the managing director, welcomes a group of students to Stevenson's, a metal-goods manufacturer, for two weeks of work experience. She gives the company's history (Q11–14, multiple choice) then walks them round a site plan (Q15–20, map labelling A–J).

💡 Listening tip: For the map, fix your position first ('we're in reception') and follow the directions like a route — 'along the corridor', 'facing you', 'on your right'. Track the moving point, not single words.
