📋 Passage Overview

This passage, 'Disappearing Delta', explains why the fertile Nile delta in Egypt is being eroded and is losing sediment. Since the Aswan dams were built, the annual floods that once carried silt to the delta have been controlled, so the coastline is retreating and the soil is becoming less fertile and more salty. Scientists discuss the causes, the risks (including saltwater intrusion), and possible responses.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Cause-and-effect science passages are full of chains (dam → less silt → erosion → salt). For 'which caused which' questions, trace the arrows and don't reverse them.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"Before the Aswan dams were built, the annual flood of the Nile deposited fertile silt across the delta each year."
Main Clausethe annual flood of the Nile deposited fertile silt across the delta each year
Subordinate / RelativeBefore the Aswan dams were built (time clause)
Grammar Note'Before …' sets the earlier period; the main clause states what used to happen.
Simple MeaningIn the past, the Nile's yearly flood spread rich silt over the delta.
中文在阿斯旺大坝建成之前，尼罗河每年的洪水都会把肥沃的淤泥沉积到三角洲各处。
"Because the dams now control the flow, far less sediment reaches the coast, and so the shoreline is being eroded."
Main Clausefar less sediment reaches the coast, and so the shoreline is being eroded
Subordinate / RelativeBecause the dams now control the flow (cause clause)
Grammar Note'Because … now …' gives the cause; 'and so' links a second consequence.
Simple MeaningThe dams hold back the flow, so little silt reaches the sea and the shore wears away.
中文由于大坝如今控制了水流，到达海岸的沉积物大为减少，因此海岸线正在被侵蚀。
"As the fresh water is held back, salt water from the sea is able to move further inland, threatening farmland."
Main Clausesalt water from the sea is able to move further inland
Subordinate / RelativeAs the fresh water is held back (cause/time clause); threatening farmland (participle result)
Grammar Note'As …' shows cause; the -ing participle 'threatening' shows the effect.
Simple MeaningWith less fresh water flowing out, seawater pushes inland and endangers farmland.
中文由于淡水被拦截，海水得以进一步向内陆移动，威胁着农田。
"Scientists warn that, unless action is taken, large areas of the delta could become too salty to farm."
Main ClauseScientists warn that … large areas of the delta could become too salty to farm
Subordinate / Relativeunless action is taken (conditional clause)
Grammar Note'unless' = 'if … not'; 'too … to …' shows an excessive degree preventing farming.
Simple MeaningExperts say that without action, much of the delta may be too salty for crops.
中文科学家警告说，除非采取行动，否则三角洲的大片地区可能会因盐分过高而无法耕种。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & Effect chain — Aswan dams → floods controlled → less silt/sediment → coastal erosion + less fertile soil. Follow the chain in order.

Before vs After — Before the dams: yearly floods brought silt. Now: silt is trapped upstream. Contrast the two periods.

Consequence — Less fresh water flowing out → saltwater intrusion → farmland at risk. One problem leads to another.

Warning / Prediction — 'Unless action is taken…' signals a conditional future risk. Note the writer's cautionary tone.
