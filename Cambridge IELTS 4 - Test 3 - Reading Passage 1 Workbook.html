📋 Passage Overview

Micro-Enterprise Credit for Street Youth describes how the charity Street Kids International (S.K.I.) helps street children earn a living through small loans and business training. It explains why children end up on the street, gives examples of projects (a bicycle courier service, a shoe-shine collective, a Zambian enterprise scheme) and the lessons learned.

💡 Reading tip: Yes / No / Not Given here tests the writer's claims. Watch for over-general words in the statement ('any', 'only one') — the passage often qualifies them ('not for everyone', 'can be increased').

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Typically, children do not end up on the streets due to a single cause, but to a combination of factors: a dearth of adequately funded schools, the demand for income at home, family breakdown and violence.
Main Clausechildren do not end up on the streets due to a single cause, but to a combination of factors
Subordinate / Relativea dearth of adequately funded schools, the demand for income at home, family breakdown and violence (list in apposition)
Grammar Note'not … but …' contrasts one cause with many; the colon introduces the list.
Simple MeaningChildren usually reach the streets not for one reason but for several — poor schools, needing money, and family problems.
中文通常，孩子流落街头不是因为单一原因，而是多种因素的结合：资金不足的学校、家庭对收入的需求、家庭破裂和暴力。
Many children may choose entrepreneurship because it allows them a degree of independence, is less exploitative than many forms of paid employment, and is flexible enough to allow them to participate in other activities.
Main ClauseMany children may choose entrepreneurship
Subordinate / Relativebecause it allows them a degree of independence, is less exploitative…, and is flexible enough… (reason clause with three parts)
Grammar Noteone 'because' governs three parallel verb phrases (allows / is / is).
Simple MeaningMany children choose to run a business because it gives independence, is fairer than paid work, and fits around other activities.
中文许多孩子可能选择创业，因为这能带给他们一定的独立性，比许多有偿工作更少剥削，而且足够灵活，能让他们兼顾其他活动。
When children have had a major role in determining procedures, they are more likely to abide by and enforce them.
Main Clausethey are more likely to abide by and enforce them
Subordinate / RelativeWhen children have had a major role in determining procedures (time/condition clause)
Grammar Note'more likely to' + two verbs (abide by, enforce); 'them' refers back to 'procedures'.
Simple MeaningIf children help decide the rules, they are more likely to keep and uphold them.
中文当孩子在制定规则时发挥重要作用时，他们更有可能遵守并维护这些规则。
We believe that credit must be extended in association with other types of support that help participants develop critical life skills as well as productive businesses.
Main Clausecredit must be extended in association with other types of support
Subordinate / Relativethat help participants develop critical life skills as well as productive businesses (relative clause)
Grammar Note'in association with' = together with; 'as well as' links two things developed.
Simple MeaningWe think loans should always come with other support that builds both life skills and successful businesses.
中文我们认为，信贷必须与其他类型的支持结合提供，这些支持能帮助参与者培养关键的生活技能以及经营成功的企业。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Multiple causes: the passage stresses that street life results from 'a combination of factors', not a single cause.

Example → principle: three projects (Sudan/India couriers, Dominican shoe-shine, Zambia enterprise) illustrate the general approach.

Qualification: claims are hedged — 'is not for everyone', loans 'can be gradually expanded' — which matters for Yes/No/Not Given.

Conclusion with a condition: credit works only 'in association with other types of support' (matches Q13).
