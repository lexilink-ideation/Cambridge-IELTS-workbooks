📋 Passage Overview

The Dead Sea Scrolls. Around 1947, Bedouin shepherds near Qumran found ancient scrolls in clay jars. The passage covers their discovery, likely authors (the Essenes), the languages used, the unusual Copper Scroll, the scrolls' later journeys, and a 2017 decipherment. Question types: note completion (Q1–5) and TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q6–13).

💡 Reading tip: For 'ONE WORD ONLY' notes, copy the exact word. For T/F/NG, a statement about feelings or wishes (e.g. 'were disappointed') is often NOT GIVEN unless the passage clearly states the emotion.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

One of these young shepherds tossed a rock into an opening on the side of a cliff and was surprised to hear a shattering sound.
Main ClauseOne of these young shepherds tossed a rock … and was surprised to hear a shattering sound
Subordinate / Relative(no subordinate clause — two coordinated verbs share one subject)
Grammar NoteCompound predicate: 'tossed … and was surprised …'; 'to hear' is an infinitive of cause.
Simple MeaningA shepherd threw a rock into a cliff opening and was surprised to hear breaking.
中文其中一名年轻牧羊人把一块石头扔进悬崖侧面的洞口,惊讶地听到碎裂声。
The origin of the Dead Sea Scrolls, which were written around 2,000 years ago between 150 BCE and 70 CE, is still the subject of scholarly debate even today.
Main ClauseThe origin of the Dead Sea Scrolls … is still the subject of scholarly debate
Subordinate / Relativewhich were written around 2,000 years ago… (non-defining relative clause adding a date)
Grammar NoteA non-defining relative clause interrupts the subject; note the singular verb 'is' agrees with 'origin'.
Simple MeaningScholars still argue about where the scrolls, written 2,000 years ago, came from.
中文死海古卷写于约两千年前(公元前150年至公元70年),其来源至今仍是学界争论的话题。
The only exception is the scroll numbered 3Q15, which was created out of a combination of copper and tin.
Main ClauseThe only exception is the scroll numbered 3Q15
Subordinate / Relativewhich was created out of a combination of copper and tin (non-defining relative clause)
Grammar Note'numbered 3Q15' is a reduced clause ('which is numbered'); the second relative clause adds material.
Simple MeaningThe one exception is scroll 3Q15, which was made from copper and tin.
中文唯一的例外是编号3Q15的卷轴,它由铜和锡的合金制成。
Deciphered from a band of coded text on parchment, the find provides insight into the community of people who wrote it and the 364-day calendar they would have used.
Main Clausethe find provides insight into the community of people … and the 364-day calendar
Subordinate / RelativeDeciphered from a band of coded text on parchment (front reduced clause); who wrote it (relative clause); (that) they would have used (contact relative clause)
Grammar NoteFront participle clause + two relative clauses; 'would have used' is a modal past.
Simple MeaningOnce decoded, the scroll reveals its writers' community and the 364-day calendar they used.
中文这份卷轴从羊皮纸上一段编码文字破译而来,让人得以了解书写者的群体及他们可能使用的364天历法。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology of discovery. The passage runs in time order: 1947 find → analysis of authors and languages → the Copper Scroll → 1948–1954 journeys → 2017 decipherment. Time markers keep the T/F/NG answers in sequence.

Exception signalling. Phrases like 'the only exception', 'the only entire book' flag unique facts — exactly the kind of detail T/F/NG questions test (e.g. Q8 about incomplete books).

Claim vs certainty. Words like 'prevailing theory', 'thought to have', 'according to various hypotheses' show the writer is reporting uncertainty — helpful for Q7 ('agreement among academics' = FALSE).
