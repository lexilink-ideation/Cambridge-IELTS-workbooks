Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Andrew Harris, who has worked in hospital administration and previously in computer engineering, meets course advisor Monica at a college to discuss Management Diploma options. He wants to specialise in Personnel Management, and Monica explains the differences between a part-time course and a modular course, including cost, structure, and workload.

This is a typical IELTS Listening Part 3 academic discussion: two or three speakers (often a student and a tutor/advisor) discuss study plans, comparing options and weighing advantages and disadvantages.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 3 often compares two options side by side (here: part-time vs modular). Before listening, sort the gapped notes into two columns in your head. Listen for contrast words like "whereas," "on the other hand," and "the difference is" to know which option is being described.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Employer's Financial Support
Question wordingWhat will Andrew's employer pay for?
Audio meaning"They'll cover the cost of the course itself, but I'd need to take unpaid leave to study full-time, and that's just something I can't afford."
Key paraphrase"cover the cost of" = pay for
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Coursework Requirements
Question wordingAssessment on the part-time course
Audio meaning"You'll need to write an essay every month, which counts towards your final mark, plus a case study by the end and a short report every four weeks."
Key paraphrase"counts towards your final mark" = contributes to the final assessment
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Advantage of the Modular Course
Question wordingMain benefit of studying modules
Audio meaning"The big benefit is that you can study in a much more intensive way — you're fully focused, without work distractions."
Key paraphrase"the big benefit" = the main advantage
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — Disadvantage of the Modular Course
Question wordingDrawback of the modular course
Audio meaning"The downside is it works out more expensive, since you'd have no income to live on during each module."
Key paraphrase"the downside" = the disadvantage/drawback
