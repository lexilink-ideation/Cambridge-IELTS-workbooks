Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 12 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Natalie and Dave plan a tourism case study on the Horton Castle site. Dave explains the stages — research, interviews, analysis, writing (Q21–26, flow-chart) — and then the two discuss why so few tourists visit, whether a visitor centre would work, and how to protect the site (Q27–30, multiple choice).

💡 Listening tip: Q27–30 all begin 'Natalie and Dave agree…' or 'What does Dave say…'. That means one speaker may raise a wrong idea first and then be corrected. Natalie's 'access is difficult' is immediately dated by Dave ('that report was written ten years ago… that's been improved now').

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Student-discussion tasks reward hearing the correction, not the first statement.
Q21 — what else to note
Option wording
sources
What you hear
'Did you remember to keep a record of where you got the information from?'
Key paraphrase
where you got the information from = sources
Your turn
Q26 — the ending
Option wording
solutions
What you hear
'in a normal report we'd end with some suggestions to deal with the problem… but in a case study we end up with a question'
Key paraphrase
suggestions to deal with the problem = solutions; the flow-chart says do NOT end with these
Your turn
Q27 — why few visitors
Option wording
the publicity is poor
What you hear
'you'd never realise it from the castle website — maybe that's the problem' / 'it's really dry and boring'
Key paraphrase
poor publicity = a dry, boring website. Access is ruled out: 'that report was written ten years ago… that's been improved now'
Your turn
Q29 — the town of Horton
Option wording
There are few people of working age
What you hear
'the young people all get out as fast as they can to get jobs in the city, and the only people left are children and those who've retired'
Key paraphrase
few of working age = only children and retired people remain → B
Your turn
