Cambridge IELTS 19 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

📋 Quick Review

Liz Fuller, a running coach, gives a podcast for people thinking about taking up running. She discusses the limits of online training programmes, tips for new runners, the reasons some club members were slow to join, and her own running journey. Expect two 'choose TWO' tasks (Q11–14), a matching task (Q15–18) and multiple choice (Q19–20).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task (Q15–18), each member has ONE reason they didn't join sooner — a lack of confidence, a dislike of running, or a lack of time. Listen for the feeling behind each story, not just the events.
