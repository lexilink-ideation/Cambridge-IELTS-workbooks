📋 Quick Review

A student gives a talk on the history of pockets in European clothing. She explains why she chose the topic, then traces how pockets developed differently for men and women from the 18th century onward, ending with the rise of the handbag. This is note completion — ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: The notes follow the talk's structure (men's pockets, then women's). Predict the word type for each gap — a garment, a material, a body part — before you listen, and don't be fooled by extra detail around the answer.
