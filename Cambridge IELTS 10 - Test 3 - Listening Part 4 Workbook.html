📋 Quick Review

A lecture on self-regulatory focus theory (Tori Higgins) and leadership. People either approach pleasure (promotion goals) or avoid pain (prevention goals). Two factors decide which — the chronic factor (personality) and the situational factor. The second half links this to transformational and transactional leaders.

这是一篇心理学/管理学讲座。全篇建立在两组对立概念之上,只要抓住这两组对立,答案就会自动出现。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY. This lecture is built on parallel pairs — promotion/prevention, chronic/situational, ideal self/ought self, transformational/transactional. When you hear the first half of a pair, prepare for the second. Q33 is unusual: the gap is the name of the factor itself (Situational).

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Section 4 notes are a skeleton of the lecture. Notice how often the note uses a different word from the speaker — that is where marks are won and lost. 笔记用词与讲座用词几乎总是不同。
Q32 — 'comes from one's personality'
Note wording
The Chronic Factor comes from one's ___
Audio wording
'This factor is connected to a person's personality and says that each person has a basic tendency…'
Key paraphrase
comes from = is connected to
Trap
'basic tendency' is tempting but does not fit the phrase 'comes from one's ___'.
Your turn
Q34 — 'when with a friend'
Note wording
we are more likely to focus on promotion goals when with a ___
Audio wording
'we are more likely to be thinking about pleasure and to have promotion goals when we are spending time with a friend'
Key paraphrase
when with = when spending time with
Trap
'working on an important project for our boss' is the contrasting example and is already printed.
Your turn
Q36 — 'leadership behaviour and style'
Note wording
Leadership behaviour and ___ affects people's focus
Audio wording
'the way leaders behave, or their style of leading, can affect the focus that followers adopt'
Key paraphrase
behaviour = the way leaders behave; style = style of leading
Trap
The two nouns appear in the same clause — you must take the second one.
Your turn
Q38 — 'a clear vision'
Note wording
passionately communicate a clear ___
Audio wording
'Their speech is passionate and conveys a definitive vision'
Key paraphrase
communicate = convey; clear = definitive
Trap
'change' appears in the previous sentence ('highlight change') — a strong distractor.
Your turn
Q39 — 'create structures'
Note wording
create ___ to make expectations clear
Audio wording
'focus on developing clear structures that tell their followers exactly what is expected of them'
Key paraphrase
create = develop; make expectations clear = tell followers exactly what is expected
Trap
'rewards' and 'punishment' come next — they belong to the following bullet, not this one.
Your turn
