Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A man phones Kingswell Sports Club to ask about membership and facilities. A woman staff member answers his questions about the club's activities, classes, and the three membership schemes (Gold, Silver, Bronze). At the end, she explains how to book an instructor's assessment and gives him a contact name and phone number.

This is a classic IELTS Listening Part 1 "form/notes completion" task — a phone enquiry between two speakers, one asking for information and one providing it.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 1, information is almost always given in the same order as the questions. Watch out for the speaker correcting themselves ("oh no, sorry, it's actually...") — the corrected version is always the answer, not the first number given.
