Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Sandy Fisher, a fitness manager, welcomes new visitors to an LP Leisure Club. She describes the facilities (especially the pool and the fitness suite), explains what happens at your personal assessment, and then compares Gold and Premier membership.

这是一段“健身俱乐部导览讲话”。结构非常清楚:设施 → 个人评估 → 会员种类,跟着这三个小标题走就不会迷路。

💡 Listening tip: Q11–12 is a choose TWO letters question with the key word recently been improved — you need facilities described with words like expanded or recently refurbished, not just facilities that exist. Q13–20 allow NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS, so two-word answers such as 'health problems' and 'safety rules' are fine here.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

In Part 2 the question sheet is nearly always a rewrite of the speaker's words. Learn the four rewrites below and you will recognise the same tricks in every other test. 学会这四种改写方式,以后每场考试都能用。
Q11–12 — 'recently been improved'
Question wording
Which TWO facilities have recently been improved?
Audio wording
'now we've expanded it to eight lanes' / 'our recently refurbished fitness suite'
Key paraphrase
improved = expanded / refurbished / new equipment
Trap
'the outdoor pool' is mentioned only to say it does not exist.
Your turn
'the gym' vs 'fitness suite'
Question wording
the gym
Audio wording
'our recently refurbished fitness suite'
Key paraphrase
gym = fitness suite = the room with exercise equipment
Trap
The word 'gym' is never said. If you wait for it, you lose the mark.
Your turn
Q14 — 'will be explained to you'
Note wording
The ___ will be explained to you before you use the equipment.
Audio wording
'The trainer will then take you through the safety rules for using the equipment'
Key paraphrase
explain to you = take you through
Trap
The passive in the notes hides who does it — the trainer.
Your turn
Q17 — 'are given'
Note wording
Gold members are given ___ to all the LP clubs.
Audio wording
'Gold membership entitles you to free entry at all LP Clubs'
Key paraphrase
be given X = be entitled to X
Trap
'Reciprocal arrangements' belongs to Premier, not Gold.
Your turn
Q18 — 'are given priority'
Note wording
Premier members are given priority during ___ hours.
Audio wording
'LP gives booking preferences to Premier members at peak times'
Key paraphrase
priority = booking preference; hours = times
Trap
You must change 'times' to fit the printed word 'hours' — the answer is only 'peak'.
Your turn
