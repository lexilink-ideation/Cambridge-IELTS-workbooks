Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Adam and Michelle, prepare a presentation on the Laki volcanic eruption of 1783 in Iceland. They discuss why it matters, the observations made at the time, Benjamin Franklin's role, and its consequences in different countries. Expect multiple choice (Q21–24), choose TWO (Q25/26) and matching countries to comments (Q27–30).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task (Q27–30), each country is discussed in turn. Listen for the effect named — poisoned animals, most deaths, deaths of young people, a harsh winter — and match it to the comment, not just to the country name.
