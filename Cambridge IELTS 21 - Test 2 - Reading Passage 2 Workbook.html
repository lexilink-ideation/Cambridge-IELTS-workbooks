📋 Quick Review

This passage describes Mapungubwe, an early southern African kingdom that flourished between the 11th and 13th centuries. It covers its farming and cattle wealth, its layout and burials, its long-distance trade (glass beads from India, gold and ivory), its pottery and gold artefacts, and its eventual decline.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 2 uses paragraph-matching (A–G), a "choose TWO" question, and summary completion. For paragraph matching, the ideas are paraphrased and appear in any order — scan for the specific detail, not matching words.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"The area controlled by the rulers of Mapungubwe had at its heart a large sandstone plateau, which was easily defended due to its inaccessibility."
Main ClauseThe area … had at its heart a large sandstone plateau
Subordinate / Relative Clausewhich was easily defended due to its inaccessibility (relative clause)
Grammar Note"controlled by the rulers" is a reduced relative describing "area". "which" adds information; "due to" = because of.
Simple MeaningAt the centre of the kingdom was a big sandstone plateau that was hard to reach and so easy to defend.
中文马蓬古布韦统治者所控制的地区，其核心是一大片砂岩高原，因难以到达而易于防守。
"Archaeology has revealed extensive layers of bones and manure, which indicate that from the 9th century CE there were large cattle herds, the traditional source of political power in southern African communities."
Main ClauseArchaeology has revealed extensive layers of bones and manure
Subordinate / Relative Clausewhich indicate that … there were large cattle herds (relative) + "the traditional source of political power" (appositive)
Grammar Note"which indicate that …" comments on the finds. The final phrase is an appositive renaming "cattle herds".
Simple MeaningDigs found deep layers of bones and dung, showing there were big cattle herds from the 9th century — and cattle meant political power.
中文考古发现了大量骨骼和粪便层，表明自公元9世纪起便有大群牛，而牛正是南部非洲社会传统的政治权力来源。
"The presence of glass beads, almost certainly from India, indicate there was trade of some sort with other states on the coast who, in turn, traded with merchants travelling from India by sea."
Main ClauseThe presence of glass beads … indicate there was trade … with other states on the coast
Subordinate / Relative Clausewho, in turn, traded with merchants travelling from India by sea (relative clause)
Grammar Note"almost certainly from India" is a parenthetical. "who … in turn" shows a chain of trade. "travelling …" is a participle.
Simple MeaningGlass beads (very likely Indian) show Mapungubwe traded with coastal states, which themselves traded with Indian sea merchants.
中文玻璃珠（几乎可以肯定来自印度）的存在表明，它与沿海各邦有某种贸易，而这些沿海邦又与从印度经海路而来的商人贸易。
"The kingdom of Mapungubwe was already in decline by the late 13th century, probably because overpopulation placed too much stress on local resources, a situation that may have been brought to a crisis point by a series of droughts."
Main ClauseThe kingdom of Mapungubwe was already in decline by the late 13th century
Subordinate / Relative Clausebecause overpopulation placed too much stress on local resources … (reason) + "a situation that may have been brought to a crisis point by … droughts" (relative)
Grammar Note"because" gives the cause. "a situation that …" is an appositive + relative clause explaining the result. "may have been" = past possibility.
Simple MeaningBy the late 1200s the kingdom was declining, probably because too many people strained resources — a problem droughts may have pushed to crisis.
中文到13世纪末，马蓬古布韦王国已经衰落，很可能是因为人口过多给当地资源造成过大压力，而一连串干旱可能把这一局面推向危机。
"A type of decoration, found nowhere else except Great Zimbabwe, involved the crafting of gold into small rectangular sheets and carving geometrical patterns into it."
Main ClauseA type of decoration … involved the crafting of gold into small rectangular sheets and carving geometrical patterns into it
Subordinate / Relative Clausefound nowhere else except Great Zimbabwe (reduced relative)
Grammar Note"found nowhere else …" = which was found nowhere else. Two gerund phrases ("crafting …", "carving …") name the technique.
Simple MeaningThis decoration — seen only here and at Great Zimbabwe — meant shaping gold into small rectangles and carving patterns on them.
中文一种除大津巴布韦外别处未见的装饰工艺，是将黄金加工成小长方形薄片，并在其上雕刻几何图案。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Track how wealth, trade and power connect in the argument.

Cause & Effect

Cattle herds and farming → a food surplus → goods to trade → stronger political power. And later: overpopulation + droughts → stress on resources → decline. Follow each chain.

Evidence (archaeology → conclusion)

The writer moves from find to inference: bones/manure → large herds; spindle whorls → weaving; glass beads → trade with India; large-scale pottery → professional potters.

Comparison

Gold decoration was "found nowhere else except Great Zimbabwe" — a comparison linking the two kingdoms. Comparisons like this often appear in matching and TWO-letter questions.

Chronology

Dates structure the passage: 9th c. (cattle) → 10th c. (cotton) → 11th c. (hilltop) → mid-13th c. (peak, 5,000 people) → late 13th c. (decline). Use dates to locate answers fast.
