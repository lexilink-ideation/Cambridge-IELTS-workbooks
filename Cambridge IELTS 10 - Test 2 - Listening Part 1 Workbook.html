Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

An interviewer stops a woman called Luisa Hardie in the street to carry out a transport survey for the local council. The first half collects her personal details; the second half asks for her suggestions for improvement and what would encourage her to cycle to work.

这是“街头问卷调查”场景。前半段考拼写、门牌号、邮编、职业,后半段考建议类名词。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER throughout. Notice how often the speaker corrects the interviewer: 'Is the postcode GT7?' — 'It's actually G-T-8.' The word actually is your warning that the first version is wrong.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The notes on the question paper compress what people actually say. Practise expanding the note back into a full sentence — that is exactly what your brain must do in real time. 把笔记还原成完整句子。
Q4 — 'Occupation'
Note wording
Occupation: ___
Audio wording
'could I ask what your job is? Are you a student?' — 'I've actually just finished my training. I'm a hairdresser.'
Key paraphrase
occupation = job; 'I'm a ___' gives the answer directly
Trap
'student' and 'training' are both said before the real answer.
Your turn
Q5 — 'Reason for visit to town'
Note wording
Reason for visit to town: to go to the ___
Audio wording
'it's not for shopping today, which would be my normal reason, but to see the dentist'
Key paraphrase
go to the dentist = see the dentist
Trap
'shopping' is said first and is her usual reason — but not today's.
Your turn
Q7 — 'more frequent ___'
Note wording
more frequent ___
Audio wording
'I don't think there are enough trains. That's why I don't use them — you have to wait so long.'
Key paraphrase
more frequent = not enough / you have to wait so long
Trap
The word 'frequent' is never spoken. You must infer it from 'wait so long'.
Your turn
Q8 — 'having ___ parking places'
Note wording
having ___ parking places for bicycles
Audio wording
'where I work there are no safe places to leave your bikes'
Key paraphrase
parking places for bicycles = places to leave your bikes
Trap
The audio is negative ('no safe places'); the note is positive ('having safe parking places').
Your turn
Q10 — 'the opportunity to have cycling ___'
Note wording
the opportunity to have cycling ___ on busy roads
Audio wording
'I wouldn't be all that confident about cycling on such busy roads. I'd like to see you offering training for that.'
Key paraphrase
the opportunity to have = you offering
Trap
'training' was used much earlier about her hairdressing course — do not assume it is unavailable again.
Your turn
