📋 Passage Overview

The concept of intelligence. This academic passage explores how ordinary people (not just scientists) think about intelligence. It distinguishes people's 'implicit theories' from formal 'explicit' ones, gives four reasons why everyday conceptions matter, and compares different theories of intelligence.

💡 Reading tip: For 'which section contains…', turn the prompt into a keyword. For Yes/No/Not Given, decide whether the writer claims the statement — not whether it is generally true.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Looked at in one way, everyone knows what intelligence is; looked at in another way, no one does.
Main Clauseeveryone knows what intelligence is … no one does
Subordinate / RelativeLooked at in one way / in another way (participial adverbials)
Grammar NoteTwo balanced clauses create a paradox; 'looked at…' sets each viewpoint.
Simple MeaningFrom one angle everyone understands intelligence; from another, nobody really does.
中文从一个角度看，人人都知道智力是什么；从另一个角度看，没有人真正知道。
People all have unconscious notions — known as 'implicit theories' — of intelligence, but no one knows for certain what it actually is.
Main ClausePeople all have unconscious notions of intelligence, but no one knows for certain what it actually is
Subordinate / Relativeknown as 'implicit theories' (reduced relative, set off by dashes)
Grammar NoteThe dashes insert a definition; 'but' contrasts having notions with truly knowing.
Simple MeaningEveryone has hidden ideas ('implicit theories') about intelligence, yet no one is sure what it truly is.
中文人们都有关于智力的无意识观念——即“内隐理论”——但没有人确切知道智力究竟是什么。
To better understand the judgments people make about their own and others' abilities, it is useful to learn about people's implicit theories.
Main Clauseit is useful to learn about people's implicit theories
Subordinate / RelativeTo better understand the judgments people make… (infinitive of purpose, fronted)
Grammar NoteThe fronted infinitive states the purpose; 'it is useful to…' is the main idea.
Simple MeaningTo grasp how people judge abilities, it helps to study their implicit theories.
中文要更好地理解人们对自己和他人能力的判断，了解人们的内隐理论是有帮助的。
Parents' implicit theories of their children's language development will determine at what ages they will make various corrections in their children's speech.
Main ClauseParents' implicit theories … will determine at what ages they will make various corrections
Subordinate / Relativeat what ages they will make various corrections (embedded question as object)
Grammar NoteThe embedded 'at what ages…' clause is the object of 'determine'.
Simple MeaningWhat parents believe about language growth decides when they correct their children's speech.
中文父母对孩子语言发展的内隐理论，将决定他们在孩子多大年龄时纠正其言语。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Paradox opener. The passage begins with a paradox (everyone/no one knows what intelligence is) to justify studying people's implicit theories. This framing sets up the whole passage.

Listing reasons. The writer gives 'four reasons' why everyday conceptions matter. Numbered reasons (first, second…) are strong anchors for locating information.

Implicit vs explicit. Keep the key contrast clear: implicit theories are hidden/assumed; explicit theories are stated by researchers. Several questions test whether you can tell them apart.
