📋 Passage Overview

The future of work. Experts discuss how automation and AI (especially the 'algorithmication' of knowledge jobs) will reshape work — the risks to expertise and the more optimistic view that jobs will change rather than vanish. Question types: multiple choice (Q27–30), summary from a list (Q31–34) and matching people (Q35–40).

💡 Reading tip: For matching-people questions, first note each person's main claim, then match the paraphrased statement to whoever argues it — the same idea is worded very differently.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Algorithms are capable of learning from data to undertake tasks that previously needed human judgement, such as reading legal contracts, analysing medical scans and gathering market intelligence.
Main ClauseAlgorithms are capable of learning from data to undertake tasks
Subordinate / Relativethat previously needed human judgement (relative); such as… (examples)
Grammar NoteRelative clause defines the tasks; 'such as' lists parallel -ing examples.
Simple MeaningBy learning from data, algorithms can now do jobs that once needed human judgement.
中文算法能够从数据中学习，去完成以往需要人类判断的任务。
In cases like this, Pachidi believes, a short-sighted view begins to creep into working practices whereby workers learn through the 'algorithm's eyes' and become dependent on its instructions.
Main Clausea short-sighted view begins to creep into working practices
Subordinate / RelativePachidi believes (comment clause); whereby workers learn… and become dependent (relative of manner)
Grammar Note'whereby' = 'by which'; inserted 'Pachidi believes' reports the source.
Simple MeaningPachidi thinks workers start to rely on the algorithm and only see things its way.
中文帕基迪认为，工人开始通过“算法的眼睛”看问题并依赖它的指令，形成短视的工作方式。
If in 30 years, half of 100 jobs are being carried out by robots, that doesn't mean we are left with just 50 jobs for humans.
Main Clausethat doesn't mean we are left with just 50 jobs for humans
Subordinate / RelativeIf in 30 years, half of 100 jobs are being carried out by robots (conditional)
Grammar NoteFirst conditional used to challenge a 'fixed number of jobs' assumption.
Simple MeaningRobots doing 50 jobs doesn't mean only 50 jobs remain for people.
中文即使30年后机器人做了100份工作中的一半，也不意味着只剩50份工作给人类。
It's the laws that restrict the supply of capital to the job market, not the advent of new technologies, that causes unemployment.
Main ClauseIt's the laws … that causes unemployment
Subordinate / Relativethat restrict the supply of capital to the job market (relative); not the advent of new technologies (contrast insertion)
Grammar NoteCleft sentence ('It's X that…') for emphasis; contrast with 'not…'.
Simple MeaningUnemployment comes from laws limiting capital, not from new technology.
中文造成失业的是限制资本流入就业市场的法律，而非新技术的出现。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Opposing views: the passage sets a cautious voice (Pachidi — expertise is at risk) against optimistic voices (Low and McGaughey — jobs change, not vanish). Matching-people questions test whether you can keep the three positions apart.

Problem → solution: the problem is opaque, controlling algorithms; the proposed solution is to make AI 'more trustworthy and transparent'. Track this pairing across paragraphs.

Analogy: McGaughey compares the coming AI change to the industrial and corporate revolutions — 'the next revolution will be social'. Analogies like this are often tested in the final multiple-choice item.
