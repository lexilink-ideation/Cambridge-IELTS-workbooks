Listening Part 2

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 2 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Dan Pearman, founder of a small UK charity called Pedal Power, gives a talk about how the charity collects used bicycles from the public and ships them to developing countries, where they are sold cheaply to help local people and local economies.

He explains how the charity began, describes a financial crisis it survived, and tells listeners three practical ways they can get involved and help.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 2 is usually a monologue, so you won't have a second speaker to help you follow the structure. Listen for signal phrases like "the next thing I'd like to mention" or "moving on to" — they show the talk is shifting to a new topic, which is often exactly where the next answer appears.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / Phrase Simple Meaning 中文 Example Sentence IELTS Note
voluntary done freely, without being paid 志愿的 "He joined a voluntary organisation after graduating." Related noun: volunteer
survey to examine and measure an area of land 测量；勘测 "He did land survey work in Ecuador." Can be noun or verb
carpentry the skill of making things from wood 木工手艺 "His neighbour ran a successful carpentry business." Person: carpenter
radius the distance you can travel from a central point 半径；范围 "Without a bike he could only work within a 3km radius." Often used with "within a ___ radius"
retail price the price a customer pays in a shop 零售价 "The bikes sell at 5% of the local retail price." Contrast with wholesale price
donate to give something to help a good cause 捐赠 "The public can donate old bicycles." Noun: donation
steady continuing regularly, without big changes 稳定的 "The charity needs a steady supply of bikes." Paraphrase: constant, reliable
transform to change something completely 彻底改变 "Bicycles transformed the local economy." Noun: transformation
crisis a time of serious difficulty or danger 危机 "The charity faced a financial crisis in 2000." Plural: crises
enterprise a business project, especially a bold or new one 企业；事业 "They won an Enterprise Award worth £75,000." Listen for capitalised award names
fundraise to collect money for a cause 筹款 "You could help us fundraise with a sponsored ride." Noun: fundraising
sponsor to support an event financially, often per unit completed 赞助 "They organised a sponsored bike ride." Common IELTS collocation: sponsored event

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Why the neighbour succeeded
Question wording Why was Dan's neighbour's carpentry business successful?
Audio meaning "Because he had a bike, he could get to jobs all over the area, while other carpenters without one could only work within about three kilometres."
Key paraphrase "get to jobs all over the area" = owning a bicycle widened his customer reach
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Selling, not giving
Question wording Does the charity give the bicycles away for free?
Audio meaning "We don't just give the bikes away — we sell them, at roughly five percent of what they'd cost new in the shops."
Key paraphrase "sell them, at roughly five percent" = NOT given away free of charge
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — The 2000 crisis
Question wording What caused problems for the charity in 2000?
Audio meaning "We simply ran out of funds to cover our bills, even though containers full of bikes were sitting there ready to go."
Key paraphrase "ran out of funds" = a financial crisis / no money left
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — Ways to help financially
Question wording How can the public support the charity financially?
Audio meaning "You could put on a sponsored bike ride, or any other event, to help us raise some cash."
Key paraphrase "raise some cash" = fundraise for the charity
Your practice
