📋 Passage Overview

A second attempt at domesticating the tomato. Using the CRISPR gene-editing technique, teams in Brazil and China have 're-domesticated' the wild tomato in under three years, making it more nutritious. A US team is doing the same with the groundcherry. The passage weighs the promise and the limits of this approach. Question types: section matching (Q14–18), matching researchers (Q19–23) and sentence completion (Q24–26).

💡 Reading tip: For 'match the researcher', find the exact idea in a quotation and check who said it — the same person may match more than one statement. Do the easiest (most memorable) matches first.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

This approach relies on the revolutionary CRISPR genome editing technique, in which changes are deliberately made to the DNA of a living cell.
Main ClauseThis approach relies on the revolutionary CRISPR genome editing technique
Subordinate / Relativein which changes are deliberately made to the DNA of a living cell (relative clause with preposition + which)
Grammar Note'in which' = 'in this technique'; passive 'are made' focuses on the process.
Simple MeaningThe method uses CRISPR, a technique that deliberately changes a cell's DNA.
中文这种方法依赖革命性的CRISPR基因组编辑技术,即有意地改变活细胞的DNA。
But every time a single plant with a mutation is taken from a larger population for breeding, much genetic diversity is lost.
Main Clausemuch genetic diversity is lost
Subordinate / Relativeevery time a single plant with a mutation is taken from a larger population for breeding (time/conditional clause)
Grammar Note'every time' introduces a repeated-condition clause; main clause is passive.
Simple MeaningEach time you breed from one mutated plant, you lose a lot of genetic variety.
中文但每次从更大的群体中挑出一株带有突变的植物来繁育,都会丧失大量的遗传多样性。
The teams in Brazil and China have now used this knowledge to reintroduce these changes from scratch while maintaining or even enhancing the desirable traits of wild strains.
Main ClauseThe teams in Brazil and China have now used this knowledge to reintroduce these changes from scratch
Subordinate / Relativewhile maintaining or even enhancing the desirable traits of wild strains (adverbial clause of accompanying action)
Grammar NoteInfinitive of purpose 'to reintroduce'; 'while + -ing' shows a simultaneous action.
Simple MeaningThe teams rebuilt the useful changes from the start while keeping the wild plants' good qualities.
中文巴西和中国的团队利用这些知识从头重新引入这些变化,同时保留甚至增强野生品种的优良性状。
But she adds that taking the work further would be expensive because of the need to pay for a licence for the CRISPR technology and get regulatory approval.
Main Clauseshe adds that taking the work further would be expensive
Subordinate / Relativethat taking the work further would be expensive (noun clause); because of the need to pay… and get approval (reason phrase)
Grammar NoteReporting verb 'adds' + that-clause; gerund subject 'taking the work further'.
Simple MeaningShe says going further would cost a lot, because of licence fees and getting approval.
中文但她补充说,进一步推进成本高昂,因为需要支付CRISPR技术的许可费并获得监管批准。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Old vs new comparison. Section A contrasts 3,000 years of old domestication with under three years of CRISPR work — the basis for Q17 ('a comparison between early domestication and recent research').

Advantage vs drawback. The writer balances promise (healthier, disease-resistant crops) against problems (lost diversity, cost, regulation). Track who raises each point for the researcher-matching questions.

Attribution. Each key opinion is tied to a named researcher (Kudla, Gao, Van Eck, Jones). For Q19–23, match the idea to the quotation, not to nearby words.
