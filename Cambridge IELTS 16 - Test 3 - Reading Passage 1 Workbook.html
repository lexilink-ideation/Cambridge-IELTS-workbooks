📋 Passage Overview

Roman shipbuilding and navigation. How the Romans learned shipbuilding from the Greeks and Egyptians, the difference between fast lightweight warships and broad merchant ships, and how sailors navigated. Question types: True / False / Not Given (Q1–5) and summary completion (Q6–13, ONE WORD ONLY).

💡 Reading tip: For summary completion, predict the grammar of each gap (noun? adjective?) and scan for the paragraph that matches the surrounding words before choosing your one word.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

The Romans were not traditionally sailors but mostly land-based people, who learned to build ships from the people that they conquered, namely the Greeks and the Egyptians.
Main ClauseThe Romans were not traditionally sailors but mostly land-based people
Subordinate / Relativewho learned to build ships from the people (relative); that they conquered (relative)
Grammar Note'not X but Y' contrast; two relative clauses add detail.
Simple MeaningThe Romans were landsmen who learned shipbuilding from the peoples they conquered.
中文罗马人传统上并非水手，而多为陆居民族，他们从被征服的希腊人和埃及人那里学会造船。
Warships were built to be lightweight and very speedy, which is why they had no ballast or excess load and were built with a long, narrow hull.
Main ClauseWarships were built to be lightweight and very speedy
Subordinate / Relativewhich is why they had no ballast… and were built with a long, narrow hull (relative of result)
Grammar Note'which is why' links a design goal to its consequences.
Simple MeaningBecause warships had to be light and fast, they carried no ballast and had a narrow hull.
中文战船被造得轻便快速，因此它们不带压舱物，船体又长又窄。
It is worth noting that, contrary to popular perception, rowers were not slaves but mostly Roman citizens enrolled in the military.
Main Clauserowers were not slaves but mostly Roman citizens
Subordinate / RelativeIt is worth noting that… (introductory); contrary to popular perception (contrast insertion); enrolled in the military (reduced relative)
Grammar Note'not X but Y' corrects a common belief; reduced relative describes the citizens.
Simple MeaningUnlike what people think, rowers were soldiers, not slaves.
中文值得注意的是，与普遍看法相反，划桨手并非奴隶，而多为参军的罗马公民。

Main ClauseMediterranean shipbuilders shifted to another method
Subordinate / Relativewhich consisted of building the frame first and then proceeding with the hull (relative)
Grammar NoteA relative clause explains what the new method involved.

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Development over time: planks were first sewn → then mortise-and-tenon → then frame-first construction. Note the chronological shift; summary answers depend on which method came when.

Design → consequence: warships were light and narrow → so they stayed afloat and sailed near the coast. Follow the 'built to be X, which is why Y' pattern.

Contrast (warship vs merchant ship): narrow, fast, ballast-free warships versus broad, deep-hulled merchant ships. The summary (Q6–13) is organised around this contrast.
