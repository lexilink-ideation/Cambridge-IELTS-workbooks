📋 Quick Review

Part 3 is an academic discussion between a tutor and two students, Philip and Janice, who have both been researching how individual differences affect management in the workplace. Philip discusses why individualism matters and its effect on companies; Janice discusses how managers should identify talent, and how companies should treat new employees and manage change.

This three-speaker format is typical of Part 3 — you must track WHO is speaking (tutor vs. Philip vs. Janice) since questions often ask specifically about one speaker's opinion.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In three-way discussions, opinions often shift or get corrected mid-sentence (e.g. "No, it's not overrated..."). Also watch for the tutor's short follow-up questions — they usually introduce a change of speaker and topic, which signals the next question is about to be answered.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Q24 (Janice's view on encouragement)
Question wording"Janice thinks that employers should encourage workers who are potential leaders."
Audio meaning"managers should be targeting those employees who can take the lead in a situation and are not afraid to accept the idea of responsibility."
Key paraphrase"take the lead in a situation" = "potential leaders"
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Q25 (managers' difficulty)
Question wording"Janice suggests that managers may find it difficult to balance conflicting needs."
Audio meaning"to consider the needs of the individual on one hand and group co-operation and conformity on the other. It requires creative thinking... to avoid tension."
Key paraphrase"individual needs... and group co-operation... on the other" = "conflicting needs"
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Q26 (job applicants)
Question wording"Janice believes employers should look for job applicants who can think independently."
Audio meaning"they need to look for someone who's broken the mould and can think for themselves."
Key paraphrase"think for themselves" = "think independently"
