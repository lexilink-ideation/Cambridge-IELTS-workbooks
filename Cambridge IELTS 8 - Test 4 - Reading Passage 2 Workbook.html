📋 Passage Overview

This passage, ‘Biological control of pests’, argues that heavy use of synthetic pesticides is counter-productive: it kills useful organisms and breeds chemical-resistant ‘superbugs’ (the ‘treadmill syndrome’). Using Central-American cotton as an example, it then presents biological control — using natural enemies of pests — as a low-cost, safe alternative, and gives several successful cases from the CIBC and partner laboratories.

💡 Reading strategy tip: For matching sentence endings, first find each named agent or pest in the text, then read the sentence around it to choose the ending. For Yes/No/Not Given, watch for claims the writer never makes (e.g. whether disease pests respond faster than crop pests) — that is Not Given.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"One glaring disadvantage of pesticides’ application is that, while destroying harmful pests, they also wipe out many useful non-targeted organisms."
Main ClauseOne glaring disadvantage … is that … they also wipe out many useful non-targeted organisms
Subordinate / Relativewhile destroying harmful pests (reduced adverbial clause: while they destroy…)
Grammar Note‘the disadvantage is that …’ introduces the point; ‘while …’ = at the same time as.
Simple MeaningA big problem with pesticides is that, as they kill pests, they also kill helpful creatures.
中文使用杀虫剂的一个明显缺点是：在消灭有害害虫的同时，它们也杀死了许多有益的非目标生物。
"Because of their tremendous breeding potential and genetic diversity, many pests are known to withstand synthetic chemicals and bear offspring with a built-in resistance to pesticides."
Main Clausemany pests are known to withstand synthetic chemicals and bear offspring with a built-in resistance
Subordinate / RelativeBecause of their tremendous breeding potential and genetic diversity (adverbial of cause)
Grammar Note‘Because of + noun’ gives the cause; ‘withstand’ = survive; ‘bear offspring’ = produce young.
Simple MeaningThanks to fast breeding and genetic variety, many pests survive chemicals and have resistant young.
中文由于繁殖能力极强、遗传多样性高，许多害虫能够抵抗合成化学品，并产下天生就对杀虫剂有抗性的后代。
"In the face of the escalating perils from indiscriminate applications of pesticides, a more effective and ecologically sound strategy of biological control is fast gaining popularity."
Main Clausea more effective and ecologically sound strategy of biological control is fast gaining popularity
Subordinate / RelativeIn the face of the escalating perils from indiscriminate applications of pesticides (prepositional phrase)
Grammar Note‘In the face of …’ = because of / when confronted with; the main clause states the trend.
Simple MeaningAs careless pesticide use grows more dangerous, biological control is becoming more popular.
中文面对滥用杀虫剂带来的日益加剧的危险，一种更有效、更符合生态的生物防治策略正迅速流行起来。
"The advantage of biological control, in contrast to other methods, is that it provides a relatively low-cost, perpetual control system with a minimum of detrimental side-effects."
Main ClauseThe advantage of biological control … is that it provides a relatively low-cost, perpetual control system
Subordinate / Relativein contrast to other methods (inserted contrast) / with a minimum of detrimental side-effects (added phrase)
Grammar Note‘The advantage … is that …’ defines the benefit; ‘in contrast to’ sets up a comparison.
Simple MeaningUnlike other methods, biological control is cheap, lasting and does little harm.
中文与其他方法相比，生物防治的优势在于：它提供了一种成本相对低廉、可持续的控制系统，且有害副作用极小。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & Effect — Reckless pesticide use kills useful organisms → the ‘treadmill syndrome’ → resistant ‘superbugs’ that need ever more spraying.

Problem / Solution — Problem: chemical-resistant pests and health risks. Solution: biological control — using natural enemies — which is low-cost, self-dispersing and safe in expert hands.

Example / Evidence — Central-American cotton (spraying rose from 8 to 70 times a season) shows the problem; CIBC’s weevils and predators show biological control working.

Contrast — Chemicals give short-term gains but long-term harm; bio-control gives a ‘relatively low-cost, perpetual control system with minimal detrimental side-effects’.
