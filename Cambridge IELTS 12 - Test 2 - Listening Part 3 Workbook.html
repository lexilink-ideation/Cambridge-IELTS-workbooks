Cambridge IELTS 12 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A first tutorial between Beth Cartwright and her student James, who is taking Scandinavian Studies. She asks why he chose the course, what he plans after graduation, and how his literature paper is going (Q21–25, multiple choice). Then she gives him a step-by-step method for writing a paper on the Vikings — a flow-chart task (Q26–30).

💡 Listening tip: Flow-charts are strictly sequential, so the answers arrive in order. Listen for the tutor's staging words: 'what I suggest is…', 'then you should start…', 'Then put…', 'before writing up the paper in full'. Each stage word marks one box.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Tutorial advice is full of soft rejections. Learn to hear 'no' inside a polite sentence.
Q23 — the course that ends
Option wording
Scandinavian literature
What you hear
'the one on Scandinavian literature that's running at the moment will be replaced by more specialised courses'
Key paraphrase
will end this term = will be replaced. Swedish cinema 'will continue'; the Danish TV course starts later
Your turn
Q25 — the advice
Option wording
a study of the social background to the literature
What you hear
'get an understanding of the literature in the context of the society in which it was produced'
Key paraphrase
social background = the context of the society. 'more fruitful than placing it within the history of the genre' rejects A
Your turn
Q26 — what to read first
Option wording
student paper
What you hear
'read an assignment a student wrote last year, which is kept in the library… short and well focused'
Key paraphrase
assignment a student wrote = student paper; the textbook is rejected as too broad
Your turn
Q27 — what to borrow
Option wording
documentary
What you hear
'I'd ignore it [the film] — it's more fantasy than reality. But I've got a recording of a documentary'
Key paraphrase
'I'd ignore it… But' = rejection then replacement → documentary, not film
Your turn
