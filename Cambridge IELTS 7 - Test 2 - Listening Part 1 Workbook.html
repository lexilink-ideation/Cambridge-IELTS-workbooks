📋 Quick Review

In this Section 1 conversation, a man named Patrick Jones phones a motor insurance company's customer service line to ask about insuring his car. A female staff member asks him a series of questions and fills in a form with his personal details, his car details, and his insurance history, then gives him a quote for a new policy.

This is a classic IELTS Listening Part 1 task: a two-person, transactional telephone conversation, where you complete a form with names, addresses, numbers, and single words.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Before the audio starts, read the form headings carefully — they tell you exactly what kind of information is missing (a name, a number, a date, a relationship word). Answers usually come in the same order as the questions on the page, and letters are often spelled out, so be ready to write down single letters quickly.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening Part 1, form headings are often paraphrased in the speaker's spoken question. Recognising these matches quickly is essential for keeping pace with the audio.

Audio wording"And what relationship is he to you?"
Key paraphraserelationship to main driver = what is he to you
