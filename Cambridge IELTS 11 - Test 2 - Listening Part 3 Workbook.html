Cambridge IELTS 11 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Helen and Colin write up their Biology field trip to Rocky Bay. They go through their report section by section: aims, equipment, procedure, method, the map, their findings for each shore zone, and finally the possible sources of error.

Part 3 学术讨论:六道单选(Q21–26)+ 两组“选两个”(Q27–30)。

💡 Listening tip: The pair move through the report in order, and each section heading is announced ('Let's look at the aims first', 'Now, the next section's the procedure'). Use those headings as anchors so you never lose your place.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Options are paraphrases of long spoken sentences. Practise compressing what you hear.
Q21 — the aims
Option A
factors affecting where organisms live
What you hear
'we need something about what causes different organisms to choose particular habitats'
Key paraphrase
'what causes … to choose particular habitats' = 'factors affecting where organisms live'
Your turn
Q23 — Colin's suggestion
Option C
the amount of information provided
What you hear
'I don't think we need all these details of what time we left and what time we got back'
Key paraphrase
'we don't need all these details' = reduce the amount, not the order or the division
Your turn
Q24 — measuring wave speed
Option B
It was simple to carry out.
What you hear
'I was surprised how straightforward that was … just stand there and count the number of waves per minute'
Key paraphrase
'straightforward' = 'simple to carry out'; 'not very precise' rules out option A
Your turn
Q27/28 — the splash zone
Options A & D
lack of water; high temperatures
What you hear
'the shells prevent them from drying out' … 'cover themselves up so they don't get too hot'
Key paraphrase
'drying out' = lack of water; 'get too hot' = high temperatures
Your turn
Q29/30 — errors
Options C & E
incorrect identification; missing some organisms
What you hear
'we might have missed some organisms if they were hiding under a rock' … 'I'm not sure we identified all the species correctly'
Key paraphrase
hedging language ('might have', 'I'm not sure') signals an admitted error
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Student-discussion chunks. Notice the hedging and the polite disagreement.
1.We need something about what causes different organisms to choose particular habitats.
2.But not the piece of string to hold up in the air.
3.I don't think we need all these details of what time we left.
4.I was surprised how straightforward that was.
5.I should have gone up higher to get better visibility.
6.The shells prevent them from drying out.
7.We might have missed some organisms if they were hiding under a rock.
8.I'm not sure we identified all the species correctly though.
