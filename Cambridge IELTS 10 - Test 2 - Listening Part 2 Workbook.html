📋 Quick Review

A public talk about two new city developments: the Brackenside Open-Air Swimming Pool and the Central Park Adventure Playground. The second half describes how the playground is divided into six areas of the world, each with a representative feature.

这是“市政项目介绍”讲话。后半段是配对题:六大洲 ↔ 六个游乐特色,信息密集,必须提前熟悉选项。

💡 Listening tip: For the matching task (Q15–20) you have nine options but only six areas. Read all nine options during the pause and turn them into pictures in your head. The speaker mentions several rejected ideas ('we couldn't run to an ice rink', 'the contribution of cinema and jazz was considered') — these match the unused options C and G.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Options in a multiple-choice question never repeat the speaker's words exactly. Study these five rewrites and you will start hearing the answer instead of hunting for it. 选项永远是改写,不是原词。
Q11 — 'The idea came from'
Option A
local people
Audio wording
'the idea for these initiatives came from you, the public, in the extensive consultation exercise'
Key paraphrase
local people = you, the public
Trap
The City Council conducted the exercise and the SWRDC funded it — neither had the idea.
Your turn
Q12 — 'unusual'
Question wording
What is unusual about Brackenside pool?
Audio wording
'a recycling system — the only one of its kind in the world — which enables seawater to be used'
Key paraphrase
unusual = the only one of its kind in the world
Trap
The 1930s design 'fits in so well with many of the other buildings' — i.e. it is typical, not unusual.
Your turn
Q13 — 'raised worries'
Question wording
Local newspapers have raised worries about…
Audio wording
'quite a bit of discussion in the local press about whether there would be enough room for the number of visitors'
Key paraphrase
size of the facilities = enough room for visitors; raised worries = discussion in the press
Trap
Cost and opening date are both mentioned — but as reassurances, not worries.
Your turn
Q14 — 'has not yet been made'
Question wording
What decision has not yet been made?
Audio wording
'There'll also be a competition for the public to decide on the sculpture … you will decide which famous historical figure'
Key paraphrase
statue = sculpture; not yet decided = the public will decide
Trap
Future tense ('you will decide') marks the undecided item; past/present ('we have engaged Coral White') marks the decided ones.
Your turn
Q15 — Asia
Option E
local animals
Audio wording
'rides and equipment in the shape of snakes, orang-utans, tigers and so on — fauna native to the forests of the region'
Key paraphrase
local animals = fauna native to the region
Trap
If you do not know 'fauna', the list of animals still gives it away — listen for examples.
Your turn
