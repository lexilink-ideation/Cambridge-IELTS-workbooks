📋 Quick Review

An academic lecture examines gender differences in attitudes to saving money, opening with a 1928 quotation from writer George Bernard Shaw about men's and women's relationship to finance. The lecturer discusses what men and women tend to save for, why women are financially vulnerable later in life, and practical advice for building good saving habits.

This is a typical IELTS Listening Part 4 academic monologue — a single lecturer presents a structured talk with statistics, causes, and recommendations, and test-takers complete notes as they listen.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 is the hardest section because there is no pause between questions and the vocabulary is more academic. Use the numbering and headings on the note sheet to predict topic shifts, and don't panic if you miss one gap — move on immediately to the next.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Reason for Avoiding Finance
Question wordingMain reason women avoid financial planning
Audio meaning"Many women simply don't feel confident enough to deal with their own finances."
Key paraphrase"don't feel confident enough" = lack of confidence
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Trigger for Financial Planning
Question wordingWhat usually prompts women to think about their financial future
Audio meaning"Sadly, it's often some kind of crisis — a divorce, the death of a husband — that forces women to face up to their finances, and that's exactly the worst moment to be making big decisions."
Key paraphrase"forces women to face up to" = makes women think about; the crisis is the trigger
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Recommended Savings Split
Question wordingProportion of savings that should be low-risk
Audio meaning"Financial advisors generally suggest keeping at least seven out of every ten dollars saved in safer, low-risk investments."
Key paraphrase"seven out of every ten" = 70%
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — When to Start Planning
Question wordingRecommended age to start thinking about retirement
Audio meaning"Even women still in their early twenties really ought to be giving some thought to their pension."
Key paraphrase"giving some thought to" = starting to think about / planning for
