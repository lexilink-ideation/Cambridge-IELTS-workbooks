📋 Quick Review

A lecture on marine archaeology — the study of human interaction with seas, lakes and rivers. It describes the ancient sunken village of Atlit-Yam and then explains how autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) are transforming the field. This is a note-completion task: ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Technical lectures reuse key nouns (AUVs, seabed, cargo). Predict whether each gap is a material, a tool or a type of goods, and listen for the defining phrase just before it.
