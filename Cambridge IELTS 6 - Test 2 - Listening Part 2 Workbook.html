Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 2 — Listening Part 2

Train Information · Trebirch · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A customer calls a rail information line to ask about train services from Trebirch, a town in the South West of England. The rail staff member explains the local transport network: which station handles local vs. national trains, how frequently trains run to London, what is included in a first class ticket, and the different types of tickets available. The staff member then describes tourist attractions that can be visited by train from Trebirch.

This is a classic Part 2 monologue: primarily one speaker delivering information, answers follow in question order, and the content mixes factual details with some subtle distractors.

💡 Part 2 strategy: Read all question types before the recording begins — you have sentence completion, table completion, AND a multiple-choice set (Q18–20). For Q18–20, you must select exactly three letters from A–G. Listen carefully for attractions the speaker says are accessible now — the science museum is mentioned but is not yet open.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 6  |  🎧 Test: 2, Section 2  |  ❓ Questions: 11–20

📝 Task types: Sentence completion (Q11–14) · Table completion (Q15–17) · Multiple choice ×3 (Q18–20)

🗣️ Setting: Phone call to a rail information service about trains from Trebirch

👥 Speakers: Customer (tourist / caller) · Rail information staff member

Task breakdown

Q11–14 (Sentence completion): Four sentences about train services — which station for which type of service, frequency, and first-class ticket price inclusions. No more than two words and/or a number per blank.

Q15–17 (Table completion): A table showing four ticket types and their restrictions. Two gaps are for the ticket name and a time, one is for a booking requirement. No more than two words and/or a number per blank.

Q18–20 (Multiple choice): Choose THREE letters from A–G. Which attractions can visitors reach at present by train from Trebirch? Careful — one attraction is opening "next year" and does not count.

Q11 — Local Train Station
Question wording Local services depart from ______ railway station.
Exact script line
RAILPERSON: We've got two main train stations in the town. King Street is for local commuter lines and regional services.
Key paraphrase "local commuter lines" → "local services"  |  "is for" → "depart from"  |  Answer: King Street

Q12 — National Train Station
Question wording National services depart from the ______ railway station.
Exact script line
RAILPERSON: Then you need to go to Central Station – that's for all the national services. There are regular trains to London.
Key paraphrase "you need to go to" → "depart from"  |  "national services" appears in both — listen for the station name. Answer: Central Station

Q13 — Train Frequency (Distractor!)
Question wording Trains for London depart every ______ each day during the week.
Exact script line
RAILPERSON: They leave Trebirch every half hour on weekdays and every hour at weekends.
Key paraphrase ⚠️ Distractor: "every hour" is for weekends — wrong answer. "Each day during the week" = "on weekdays". Answer: half hour
Your practice
Q14 — First Class Inclusions
Question wording The price of a first class ticket includes ______.
Exact script line
RAILPERSON: You've got a choice of first and second class and there's a buffet car – though refreshments are included in the cost of a first class ticket.
Key paraphrase "included in the cost" → "the price includes"  |  "buffet car" is the context, but "refreshments" is the answer. Don't write "buffet car".
