📋 Quick Review

A caller asks Jack for advice on surfing holidays in Ireland. Jack recommends family holidays, describes surfing locations in County Clare and County Mayo, then covers the best month to go, the summer temperature and equipment costs. Notes, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: Several answers are numbers (a temperature, a price) and a place name (Carrowniskey). Place names are usually spelled out — write letter by letter and don't panic if it looks unusual.
