Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Part 3, two students, Nadia and Fergus, give their tutor feedback on a food science course. They discuss food safety, antibiotics, plastic pollution, food fraud and DNA testing, suggest new topics, and then describe the stages of their project — developing a new falafel product made with jackfruit.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 3 discussions move quickly between speakers who agree, disagree and add ideas. Listen for who thinks what, and for opinion signals like "I thought there were some problems with that" or "maybe too specialist". These change the answer.
