Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A customer called Andrea Brown telephones a travel agent at World Tours to ask about self-drive tours in the USA. The agent takes her personal details, then describes two possible trips in California and finishes with a table of days, distance, price and what is included.

这是典型的“电话咨询旅游产品”场景:前半段是姓名拼写、地址、邮编、电话,后半段是数字、价格、包含项目。

💡 Listening tip: Q1–Q6 say ONE WORD only — so a two-word answer is automatically wrong. Q7–Q10 say ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. The biggest trap in this part is that the speaker gives several numbers in a row (2,020 / 206 / 632 km) and you must pick the total distance.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The question paper almost never uses the speaker's exact words. Study how the note wording and the recording wording match up — this is the single most useful Section 1 skill. 题目上的措辞和录音中的措辞几乎从不相同。
Q2 — 'Heard about company from'
Note wording
Heard about company from: ___
Audio wording
'can I ask you where you heard about World Tours? … I read about you in the newspaper'
Key paraphrase
heard about = read about; the source is the medium, not a person
Trap
The agent offers 'From a friend? Or did you see an advert?' — both are rejected by 'No'.
Your turn
Q4 — 'not a ___'
Note wording
customer wants to stay in a lodge, not a ___
Audio wording
'a lodge or on the campsite' … 'I don't like the idea of staying in a tent'
Key paraphrase
not a X = I don't like the idea of X
Trap
'campsite' is the word the agent uses; 'tent' is the word the customer uses. The gap needs the customer's word.
Your turn
Q6 — 'spend time on the ___'
Note wording
At San Diego, wants to spend time on the ___
Audio wording
'That's good for beaches isn't it?' … 'We want some time for sunbathing and swimming'
Key paraphrase
spend time on the beach = sunbathing and swimming
Trap
The zoo is suggested and refused ('I don't think so').
Your turn
Q7 — 'Total distance'
Note wording
Total distance ___ km
Audio wording
'lasts twelve days and covers 2,020 kilometres. The shortest journey is 206 km and the longest is 632 kilometres'
Key paraphrase
total distance = covers X kilometres
Trap
Three numbers in one sentence. Only the first is a total.
Your turn
Q9 — the cheaper price
Note wording
Price (per person) £ ___
Audio wording
'is that cheaper then?' — 'Yes, it's almost a hundred pounds cheaper. It's £429 per person'
Key paraphrase
£429 ≈ £525 minus almost £100
Trap
You could calculate it, but do not — just write the number you hear.
Your turn
