📋 Quick Review

Sue phones the Burnham tourist office and speaks to Martin about free activities on 29 June for her family. He describes a gallery event, free concerts, a boat race, and finally Paxton Nature Reserve.

不常见的 Part 1 题型组合:前六题是三选一单选,后四题才是句子填空。

💡 Listening tip: Q1–6 are multiple choice, so read all three options in the 30-second preview and underline the difference between them. Almost every option is mentioned — you must hear which one is actually true. Q7–10 then switch to ONE WORD ONLY sentence completion.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

In multiple choice, the correct option is a paraphrase; the wrong options are usually exact words you heard.
Q3 — when concerts happen
Option B
at lunchtime
What you hear
'one or two in the morning, the majority at lunchtime, and a couple in the evening'
Key paraphrase
'the majority' = 'most' → lunchtime; 'one or two' and 'a couple' are small numbers
Your turn
Q4 — the 4 pm concert
Option A
in a museum
What you hear
'They're performing in the central library at 1 o'clock, then at 4 it's in the City Museum'
Key paraphrase
three venues, three times — you must match the time first
Your turn
Q6 — the boat team
Option A
won a regional competition earlier this year
What you hear
'came first in the West of England regional championship in May this year'
Key paraphrase
'came first' = 'won'; 'in May this year' = 'earlier this year'
Your turn
Q8 — unusual flowers
Question note
This is a particularly good time for seeing certain unusual ___
What you hear
'At the moment you can see various flowers that are pretty unusual … They're looking good right now.'
Key paraphrase
'at the moment / right now' = 'this is a particularly good time' → flowers
Your turn
Q10 — swimming
Question note
Part of the ___ has been made suitable for swimming
What you hear
'Part of it has been fenced off to make it safe for children to swim in … The lake is too deep, so swimming isn't allowed there.'
Key paraphrase
'fenced off … safe to swim in' = 'made suitable for swimming' → river
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Tourist-information chunks. Times and place names must be exact.
1.The 'Family Welcome' event runs from ten thirty until two o'clock.
2.It demonstrates how ceramics are made.
3.One or two in the morning, the majority at lunchtime.
4.At four it's in the City Museum.
5.The race starts at Offord Marina and goes as far as Summer Pool.
6.It's a good idea to bring binoculars if you have them.
7.Part of it has been fenced off to make it safe for children to swim in.
8.Parking is very limited, so it's usually much easier to go by bus.
