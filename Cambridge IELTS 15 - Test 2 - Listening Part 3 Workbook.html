Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 15 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Cathy and Graham, plan a display about the novelist Charles Dickens. They decide who the display is for (Q21–22), why they chose Dickens (Q23–24), and then match a social topic (poverty, education, crime, etc.) to each of six of his novels (Q25–30).

💡 Listening tip: 'Choose TWO' means both speakers must agree. In the matching task the six novels are said in order, but the topics are reworded — listen for the theme each novel is said to illustrate, and cross off used letters.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Options and topics are reworded. Match the meaning, not the words.

Option
B — used his publications to draw attention to social problems
What you hear
'through his books he made readers aware of injustice and hardship'
Key paraphrase
'made readers aware of injustice' = drew attention to social problems → B
Your turn
