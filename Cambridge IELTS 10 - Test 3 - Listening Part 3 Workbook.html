📋 Quick Review

Two Theatre Studies students, Rob and Mia, talk after a student production. The first half is about the report Rob must write on the play; the second half is Mia's advice on applying for the year abroad option.

这是“学生互相讨论课程”的场景。前半段是多选题(表演与报告),后半段是配对题(留学申请的五个阶段)。

💡 Listening tip: In Q26–30 you match five stages to five actions from a box of seven. The stages follow the conversation in strict order, so if you miss one, jump forward — do not go back. Two options (B: get a letter of recommendation; F: make travel arrangements) are never mentioned at all.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

In Part 3, the correct option is a short abstract summary of a long concrete story. Practise compressing what the speakers say into one abstract phrase. 把具体叙述压缩成抽象短语,就是正确选项。
Q21 — 'the way he imagined his grandfather'
Option C
the way he imagined his grandfather at work
Audio wording
'I'd visualise what he must have been like in the past, when he was sitting in his consulting room listening to his patients'
Key paraphrase
imagined = visualise what he must have been like
Trap
'He must have all sorts of stories, but he never says much about his work' explicitly kills option A.
Your turn
Q23 — 'clear guidelines'
Option A
He likes to have clear guidelines.
Audio wording
'Sophia did. That was OK — she helped us work out exactly what to do for the production. And that made me feel better.'
Key paraphrase
clear guidelines = knowing exactly what to do
Trap
Mia's follow-up ('When you understood what needed doing?') confirms it — listen for the echo question.
Your turn
Q24 — 'changing social attitudes'
Option B
changing social attitudes
Audio wording
'how relationships between children and their parents, or between the public and people like bank managers or the police were shifting'
Key paraphrase
changing social attitudes = relationships between social groups were shifting
Trap
'bank managers' may make you think of economics; 'the police' may make you think of politics.
Your turn
Q26 — 'show ability in Theatre Studies'
Action E
show ability in Theatre Studies
Audio wording
'you need to work really hard in all your theatre studies modules. Only students with good marks get places — you have to prove that you know your subject really well.'
Key paraphrase
show ability = get good marks / prove you know your subject
Trap
'competitive' and 'limited number of places' come first — they describe the situation, not the action.
Your turn
Q30 — 'plan for the final year'
Action C
plan for the final year
Audio wording
'I got so involved I forgot all about making arrangements for when I came back here for the final year. Make sure you stay in touch so they know your choices for the optional modules.'
Key paraphrase
plan for the final year = arrange your optional-module choices in advance
Trap
'make travel arrangements' (option F) sounds plausible because of 'arrangements' — but the arrangements are academic.
Your turn
