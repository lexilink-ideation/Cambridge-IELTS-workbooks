Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 3 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Part 2, Jon, who runs a vegan street food business called "Veg Out", gives a talk with advice on starting a street food business. He explains why street food is popular, the best places to sell, buying equipment, and shares real problems faced by other businesses (Thai Basil, Basque, Lou’s Kitchen, Chip Chop).

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 2 monologues use "signposting" language: "The first is…", "on the other hand", "for example". For the matching task (business → problem), listen for the specific difficulty each named business had, not just the topic.
