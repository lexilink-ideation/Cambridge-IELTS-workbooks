📋 Quick Review

Two students, Rosie and Martin, plan a presentation on woolly mammoths with their tutor. They discuss the discovery of a mammoth tooth on St Paul's Island (Alaska) by Russell Graham, why the find was significant, how the island became cut off, and how the team dated the extinction — then review what action each section of the talk needs.

💡 Listening tip: When students review 'what to do next' for each section, listen for the action decided: write down ideas, contact the researcher, ask the audience, reorder chronologically, practise, add your own viewpoint.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Answers hide behind agreement and rephrasing. Train the swap.

Question idea
Why was the tooth significant?
What you hear
'the mammoth bones previously found on the mainland were much less recent'
Key paraphrase

Your turn
