Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

📋 Quick Review

Laura Carlton phones the owner of a holiday flat because the hot water and heating are not working. He talks her through the water heater controls, tells her where to find five household items, and gives advice about parking, takeaways and the railway museum. Question types: diagram labelling (Q11–13), matching items to locations (Q14–18) and note completion (Q19–20).

💡 Listening tip: Q11–13 is a diagram of controls, so position language decides everything: 'the round one on the far left', 'the middle one… slightly larger', 'below the heating controls in the middle', 'a little square indicator under the third knob'. Sketch the layout on your question paper before the recording starts and label as you listen.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Locations and functions are described, never named. Train the swap from description to label.
Q11 — The first control
Option B
on/off switch
What you hear
'the round one on the far left - is the most important one… It's the main control switch. Make sure it's in the on position'
Key paraphrase
'main control switch' + 'on position' = on/off switch → B
Your turn
Q13 — The square indicator
Option E
warning indicator
What you hear
'a little square indicator under the third knob - that's a kind of alarm light. It'll flash if you need to reset the heater'
Key paraphrase
'alarm light' that 'flashes' = warning indicator → E (not 'electricity indicator')
Your turn
Q16 — The spare key
Option D
next to window in living room
What you hear
'the spare key to the back door is hanging on a hook on the wall by the sitting room window'
Key paraphrase
'sitting room' = living room; 'by the window' = next to the window → D (the key is FOR the back door, but not kept there)
Your turn
Q20 — Museum closing day
Note line
Railway museum closed on ______
What you hear
'not on Thursdays which is market day… it's also the only day of the week when they're not open'
Key paraphrase
'the only day when they're not open' = closed → Thursday(s)
Your turn
