📋 Quick Review

This Part 2 is a podcast monologue. Claire Lemarre, a film makeup artist with over 20 years’ experience, gives advice on how to become a makeup trainee. She talks about first jobs, big-budget films, the pressures of the job, meeting actors, makeup kits, building a portfolio, and the duties a trainee performs.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 2 one speaker talks for a long time. The multiple-choice options often use words you hear in the audio as distractors. Do not choose an answer just because you heard a word — match the whole meaning. Watch for words like but, however and don’t expect that change the meaning.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingtrainees should expect to get travel expenses
Audio meaning"Make sure your transport costs are covered"
Key paraphrasetransport costs = travel expenses

Paraphrase Example 2
Question wordingtrainees may get experience of working with different ethnicities
