Cambridge IELTS 12 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A tourist podcast about the Sheepmarket, one of the oldest parts of the city. The speaker covers who now lives there, what the side streets sell, a Young Fashion competition and parking (Q11–14, multiple choice), then guides the listener round six art and history landmarks on a map (Q15–20).

💡 Listening tip: For map labelling, read the map before the audio: name every road, mark the public gardens and Station Square, and note which locations are on corners or dead ends. Then listen only for three things — the road, the side of the road, and what it is next to or opposite.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Map tasks are built on prepositions of place. Drill them until they are instant.
Q15 — The Reynolds House
Map clue needed
road + side + neighbour
What you hear
'on the north side of Crawley Road, next to the footpath that leads to the public gardens'
Key paraphrase
north side = the upper side on the map; 'next to the footpath' pins it exactly → H
Your turn
Q17 — The Museum
Map clue needed
road + opposite + facing a junction
What you hear
'on City Road. It's on the other side of the road from the public gardens, immediately facing the junction with Hill Road'
Key paraphrase
'the other side of the road from' = opposite; 'facing the junction' = directly at the corner → F
Your turn
Q18 — The Contemporary Art Gallery
Map clue needed
a road that goes nowhere else
What you hear
'a little road that leads off Station Square… The road ends at the gallery — it doesn't go anywhere else'
Key paraphrase
'the road ends at' = a dead end / cul-de-sac → G
Your turn
Q14 — free parking
Option wording
buy something in the shops
What you hear
'if you can present a receipt from one of the local stores, you'll not be charged at all'
Key paraphrase
buy something = present a receipt from a store; 'not be charged at all' = free → B
Your turn
