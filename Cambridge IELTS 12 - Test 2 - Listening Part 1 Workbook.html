📋 Quick Review

A woman phones the Kenton Festival box office. The man on the line runs through the programme: the opening ceremony in the town centre, an evening fireworks display, then the events that run right through the festival — videos, traditional dances and concerts — and finally how to buy tickets. Note completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: Festival programmes are full of numbers and venues. Two traps here: the opening ceremony has three times (gathering 2.00, start 2.45, finish 5.30) — the notes ask for the starting time; and the market has two types (outdoor / covered).

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Programme notes compress spoken sentences into headings. Practise the swap in both directions.
Q1 — the start time
Question note
In town centre, starting at ___
What you hear
'People start gathering around 2 o'clock… the events will start at 2.45, and finish about 5.30'
Key paraphrase
starting at refers to the events, not the gathering → 2.45
Your turn
Q6 — the video topic
Question note
Videos about relationships that children have with their ___
What you hear
'all connected with relationships between children and their grandparents'
Key paraphrase
'It makes a change from children and parents' confirms it is grandparents
Your turn
Q8 — the type of dance
Question note
Performance of ___ dances
What you hear
'There isn't any ballet, I'm afraid, but there'll be a demonstration of traditional dances'
Key paraphrase
'I'm afraid… but' = the previous idea is rejected → traditional, not ballet
Your turn
Q9 — which market
Question note
Venue: the ___ market in the town centre
What you hear
'It's in the market in the town centre — the outdoor one, not the covered market'
Key paraphrase
A dash followed by 'not…' = a correction → outdoor
Your turn
