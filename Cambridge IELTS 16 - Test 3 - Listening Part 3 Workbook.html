📋 Quick Review

Two students, Adam and Rosie, prepare a presentation on diet and obesity. They review Adam's sweetener experiment (Q21&22) and Rosie's fat-content experiment (Q23&24), then discuss restaurants, exercise and what to include (Q25–30, multiple choice).

💡 Listening tip: Track WHO says each point and listen for agreement or a change of plan ('actually, I'm not sure we should…'). The answer is often the final decision, not the first idea.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

MC options reword the discussion. Focus on the point actually being made.
Menu design (Q25)
Option C
menus organised in a particular way
What you hear
'put the low-calorie items at the beginning and end… people will probably go for the food with fewer calories'
Key paraphrase
position of items on the menu = organised in a particular way
Your turn
Exercise levels (Q27)
Option B
most people overestimate how much they do
What you hear
'40% of men say they do this… but only 6% actually do the recommended amount'
Key paraphrase
claimed amount >> measured amount = they overestimate
Your turn
Leaving exercise out (Q29)
Option A
they should probably leave it out
What you hear
'our tutor did say we need to focus on causes and solutions in terms of nutrition'
Key paraphrase
a nutrition focus means exercise is dropped
Your turn
