📋 Passage Overview

'Reducing the Effects of Climate Change' by Mark Rowe surveys geo-engineering — the deliberate large-scale manipulation of the environment. Paragraphs A–D describe schemes (space sunshades, iron in the ocean, sulphur aerosols, cables on Greenland ice, birch trees, re-routed rivers); paragraphs E–H give the risks and the scientists' own doubts.

题型:段落信息配对 Q27–29 + 表格填空 Q30–36 + 人物观点配对 Q37–40。

💡 Reading tip: For 'Which paragraph contains…', do NOT read in order — scan for the distinctive detail. For matching statements to scientists (Q37–40), find each scientist's quotation marks first; the answer is almost always inside the quotation.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Such is our dependence on fossil fuels, and such is the volume of carbon dioxide already released into the atmosphere, that many experts agree that significant global warming is now inevitable.
Main Clausemany experts agree that significant global warming is now inevitable
Subordinate / Relative'Such is our dependence…, and such is the volume…, that…' = inverted result structure
Grammar NoteFronted 'Such + be + noun … that…' causes inversion. It is an emphatic way of saying 'Our dependence is so great that…'.
Simple MeaningWe rely on fossil fuels so heavily, and have released so much CO₂, that serious warming cannot now be avoided.
中文我们对化石燃料的依赖如此之深,已经排入大气的二氧化碳量如此之大,以至于许多专家一致认为显著的全球变暖已不可避免。
But while a few countries are making major strides in this regard, the majority are having great difficulty even stemming the rate of increase, let alone reversing it.
Main Clausethe majority are having great difficulty even stemming the rate of increase
Subordinate / Relativewhile a few countries are making major strides in this regard (contrast clause); 'let alone reversing it' = emphatic addition
Grammar Note'while' = 'whereas'; 'let alone' introduces an even less likely possibility.
Simple MeaningA few countries are doing well, but most cannot even slow the rise, never mind reverse it.
中文但尽管少数国家在这方面取得了重大进展,多数国家连遏制增速都非常困难,更谈不上逆转。
His scheme would employ up to 16 trillion minute spacecraft, each weighing about one gram, to form a transparent, sunlight-refracting sunshade in an orbit 1.5 million km above the Earth.
Main ClauseHis scheme would employ up to 16 trillion minute spacecraft
Subordinate / Relative'each weighing about one gram' = absolute participle phrase; 'to form a … sunshade' = purpose infinitive; 'in an orbit 1.5 million km above the Earth' = place phrase
Grammar NoteNote 'minute' here is an adjective /maɪˈnjuːt/ = 'very small', not the noun 'minute'.
Simple MeaningHe would use 16 trillion tiny one-gram spacecraft to make a see-through sunshade far above the Earth.
中文他的方案将使用多达16万亿个微型航天器,每个约重一克,在距地球150万公里的轨道上形成一面透明的、可折射阳光的遮阳罩。
Whereas the region's native evergreen pines shade the snow and absorb radiation, birches would shed their leaves in winter, thus enabling radiation to be reflected by the snow.
Main Clausebirches would shed their leaves in winter
Subordinate / RelativeWhereas the region's native evergreen pines shade the snow and absorb radiation (contrast clause); 'thus enabling radiation to be reflected by the snow' = result participle
Grammar Note'Whereas' sets up a direct two-way contrast; 'thus + -ing' expresses result.
Simple MeaningPines keep their leaves and soak up heat; birches lose theirs, so the snow can reflect the sunlight.
中文当地原生的常绿松树会遮住积雪并吸收辐射,而桦树在冬天落叶,从而使辐射能被积雪反射回去。
You may bring global temperatures back to pre-industrial levels, but the risk is that the poles will still be warmer than they should be and the tropics will be cooler than before industrialisation.
Main Clausethe risk is that the poles will still be warmer … and the tropics will be cooler
Subordinate / RelativeYou may bring global temperatures back to pre-industrial levels (concession); two parallel that-clauses after 'the risk is'
Grammar Note'may … but …' concession; two comparatives ('warmer than they should be', 'cooler than before').
Simple MeaningAverage temperatures could return to normal, but the poles would stay too warm and the tropics would become too cold.
中文你也许能把全球气温拉回工业化前的水平,但风险在于两极仍会比应有的更暖,而热带则会比工业化前更冷。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition first. Paragraph A gives the general definition of geo-engineering ('a term which generally refers to…') — that is exactly what Q29 asks for. When a question says 'a common definition of X', look for the phrase 'refers to' or 'is known as'.

Example → generalisation. Paragraph B opens with a proven small-scale example (Moscow May Day parades) before moving to ambitious proposals. Q28 ('an example of a successful use') therefore points to B.

Model / precedent. Paragraph D says the aerosol idea is 'modelled on historic volcanic explosions, such as that of Mount Pinatubo' — a project based on an earlier natural phenomenon (Q27 → D).

Balance of views. Paragraphs E–G are all risks and doubts; paragraph H swings back to support. Notice how each named scientist gets one clear position — that structure is what makes Q37–40 solvable.

Attitude words. 'cautious', 'guarded', 'certainly something to worry about', 'the best option is to avoid the need … altogether', 'little faith in'. These reveal the writer's balanced, sceptical tone.
