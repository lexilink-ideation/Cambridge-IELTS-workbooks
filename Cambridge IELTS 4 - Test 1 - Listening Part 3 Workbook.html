📋 Quick Review

Melanie, a student on a Population Studies course, visits her lecturer Dr Johnson about her second assignment. They discuss why it is late, whether she can get an extension, which journal articles to read, and a bar graph about why people change where they live. Expect multiple-choice, matching (A–G) and chart-labelling.

💡 Listening tip: In the article-matching task, listen for the advice verb — 'really worth reading', 'just look at the part on…', 'I wouldn't bother with that'. The recommendation, not the topic, gives the answer.
