📋 Quick Review

An informal phone call between two friends. Susie has just started working at the newly re-opened public library and tells Paul what it now offers: new book sections, a community room, activities for children, events and services for adults, a shop and parking. Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: This is a friendly chat, not an announcement, so the answers are buried in ordinary conversation. Use the printed headings ('For younger children', 'For adults', 'Other information') to stay in place — and remember the instruction is ONE WORD ONLY, so 'blood sugar' would be wrong; only sugar fits.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Notes turn casual speech into formal phrases. Practise moving between the two registers.
Q3 — the community room
Question note
a community room for meetings (also possible to ___ there)
What you hear
'It can be hired out for meetings, but at other times people can use it to study'
Key paraphrase
also possible to ___ = at other times people can use it to ___ → study
Your turn
Q6 — the author's talk
Question note
a local author talks about a novel based on a real ___
What you hear
'she based the story on a crime that actually took place here years ago'
Key paraphrase
a real ___ = a ___ that actually took place → crime
Your turn
Q7 — the IT service
Question note
IT support is available on Tuesdays — no ___ is necessary
What you hear
'they don't need to make an appointment or anything, they just turn up'
Key paraphrase
no ___ is necessary = they don't need to make an ___ → appointment
Your turn
Q8 — the health check
Question note
free check of blood ___ and cholesterol levels
What you hear
'measure the level of sugar in your blood, and they check cholesterol levels at the same time'
Key paraphrase
blood ___ = the level of ___ in your blood → sugar (one word only!)
Your turn
