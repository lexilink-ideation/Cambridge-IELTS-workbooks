📋 Quick Review

A lecturer gives an introductory geography lecture. The talk explains why we study geography, its two main branches and specific study areas, and what geographers actually do — collecting data (censuses, satellite images), analysing it (looking for patterns), and publishing findings as maps, aerial photos and Landsat pictures. You complete a set of notes (ONE WORD ONLY).

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 is a single academic lecture with no breaks. The notes follow the talk in order, so read the headings first and predict the type of word needed (noun, verb, etc.). 'ONE WORD ONLY' means a two-word answer is wrong — choose the single key noun.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ35: geography helps us understand our surroundings and the associated ___.
What you hear / read"we wouldn't be able to identify all the problems that relate to them."
Key paraphrase'associated problems' = problems that relate to our surroundings → problems.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptQ36: collect information in the form of ___ using computer and satellite technology.
What you hear / read"We also need images of the earth's surface which we can produce by … computer-generation … or satellite relays."
Key paraphrase'in the form of images' — single noun answer: images.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptQ38: a two-dimensional map will always have some ___.
What you hear / read"You can't exactly replicate something three-dimensional … on flat paper … there'll always be a certain degree of distortion."
Key paraphraseflat map of round Earth → distortion.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4
Question / promptQ40: Landsat pictures used for monitoring ___ conditions.
What you hear / read"you'll all be familiar with the information they give us about the weather."
Key paraphrase'weather conditions' → weather.
Your practice
