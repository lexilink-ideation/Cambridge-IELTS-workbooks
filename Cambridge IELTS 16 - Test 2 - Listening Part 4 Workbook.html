📋 Quick Review

A talk on dancing and health: how dancing lifts mood, why group dancing feels better, and its many benefits for older people and for weight (including Zumba). Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Benefit-list talks move quickly from one advantage to the next. Predict a noun for each gap and listen for the exact word — 'balance', 'brain', 'motivation' all sound ordinary but must be spelled right.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The note sheet paraphrases the talk. Learn to hear the exact single noun.
Fitness barrier (Q33)
Question note
anyone can join, even those with low ___
What you hear
'no barriers to participation… even those whose standard of fitness is quite low'
Key paraphrase
'standard of fitness is low' = low fitness
Your turn
Fewer falls (Q34)
Question note
dancing improves ___, lowering the risk of falls
What you hear
'lowers the risk of falls… by helping people to improve their balance'
Key paraphrase
the improved thing that prevents falls = balance
Your turn
Zumba calories (Q38)
Question note
a class of 40 minutes burns about 370 ___
What you hear
'a class lasting 40 minutes burns about 370 calories'
Key paraphrase
write the counted unit = calories
Your turn
