📋 Passage Overview

How tennis rackets have changed. The passage explains how small, often invisible modifications to tennis rackets — especially to the strings and frame — have transformed the professional game. It uses Andy Murray, the Bryan brothers, Pete Sampras and others as examples, and traces the history from natural gut strings to modern co-polyester. Question types: True/False/Not Given and note completion.

💡 Reading tip: For TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN, decide carefully between FALSE (the text says the opposite) and NOT GIVEN (the text simply does not say). Q1 and Q3 are classic NOT GIVEN traps — the passage gives related information but never confirms the exact claim.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

It was an incredible achievement by any standard — made even more remarkable by the fact that he did this during a period considered to be one of the strongest in the sport's history.
Main ClauseIt was an incredible achievement.
Subordinate / Relative'made even more remarkable by the fact that…' (participial phrase) + 'considered to be one of the strongest' (reduced relative).
Grammar NoteA dash adds extra comment; 'the fact that + clause' names the reason.
Simple MeaningHis win was amazing, and even more so because rivals then were so strong.
中文这在任何标准下都是了不起的成就——而当时正值该项运动史上最强的时期之一，这让成就更显非凡。
Consequently, the specific rackets used by the world's elite are not actually readily available to the public; rather, each racket is individually made to suit the player who uses it.
Main ClauseThe rackets used by the elite are not readily available to the public.
Subordinate / Relative'rather, each racket is individually made' (contrast clause) + 'who uses it' (relative clause).
Grammar Note'rather' signals correction/contrast; the semicolon joins two related ideas.
Simple MeaningTop players' rackets aren't sold to ordinary people; each one is custom-made.
中文因此，顶尖选手所用的球拍其实并不面向公众出售；相反，每支球拍都是为使用者量身定制的。
At one time, all tennis rackets were strung with natural gut made from the outer layer of sheep or cow intestines.
Main ClauseAll tennis rackets were strung with natural gut.
Subordinate / Relative'made from the outer layer of…' (reduced relative describing 'gut').
Grammar NotePassive 'were strung' + past participle 'made' reduces 'which was made'.
Simple MeaningLong ago, every racket used natural string from animal intestines.
中文曾经，所有网球拍都用天然肠线穿弦，这种线取自羊或牛肠的外层。
It's a perfect fit for the style of tennis now played, where players tend to battle it out from the back of the court rather than coming to the net.
Main ClauseIt's a perfect fit for the style of tennis now played.
Subordinate / Relative'where players tend to battle it out…' (relative clause of place/manner).
Grammar Note'rather than + -ing' contrasts two behaviours.
Simple MeaningCo-polyester suits today's game, where players rally from the baseline instead of rushing the net.
中文它非常契合如今的打法：球员多在底线相持，而不是上网。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & effect: 'as the line between winning and losing becomes thinner… even these slight changes become more important.' A small margin (cause) makes tiny modifications matter (effect).

Contrast: 'One was visible and widely publicised… Another change was so subtle as to pass unnoticed.' The writer sets a noticed change against an unnoticed one.

Classification: 'Modifications can be divided into two categories: those to the string bed and those to the racket frame.' Recognising this split helps you locate frame-related answers (weights, handle, grips).

Example signalling: 'Take the US professional players Mike and Bob Bryan, for example…' — 'for example' tells you a general claim (customisation) is about to be illustrated.
