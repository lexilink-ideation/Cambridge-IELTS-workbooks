📋 Quick Review

Frank, who has just retired and moved to Southoe, phones Jane, who co-ordinates voluntary work in the village. She lists jobs at the library and lunch club, help needed for individual residents next week, and three social events. Notes + a table, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Part 1 mixes notes and a small table. In the table, follow the columns (date · event · location · help needed) so you drop each answer into the right row. Predict a noun for every gap.
