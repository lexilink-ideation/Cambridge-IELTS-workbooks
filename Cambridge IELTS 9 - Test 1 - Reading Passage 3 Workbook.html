Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 1 — Reading Passage 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Passage Overview

The history of the tortoise. The writer explains that all life began in the sea, that many land animals later returned to the water, and that turtles and tortoises are a special case. Using bone measurements plotted on a triangular graph, Joyce and Gauthier showed that two ancient fossils were land tortoises — and that today's land tortoises are descended from ancestors who went back to the sea and then came ashore again: a 'remarkable double return'. Question types: short answer (Q27–30), True/False/Not Given (Q31–33), flow-chart completion (Q34–39) and multiple choice (Q40).

💡 Reading tip: Flow-chart completion (Q34–39) follows the passage in order and mirrors the method paragraph. Find the sentence that begins 'Walter Joyce and Jacques Gauthier… obtained three measurements' and read straight down from there — Steps 1–4 appear in exactly that sequence. Q40 asks for the writer's main point, so use the final paragraph ('a remarkable double return'), not an interesting detail.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

At various points in evolutionary history, enterprising individuals within many different animal groups moved out onto the land, sometimes even to the most parched deserts, taking their own private seawater with them in blood and cellular fluids.
Main ClauseEnterprising individuals… moved out onto the land.
Subordinate / Relative'taking their own private seawater with them in blood and cellular fluids' = participial phrase adding a simultaneous action.
Grammar Note'-ing' participle after the main clause = 'and they took…'. The metaphor 'private seawater' means blood and body fluids are salty like the sea.
Simple MeaningOver time, adventurous animals from many groups moved onto land — even into deserts — carrying salty fluids in their bodies like a portable sea.
中文在进化史的不同阶段，许多动物类群中敢于开拓的个体走上陆地，有时甚至进入最干旱的沙漠，并在血液和细胞液中携带着属于自己的「海水」。
Nevertheless, a good number of thoroughgoing land animals later turned around, abandoned their hard-earned terrestrial re-tooling, and returned to the water again.
Main ClauseA good number of land animals turned around, abandoned…, and returned to the water.
Subordinate / RelativeThree parallel past-tense verbs sharing one subject.
Grammar Note'Nevertheless' contrasts with the previous sentence about the difficulty of moving to land. 'Terrestrial re-tooling' is an engineering metaphor for evolutionary redesign.
Simple MeaningEven so, many fully land-adapted animals later gave up those adaptations and went back into the water.
中文然而，相当多完全适应陆地的动物后来却掉头而返，放弃了来之不易的陆生「改造」，重新回到水中。
However, they are, in one respect, less fully given back to the water than whales or dugongs, for turtles still lay their eggs on beaches.
Main ClauseThey are less fully given back to the water than whales or dugongs.
Subordinate / Relative'in one respect' (adverbial insertion) + 'for turtles still lay their eggs on beaches' (reason clause).
Grammar Note'for' = 'because' in formal writing. Comparative 'less… than' ranks turtles against whales.
Simple MeaningTurtles have returned to the sea less completely than whales, because they still lay eggs on land.
中文不过，在某一方面，它们回归水中的程度不如鲸或儒艮彻底，因为海龟仍然在沙滩上产卵。
They used a kind of triangular graph paper to plot the three measurements against one another.
Main ClauseThey used a kind of triangular graph paper.
Subordinate / Relative'to plot the three measurements against one another' = infinitive of purpose.
Grammar Note'to + verb' explains why the tool was used. This is the sentence behind Q35.
Simple MeaningThey drew the three measurements on triangular graph paper so the values could be compared.
中文他们使用一种三角形坐标纸，把这三项测量数据相互对照绘制出来。
Today's land tortoises constitute a single branch, deeply nested among branches consisting of aquatic turtles.
Main ClauseToday's land tortoises constitute a single branch.
Subordinate / Relative'deeply nested among branches' (past-participial phrase) + 'consisting of aquatic turtles' (present-participial phrase describing 'branches').
Grammar NoteTwo stacked participles — one passive in sense ('nested'), one active ('consisting'). This is the core evidence for the double return.
Simple MeaningLand tortoises form just one branch, sitting deep inside a family tree made mostly of water-living turtles.
中文今天的陆龟仅构成一个分支，深深嵌套在由水生龟类组成的诸多分支之中。
Rather, their ancestors were among those who went back to the water, and they then re-emerged back onto the land in (relatively) more recent times.
Main ClauseTheir ancestors were among those…, and they then re-emerged back onto the land.
Subordinate / Relative'who went back to the water' (defining relative clause).
Grammar Note'Rather' corrects the assumption in the previous sentence. Note the prefix pattern: re-emerged, re-tooling, re-turned.
Simple MeaningInstead, their ancestors returned to the sea, and only later did they come back onto land again.
中文相反，它们的祖先曾属于重返水中的那一批，之后在（相对）较近的年代又重新回到陆地。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology (deep time): sea → land (plants first, then animals) → back to sea (whales, turtles) → back to land again (tortoises). Holding this four-stage timeline in your head makes Q40 almost automatic.

Comparison & degree: 'Seals have only gone part way back… on the way to extreme cases such as whales and dugongs… turtles are, in one respect, less fully given back to the water than whales.' The writer is ranking animals along a scale of how completely they returned to water.

Definition by example: 'Ichthyosaurs were reptilian contemporaries of the dinosaurs, with fins and streamlined bodies. The fossils look like dolphins and they surely lived like dolphins, in the water.' The example both defines a term and supplies Q30 and Q33.

Problem → method → outcome: the problem ('how can we tell whether fossil animals lived on land or in water?') is answered by a four-step method with a stated Outcome after each step. Flow-chart questions almost always follow this shape.

Cause & effect: land tortoises are nested among aquatic branches (evidence) → therefore they cannot have stayed on land continuously (conclusion) → therefore their ancestors returned to water and came back (final claim).

Contrast with expectation: 'You might think, therefore, that modern land tortoises have probably stayed on land ever since… But apparently not.' The writer deliberately raises the wrong assumption before overturning it — a favourite IELTS structure.

Author's attitude: lightly witty and admiring — 'enterprising individuals', 'their hard-earned terrestrial re-tooling', 'a remarkable double return'. The word remarkable in the last paragraph is the strongest signal for Q40.
