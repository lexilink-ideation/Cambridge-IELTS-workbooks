Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 11 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Roger Brown phones the Youth Council because he wants to stand for election. Caroline, the administrator, takes down his personal details: where he lives, his address and postcode, his job, his subjects, his hobbies, the group he wants to work with, and finally an appointment time and phone number.

典型的“报名登记”场景:拼写、邮编、时间、手机号全都要写对。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. Two answers here are 'transcription' answers — a spelled surname (Q2) and a postcode (Q3) — and two are numbers (Q9 time, Q10 mobile). Practise British letter sounds B/P/V/E and the pause pattern of a UK postcode: two letters + number, then number + two letters.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Personal-details forms rarely use the speaker's exact words. Train the swap.
Q1 — accommodation
Question note
Currently staying in a ___ during the week
What you hear
'I'm looking for a flat to rent here, so I'm in a hostel from Monday to Friday'
Key paraphrase
'from Monday to Friday' = 'during the week'; the flat is only a plan → hostel
Your turn
Q4 — occupation
Question note
student and part-time job as a ___
What you hear
'I've just finished a short-term contract as a courier, and now I'm working as a waiter'
Key paraphrase
'just finished' cancels 'courier'; 'now I'm working as' gives the answer → waiter
Your turn
Q6 — hobby
Question note
does a lot of ___
What you hear
'I spend quite a lot of time cycling — both around town … and also long-distance'
Key paraphrase
'spend quite a lot of time -ing' = 'does a lot of' → cycling
Your turn
Q8 — who he wants to help
Question note
wants to work with young people who are ___
What you hear
'my sister is blind, so I'm particularly interested in working with disabled young people'
Key paraphrase
'blind' is the personal reason; the category word is disabled
Your turn
Q9 — the time
Question note
next Monday at ___ pm
What you hear
'He generally leaves around 5.30.' — 'Well, would 4.30 be OK? My last class finishes at 4'
Key paraphrase
5.30 and 4.00 are distractors; the agreed time is 4.30
Your turn
