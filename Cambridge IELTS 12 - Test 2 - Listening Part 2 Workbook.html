📋 Quick Review

A tour organiser briefs a group about a theatre trip to Munich. First the practical arrangements — flight, airport meeting, hotel rates, meals and a talk by a director (Q11–15, multiple choice). Then a day-by-day preview of the five plays, matched to comments about each performance (Q16–20).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task the speaker never uses the exact wording of the options. 'The premiere' = performed for the first time; 'the garden of a palace' = performed outdoors; 'the mayor and other dignitaries' = attended by officials. Read all seven options before the audio starts.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Matching tasks are pure paraphrase. Train the option-wording ⇄ speaker-wording swap.
Q16 — Wednesday
Option wording
The play will be performed for the first time
What you hear
'The play we're seeing on Wednesday evening is a modern one, and we're going to the premiere'
Key paraphrase
premiere = first performance → F. Note the trap: 'the playwright is ill and is unlikely to be able to attend' rules out A
Your turn
Q17 — Thursday
Option wording
The play was written to celebrate an anniversary
What you hear
'commissioned to mark a hundred years since the birth in the town of a well-known scientist'
Key paraphrase
written to celebrate = commissioned to mark; anniversary = a hundred years since → B
Your turn
Q18 — Friday
Option wording
The play will be performed outdoors
What you hear
'it'll be in the garden of a palace… there won't be much protection from the wind'
Key paraphrase
outdoors = in the garden; the wind detail confirms it → E
Your turn
Q19 — Saturday
Option wording
The performance will be attended by officials from the town
What you hear
'the mayor and all the other dignitaries of the town will be attending'
Key paraphrase
officials = dignitaries → G. The band plays at the reception afterwards, so D does not fit
Your turn
