📋 Passage Overview

Should we try to bring extinct species back to life? The passage surveys de-extinction — from the passenger pigeon and the Tasmanian tiger to the woolly mammoth. Scientists explain the cloning and gene-editing methods, the ecological reasons for reviving species, and doubts about whether a truly identical animal can ever return. Question types: paragraph matching (Q14–17), summary completion (Q18–22) and matching statements to people (Q23–26).

💡 Reading tip: With several named scientists, underline each name and read only the sentences around it. For the mammoth summary, the answers follow the order of that section, so track it line by line.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

The basic premise involves using cloning technology to turn the DNA of extinct animals into a fertilised embryo, which is carried by the nearest relative still in existence before being born as a living animal.
Main ClauseThe basic premise involves using cloning technology to turn the DNA … into a fertilised embryo
Subordinate / Relative'which is carried by the nearest relative still in existence' = relative clause; 'before being born as a living animal' = time phrase
Grammar NoteVerb + gerund ('involves using') + infinitive of purpose ('to turn'); a relative clause adds the surrogate.
Simple MeaningThe idea is to clone extinct DNA into an embryo, carried by the closest living relative, and finally born alive.
中文其基本原理是利用克隆技术,把灭绝动物的DNA变成受精胚胎,由现存最近的亲缘物种孕育,最终作为活体动物出生。
He points out that in the decades since the thylacine went extinct, there has been a spread in a 'dangerously debilitating' facial tumour syndrome which threatens the existence of the Tasmanian devils.
Main ClauseHe points out that … there has been a spread in a … facial tumour syndrome
Subordinate / Relative'in the decades since the thylacine went extinct' = time phrase; 'which threatens the existence of the Tasmanian devils' = relative clause defining the syndrome
Grammar NoteReporting verb 'points out' + that-clause containing a time phrase and a relative clause.
Simple MeaningHe notes that since the thylacine died out, a devastating facial cancer has spread and now endangers Tasmanian devils.
中文他指出,自袋狼灭绝以来的几十年里,一种“极具破坏性”的面部肿瘤综合症蔓延开来,威胁着塔斯马尼亚袋獾的生存。
By pinpointing which genetic traits made it possible for mammoths to survive the icy climate of the tundra, the project's goal is to return mammoths, or a mammoth-like animal, to the region.
Main Clausethe project's goal is to return mammoths … to the region
Subordinate / Relative'By pinpointing which genetic traits made it possible for mammoths to survive the icy climate' = means phrase containing an embedded question
Grammar NoteFront 'By + -ing' means phrase (with an indirect question) + main clause 'the goal is to…'.
Simple MeaningBy finding out which genes let mammoths survive the icy tundra, the project aims to return mammoths, or something like them, to the region.
中文通过确定是哪些基因特征使猛犸象能够在冰冷的苔原气候中生存,该项目的目标是让猛犸象或类似猛犸象的动物重返该地区。
There is no way to bring back something that is 100 per cent identical to a species that went extinct a long time ago.
Main ClauseThere is no way to bring back something
Subordinate / Relative'that is 100 per cent identical to a species' = relative clause defining 'something'; 'that went extinct a long time ago' = relative clause defining 'a species'
Grammar Note'There is no way to…' + two stacked relative clauses.
Simple MeaningYou simply cannot revive something exactly identical to a species that died out long ago.
中文无法让一种与很久以前灭绝的物种百分之百相同的东西复活。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Example structure. The passage moves through examples — passenger pigeon → thylacine → woolly mammoth. Each shows a different reason or method, so the paragraph-matching answers (Q14–17) depend on spotting the specific example.

Claim vs doubt. Enthusiasts (Novak, Archer, Church) are balanced against a sceptic (Shapiro). Matching statements to people (Q23–26) rewards reading the opinion verbs around each name.

Cause and effect. Mammoth traits (thick hair, ears, insulating fat) → reduced heat loss → survival in tundra; repopulating the tundra → fewer carbon emissions. This chain drives the summary (Q18–22).
