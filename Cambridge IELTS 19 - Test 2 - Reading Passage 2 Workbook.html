📋 Passage Overview

Athletes and stress. This passage explains how professional athletes experience performance stress, using tennis player Emma Raducanu as an example. It contrasts 'challenge' and 'threat' states, explains the roles of adrenaline and cortisol, describes anxiety symptoms, and lists strategies for responding positively. Question types: paragraph matching, sentence completion and two 'choose TWO' tasks.

💡 Reading tip: This passage is built on a contrast — challenge state (good) vs threat state (bad). Keep that opposition in mind; many questions test which hormone or outcome belongs to which state.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

An athlete may feel stressed about an event if they feel the demands on them are greater than they can handle.
Main ClauseAn athlete may feel stressed about an event.
Subordinate / Relative'if they feel the demands on them are greater than they can handle' (conditional + noun clause).
Grammar Note'if' introduces the condition; 'the demands… are greater than…' compares two things.
Simple MeaningAthletes feel stressed when demands seem bigger than what they can manage.
中文如果运动员觉得对自己的要求超出了能应付的范围，就可能对某项赛事感到压力。
Typically, the more resources a person feels they have in handling the situation, the more positive their stress response.
Main ClauseTheir stress response is more positive.
Subordinate / Relative'the more resources a person feels they have…, the more positive…' (correlative comparative).
Grammar Note'the more…, the more…' links two changing amounts.
Simple MeaningThe more able people feel to cope, the more positive their stress response.
中文通常，一个人觉得自己应对局面的资源越多，其压力反应就越积极。
During a challenge state, adrenaline increases the amount of blood pumped from the heart and expands the blood vessels, which allows more energy to be delivered to the muscles and brain.
Main ClauseAdrenaline increases the blood pumped from the heart and expands the blood vessels.
Subordinate / Relative'which allows more energy to be delivered…' (relative comment clause).
Grammar Note'which' refers to the whole previous idea and states its result.
Simple MeaningIn a challenge state, adrenaline sends more blood and energy to muscles and brain.
中文在挑战状态下，肾上腺素增加心脏泵出的血量并扩张血管，从而向肌肉和大脑输送更多能量。
Developing psychological skills, such as visualisation, can also help decrease physiological responses to threat.
Main ClauseDeveloping psychological skills can help decrease physiological responses to threat.
Subordinate / Relative'such as visualisation' (example phrase inserted).
Grammar Note'such as' gives an example of the noun 'psychological skills'.
Simple MeaningMental skills like visualisation can lower the body's threat responses.
中文培养可视化等心理技能，也有助于降低身体对威胁的生理反应。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition & contrast: a 'challenge state' (positive response, good performance) vs a 'threat state' (negative response, poor performance). This binary organises the whole passage.

Cause & effect (hormones): adrenaline → more blood/energy → better performance; cortisol → tighter vessels, higher pressure → worse decisions. Track which hormone drives which outcome.

Example: Raducanu at Wimbledon illustrates a threat response — 'larger audience, higher expectations… a more skilful opponent' increased demands beyond her resources.

Solution list: paragraph F gives strategies (positive language, reframing heart rate as excitement, visualisation, competitive training) — useful for Q15 and Q21–22.
