Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Victor and Olivia, plan a presentation about the Norwegian explorer Thor Heyerdahl. Victor will cover his early life; Olivia will cover his adult life, including the famous raft journey across the Pacific and his work on Easter Island.

这是“两人合作准备presentation”的经典场景。注意:两人会互相纠正,被纠正的那个说法就是干扰项。

💡 Listening tip: Q21–24 are two choose TWO questions. In these, one speaker often names a wrong item and the other corrects it — 'No, it was climbing that he spent his time on as a young man.' Cross out an option the moment you hear 'No,' or 'Actually,'.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Multiple-choice options are always rewritten. Notice especially how a concrete detail in the audio becomes an abstract option on the page. 录音里是具体细节,选项里是抽象概括。
Q23/24 — 'an isolated place'
Option B
to experience an isolated place
Audio wording
'to live wild in a remote location with few inhabitants, cut off by the sea, which is what they were aiming to do'
Key paraphrase
isolated = remote + few inhabitants + cut off
Trap
'learn survival skills' happened before they left home, so it is preparation, not purpose.
Your turn
Q25 — why migration was thought impossible
Option A
the fact that Eastern countries were far away
Audio wording
'because of the huge, empty stretch of ocean that lies between the islands and the nearest inhabited land'
Key paraphrase
far away = a huge empty stretch of ocean between them
Trap
Winds and currents (option C) are what Heyerdahl studied, not the academics' objection.
Your turn
Q26 — the main reason for the voyage
Option C
to test a new theory
Audio wording
'it was more a matter of simply trying out his idea, to see if migration from the east was possible'
Key paraphrase
test a theory = try out his idea to see if it was possible
Trap
Victor first suggests 'private reasons … to show others that he could have spectacular adventures' (option B) — then agrees with Olivia.
Your turn
Q27 — 'authentic construction methods'
Option C
the use of authentic construction methods
Audio wording
'he use only ancient techniques and local materials to build his raft'
Key paraphrase
authentic construction methods = ancient techniques + local materials
Trap
Speed (97 days) is mentioned right afterwards, and 'first person' is explicitly denied.
Your turn
Q29 — 'greatest influence'
Option B
the development of archaeological methodology
Audio wording
'he was the first person to establish what modern academics call practical archaeology. I mean, that they try to recreate something from the past today'
Key paraphrase
methodology = practical archaeology = recreating the past
Trap
Option A is directly contradicted: his Polynesian-origin ideas 'have been completely discredited'.
Your turn
