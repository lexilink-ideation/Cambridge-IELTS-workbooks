Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 17 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two veterinary-science students, Diana and Tim, compare their work experience on farms (Q21–26, multiple choice) and then give opinions on four course modules (Q27–30, matching). Listen for who did what, and for agreement / disagreement between the two speakers.

💡 Listening tip: In two-speaker discussions, track who says each thing. Questions often ask what 'both' students found, or what one of them personally experienced — the answer is the point they AGREE on, not the first opinion you hear.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The options reword what the students say. Focus on meaning and on WHO said it.
Diana's milk mistake (Q25)
Option C
She made a mistake when storing milk.
What you hear
'I poured some milk from the wrong cow in with the milk for humans'
Key paraphrase
putting milk in the wrong container = a storage mistake
Your turn
Vets and farming (Q26)
Option C
Some jobs could be done by the farmer rather than a vet.
What you hear
'some of the stuff the vets do… he'd be quite capable of doing himself'
Key paraphrase
the farmer could do it = a job need not go to a vet
Your turn
Diet and nutrition (Q28)
Option E
Tim was shocked at something he learned.
What you hear
'limited checking into whether pet food is contaminated… I thought that was terrible'
Key paraphrase
'terrible' = shocked
Your turn
