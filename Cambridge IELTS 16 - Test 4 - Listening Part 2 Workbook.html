📋 Quick Review

At a local council meeting, Councillor Thornton reports on traffic and highways (Q11–14, multiple choice) and then on proposed improvements to the recreation ground (Q15–20, map labelling A–I).

💡 Listening tip: For the map, use the fixed features (the river to the north, the Community Hall, the line of trees) as anchors, and follow words like 'opposite', 'beyond the trees', 'next to'.

Option C
changes in the type of traffic

Key paraphrase
