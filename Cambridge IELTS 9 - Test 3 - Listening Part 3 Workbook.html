Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A study-skills tutorial: Dr Schulmann helps Caroline Benning plan her dissertation. They identify her topic, her strengths and weaknesses, then work through four possible strategies — each with a benefit and a problem — before agreeing recommendations and the next tutorial date. Note and table completion — NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: The table has a strict Benefits / Problems structure. Every strategy is introduced positively and then undercut with 'The drawback is…', 'unfortunately…', 'but the problem is…'. Train your ear on those three signals — the Problems column answers always follow them. For Q30, only the DATE is needed; 'January' is already printed.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The table compresses conversation into two or three words. Practise the reduction.
Q24 — Benefit of peer discussion
Table cell
increases ______
What you hear
'It really helps build confidence, you know, having to present something to others'
Key paraphrase
'helps build' = 'increases' → confidence
Your turn
Q25 — Problem of peer discussion
Table cell
dissertations tend to contain the same ______
What you hear
'The drawback is that everyone in the group seems to share the same ideas… they keep being repeated in all the dissertations'
Key paraphrase
'The drawback is' signals the Problems column → ideas
Your turn
Q27 — Problem of Student Support
Table cell
limited ______

'unfortunately there are only a few places'
Key paraphrase
'only a few' = 'limited' → places

Q29 — Reading recommendation
Note line
