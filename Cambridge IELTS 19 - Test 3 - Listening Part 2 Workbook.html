📋 Quick Review

A presenter interviews Jenny Morgan, organiser of a children's book festival. She describes six workshops, recommends a book called Alive and Kicking, and gives advice to parents about encouraging reading. Expect a matching task (Q11–16) and two 'choose TWO' tasks (Q17–20).

💡 Listening tip: In the workshop matching task, each workshop has ONE key feature (a disability focus, a drama activity, an age group, an unhappy feeling, a prize-winning author, a culture). Listen for that single defining detail, not every word said about each workshop.
