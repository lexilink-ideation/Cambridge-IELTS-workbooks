Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 8 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A tutor gives Sandra feedback on the written proposal her group is entering for a Geography Society field-trip competition. They discuss how to improve the writing and layout, then talk about the destination — the Navajo Tribal Park — including what to include, its size, transport and things to see. This is an academic discussion between two speakers.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 3 is a discussion, often about a piece of student work. Listen for advice and evaluation language ('you could …', 'it would be better if …', 'I'd agree with …', 'not immediately relevant'). The tutor's suggestions are usually the answers, and speakers often reject a distractor before giving the real point.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ21: The tutor thinks Sandra's proposal …
What you hear / read"I've made a few notes about things which could have been better sequenced."
Key paraphrase'should be re-ordered in some parts' = better sequenced → answer A.
Your practice

What you hear / read"you've used complex structures and long sentences … cut them down a bit."
Key paraphrase'shortened her sentences' = cut them down → answer C.
