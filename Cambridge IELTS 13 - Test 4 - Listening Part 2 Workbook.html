📋 Quick Review

Annie welcomes visitors to the Snow Centre in New Zealand and describes the week's activities: cross-country skiing, a dog-sled trip, a team relay event, a snow-shoe expedition to Mount Frenner, and the mountain hut. She then describes four ski trails (Q17–20, matching from a box A–F).

💡 Listening tip: Trail-matching answers hide inside conditional and comparative sentences: 'regardless of their age or skill', 'if you've fully mastered the techniques', 'check with us before you set out'. Underline the evaluation, not the scenery.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Tourist-briefing answers are wrapped in friendly, indirect language. Translate each into a plain statement.
Q11 — the recommendation
Question wording
the visitors should get away from the regular trails
What you hear
'there are marked trails, but you can also leave these and go off on your own — and that's an experience not to be missed'
Key paraphrase
leave the marked trails + not to be missed = recommended → A
Your turn
Q15 — what the price includes
Question wording
The cost includes a supply of drinking water.
What you hear
'it's got cooking facilities, firewood and water for drinking' … 'we can take your luggage up… for just ten dollars'
Key paraphrase
luggage costs extra and food is your own → only water is included → A
Your turn
Q18 — Pine Trail
Box wording
D — It demands a lot of skill.
What you hear
'if you're nervous about skiing, leave this one to the experts!… if you've fully mastered the techniques needed for hills, it's great fun'
Key paraphrase
leave it to the experts + fully mastered = demands a lot of skill
Your turn
Q20 — Loser's Trail
Box wording
E — It may be closed in bad weather.
What you hear
'if it's snowing or windy, check with us before you set out to make sure the trail's open that day'
Key paraphrase
'make sure it's open' implies it is sometimes closed
Your turn
