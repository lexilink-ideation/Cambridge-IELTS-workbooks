The History of Moving Pictures · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A lecturer gives a talk on the history of moving pictures — from a 19th-century photographic experiment to the first talking films. The lecture covers: a famous horse-racing bet settled by photography (1877); Thomas Edison's role and the first motion picture system; the weight problem limiting public viewing; rival European systems (1895); the film-breakage problem and the Lantham Loop solution; the first movie; and early sound technology.

This is a classic Part 4 academic lecture: one speaker, dense information, chronological structure. Q31–37 require identifying which of three options (A/B/C) matches the speaker's precise meaning — paraphrase accuracy is critical. Q38–40 are sentence completions from verbatim script lines.

💡 Part 4 strategy: Read all A/B/C options for Q31–37 before listening. The lecture follows a clear timeline — mark where each question answer likely falls before the audio starts. For Q31–37, the wrong options often use words from the script but change the meaning slightly. Pay close attention to quantifiers ("some" vs "all"), subjects ("filmed" vs "saw"), and sequence ("then" vs "at the same time").

💬 Useful Phrases & Collocations

Phrase from the Script Meaning / Why it Matters 中文
all the hooves were off the ground in some of the photos Q31 source. "All hooves" = all four feet. Correct answer: A (all feet off the ground). "Some of the photos" means not all — but the fact was confirmed. 有些照片中马蹄全部腾空
put all the existing technologies together to make the first entire motion picture system Q32 source. The Scotsman combined available technology — he didn't invent new technology or use only Edison's technology. Answer: B. 将现有技术整合，制造出第一套完整的活动影像系统
The camera weighed over 200 kilograms and only one person at a time could see the film Q33 source. Two facts: heavy camera (option C) and only one viewer at a time (option A). The question asks for ONE major problem — the camera's weight (200 kg) was the structural issue that limited public use. Answer: C. 摄影机重逾200公斤，一次只能一人观影
a number of rival European systems started to appear once people had heard about it Q34 source. "heard about" = "been told about." They hadn't seen or used it — just heard. Answer: A. 人们听闻此事后，欧洲各地纷纷出现竞争系统
three systems were all developed… independently of each other. I guess the most famous of these was by the Lumière Brothers from France Q35 source. Three systems, developed independently (not together). The famous one = Lumière Brothers = French = working alone. Answer: A. 三套系统各自独立研发；最著名的是法国卢米埃兄弟的作品
films had a tendency to break when they were being played – a problem which was caused by the tension between the two wheels, or 'reels' Q36 source. Breaking "when played" = projector problem. Reels are part of the projection mechanism. Answer: C (the film projector). 影片在放映时容易断裂——由两个卷轴之间的张力造成
the simple addition of a third reel between the two main reels, and this took all the tension away Q37 source. One extra reel between two = Lantham Loop. "Took all the tension away" = removing tension. NOT adding three more reels (B). Answer: A. 在两个主卷轴之间增加第三个卷轴，消除张力
the making of The Great Train Robbery – the very first movie made Q38 source. The first film ever made. Full name: "The Great Train Robbery." The question asks for the title after "The ___." Answer: Great Train Robbery. 《火车大劫案》——史上第一部电影
in 1926 when sound effects were first used on a film Q39 source. The blank comes before "were used for the first time on film in 1926." Answer: Sound effects. 1926年，音效首次被运用于电影
they put subtitles on the film… to compensate for this poor sound quality Q40 source. "Compensate for" = "because of." Answer: poor sound quality. The subtitles were a fix, not a feature. 为弥补糟糕的音质，他们在影片底部添加了字幕

✅ Answer Key

Exercise 1 — Multiple Choice A/B/C (Q31–37)

31.
A — all feet off the ground
Script: "all the hooves were off the ground in some of the photos." "Hooves" = feet. B is the opposite — "at least one foot ON the ground."
32.
B — used available technology to make a new system
Script: "put all the existing technologies together." "Existing" = available. A is wrong — he used all technologies, not just Edison's. C is wrong — he was not already an expert; he had to study the systems first.
33.
C — the camera was very heavy
Script: "The camera weighed over 200 kilograms." ⚠️ A says "only one person could be FILMED" — but the script says one person could SEE the film (viewer, not subject). C = camera weight = the structural design problem.
34.
A — been told about the American system
Script: "once people had heard about it." "Heard about" = "been told about." B (seen) and C (used) imply direct experience — the script only says they heard.
35.
A — a French team working alone
Script: "independently of each other … the most famous … by the Lumière Brothers from France." B is wrong — they did NOT work together with Germans. C is wrong — the word "cinema" comes from the FRENCH cinematographe, not the German bioskop.
36.
C — the film projector
Script: films broke "when they were being played" due to reel tension. "Being played" = the projection/playback process = the film projector. The problem is mechanical, not about subject matter (A) or the camera (B).
37.
A — removing tension between the film reels
Script: "took all the tension away." ⚠️ B says "three MORE film reels" — wrong. The total became three (two + one extra), not three additional. C is wrong — all reels stayed; the method was adding one new one, not enhancing existing ones.

Exercise 2 — Sentence Completion (Q38–40)

38.
Great Train Robbery
Script: "the making of The Great Train Robbery – the very first movie made." The "The" is printed in the question, so the blank = "Great Train Robbery" (three words). Don't confuse with The Lights of New York or The Jazz Singer.
39.
Sound effects
Script: "in 1926 when sound effects were first used on a film." Note: this was NOT full dialogue. The Jazz Singer (1927) featured actual talking; The Lights of New York (1928) was the first all-talking film.
40.
poor sound quality
Script: "they put subtitles on the film… to compensate for this poor sound quality." Three words. "Compensate for" = "because of" in the question sentence.

Exercise 4 — Precision

i.
B — "in some of the photos"
Not ALL photos showed all hooves off the ground — only "some." The fact was still proven, but the qualifier matters.
ii.
B — see the film
"only one person at a time could SEE the film" — this is a viewer limitation, not a filming limitation. "Be filmed" is a completely different meaning.
iii.
A — One (a third reel)
"the simple addition of A third reel between the two main reels" — one extra reel, making three total. Not three extra reels.
