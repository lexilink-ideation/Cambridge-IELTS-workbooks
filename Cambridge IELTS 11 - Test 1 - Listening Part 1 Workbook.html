Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 11 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A woman telephones a Village Hall official because she wants to hire a room for a charity dinner on 1 September. The official explains the two rooms, the price, the deposit, and then the rules she must follow before, during and after the event.

因为是“租场地”的电话咨询,你会听到大量名称拼写、价格、押金、规定的信息。

💡 Listening tip: The instruction is ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. The notes are organised under three headings — Before / During / After the event — so use those headings to keep your place. Q1 is a spelled-out name (C-H-A-R-L-T-O-N) and Q2 is a price, so have your pen ready from the very first second.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

IELTS notes never copy the speaker word for word. Train yourself to hear the idea behind the note.
Q3 — the deposit
Question note
+ £250 deposit (___ payment is required)
What you hear
'we do insist that this is paid in cash, we don't take cards for that'
Key paraphrase
'we insist … paid in cash' = 'cash payment is required' → cash
Your turn
Q4 — what is included
Question note
Cost includes use of tables and chairs and also ___
What you hear
'And what about parking?' 'Yeah, that's all included.'
Key paraphrase
The answer comes in the woman's question, confirmed by 'that's all included' → parking
Your turn
Q6 — arranging entry
Question note
Need to contact caretaker (Mr Evans) in advance to arrange ___
What you hear
'you'll need to contact the caretaker … to make the arrangements for entry'
Key paraphrase
'make the arrangements for entry' = 'to arrange entry' → entry
Your turn
Q9 — the floor
Question note
The ___ must be washed and rubbish placed in black bags
What you hear
'Sweep the floors and empty the bins?' — 'Well, actually they have to be washed, not just swept.'
Key paraphrase
The woman guesses; the official corrects her. Follow the correction → floor / floors
Your turn
Q10 — decorations
Question note
All ___ must be taken down
What you hear
'Is it OK if we can have decorations in the room?' — 'Yes, but you must take them down afterwards.'
Key paraphrase
'take them down afterwards' = 'must be taken down' → decoration(s)
Your turn
