📋 Passage Overview

The White Horse of Uffington. About England's ancient hill figures ('geoglyphs') cut into chalk downland, focusing on the mysterious Uffington White Horse — how the figures were made, why most disappeared, and how the Horse was re-dated. Question types: True / False / Not Given (Q1–8) and notes completion (Q9–13).

💡 Reading tip: For True/False/Not Given, statements follow the passage order. If a claim isn't confirmed OR denied in the text, it is Not Given — resist guessing from logic.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Although the majority of these geoglyphs date within the last 300 years or so, there are one or two that are much older.
Main Clausethere are one or two that are much older
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough the majority… date within the last 300 years (concession); that are much older (relative)
Grammar Note'Although' concedes a general fact before stating the exception.
Simple MeaningMost hill figures are recent, but a few are far older.
中文尽管大多数地画只有约300年历史，但也有一两处要古老得多。
One reason that the vast majority of hill figures have disappeared is that when the traditions associated with the figures faded, people no longer bothered to clear away the grass.
Main ClauseOne reason … is that …
Subordinate / Relativethat the vast majority… have disappeared (relative); when the traditions… faded (time); that people no longer bothered… (noun clause)
Grammar Note'One reason is that…' introduces a cause; nested time clause adds the trigger.
Simple MeaningMost figures vanished because, once the customs died out, no one cleared the grass.
中文大多数地画消失的一个原因是：与之相关的传统淡去后，人们不再清除杂草。
Over hundreds of years the outlines would sometimes change due to people not always cutting in exactly the same place, thus creating a different shape to the original.
Main Clausethe outlines would sometimes change
Subordinate / Relativedue to people not always cutting… (cause); thus creating a different shape… (result participle)
Grammar Note'due to + noun/-ing' gives the cause; 'thus + -ing' gives the result.
Simple MeaningBecause people didn't always cut in the same spot, the shape slowly changed.
中文数百年间，因人们并非总在同一处切割，轮廓有时会改变，从而形成与原形不同的形状。
The fact that any ancient hill figures survive at all in England today is remarkable.
Main ClauseThe fact … is remarkable
Subordinate / Relativethat any ancient hill figures survive at all in England today (appositive noun clause)
Grammar Note'The fact that…' packs a whole clause into the subject.
Simple MeaningIt's amazing that any old hill figures still exist in England.
中文如今英格兰还能有任何古老地画留存，这本身就令人惊叹。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

General rule + exception: most geoglyphs are recent, but the Uffington Horse is far older. TFNG items often test whether you can separate the rule from its exception.

Cause → effect (disappearance): traditions fade → grass is not cleared → the figure vanishes. Follow this chain to answer 'Many geoglyphs are no longer visible' (TRUE).

Uncertainty markers: 'many historians are convinced… others believe…' shows an unresolved debate about the Long Man — so 'Historians have come to an agreement' is FALSE.
