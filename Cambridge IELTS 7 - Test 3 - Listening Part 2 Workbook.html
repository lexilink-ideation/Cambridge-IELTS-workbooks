📋 Quick Review

This is a monologue — a single speaker gives a talk to a club about a "Charity Sponsored Walking Holiday for Education Aid" in the fictional Semira Mountains. The speaker explains how sponsorship works, the fitness and age requirements, what walkers must carry, and local culture (carpet-weaving, historic sites). In the second half, the speaker outlines a day-by-day itinerary from arrival to departure.

This is typical of Section 2: one speaker, general/public information, mixing multiple-choice questions with note/table completion.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Section 2 monologues, information is usually presented in the same order as the questions, but signposting words ("Firstly... Secondly... Finally...", "moving on to...", "let's have a look at...") tell you when a new topic starts. For itinerary/table questions, keep track of the day number as an anchor point.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Study each example, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Duration
Question wordingOn the holiday, you will be walking for...
Audio meaning"a ten-day holiday... with eight days actual walking"
Key paraphrasetotal trip length ≠ actual walking days — listen for the distinction
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Proportion
Question wordingWhat proportion of the money goes to charity?
Audio meaning"about thirty-five per cent will go on your expenses, and that leaves sixty-five per cent guaranteed to go to the charity"
Key paraphrase"proportion" = percentage remaining after subtracting expenses
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Fitness requirement
Question wordingWhen you start the trek you must be...
Audio meaning"you will need to be extremely fit if you aren't now"
Key paraphrase"must be" = "will need to be" (a requirement by departure, not necessarily right now)
