📋 Passage Overview

Stadiums: past, present and future. The writer argues that stadiums, among the oldest urban buildings, can be versatile and drive city development. Examples include the Roman amphitheatre at Arles (fortress → village → arena), and modern strategies of the stadium as an 'urban hub' and as a 'power plant'. Question types: section matching (Q14–17), summary completion (Q18–22) and 'choose TWO' (Q23–26).

💡 Reading tip: For 'which section contains…', scan for the specific detail (figures, examples, a negative attitude), not for matching words. For summary completion with 'ONE WORD ONLY', the answer must fit both the meaning AND the grammar of the gap.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Today, however, stadiums are regarded with growing scepticism, as construction costs can soar above £1 billion, and stadiums finished for major events have notably fallen into disuse and disrepair.
Main Clausestadiums are regarded with growing scepticism
Subordinate / Relativeas construction costs can soar… and stadiums… have fallen into disuse (reason clause introduced by 'as')
Grammar Note'as' = 'because'; two reasons joined by 'and'.
Simple MeaningPeople now doubt stadiums because they are hugely expensive and often end up unused.
中文然而如今,人们对体育场越来越怀疑,因为建造成本可能超过十亿英镑,而为大型赛事建的体育场往往闲置失修。
Built by the Romans in 90 AD, it became a fortress with four towers after the fifth century, and was then transformed into a village containing more than 200 houses.
Main Clauseit became a fortress… and was then transformed into a village
Subordinate / RelativeBuilt by the Romans in 90 AD (reduced participle clause = 'It was built…'); containing more than 200 houses (reduced relative clause)
Grammar NoteFront participle sets background; two past verbs show the sequence.
Simple MeaningThe Romans built it in 90 AD; later it became a fortress, then a village of 200+ houses.
中文它由罗马人于公元90年建造,五世纪后变成有四座塔楼的堡垒,随后又改造成一个有二百多户人家的村庄。
Many such stadiums are situated in suburban areas, designed for sporting use only and surrounded by parking lots.
Main ClauseMany such stadiums are situated in suburban areas
Subordinate / Relativedesigned for sporting use only and surrounded by parking lots (two reduced passive clauses describing the stadiums)
Grammar NotePast participles 'designed' and 'surrounded' act like 'which are designed… and surrounded…'.
Simple MeaningMany of these stadiums sit in the suburbs, used only for sport and ringed by car parks.
中文许多此类体育场位于郊区,只用于体育用途,四周被停车场包围。
There's a growing trend for stadiums to be equipped with public spaces and services that serve a function beyond sport, such as hotels, retail outlets and green space.
Main ClauseThere's a growing trend for stadiums to be equipped with public spaces and services
Subordinate / Relativethat serve a function beyond sport (relative clause defining the services)
Grammar Note'There is a trend for X to be…' + relative clause + 'such as' examples.
Simple MeaningMore and more stadiums now include non-sport facilities like hotels, shops and parks.
中文越来越多的体育场配备了超出体育功能的公共空间和服务,如酒店、零售店和绿地。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Claim + evidence. The writer's claim is that stadiums are versatile; each example (Arles, Verona, Lucca) is evidence supporting it. Question 18–22 all come from this evidence block.

Comparison / contrast. Section D contrasts 20th-century stadiums (suburban, single-use, car-dependent) with ancient amphitheatres — this is the source of the two negative features in Q23–24.

Classification of strategies. The writer groups modern ideas into two named types: the stadium as an 'urban hub' and as a 'power plant'. Recognising these labels locates the facility examples (Q16) and the renewable-energy point (Q25–26).
