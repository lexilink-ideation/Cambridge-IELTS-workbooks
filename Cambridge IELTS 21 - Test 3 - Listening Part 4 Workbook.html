📋 Quick Review

This Part 4 is an academic lecture on invasive species — animals or plants introduced by humans that then harm an environment. It explains how they cause damage, how they spread, gives examples (the cane toad in Australia; Japanese knotweed, rhododendrons and grey squirrels in the UK), and describes how the problem can be tackled.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 note-completion needs ONE word, usually a noun. Listen for cause-and-effect chains — invasive species may bring a disease, put an ecosystem at risk, or carry a virus. The exact word is the one the speaker stresses.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingnative species have no way to fight a new disease
Audio meaning"a disease … which native species have never faced before and therefore have no defences against"
