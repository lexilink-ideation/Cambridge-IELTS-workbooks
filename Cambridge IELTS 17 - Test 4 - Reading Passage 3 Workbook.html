📋 Passage Overview

Timur Gareyev – blindfold chess champion. Gareyev can play dozens of chess games at once entirely in his head. Researchers at UCLA tested his memory (normal on standard tests) and scanned his brain, finding unusually strong connections in the attention-control and visual networks. Question types: paragraph matching (Q27–32), TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q33–36) and summary completion (Q37–40).

💡 Reading tip: For 'which paragraph contains…', a letter may be used more than once, so don't cross paragraphs off. Match the idea (an earlier example, a claim, a reason), not a single keyword.

Main Clauseresearchers at the University of California in Los Angeles called him in for tests
Subordinate / RelativeIn the hope of understanding how he and others like him can perform such mental feats (purpose phrase containing an indirect question)
Grammar NoteFront purpose phrase 'In the hope of + -ing'; embedded question 'how… can perform…'.
Simple MeaningTo understand how such feats are possible, UCLA researchers invited him for tests.

Not only must the positions of each piece on every board be memorised, they must be recalled faithfully when needed, updated with each player's moves, and then reliably stored again.
Main Clausethe positions … must be memorised … recalled … updated … and stored again
Subordinate / RelativeNot only … (fronted negative triggering inversion 'must the positions … be memorised')
Grammar NoteFronted 'Not only' forces inversion; a chain of passive verbs lists the mental steps.
Simple MeaningHe must memorise every piece's position, recall it exactly, update it after each move, and store it again.
中文不仅要记住每块棋盘上每个棋子的位置,还要在需要时准确回忆、根据每位棋手的走法更新,然后再可靠地重新储存。
Though the results are tentative and as yet unpublished, the scans found much greater than average communication between parts of Gareyev's brain that make up the frontoparietal control network.
Main Clausethe scans found much greater than average communication between parts of Gareyev's brain
Subordinate / RelativeThough the results are tentative and as yet unpublished (concession clause); that make up the frontoparietal control network (relative clause)
Grammar Note'Though' = 'although'; comparative 'greater than average'; relative clause defines the brain parts.
Simple MeaningAlthough the findings are early, scans showed unusually strong links in his attention-control brain network.
中文尽管结果尚属初步且未发表,扫描却发现加雷耶夫大脑中构成额顶控制网络的各部分之间的联系远高于平均水平。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Feat + explanation. The passage first describes the feat (many games at once) and then the scientific search for how it's possible — separating description from explanation helps you place Q28 and Q30.

Normal vs exceptional. A key contrast: Gareyev is normal on standard memory tests but exceptional in brain connectivity. Recognising this contrast answers Q29 and the summary (Q37–40).

Reason within a stage. The end of a game is hard because exhaustion sets in and recall gets patchy — a cause-within-a-process detail that answers Q32.
