📋 Passage Overview

This passage explains how scientists collect ants. It notes that the method depends on the purpose (taxonomy vs ecology), which are ‘not always compatible’, then describes four techniques — hand collecting, using bait, sampling ground litter (with a funnel), and pitfall traps — with the advantages and drawbacks of each, including how to preserve the specimens.

💡 Reading strategy tip: For the classification task, make a quick table of the four methods and their key strength before you read the statements. For diagram labelling and completion, copy the passage’s exact words and stay within the word limit (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"The exact method used will depend on the final purpose of the collections."
Main ClauseThe exact method used will depend on the final purpose of the collections
Subordinate / Relative(reduced relative) used = that is used
Grammar Note‘depend on’ = be decided by; ‘the method used’ omits ‘that is’.
Simple MeaningWhich method you choose is decided by why you are collecting the ants.
中文所用的具体方法将取决于采集的最终目的。
"The taxonomist sometimes overlooks whole species in favour of those groups currently under study, while the ecologist often collects only a limited number of specimens of each species."
Main ClauseThe taxonomist sometimes overlooks whole species … while the ecologist often collects only a limited number of specimens
Subordinate / Relativein favour of those groups currently under study (prepositional phrase) / while … (contrast clause)
Grammar Note‘in favour of’ = preferring; ‘while’ contrasts the two researchers’ habits.
Simple MeaningTaxonomists may ignore some species to focus on their group, whereas ecologists take few of each species.
中文分类学家有时会忽略整类物种，而偏重于当前研究的类群；生态学家则往往只采集每个物种的少量标本。
"When possible, collections should be made from nests or foraging columns and at least 20 to 25 individuals collected."
Main Clausecollections should be made from nests or foraging columns and at least 20 to 25 individuals collected
Subordinate / RelativeWhen possible (reduced adverbial clause: when it is possible)
Grammar NotePassive ‘should be made / collected’ gives instructions; ‘When possible’ = if you can.
Simple MeaningIf you can, take ants from nests or columns and collect at least 20–25 of them.
中文在可能的情况下，应从蚁巢或觅食队列中采集，并至少采集20到25只个体。
"One advantage of pitfall traps is that they can be used to collect over a period of time with minimal maintenance and intervention."
Main ClauseOne advantage of pitfall traps is that they can be used to collect over a period of time
Subordinate / Relativewith minimal maintenance and intervention (prepositional phrase)
Grammar Note‘The advantage is that …’ defines the benefit; ‘with minimal …’ adds how.
Simple MeaningA benefit of pitfall traps is that they keep collecting for a long time with little effort.
中文陷阱式诱捕器的一个优点是：它们几乎无需维护和干预，就能在一段时间内持续采集。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Purpose determines method — The method depends on the goal: taxonomy wants long series from one nest; ecology wants as many species as possible. The two aims ‘are not always compatible’ (Q30).

Classification of methods — Four methods: hand collecting, using bait, sampling ground litter, and pitfall traps — each with strengths and weaknesses. This underlies Q31–36.

Cause & Effect — In the funnel method, drying/heat drives ants downward → they fall into alcohol. Sifting litter first concentrates it and lets more be sampled.

Advantage / Disadvantage — Pitfall traps need little maintenance (advantage) but miss species that avoid them (disadvantage); alcohol evaporates, so a non-alcoholic preservative is used (Q36).
