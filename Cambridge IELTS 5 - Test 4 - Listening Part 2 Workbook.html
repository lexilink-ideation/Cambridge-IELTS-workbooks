📋 Quick Review

This is a talk about a sports/community club and the jobs done by volunteers. The speaker describes the clubhouse, a picnic and prizes, meeting times (Wednesday and Sunday afternoons), and volunteer duties such as collecting fees, sending newsletters and supervising teams. Expect note completion with words and numbers.

💡 Listening tip: When notes list duties or times, listen for the verb + object ('collect the fees', 'send the newsletter'). Write the verb too if the gap needs an action.
