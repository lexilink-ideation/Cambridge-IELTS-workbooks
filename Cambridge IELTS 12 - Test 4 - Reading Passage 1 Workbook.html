📋 Passage Overview

'The History of Glass' — a chronological survey from natural obsidian spear tips, through Roman secrecy and Venetian craftsmanship, to Ravenscroft's lead crystal, the repeal of the Excise Act, the Crystal Palace, and the machines of Ashley and Owens — ending with today's global industry and the case for recycling. Question types: note completion (Q1–8) and TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q9–13).

💡 Reading tip: A date-driven passage gives you free navigation. Circle every date in the text (4000 BC, 1500 BC, 1st century BC, 476 AD, 10th century, 1845, 1851, 1887, 1907) and the note-completion answers will appear in exactly that order.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Historians have discovered that a type of natural glass — obsidian — formed in places such as the mouth of a volcano as a result of the intense heat of an eruption melting sand — was first used as tips for spears.
Main ClauseHistorians have discovered that a type of natural glass … was first used as tips for spears
Subordinate / Relative'obsidian' = appositive naming the glass; 'formed in places such as the mouth of a volcano' = reduced relative clause (= 'which was formed…'); 'as a result of the intense heat of an eruption melting sand' = cause phrase containing a gerund clause.
Grammar NoteTwo dashes hold a long insertion. Skip everything between the dashes on first reading: Historians have discovered that a type of natural glass was first used as tips for spears.
Simple MeaningHistorians found that obsidian — natural glass made when volcanic heat melts sand — was first used to tip spears.
中文历史学家发现,一种天然玻璃——黑曜石——形成于火山口等地,由喷发的高温熔化砂石而成,它最早被用作矛尖。
However, they guarded the skills and technology required to make glass very closely, and it was not until their empire collapsed in 476 AD that glass-making knowledge became widespread throughout Europe and the Middle East.
Main Clausethey guarded the skills and technology … very closely
Subordinate / Relative'required to make glass' = reduced passive relative clause; the second half is an 'it was not until… that…' cleft.
Grammar Note'It was not until X that Y' = Y only happened after X. This structure is common in historical writing and is what makes Q5 answerable.
Simple MeaningThe Romans kept glass-making secret, and only after their empire fell in 476 AD did the knowledge spread.
中文然而,罗马人把制作玻璃所需的技术与工艺守得很严,直到公元476年他们的帝国崩溃,制玻璃的知识才在欧洲和中东广泛传播。
He attempted to counter the effect of clouding that sometimes occurred in blown glass by introducing lead to the raw materials used in the process.
Main ClauseHe attempted to counter the effect of clouding
Subordinate / Relative'that sometimes occurred in blown glass' = defining relative clause; 'by introducing lead to the raw materials' = method phrase; 'used in the process' = reduced passive relative clause.
Grammar Note'counter X by doing Y' = solve X by means of Y. The problem (clouding) and the solution (lead) are both answers.
Simple MeaningTo stop blown glass turning cloudy, he added lead to the raw materials.
中文他试图通过在原料中加入铅,来消除吹制玻璃中有时出现的浑浊现象。
Before that time, heavy taxes had been placed on the amount of glass melted in a glasshouse, and were levied continuously from 1745 to 1845.
Main Clauseheavy taxes had been placed on the amount of glass melted in a glasshouse … and were levied continuously
Subordinate / Relative'melted in a glasshouse' = reduced passive relative clause modifying 'glass'.
Grammar NotePast perfect passive ('had been placed') for a period ending before another past point, then simple past passive ('were levied').
Simple MeaningUntil 1845 there were heavy taxes on how much glass a glasshouse melted, charged continuously for a hundred years.
中文在此之前,对玻璃厂熔制的玻璃数量征收重税,并自1745年至1845年连续征收。
Then in 1907, the first fully automated machine was developed in the USA by Michael Owens — founder of the Owens Bottle Machine Company — and installed in its factory.
Main Clausethe first fully automated machine was developed … and installed in its factory
Subordinate / Relative'founder of the Owens Bottle Machine Company' = appositive naming Owens.
Grammar NoteTwo passive verbs share one subject; the appositive between dashes tells you Owens founded the company, which is exactly why Q10 is FALSE.
Simple MeaningIn 1907 Michael Owens, who had founded his own bottle machine company, built the first fully automatic machine and installed it in his own factory.
中文随后在1907年,美国的迈克尔·欧文斯——欧文斯制瓶机公司的创办人——研制出第一台全自动机器,并将其安装在该公司的工厂中。
As less energy is needed to melt recycled glass than to melt down raw materials, this also saves fuel and production costs.
Main Clausethis also saves fuel and production costs
Subordinate / Relative'As less energy is needed to melt recycled glass than to melt down raw materials' = reason clause containing a comparison.
Grammar Note'As' = because; 'less … than' compares two infinitive phrases. Note the direction of the comparison — recycled glass needs less energy, so Q13 is FALSE.
Simple MeaningBecause melting recycled glass takes less energy than melting raw materials, it saves fuel and money.
中文由于熔化回收玻璃所需的能量少于熔化原材料,这也节省了燃料和生产成本。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Strict chronology. The passage marches forward: natural obsidian → 4000 BC glazes → 1500 BC hollow containers → 1st century BC glass blowing → 1st century AD colourless glass → 476 AD the secret spreads → 10th century Venice → 17th century Ravenscroft → 1845 repeal → 1851 Crystal Palace → 1887 Ashley → 1907 Owens → WWI → today. Every note-completion answer sits on this timeline.

Problem → invention → consequence. Clouding in blown glass (problem) → adding lead (invention) → softer, easier to decorate, higher refractive index (consequences) → optical lenses, telescopes, microscopes become possible. Follow the chain and Q6 and Q7 fall out together.

Obstacle removed → growth. Heavy taxes 1745–1845 blocked the British industry; the repeal of the Excise Act removed the block, and the Crystal Palace then launched glass as a building material. 'Before that time…' is the phrase that marks the obstacle.

Comparison of scale. Ashley's 200 bottles per hour is described as 'more than three times quicker than any previous production method'; Owens' machine produced 'an impressive 2,500'. Comparative claims are where TRUE/FALSE questions live — check whether the text really says 'the fastest'.

Argument at the end. The final paragraph shifts from history to persuasion: recycling saves landfill, energy, fuel, production costs and quarrying. Note the causal connectives ('As less energy is needed… this also saves…'), which reverse the claim in Q13.
