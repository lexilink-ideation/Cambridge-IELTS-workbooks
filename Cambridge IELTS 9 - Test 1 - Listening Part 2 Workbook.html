Listening Part 2

Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A radio presenter, Jane, tells listeners in Bradcaster about a new Sports World store opening at the weekend. She describes the shop's location and floors, the opening-day guest (champion runner Paul King), the competitions and prizes, and a discounted fitness test. Question types: note completion (Q11–16), multiple choice (Q17–18) and choose TWO letters (Q19–20).

💡 Listening tip: This monologue is full of paired numbers — ten days vs fourteen days, fifteen questions vs twenty questions, £30 vs half price. Each time you hear two numbers close together, ask yourself which one belongs to this shop and which belongs to the other one. Q11–16 need ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER only.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 2 answers are hidden inside longer descriptive sentences. Practise stripping them down.
Q12 — Location
Question note
located in the shopping centre to the ______ of Bradcaster
What you hear
'It's going to be in the shopping centre to the west of Bradcaster… the original shop in the north of the town'
Key paraphrase
two compass points appear — 'the original shop' marks the OLD store (north), so the new one is west
Your turn
Q14 — Delivery time
Question note
can get you any item within ______ days
What you hear
'they promise to get it for you in ten days. Unlike the other store, where it can take up to fourteen days.'
Key paraphrase
'Unlike the other store' is the contrast signal — fourteen belongs to the competitor
Your turn
Q17 — The champion athlete
Question
A champion athlete will be in the shop…
What you hear
'open from 9.00 am this Saturday… he will be staying around until about midday'
Key paraphrase
9 am to midday on Saturday = Saturday morning only → A (not all day, not the whole weekend)
Your turn
Q18 — The quiz prize
Question
The first person to answer 20 quiz questions correctly will win…
What you hear
'answer fifteen out of twenty correctly to win a signed DVD… the first person to get all the questions correct gets a year's free membership of the Bradcaster Gym'
Key paraphrase
the DVD (B) and the calendar (C) go to other people — read the question stem carefully → A
Your turn
