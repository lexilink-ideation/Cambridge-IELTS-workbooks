📋 Passage Overview

Alexander Henderson (1831-1913). A biographical passage about a Scottish-born photographer who emigrated to Canada in 1855 and became a well-known landscape photographer. It traces his wealthy family background, his reluctant training as an accountant, his move to Montreal, and his photographic career, including his work for Canadian railways.

💡 Reading tip: Biographies are chronological. For True/False/Not Given, check dates and the order of events carefully — 'Not Given' means the text simply doesn't say, even if the statement seems likely.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Although he never liked the prospect of a business career, he stayed with it to please his family.
Main Clausehe stayed with it
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough he never liked the prospect of a business career (concession); to please his family (purpose)
Grammar Note'Although' concedes his dislike; 'to please' gives his reason for continuing anyway.
Simple MeaningHe disliked a business career but kept at it to make his family happy.
中文尽管他从不喜欢从商的前景，但为了让家人满意，他还是坚持了下来。
Besides its residence in Edinburgh, the family owned Press Estate, 650 acres of farmland about 35 miles southeast of the city.
Main Clausethe family owned Press Estate
Subordinate / RelativeBesides its residence in Edinburgh (addition); 650 acres … southeast of the city (appositive)
Grammar Note'Besides' adds to the previous point; the appositive renames and describes Press Estate.
Simple MeaningAs well as a home in Edinburgh, the family owned a 650-acre farm nearby.
中文除了在爱丁堡的住所外，这个家族还拥有距城东南约35英里、面积650英亩的普雷斯庄园。
People bought Henderson's photos because photography took up considerable time and the equipment was heavy.
Main ClausePeople bought Henderson's photos
Subordinate / Relativebecause photography took up considerable time and the equipment was heavy (reason clause)
Grammar Note'because' introduces two linked reasons joined by 'and'.
Simple MeaningPeople bought his photos because taking photos was slow and the gear was heavy.
中文人们购买亨德森的照片，是因为摄影耗时且设备沉重。
In October 1855, however, he emigrated to Canada with his wife and they settled in Montreal.
Main Clausehe emigrated to Canada … and they settled in Montreal
Subordinate / RelativeIn October 1855, however (time + contrast connector)
Grammar Note'however' marks a turning point in the narrative; two clauses joined by 'and'.
Simple MeaningIn October 1855, though, he moved to Canada with his wife and settled in Montreal.
中文然而，1855年10月，他与妻子移居加拿大，定居蒙特利尔。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology & dates. Anchor each statement to a year (1831 born, 1855 emigrated, 1866 studio, 1875-1897 railway work). Dates are the fastest way to check True/False/Not Given.

Cause and effect. Note the reasons given: he stayed in business to please his family; people bought photos because equipment was heavy and photography slow.

What the text does NOT say. Several statements (e.g. surprise at an experiment, similarity to Notman's work) are 'Not Given' because the passage never comments on them. Resist using outside knowledge.
