Cambridge IELTS 7 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Homestay Application · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A homestay officer interviews Keiko Yuichini, a 28-year-old Japanese student, in order to complete her homestay application form. The officer checks her passport details, asks about the length of her stay, her course, and then her preferences for a host family — including attitudes to children, pets, food, hobbies and transport.

This is a classic Part 1 form-completion task: two speakers, a short administrative interview, and answers that appear in the same order as the questions — mostly names, numbers, and short personal-preference details.

💡 Part 1 strategy: Before you listen, look at the form headings and predict the answer type for each gap (a number, a length of time, a course name). Watch for the speaker changing their mind mid-sentence (e.g. "I prefer... I mean I like young children, but...") — the final statement is always the true answer.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 7  |  🎧 Test: 4, Section 1  |  ❓ Questions: 1–10

📝 Task type: Form completion (Q1–6) + short-answer questions (Q7–10)

🗣️ Setting: An interview to complete a Homestay Application form

👥 Speakers: Officer (interviewer) · Student (Keiko, a Japanese applicant)

Task breakdown

Q1–2: First name and passport number.

Q3–4: Length of homestay and the course she is enrolled in.

Q5–6: Family preferences — children and pets.

Q7–8: Favourite food and sport.

Q9–10: Preferred transport and when she will get her address.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

IELTS Listening questions never use the exact same words as the audio. Study each contrast below, note the key paraphrase, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — Length of Homestay
Question wordingLength of homestay: approx ______
Audio meaningThe officer asks how long she plans to stay with the homestay family. She gives an approximate time, then adds she might stay longer if she enjoys it.
Key paraphrase"about four months...longer if I like it" = the estimated/approximate length is four months
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — Family Preference (Children)
Question wordingFamily preferences: no ______
Audio meaningShe starts to say she likes young children, then corrects herself and says she'd actually prefer to live with older people/adults around her own age.
Key paraphrase"but I'd like to be with older people" = she does NOT want a family with young children
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — Food Preference
Question wordingWhat does the student particularly like to eat?
Audio meaningShe says she is not vegetarian (a distractor), does not eat much meat, then names her favourite food type.
Key paraphrase"I really like seafood" = her particular favourite food, despite not being vegetarian
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — Preferred Transport
Question wordingWhat mode of transport does the student prefer?
Audio meaningShe mentions taking the bus into the city sometimes, but complains buses are "always late" — then names the transport she actually prefers.
Key paraphrase"I like catching the train...much faster" = train is her preferred mode of transport, not bus
