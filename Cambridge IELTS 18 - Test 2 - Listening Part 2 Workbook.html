Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 2 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Mark Reynolds, Communications Manager, presents plans for a new housing development on the edge of Nunston. He explains why the site was chosen, the positive feedback received, then labels a map of the development. Expect two choose TWO tasks (Q11/12 and Q13/14) and map labelling (Q15–20).

💡 Listening tip: For map labelling, first find the fixed points — the two entrances, the main road, London Road, the lake and the compass (N). Then follow direction words: on the western side, just below, to the right of, adjoining, between the two entrances, it curves round to the left.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Watch for distractors — the airport and cyclist facilities are mentioned but are NOT the answers.
Why the site (Q11/12)
Option (E)
It will be convenient for workers.
What you hear
'because of the new industrial centre… there's a lot of demand for housing for employees'
Key paraphrase
housing for employees near the industrial centre = convenient for workers → E
Your turn
A distractor (the airport)
Tempting option (C)
good connections with the airport
What you hear
'although that's certainly convenient, it wasn't one of our major criteria'
Key paraphrase
the airport is rejected — do NOT choose C
