📋 Passage Overview

This passage asks whether humans can communicate by thought alone — the science of telepathy. It describes the ganzfeld experiments, in which a relaxed 'receiver' tries to identify an image a 'sender' concentrates on, and the debate over the results: sceptics point to flaws such as sensory leakage and fraud, while supporters argue that small effects need large samples and meta-analysis to appear. Newer autoganzfeld tests use computers to reduce human error.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 3 is opinion- and argument-based. For matching-ending and summary-completion tasks, first find the exact place the idea is discussed, then choose the option that paraphrases it. Keep clear in your mind which side (sceptic vs supporter) each sentence belongs to.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"For more than a century the issue of telepathy has divided the scientific community, and even today it still sparks bitter controversy among top academics."
Main Clausethe issue of telepathy has divided the scientific community … it still sparks bitter controversy
Subordinate / RelativeFor more than a century / even today (adverbial time phrases)
Grammar NoteTwo main clauses joined by 'and'; time phrases stress the long-running dispute.
Simple MeaningScientists have argued about telepathy for over 100 years and still do.
中文一个多世纪以来，心灵感应的问题一直分裂着科学界，即便今天仍在顶尖学者中引发激烈争论。
"Reports of telepathic experiences … led parapsychologists to suspect that telepathy might involve 'signals' that were so faint that they were usually swamped by normal brain activity."
Main ClauseReports … led parapsychologists to suspect …
Subordinate / Relativethat telepathy might involve signals … / that were so faint that they were … swamped (noun + relative + result clause)
Grammar Note'led … to suspect that' introduces a belief; nested 'so faint that' = result.
Simple MeaningBecause of such reports, researchers thought telepathic signals were too weak to notice under normal brain activity.
中文关于心灵感应体验的报告使超心理学家怀疑：这种感应可能涉及极其微弱、通常被正常脑活动淹没的“信号”。
"Just because chance had been ruled out as an explanation did not prove telepathy must exist; there were many other ways of getting positive results."
Main Clause(it) did not prove telepathy must exist
Subordinate / RelativeJust because chance had been ruled out … (adverbial clause of reason)
Grammar Note'Just because … (this) does not prove …' — rejecting a false inference; semicolon adds evidence.
Simple MeaningEven if the results were not just luck, that alone does not show telepathy is real.
中文仅仅排除了偶然性，并不能证明心灵感应必然存在；获得正面结果还有许多其他途径。
"If, as current results suggest, telepathy produces hit-rates only marginally above the 25 per cent expected by chance, it's unlikely to be detected by a typical ganzfeld study."
Main Clauseit's unlikely to be detected by a typical ganzfeld study
Subordinate / RelativeIf … telepathy produces hit-rates only marginally above 25 per cent (conditional clause with inserted 'as current results suggest')
Grammar NoteFirst conditional 'If …, it's unlikely …'; the comma insertion adds evidence.
Simple MeaningIf telepathy only just beats chance, a normal small study probably won't detect it.
中文如果正如目前结果所示，心灵感应的命中率仅略高于偶然的25%，那么一般的全域实验很可能检测不到它。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Claim vs Counter-claim — Some researchers say ganzfeld results are 'compelling evidence'; others think the field is 'on the brink of collapse'. Track opposing views — they power Q27–30.

Cause & Effect — Reports of telepathy during meditation led researchers to design the ganzfeld's relaxing conditions (Q28). Discovering a mechanism would transform parapsychology (Q29).

Problem / Solution — Problem: positive results could come from sensory leakage or fraud. Solution: autoganzfeld — computers do key tasks to limit human involvement.

Evidence & Statistics — Random guessing = 25% hit-rate; ganzfeld showed >30%; Edinburgh musicians reached 56%. 'It takes large samples to detect small effects' explains why meta-analysis matters.
