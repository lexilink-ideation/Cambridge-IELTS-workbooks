📋 Quick Review

A lecture on designing a public building, illustrated by the new Taylor Concert Hall. The speaker covers the three things an architect must consider (function, context, symbolic idea), then the location, the concept of a 'mystery', the building's design, the auditorium's acoustics, and the critics' reaction.

建筑主题 Part 4:笔记按“引言 → 选址 → 设计 → 评价”推进,词汇偏技术。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY. Several gaps need a concrete noun (factory, canal, bridge, box, screen, rubber, curtains) and two need an adjective (curved, international). Predict the word class from the words around the gap — e.g. 'are ___ in shape' must be an adjective.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 4 notes compress the lecturer's clauses. Practise expanding each note back into the original sentence.
Q31 — social context
Question note
physical and ___ context
What you hear
'this includes its physical location, obviously, but it also includes the social meaning of the building, how it relates to the people it's built for'
Key paraphrase
'the social meaning … how it relates to the people' → social
Your turn
Q34 — the bridge
Question note
It's approached by a ___ for pedestrians
What you hear
'As people approach the entrance they therefore have to cross over a bridge'
Key paraphrase
'approach the entrance … cross over a bridge' = 'approached by a bridge'
Your turn
Q36 — the screen
Question note
One exterior wall acts as a large ___
What you hear
'At night-time, projectors are switched on and it functions as a huge screen'
Key paraphrase
'functions as' = 'acts as'; 'huge' = 'large' → screen
Your turn
Q38 — curved walls
Question note
the walls … are ___ in shape
What you hear
'In order to improve the acoustic properties … they are not straight, they are curved'
Key paraphrase
the negative-then-positive pattern ('not X, they are Y') always gives the answer
Your turn
Q40 — international style
Question note
Some critics say the ___ style is inappropriate
What you hear
'they criticise the style of the design as being international rather than local'
Key paraphrase
'criticise … as being international rather than local' → international
Your turn
