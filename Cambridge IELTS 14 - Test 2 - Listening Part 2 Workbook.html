📋 Quick Review

A tour guide gives visitors information about Branley Castle before they arrive — its history over more than a thousand years, what the visit will include, and where things are on the castle plan. Expect multiple-choice questions (Q11-15) and a map/plan labelling task (Q16-20).

💡 Listening tip: For the map task, orient yourself first (entrance, gates, towers, river). Follow direction words carefully: 'first left', 'past the bridge', 'turn right before you get there'.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

MCQ answers hide behind paraphrase; map answers hide behind directions. Train both.
Why a new house
Question idea
Why did they build a new house for the Queen?
What you hear
'rather than repair the guest rooms, they'd make a new house … out of wood'
Key paraphrase
'rather than repair' = instead of repairing; the choice was to build (in wood)
Your turn
Direction to the shop
Question idea
Which building is the shop?
What you hear
'take the first left after the main entrance … and take the first right'
Key paraphrase
follow each step exactly; the endpoint is the labelled building
Your turn
