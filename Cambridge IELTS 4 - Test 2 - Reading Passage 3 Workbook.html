📋 Passage Overview

Play is a serious business asks why animals and children play, given that it costs energy and can even cost lives. Old theories (skills practice, fitness training) are questioned. The newest idea is that play builds bigger brains: playfulness links to brain size, and a 'sensitive period' lets play shape the developing brain. Nine paragraphs (A–I) with matching tasks.

💡 Reading tip: 'Which paragraph contains…' tasks reward skimming for the main idea of each paragraph. Label each paragraph with two or three words first (hazards, timing, brain chemistry…), then match.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Play may look like a carefree and exuberant way to pass the time before the hard work of adulthood comes along, but there's much more to it than that.
Main ClausePlay may look like a … way to pass the time …, but there's much more to it than that
Subordinate / Relativebefore the hard work of adulthood comes along (time clause)
Grammar Note'may look like … but there's much more to it' sets up appearance vs reality.
Simple MeaningPlay seems like a relaxed way to pass time before adult life, but it is more important than that.
中文游戏看似只是成年前的一种无忧无虑、充满活力的消磨时间的方式，但其意义远不止于此。
If play evolved to build muscle or as a kind of endurance training, then you would expect to see permanent benefits.
Main Clausethen you would expect to see permanent benefits
Subordinate / RelativeIf play evolved to build muscle or as a kind of endurance training (conditional clause)
Grammar Notesecond conditional (If + past, would + verb) tests a hypothesis.
Simple MeaningIf play existed to build strength, its benefits should last into adulthood.
中文如果游戏是为了增强肌肉或作为一种耐力训练而进化的，那么你会期望看到持久的益处。
Because large brains are more sensitive to developmental stimuli than smaller brains, they require more play to help mould them for adulthood.
Main Clausethey require more play to help mould them for adulthood
Subordinate / RelativeBecause large brains are more sensitive to developmental stimuli than smaller brains (reason clause)
Grammar Note'Because…' gives the reason; 'to help mould' = infinitive of purpose.
Simple MeaningSince big brains respond more to developmental input, they need more play to shape them.
中文由于大脑越大，对发育刺激越敏感，因此需要更多的游戏来帮助塑造它，为成年做准备。
By allowing link-ups between brain areas that might not normally communicate with each other, play may enhance creativity.
Main Clauseplay may enhance creativity
Subordinate / RelativeBy allowing link-ups between brain areas that might not normally communicate (gerund phrase); that might not normally communicate (relative clause)
Grammar Note'By + gerund' shows the means; the relative clause defines 'brain areas'.
Simple MeaningBy connecting brain areas that usually don't talk to each other, play may boost creativity.
中文通过让通常不会相互沟通的脑区连接起来，游戏可能会增强创造力。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Appearance vs reality: play 'looks like' idle fun, 'but there's much more to it' — the passage's opening move.

Cost–benefit reasoning: play is expensive (energy, lives), so there 'must be a reason' — this drives the search for its function.

Theory tested and rejected: the exercise theory and skills-training hypothesis are each raised, then undercut by evidence (Byers, Caro).

New explanation: play builds brains — supported by Pellis (brain size), Barton (environmental data), Bekoff (variety) and Siviy (brain chemistry).
