📋 Quick Review

A guide welcomes visitors to Green Vale Agricultural Park, which has just opened. Using a wall plan, the speaker points out where each area is (rare breeds, grazing, forest, fish farms, market garden, and more), then explains opening times, how to get around, the animals kept, the purpose of the Rare Breeds section, seasonal events, and the shop. This Part 2 combines a map/plan-labelling task with multiple-choice questions.

💡 Listening strategy tip: For the plan-labelling task, orient yourself first: find 'YOU ARE HERE' and the compass (north/east/south). Then track direction words — 'next to', 'moving east', 'south of', 'in the middle', 'to the east of those'. For the multiple-choice items, listen for the answer that is stated, not just a word you recognise.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ11: the MAIN purposes of the park are …
What you hear / read"Our remit is to give educational opportunities … as well as to offer research sites for … scientists."
Key paraphrase'research and education' → answer B (not entertainment).
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptQ15: When are the experimental areas closed?
What you hear / read"can be visited … for almost all the year … when certain areas are being used for … experiments … temporarily out of bounds."
Key paraphraseClosed only briefly → 'a short time every year' (C).
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptQ18: main purpose of the Rare Breeds Section …
What you hear / read"not as such to preserve rare animals but to maintain the diversity of breeds to broaden the gene pool."
Key paraphrase'keep a variety of breeds' = maintain diversity → answer B, not 'save unusual animals'.

Paraphrase Example 4
Question / promptQ19: What can you see at present?
What you hear / read"John Havers, our expert fly fisherman, is currently giving displays on the lake."
Key paraphrase'at the present time' → a demonstration of fishing (C); blossom/geese are May events.
