📋 Quick Review

Dan Beagle gives a talk on foraging for wild mushrooms. He warns about safety, corrects some common myths, and gives advice on where to pick, going with an expert, conservation, storage and trying new varieties. Expect two choose TWO tasks (Q11/12 and Q13/14) and multiple choice (Q15–20).

💡 Listening tip: Note the difference between the two 'choose TWO' tasks: Q11/12 asks for warnings (things NOT to do), while Q13/14 asks which ideas are correct. Read the stems carefully or you will pick the wrong pair.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The speaker states myths to reject and facts to accept — listen for 'don't listen to people who…' vs 'it's true that…'.
Bright mushrooms (Q13/14)
Correct idea (D)
Brightly coloured mushrooms can be edible.
What you hear
'Don't listen to people who tell you it's only OK to eat mushrooms that are pale or dull… some edible mushrooms are bright red'
Key paraphrase
bright red ones can be edible → D
Your turn
