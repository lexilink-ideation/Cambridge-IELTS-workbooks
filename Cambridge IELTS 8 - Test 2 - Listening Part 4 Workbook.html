Listening Part 4

Cambridge IELTS 8 — Test 2 — Listening Part 4

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A student researcher, Shona, describes a project on what people in Britain think of general-practice doctors, compared with other countries. She explains how she set up the study, who she included, her use of a questionnaire and focus group, what her early results show, and the problems she met with individual interviewees. The tasks are multiple-choice plus a matching task (which statement applies to each person).

💡 Listening strategy tip: This Part 4 is a first-person account, not a lecture of facts. Track the researcher's choices and reasons ('I decided to …', 'my aim was …'). For the matching task (Q37–40), each option is used once — listen for what each specific person did (pulled out, wanted confidentiality, kept changing their mind, refused to say).

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ31: To set up the programme, Shona got …
What you hear / read"I approached postgraduates … in overseas departments … and sometimes their tutors got involved too."
Key paraphrase'information from her tutor's contacts' — she used academic contacts abroad → answer C.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptQ33: In her questionnaire her aim was …
What you hear / read"rather than limiting it to one specific point, I wanted to include as much variety as possible."
Key paraphrase'to get a wide range of data' = as much variety as possible → answer A.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptQ34: Initial results about British medical services show …
What you hear / read"despite … newspapers … it is mainly the … care … in hospitals that they are worried about."
Key paraphrasePress picture ≠ reality → 'current concerns are misrepresented by the press' (A).
