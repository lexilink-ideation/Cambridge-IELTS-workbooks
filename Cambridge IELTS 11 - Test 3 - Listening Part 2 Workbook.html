📋 Quick Review

Shona Ferguson from Barford town council speaks at a public meeting about how the town has changed over 50 years — buses, roads, shopping, medical facilities and employment (Q11–15) — and then outlines the current plans for five town facilities (Q16–20).

后半部分是“从选项框里选”题型:五个设施配五种计划,必须听清动词。

💡 Listening tip: For Q16–20 the seven options are all short verb phrases (move / extend hours / refurbish / change use / reduce hours / new management / expand). Turn each into a keyword before you listen: move, later, redecorate, different use, earlier closing, who runs it, bigger.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Matching-from-a-box questions test verb meaning. Learn these five verbs cold.
Q16 — expanded
Option G
It will be expanded.
What you hear
'the company has bought some adjoining land and this will be used to increase the number of parking spaces'
Key paraphrase
'increase the number of parking spaces' = 'expanded'
Your turn
Q17 — new location
Option A
It will move to a new location.
What you hear
'The Grand, the old cinema in the high street, will close at the end of the year, and reopen on a different site'
Key paraphrase
'reopen on a different site' = 'move to a new location'
Your turn
Q18 — refurbished
Option C
It will be refurbished.
What you hear
'it will close for six weeks to be made safe and redecorated'
Key paraphrase
'made safe and redecorated' = 'refurbished'; note the demolition threat did NOT happen
Your turn
Q19 — extended hours
Option B
It will have its opening hours extended.
What you hear
'funding to keep the library open later into the evening twice a week'
Key paraphrase
'open later into the evening' = 'opening hours extended'
Your turn
Q20 — new management
Option F
It will have new management.
What you hear
'the council will no longer be in charge … it will become the responsibility of a national body'
Key paraphrase
'no longer in charge … the responsibility of a national body' = 'new management'
Your turn
