📋 Quick Review

A student, Anu Bhatt from India, visits a university accommodation office to apply for a place in a hall of residence. The man fills in a form with her, asking about her name, date of birth, course, catering, diet, room type, interests and her priorities for choosing a hall. This is form completion — ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.

💡 Listening tip: Form-completion answers arrive in the exact order printed on the page. Two items are spelled out (the surname Bhatt) or said as a date (31 March 1972) — write letters and numbers as raw data first, then tidy them. Note the strict ONE WORD limit: for Q5 the word '(red)' is already printed, so you write only meat.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The form uses compressed noun phrases; the speakers use natural sentences. Bridge the two.
Q4 — Number of years in hall
Form line
Number of years planned in hall: ______
What you hear
'it'll take three years but I'd only like to stay in hall for two'
Key paraphrase
'three years' = the COURSE; 'stay in hall for two' answers the form → 2
Your turn
Q6 — Preferred room type
Form line
Preferred room type: a single ______
What you hear
'a single bedsit which actually has more space and better facilities… the bedsit sounds the best option'
Key paraphrase
'single study bedroom' and 'shared' are offered first and rejected → bedsit
Your turn
Q8 — Priority about other students
Form line
to be with other students who are ______
What you hear
'a hall where there are other mature students' — 'halls which tend to cater for slightly older students'
Key paraphrase
mature = slightly older; either word is accepted
Your turn
Q10 — Socialising space
Form line
to have a ______ area for socialising
What you hear
'somewhere with a shared area, a TV room for example… It's a good way to socialise'
Key paraphrase
'a TV room' is only an example — the form word is shared
Your turn
