✏️ Paraphrase Training

Options and flow-chart labels reword the discussion. Match the meaning.
Forgetting dreams (Q21)
Option B
we might otherwise be confused about what is real
What you hear
'we might get mixed up about what actually happened and what we dreamed'
Key paraphrase
'mixed up about what happened' = confused about what is real
Your turn
The nap study (Q23)
Option C
failed to reach any clear conclusions
What you hear
'they didn't really find an answer… the results didn't show any obvious patterns'
Key paraphrase
'no obvious patterns / no answer' = no clear conclusions
Your turn
