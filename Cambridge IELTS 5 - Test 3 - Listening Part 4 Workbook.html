📋 Quick Review

This is an academic lecture on recycling and waste. The speaker gives statistics on how much waste is recycled, mentions incineration plants and drop-off collection points, and describes how recycled materials become new products — soil conditioner, containers, pencils and even business cards. Expect number and noun completion.

💡 Listening tip: Section 4 is a monologue with no repetition. Read the gaps first and predict whether each answer is a number, percentage or noun so you can catch it on the first pass.
