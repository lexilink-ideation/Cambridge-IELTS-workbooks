Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 12 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Margaret Smith phones Pembroke Cycling Holidays about an advert for cycle tour leaders. Bob takes her details: the kind of job she wants, her career change, her experience, her voluntary work, her availability, a food allergy, her address and postcode, and finally the interview arrangements. Note completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: Enquiry calls always end with an address, a postcode and a date — the three places where careless spelling loses marks. Write the postcode with no space (DG74PH) and check 'P Papa or B Bravo' style clarifications, which exist precisely because the letter is easy to mishear.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Enquiry-call notes rewrite spoken answers as formal bullet points. Practise both directions.
Q1 — the kind of job
Question note
wants a ___ job
What you hear
'Are you looking for a permanent job?' — 'No, temporary.'
Key paraphrase
The interviewer's word is the wrong one. Listen for the correction → temporary
Your turn
Q3 — where she has led trips
Question note
has led cycle trips in ___
What you hear
'I've led several bike tours in Africa. The trip to India that I had arranged to lead next month has now been cancelled'
Key paraphrase
'has led' = past experience; the India trip never happened → Africa
Your turn
Q4 — the club
Question note
voluntary work with members of a ___ club
What you hear
'I'm a volunteer worker in a youth club… Before that I helped out in a cycling club'
Key paraphrase
'currently doing' = the present job, not 'before that' → youth
Your turn
Q9 — the interview day
Question note
interview at 2.30 pm on ___
What you hear
'If you could return the application form by Friday this week, we can interview you on Tuesday next week'
Key paraphrase
Three days are mentioned (Friday = deadline, Monday = travel, Tuesday = interview) → Tuesday
Your turn
