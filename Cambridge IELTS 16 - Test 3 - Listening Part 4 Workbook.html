📋 Quick Review

A lecture on hand knitting — why interest is rising again, the benefits of knitting as a craft, and what archaeology tells us about early knitting (shapes, needles, yarns and regional styles). Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Sub-headings ('Benefits of knitting', 'Early knitting') keep you on track. Predict the part of speech: most answers here are single nouns or adjectives.
