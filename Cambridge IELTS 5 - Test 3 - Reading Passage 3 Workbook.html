📋 Passage Overview

This passage, 'The Return of Artificial Intelligence', describes how AI fell out of favour after early hype in the 1980s but is quietly making a comeback. It explains why early expectations were disappointed, how a new, more modest and practical approach — focused on solving real business problems rather than imitating human thought — is succeeding, and why AI companies are re-emerging.

💡 Reading strategy tip: This passage has seven paragraphs A–G. For paragraph-matching and TFNG, distinguish the writer's report of past failure from the current revival — the timeline is the key to many answers.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"After the exaggerated claims of the 1980s failed to materialise, many investors abandoned artificial intelligence."
Main Clausemany investors abandoned artificial intelligence
Subordinate / RelativeAfter the exaggerated claims of the 1980s failed to materialise (time/cause clause)
Grammar Note'After … failed to …' gives the reason; 'materialise' = actually happen.
Simple MeaningWhen the big 1980s promises didn't come true, investors gave up on AI.
中文在20世纪80年代那些夸大的说法未能实现之后，许多投资者放弃了人工智能。
"Rather than trying to mimic human thought, the new approach focuses on solving specific, practical problems."
Main Clausethe new approach focuses on solving specific, practical problems
Subordinate / RelativeRather than trying to mimic human thought (contrast phrase = 'instead of')
Grammar Note'Rather than + -ing' contrasts the old aim with the new one.
Simple MeaningInstead of copying human thinking, AI now tackles particular real-world tasks.
中文新方法不再试图模仿人类思维，而是专注于解决具体的、实际的问题。
"Because it now delivers useful results quietly, artificial intelligence has begun to attract investment once again."
Main Clauseartificial intelligence has begun to attract investment once again
Subordinate / RelativeBecause it now delivers useful results quietly (cause clause)
Grammar Note'Because … now …' shows the reason; 'once again' signals the revival.
Simple MeaningAs AI now quietly produces useful results, money is flowing back into it.
中文由于如今它能悄然带来有用的成果，人工智能又开始吸引投资了。
"Researchers, some of them funded by a government agency, reckon that this modest approach is the most promising."
Main ClauseResearchers … reckon that this modest approach is the most promising
Subordinate / Relativesome of them funded by a government agency (non-defining participle phrase)
Grammar NoteThe inserted phrase adds detail about the researchers; 'reckon that' reports their view.
Simple MeaningResearchers, some paid by a government body, think this modest approach works best.
中文一些由政府机构资助的研究人员认为，这种适度的方法最有前景。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Rise → Fall → Return — 1980s hype → failure/disillusion → quiet revival. The whole passage follows this arc.

Old vs New approach — Old: mimic human thought (failed). New: practical, modest problem-solving (works). Contrast 'rather than'.

Cause & Effect — Useful results → renewed investment → new companies emerge. Result drives result.

Author's stance — Cautiously optimistic: AI succeeds precisely because expectations are now realistic. Note the calm, 'behind the scenes' framing.
