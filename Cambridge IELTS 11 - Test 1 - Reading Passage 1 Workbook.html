Cambridge IELTS 11 — Test 1 — Reading Passage 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Passage Overview

'Crop-growing skyscrapers' asks how we will feed three billion extra people by 2050. It explains the idea of the vertical farm — a multi-storey building where crops grow in controlled conditions in the middle of a city — then lists the claimed advantages and the main drawback (artificial light). It ends by saying most experts think urban rooftops are more likely than skyscrapers.

题型:句子填空 Q1–7(NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS)+ TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN Q8–13。

💡 Reading tip: Sentence completion answers appear in passage order, so Q1's answer is near the start and Q7's near the end. For T/F/NG, FALSE requires the passage to say the opposite; NOT GIVEN means the passage never discusses the point at all.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

An estimated 10⁹ hectares of new land (about 20% larger than Brazil) will be needed to grow enough food to feed them, if traditional farming methods continue as they are practised today.
Main ClauseAn estimated 10⁹ hectares of new land will be needed
Subordinate / Relativeto grow enough food to feed them (purpose infinitive); if traditional farming methods continue as they are practised today (conditional clause); '(about 20% larger than Brazil)' = bracketed comparison
Grammar NotePassive future 'will be needed' + purpose infinitive + first conditional 'if'-clause placed at the end.
Simple MeaningIf we farm the old way, we will need a huge area of new land — bigger than Brazil — to feed everyone.
中文如果继续沿用今天的传统耕作方式,估计还需要约10亿公顷新土地(比巴西还大约20%)才能种出足够的粮食。
Situated in the heart of urban centres, they would drastically reduce the amount of transportation required to bring food to consumers.
Main Clausethey would drastically reduce the amount of transportation
Subordinate / RelativeSituated in the heart of urban centres (reduced participle clause = 'Because they would be situated…'); required to bring food to consumers (reduced relative = 'that is required')
Grammar NoteTwo reduced clauses around one short main clause — very typical of IELTS Reading.
Simple MeaningBecause they stand in city centres, far less transport would be needed to deliver food.
中文由于坐落在城市中心,它们将大幅减少把食物运送给消费者所需的运输量。
Although the system would consume energy, it would return energy to the grid via methane generation from composting non-edible parts of plants.
Main Clauseit would return energy to the grid
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough the system would consume energy (concession clause); 'via methane generation from composting…' = prepositional phrase of method
Grammar Note'Although' sets up a concession: the disadvantage is admitted, then outweighed.
Simple MeaningThe system uses energy, but it also makes energy by turning plant waste into methane.
中文虽然该系统会消耗能源,但它可以通过将植物不可食用部分堆肥产生甲烷,把能源送回电网。
Without it, those plants nearest the windows would be exposed to more sunlight and grow more quickly, reducing the efficiency of the system.
Main Clausethose plants nearest the windows would be exposed to more sunlight and grow more quickly
Subordinate / RelativeWithout it (conditional prepositional phrase = 'If there were no artificial light'); reducing the efficiency of the system (result participle)
Grammar Note'Without + noun' replaces an if-clause; '-ing' at the end shows the result.
Simple MeaningIf there were no artificial light, plants by the window would grow faster than the rest, so the system would work less well.
中文如果没有人工照明,靠窗的植物会接受更多阳光、长得更快,从而降低整个系统的效率。
While it is possible that much of our food will be grown in skyscrapers in future, most experts currently believe it is far more likely that we will simply use the space available on urban rooftops.
Main Clausemost experts currently believe (that) it is far more likely that we will simply use the space available on urban rooftops
Subordinate / RelativeWhile it is possible that much of our food will be grown in skyscrapers in future (concession clause)
Grammar Note'While' = 'although'; two embedded that-clauses; comparative 'far more likely'.
Simple MeaningSkyscraper farming may happen, but experts think rooftop farming is much more likely.
中文虽然将来我们大部分食物有可能在摩天大楼中种植,但多数专家目前认为,更可能的是我们只会利用城市屋顶的空间。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Problem → proposed solution → evaluation. Paragraphs 1–2 give the problem (population growth, damaged land); paragraphs 3–5 give the solution (vertical farms) and its advantages; paragraphs 6–8 evaluate it and prefer rooftops. Knowing this shape tells you roughly where each answer lives.

Claim vs. fact. Watch the reporting verbs: 'proponents claim', 'supporters claim many potential advantages', 'most experts currently believe'. These mark opinions, and they matter for T/F/NG (Q10, Q12).

Contrast signals. 'What is new is…', 'However', 'A major drawback… however', 'While it is possible…' — each of these turns the argument. Q5 and Q7 both sit immediately after a contrast marker.

Cause and effect chains. No artificial light → plants near windows get more sun → they grow faster → efficiency falls. IELTS often tests one link in such a chain rather than the whole chain.
