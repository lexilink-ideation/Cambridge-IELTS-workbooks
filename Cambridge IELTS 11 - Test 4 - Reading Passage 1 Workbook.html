📋 Passage Overview

'Research using twins' explains how identical and fraternal twins let scientists separate nature from nurture. It traces the field from Francis Galton (1875) to Thomas Bouchard's Minnesota Study of Twins Reared Apart, then introduces a third force — epigenetics — with comments from geneticist Danielle Reed.

题型:T/F/NG Q1–4 + 人物配对 Q5–9 + 词库摘要 Q10–13。

💡 Reading tip: For Q5–9, three researchers appear in a fixed order in the passage (Galton → Bouchard → Reed). Find the paragraph for each name first, then match statements to it. 'NB You may use any letter more than once' means a name can be the answer twice.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Because identical twins come from a single fertilized egg that splits into two, they share virtually the same genetic code.
Main Clausethey share virtually the same genetic code
Subordinate / Relative'Because identical twins come from a single fertilized egg' = reason clause; 'that splits into two' = defining relative clause
Grammar NoteReason clause first, main clause second — the standard order for explaining a scientific fact.
Simple MeaningIdentical twins come from one egg that divided, so their genes are almost the same.
中文由于同卵双胞胎来自一个分裂为二的受精卵,他们的基因编码几乎完全相同。
If identical twins are more similar to each other with respect to an ailment than fraternal twins are, then vulnerability to the disease must be rooted at least in part in heredity.
Main Clausethen vulnerability to the disease must be rooted at least in part in heredity
Subordinate / Relative'If identical twins are more similar … than fraternal twins are' = conditional clause with a comparative and ellipsis ('than fraternal twins are (similar)')
Grammar NoteFirst conditional with a comparative; 'must be' expresses logical deduction, not obligation.
Simple MeaningIf identical twins share an illness more often than fraternal twins do, genes play some part in it.
中文如果同卵双胞胎在某种疾病上的相似度高于异卵双胞胎,那么对该病的易感性至少部分源自遗传。
In broad terms, the heritability of a trait measures the extent to which differences among members of a population can be explained by differences in their genetics.
Main Clausethe heritability of a trait measures the extent
Subordinate / Relative'to which differences among members of a population can be explained by differences in their genetics' = relative clause with preposition ('the extent to which')
Grammar Note'the extent to which…' is a high-value academic phrase — useful in Writing Task 2.
Simple MeaningHeritability measures how far genetic differences explain differences between people.
中文大体而言,某一性状的遗传力衡量的是:一个群体成员之间的差异在多大程度上能由他们的基因差异来解释。
If you think of our DNA as an immense piano keyboard and our genes as the keys — each key symbolizing a segment of DNA responsible for a particular note, or trait, and all the keys combining to make us who we are — then epigenetic processes determine when and how each key can be struck, changing the tune being played.
Main Clausethen epigenetic processes determine when and how each key can be struck
Subordinate / Relative'If you think of our DNA as … and our genes as the keys' = conditional; the dash section is a long appositive explanation; 'changing the tune being played' = result participle
Grammar NoteAn extended metaphor inside a conditional. Read the dashes as a footnote and keep the If…then skeleton in view.
Simple MeaningIf DNA is a piano and genes are its keys, epigenetics decides when and how each key is played.
中文如果把我们的DNA想象成一架巨大的钢琴键盘,基因就是琴键——每个键代表一段负责某一音符或性状的DNA,所有键组合起来造就了我们——那么表观遗传过程决定每个键何时以及如何被敲响,从而改变正在演奏的曲调。
Studies of animals, for example, have shown that when a rat experiences stress during pregnancy, it can cause epigenetic changes in a fetus that lead to behavioral problems as the rodent grows up.
Main ClauseStudies of animals … have shown
Subordinate / Relative'that when a rat experiences stress during pregnancy, it can cause epigenetic changes in a fetus' = reported clause containing a time clause; 'that lead to behavioral problems' = relative clause; 'as the rodent grows up' = time clause
Grammar NoteFour levels of subordination. Find 'have shown' first, then unpack.
Simple MeaningIn rats, stress during pregnancy causes epigenetic changes in the unborn young that later cause behaviour problems.
中文例如,动物研究表明,当大鼠在怀孕期间经历压力时,可能引起胎儿的表观遗传变化,并在其长大后导致行为问题。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Two research strategies. Paragraph 3 states them explicitly: comparing identical twins with each other (to isolate environment) and comparing identical with fraternal twins (to measure inheritance). The whole passage hangs on this pair.

Chronology of the field. 1875 Galton → 1980s studies of separated twins → Bouchard's Minnesota Study (137 pairs, 15,000+ questions) → 'lately' epigenetics. Dates and 'lately/recent' keep the sections apart.

Metaphor as explanation. The piano keyboard (DNA), the keys (genes), and the way they are struck (epigenetics); then Reed's pen-and-pencil image. Metaphors are never the answer, but they show you which paragraph you are in.

Three-part causation. Nature + nurture were long assumed to be the whole story; epigenetics is presented as a 'third component' which sometimes bridges the two and sometimes acts alone. Q4 tests exactly this.

Contrast then and now. Reed: '50 years ago things like heart disease were thought to be caused entirely by lifestyle. Schizophrenia was thought to be due to poor mothering.' The past-belief / present-knowledge contrast is a common IELTS structure.
