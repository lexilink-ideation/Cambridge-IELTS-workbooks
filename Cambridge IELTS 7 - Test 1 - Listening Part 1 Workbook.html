📋 Quick Review

In this Part 1 conversation, a woman phones the Land Transport Information desk at Toronto Airport to ask how to travel about 147 miles to a town called Milton. A male agent explains the transport options (car hire, taxi, Greyhound bus, Airport Shuttle) and then helps her make a booking, filling in a form with her personal and travel details.

This is a classic "enquiry + booking form" Part 1 pattern: first general questions and answers about services, then a shift into completing a form with names, dates, numbers, and spellings.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 1 always has two speakers and moves from general information to specific personal details (name, date, flight number, credit card number). Numbers, letters, and dates are usually said clearly but only once — get ready to write the moment you hear a key word like "date," "name," or "number."
