📋 Quick Review

Joanna discusses her Master's psychology study with her supervisor. The study looked at six female professional musicians and how they choose concert dress. They talk about who the subjects were, why she used telephone interviews, how her focus changed, and what she found.

三组“选两个”(Q21–26)+ 四道单选(Q27–30)。选两个题最容易漏掉“all”这个限定词。

💡 Listening tip: Q21–22 ask what all the subjects shared. Options that are only true of some ('quite a few had won prizes') are wrong. Underline words like all, every, both in the question stem before you listen.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

'Choose TWO' options are short; the spoken evidence is long and hedged. Practise matching them.
Q21/22 — recordings
Option B
They had all made music recordings.
What you hear
'I wasn't specifically looking for artists who'd produced recordings but this is something that's just taken for granted these days and they all had'
Key paraphrase
the key words are the last three: 'they all had' → B
Your turn
Q23/24 — rich data
Option A
It meant rich data could be collected.
What you hear
'I don't think using the phone meant I got less rich data, rather the opposite in fact'
Key paraphrase
double negative: 'not less … rather the opposite' = 'more' → A
Your turn
Q27 — why women only
Option C
women's code of dress is less strict than men's
What you hear
'in social terms the choices they have are more limited … they'd really upset audiences if they strayed away from quite narrow boundaries'
Key paraphrase
men have 'narrow boundaries' → women's code is less strict
Your turn
Q29 — the audience
Option A
The musicians' choice of clothing is linked to respect for the audience.
What you hear
'the audience had certain expectations and it was up to them as performers to fulfil these expectations to show a kind of esteem'
Key paraphrase
'esteem' = 'respect' → A
Your turn
Q30 — sports science
Option C
the way clothing may protect against physical injury
What you hear
'the movements they carry out are very intensive and repetitive, so … some features of sports clothing could safeguard the players from the potentially dangerous effects'
Key paraphrase
'safeguard from dangerous effects' = 'protect against physical injury'
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Supervision-meeting chunks. Notice the hedging and the double negatives.
1.They'd done quite extensive tours in different continents.
2.Between twenty-five and twenty-nine — the mean was twenty-seven point eight.
3.I don't think using the phone meant I got less rich data.
4.That focus didn't emerge immediately.
5.You'd expect there to be rules about it in orchestras, but that's quite rare.
6.They'd really upset audiences if they strayed away from quite narrow boundaries.
7.It was up to them as performers to fulfil these expectations.
8.They avoid clothing that obviously restricts their movements.
