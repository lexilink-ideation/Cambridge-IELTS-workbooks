Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

📋 Quick Review

A visitor talk about Manham Port, a historic port with a thousand years of history. The first half traces the port's rise (tin, then the Industrial Revolution), its decline, and the work of the Manham Trust. The second half recommends three attractions: the copper mine, the village school and the old sailing ship 'The George'.

这是“旅游景点介绍”类讲话。前半段考历史因果(选择题),后半段考景点特色与建议(表格题)。

💡 Listening tip: Q11–15 are cause-and-effect questions ('Why did…?', 'What caused…?'). The answer is almost always in a because, so, or proved a handy place to clause. Q16–20 allow NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS, so 'guided tour' is acceptable for Q19.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Tour talks describe things; exam options label them. Practise turning a description into a label — that is the whole skill. 导览讲话给的是描述,选项给的是标签。
Q11 — 'convenient for river transport'
Option B
It was convenient for river transport.
Audio wording
'the highest navigable point of the Avon — boats can go no higher up this river — and proved a handy place to load and unload cargo'
Key paraphrase
convenient = handy; river transport = boats / loading cargo
Trap
'on a bend in the great River Avon' and 'the sea … is over 23 miles away' both distract towards option C.
Your turn
Q12 — 'increase in demand for metals'
Option B
the increase in demand for metals
Audio wording
'when a tremendous need for metals of all kinds developed, that Manham expanded'
Key paraphrase
increase in demand = a tremendous need … developed
Trap
Tin (option C) is mentioned in the same sentence, but 900 years ago — 'it wasn't until the Industrial Revolution … that'.
Your turn
Q13 — 'shortage of fuel'
Option A
shortage of fuel
Audio wording
'there was not enough coal in the local area, so the rocks containing minerals had to be shipped long distances'
Key paraphrase
shortage of fuel = not enough coal
Trap
'shipped long distances' may suggest option B (poor transport) — but the shipping is the result, not the cause.
Your turn
Q15 — 'rebuild the port complex'
Option C
rebuild the port complex
Audio wording
'to match installations with maps of the original port complex and to set about reconstructing it'
Key paraphrase
rebuild = reconstruct
Trap
'remove undergrowth to find the original outlines' matches option A — but that is only the preliminary step.
Your turn
Q16 — 'specially adapted miners' trains'
Table wording
specially adapted miners' ___ take visitors into the mountain
Audio wording
'Travel on converted mining trains and journey into the depths of the mountain'
Key paraphrase
specially adapted = converted; miners' trains = mining trains
Trap
'pumping machines' comes next and is a strong distractor.
Your turn
