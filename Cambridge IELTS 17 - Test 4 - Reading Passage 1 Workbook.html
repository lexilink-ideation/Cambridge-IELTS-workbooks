📋 Passage Overview

Bats to the rescue. Zoologist Ricardo Rocha's study shows that several native insectivorous bats feast on rice pests around Madagascar's farmland, giving farmers free pest control and easing pressure to clear forest. The passage also covers the study's methods, disease-carrying pests the bats eat, and local attitudes to bats. Question types: TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN (Q1–6) and table completion (Q7–13).

💡 Reading tip: Table completion answers appear in passage order and are copied exactly ('ONE WORD ONLY'). For T/F/NG, read the whole relevant sentence — a small detail (e.g. 'insects destroy forests') can flip TRUE to FALSE.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

And a key reason for this destruction is that insect pests are destroying vast quantities of what is grown by local subsistence farmers, leading them to clear forest to create new paddy fields.
Main Clausea key reason for this destruction is that insect pests are destroying vast quantities of what is grown
Subordinate / Relativethat insect pests are destroying… (noun clause = the reason); what is grown by local subsistence farmers (nominal relative clause); leading them to clear forest… (participle clause of result)
Grammar Note'the reason is that…' + embedded 'what' clause + '-ing' result clause.
Simple MeaningPests destroy so much of the farmers' crops that the farmers clear forest for new fields.
中文造成这种破坏的一个关键原因是,害虫毁掉了当地自给农民种植的大量作物,促使他们砍伐森林开辟新的稻田。
Co-leading an international team of scientists, Rocha found that several species of indigenous bats are taking advantage of habitat modification to hunt insects swarming above the country's rice fields.
Main ClauseRocha found that several species of indigenous bats are taking advantage of habitat modification
Subordinate / RelativeCo-leading an international team of scientists (front participle clause); that several species… are taking advantage… (noun clause); swarming above the country's rice fields (reduced relative clause describing insects)
Grammar NoteFront participle sets context; reporting verb 'found' + that-clause; 'swarming' = 'which swarm'.
Simple MeaningLeading a team, Rocha found that native bats use the changed habitat to hunt insects over the rice fields.
中文罗查共同领导一个国际科学团队,发现几种本土蝙蝠正利用栖息地的改变,捕食在稻田上空成群的昆虫。
The recordings revealed that bat activity over rice fields was much higher than it was in continuous forest – seven times higher over flat fields, and sixteen times higher over fields on hillsides.
Main ClauseThe recordings revealed that bat activity over rice fields was much higher
Subordinate / Relativethan it was in continuous forest (comparative clause); (the two 'times higher' phrases are appositive detail)
Grammar NoteComparative 'much higher than…'; the dash adds specific multipliers.
Simple MeaningBats were far more active over rice fields than in unbroken forest — up to sixteen times more on hillside fields.
中文录音显示,蝙蝠在稻田上空的活动远高于连续森林——平地稻田高七倍,山坡稻田高十六倍。
At the same time, however, they are associated with sacred caves and the ancestors, so they can be viewed as beings between worlds, which makes them very significant in the culture of the people.
Main Clausethey are associated with sacred caves and the ancestors, so they can be viewed as beings between worlds
Subordinate / Relativewhich makes them very significant in the culture of the people (non-defining relative clause commenting on the whole idea)
Grammar Note'so' shows result; sentential 'which' refers back to the whole preceding clause.
Simple MeaningBecause bats are linked with sacred caves and ancestors, people see them as in-between beings — culturally important.
中文然而与此同时,它们又与圣洞和祖先相联系,因而被视为介于两个世界之间的存在,这使它们在当地文化中十分重要。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause and effect chain. Pests → crop loss → forest clearing → habitat loss. The bats break the chain by eating pests, easing the pressure to clear forest. Tracking this chain answers Q1 and Q4.

Method → findings. The study uses ultrasonic recorders (to find feeding spots) and DNA barcoding of droppings (to identify prey). The table (Q7–13) follows Aim → Method → Findings → Recommendation.

Mixed attitudes. The passage balances the bats' benefits (pest control, eating disease carriers) with local ambivalence (a protein source, making buildings unclean, cultural significance) — the source of Q10–12.
