📋 Passage Overview

Titled What Do Whales Feel?, this passage examines the senses of cetaceans (whales, dolphins and porpoises). Smell and taste are reduced or absent; touch is actually well developed; vision varies by species and habitat; and the acoustic (hearing) sense is the best developed of all. Questions are a table and short NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS answers.

💡 Reading tip: For table completion, scan for the species or sense in each row, then read around it. Copy words exactly from the passage and stay within the word limit (here, three words).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

It has been speculated that, as the blowholes evolved and migrated to the top of the head, the neural pathways serving sense of smell may have been nearly all sacrificed.
Main ClauseIt has been speculated that … the neural pathways … may have been nearly all sacrificed
Subordinate / Relativeas the blowholes evolved and migrated to the top of the head (adverbial clause of time/cause)
Grammar Note'It has been speculated that…' = impersonal passive (hedging); 'as' shows a parallel process over time.
Simple MeaningScientists think that as blowholes moved to the top of the head, the smell nerves were mostly lost.
中文有人推测，随着鼻孔进化并移到头顶，负责嗅觉的神经通路可能几乎全部退化了。
However, the position of the eyes so restricts the field of vision in baleen whales that they probably do not have stereoscopic vision.
Main Clausethe position of the eyes so restricts the field of vision … that they probably do not have stereoscopic vision
Subordinate / Relativeso … that … (result construction)
Grammar Note'so + verb/adjective … that …' expresses degree and result.
Simple MeaningBecause their eyes are placed on the sides, baleen whales see a limited area and probably cannot see in 3-D.
中文然而，须鲸眼睛的位置极大地限制了视野，以致它们可能没有立体视觉。
Such variation can no doubt be explained with reference to the habitats in which individual species have developed.
Main ClauseSuch variation can no doubt be explained
Subordinate / Relativewith reference to the habitats in which individual species have developed (prepositional + relative clause)
Grammar Note'in which' = where; 'with reference to' = by looking at.
Simple MeaningThese differences can be explained by the different habitats each species evolved in.
中文这种差异无疑可以用各物种所进化的栖息环境来解释。
Although the senses of taste and smell appear to have deteriorated, and vision in water appears to be uncertain, such weaknesses are more than compensated for by cetaceans' well-developed acoustic sense.
Main Clausesuch weaknesses are more than compensated for by cetaceans' well-developed acoustic sense
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough the senses of taste and smell appear to have deteriorated, and vision … appears uncertain (concession)
Grammar Note'Although' introduces two concessions; 'more than compensated for' = a passive phrasal verb.
Simple MeaningEven though taste, smell and vision are weak, cetaceans' excellent hearing more than makes up for it.
中文尽管味觉和嗅觉似乎已退化、水中视觉也不确定，但鲸类高度发达的听觉足以弥补这些不足。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Correction of a common belief: touch was 'sometimes described as weak too, but this view is probably mistaken' — the writer overturns an assumption with evidence (trainers' reports, frequent contact).

Comparison across species: baleen vs toothed; bottlenose dolphin vs river dolphins — vision depends on habitat (clear open water vs turbid rivers).

Cause & effect via habitat: clear water → better vision; muddy rivers → reduced/blind vision (the Indian susus are blind).

Compensation logic: weak senses (smell/taste/vision) are 'more than compensated for' by the strong acoustic sense — the passage's main organising idea.
