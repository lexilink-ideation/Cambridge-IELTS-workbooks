Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A woman from an employment agency talks a man through two jobs at Milo's Restaurants. She describes the staff benefits and the person they are looking for, then compares two vacancies — a breakfast supervisor in Wivenhoe Street and a junior chef at City Road. Expect note completion (Q1–5, ONE WORD) and table completion (Q6–10).

💡 Listening tip: When a table compares two options, keep your eyes moving across the columns. Wages are a classic trap — you hear £9.50 (his old job) before the real answer £9.75, so listen to the end of the sentence.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The spoken wording is longer than the note; learn to compress it to one word.

Table note
Making sure ___ is clean
What you hear
'checking all the procedures for cleaning the equipment are being followed'

cleaning the equipment → the noun is equipment

The salary (Q8)
Table note
Starting salary £___ per hour
What you hear
'to begin with, you'd be getting £9.75… goes up to £11.25 after three months'
Key paraphrase
'to begin with' = starting salary → 9.75 (not 11.25)
