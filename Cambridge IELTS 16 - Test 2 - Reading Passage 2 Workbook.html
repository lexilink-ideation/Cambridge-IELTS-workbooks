📋 Passage Overview

I contain multitudes. Wendy Moore reviews Ed Yong's book about microbes — the trillions of bacteria living in and around us. It argues we should stop seeing microbes as the enemy and understand our partnership with them. Question types: multiple choice (Q14–16), summary from a list (Q17–20) and Yes / No / Not Given (Q21–26).

💡 Reading tip: Yes/No/Not Given asks about the writer's claims/opinions, not just facts. Look for evaluative language ('may', 'surprisingly', 'we should') to find the writer's view.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

And, as Yong explains in his utterly absorbing and hugely important book, we mess with them at our peril.
Main Clausewe mess with them at our peril
Subordinate / Relativeas Yong explains in his … book (comment clause)
Grammar NoteAn 'as…' comment clause attributes the claim to the author.
Simple MeaningYong shows that harming our microbes is dangerous for us.
中文正如杨在他这本极其引人入胜又极为重要的书中所言，我们扰乱微生物是自食其果。
So 'bad' microbes are just microbes out of context.
Main Clause'bad' microbes are just microbes out of context
Subordinate / Relative— (no subordinate clause; 'So' links to the previous idea)
Grammar NoteA short, emphatic definition; 'out of context' does the key work.
Simple MeaningA microbe is only 'bad' when it's in the wrong place.
中文所谓“坏”微生物，不过是处在错误位置的微生物。
Writing with lightness and panache, he has a knack of explaining complex science in terms that are both easy to understand and totally enthralling.
Main Clausehe has a knack of explaining complex science
Subordinate / RelativeWriting with lightness and panache (participle phrase); that are both easy to understand and totally enthralling (relative)
Grammar NoteFronted participle phrase + relative clause describing 'terms'.
Simple MeaningHe explains hard science in a clear, exciting way.
中文他文笔轻快而洒脱，善于用既易懂又引人入胜的语言解释复杂科学。
Microbes that sit contentedly in the human gut can become deadly if they find their way into the bloodstream.
Main ClauseMicrobes … can become deadly
Subordinate / Relativethat sit contentedly in the human gut (relative); if they find their way into the bloodstream (conditional)
Grammar NoteRelative clause defines which microbes; conditional gives the danger.
Simple MeaningGut microbes are harmless there but dangerous in the blood.
中文安居于人体肠道的微生物，一旦进入血液就可能致命。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Reframing (attitude): the writer flips a common belief — microbes aren't 'the enemy'; our relationship is a partnership. Yes/No items reward spotting this shift in attitude.

Definition by contrast: 'bad' microbes are simply microbes 'out of context' — good vs bad depends on location, not type. Watch how the passage redefines a familiar word.

Problem → contributing causes: a growing bacterial imbalance is linked to poor nutrition, overuse of antibiotics and excessive cleanliness. Summary answers come straight from this cause list.
