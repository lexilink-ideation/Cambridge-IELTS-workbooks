📋 Quick Review

Three students — Rosa, Mick and Pete — plan an assignment on methods of collecting data in social-science research. They confirm the essay title and word counts, discuss the drawbacks of questionnaires versus interviews, and note down recommended books with their authors, publishers and dates. Expect notes, a two-answer multiple choice, and a table.

💡 Listening tip: For the reference table, listen for the fixed order — author, then title, then publisher, then year. Names are often spelt (M-E-H-T-A) and years are given in full ('nineteen eighty-eight').
