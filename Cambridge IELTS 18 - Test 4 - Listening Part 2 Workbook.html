📋 Quick Review

A guide welcomes visitors to the Museum of Farming Life. She gives the building's history, practical information (entry, cloakroom), then describes the museum's areas one by one. Expect multiple choice (Q11–14) and a matching task — matching information to each area of the museum (Q15–20).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task, each area of the museum is described once and the options (A–H) are paraphrased — 'an expert is here today' becomes 'a specialist cheesemaker will be giving demonstrations'. Underline a keyword in each option first.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Each option is re-worded in the talk — train the swap from option to spoken clue.
Four Seasons (Q15)
Option (F)
It features something created by students.
What you hear
'designed especially for the museum by a group of young people on a film studies course'
Key paraphrase
designed by film-studies students = created by students → F
Your turn
