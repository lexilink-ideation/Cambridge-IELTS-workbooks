📋 Quick Review

A speaker introduces a university Counselling Service to new (mostly overseas) students. She covers academic counselling, causes of stress and personal crisis, loss of self-esteem, diet, and loans for books, equipment and living costs, then gives figures for last year's work. Expect ten multiple-choice questions.

💡 Listening tip: In a long multiple-choice monologue, read all the options in the gap first. Listen for the option that is paraphrased — the speaker rarely uses the exact words printed on the page.
