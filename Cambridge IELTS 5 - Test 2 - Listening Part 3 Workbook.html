Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A college tutor holds a feedback tutorial with two English-language students, Cristina and Ibrahim, who recently made a short video together called "Student Life" as part of their course.

They discuss what they enjoyed, which listening and language skills the project helped them practise, what they learned more generally, and what they would do differently if they made another video.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 3 usually has two or three speakers with different opinions. Before listening, note whose name is linked to each question — the speakers often disagree or mention different points, so questions frequently ask you to identify who said what.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / Phrase Simple Meaning 中文 Example Sentence IELTS Note
tutorial a meeting with a teacher to discuss your work 辅导课；面谈 "They had a tutorial about their video project." Common in Part 3 academic contexts
feedback comments about how well something was done 反馈意见 "The tutor gave feedback on the film." Uncountable noun — no plural "feedbacks"
footage recorded video material 影像素材 "They filmed footage at a student's home." Paraphrase for "video material"
operate to use or work a machine 操作 "Cristina learned to operate the camera." Paraphrase: use / handle a machine
compromise to agree by giving up part of what you want 妥协 "Group work taught her to compromise." Can be verb or noun
tolerate to accept something you don't like, without complaining 容忍 "She learned to tolerate different opinions." Adjective: tolerant
teamwork people working together effectively 团队合作 "Ibrahim valued the teamwork involved." Uncountable noun
informal casual; not formal in style 非正式的 "They used a lot of informal vocabulary." Opposite: formal
organise to plan and arrange something 组织安排 "They needed to organise their roles early." Noun: organisation
mix with to socialise with a group of people 与...交往 "They don't usually mix with other classes." Phrasal verb — separable
natural speed the normal, unslowed pace people speak at 正常语速 "Listening at natural speed was difficult." Common contrast: slowed-down speech in class
role a person's job or part within a group task 角色；职责 "They should decide roles at the start." Collocation: assign/divide roles

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Cristina's favourite part
Question wording What did Cristina find most enjoyable about the project?
Audio meaning "For me, the best part was getting to use the video camera myself, since I'd never touched one before."
Key paraphrase "getting to use... for the first time" = most enjoyable / best part
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Ibrahim's new experience
Question wording What was new for Ibrahim during the project?
Audio meaning "It gave me the chance to get to know people from other classes — normally we only really mix with other English learners."
Key paraphrase "get to know people from other classes" = a new social experience
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Ibrahim's listening practice
Question wording What language skill did Ibrahim find most useful to practise?
Audio meaning "Listening to the British students chatting away at their normal speed was tough, because they used so much vocabulary I just didn't recognise."
Key paraphrase "chatting away at normal speed" = natural-speed listening practice
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — Cristina's future plan
Question wording What would Cristina do differently next time?
Audio meaning "Next time, I think we should sit down right at the start and agree who's doing what at each stage."
Key paraphrase "agree who's doing what at each stage" = plan roles at the very beginning
Your practice
