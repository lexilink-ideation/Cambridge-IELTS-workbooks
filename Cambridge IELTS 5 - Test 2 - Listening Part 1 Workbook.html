Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A man contacts North College Library to ask about joining as a member of the public (he is not a student there). A librarian answers his questions about registration, cost, borrowing rules, computer bookings, newspapers, the photocopier, and a creative writing class run at the library.

This is a typical Listening Part 1 conversation: two speakers, one asking questions and one giving practical, factual information such as prices, numbers, and requirements.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 1, information is almost always given in the same order as the questions on the page. Before you listen, underline the key words in each question (numbers, dates, "how many," "how much") — this tells you exactly what kind of answer to expect and helps you follow along without getting lost.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / Phrase Simple Meaning 中文 Example Sentence IELTS Note
enquire to ask for information 询问 "He phoned to enquire about joining the library." Paraphrase: ask about / find out about
register to officially put your name on a list to join something 登记；注册 "You need to register before you can borrow books." Often paired with "as a member"
identification (ID) an official document that proves who you are 身份证明 "Please bring two forms of identification." Listen for specific examples: driving licence, bank statement
statement an official document listing account activity （银行）对账单 "A bank statement shows your name and address." Common as "proof of address"
annual happening once every year 每年的；年度的 "There is an annual fee to join." Paraphrase: per year / yearly
membership the state of belonging to a group or organisation 会员资格 "His membership allows him to borrow books." Collocation: full membership, free membership
borrow to take something and give it back later 借（阅） "You can borrow up to eight items." Not "loan" (that's what the library does)
fine money you must pay as a penalty 罚款 "There is a minimum fine for late books." Homophone trap: fine (罚款) vs. fine (好的)
reserve / book to arrange to use something at a future time 预订 "You can book a computer 48 hours ahead." "Book" here is a verb, not a noun
resident based permanently in one place 常驻的 "He is the library's resident author." Different from "resident" meaning "someone who lives somewhere"
tutor a teacher, especially one who guides a small class 导师；辅导老师 "The creative writing tutor is a published author." Listen for full name spellings after this word
photocopier a machine that makes copies of documents 复印机 "You need a card to use the photocopier." Commonly misspelled — practise it!

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Minimum age
Question wording What is the minimum age to join the library?
Audio meaning The librarian says the caller "will have to be at least eighteen" to join.
Key paraphrase "at least eighteen" = the minimum age is 18
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Cost of joining
Question wording How much does membership cost per year?
Audio meaning "The annual fee is £125, unless you're already a student elsewhere with a current card — then it's just £25."
Key paraphrase "annual fee" = the cost per year
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — Borrowing limit
Question wording How many books can members of the public borrow?
Audio meaning "As a member of the public, you're allowed eight items at once, though students can take out twelve."
Key paraphrase "allowed... at once" = can borrow at the same time
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — Booking computers
Question wording How far in advance can computers be reserved?
Audio meaning "You can book a computer up to two days ahead, though most people just reserve one for the next day."
Key paraphrase "two days ahead" = 48 hours in advance
Your practice
