Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

📋 Quick Review

A student, Peter Chin, phones King's Restaurant about a job vacancy. The woman who answers (Samira Manuja) explains the type of work, the hours, the branch location, the pay, the extra benefits and the qualities they want, then arranges an interview. This is a classic job enquiry Part 1 — expect a spelled name, a street name, a price and a date.

💡 Listening tip: The instruction says NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER. Two answers here are spelled out letter by letter (Hillsdunne, Manuja) — write the letters down as you hear them and only tidy the spelling afterwards. Also watch for distractor numbers: the man mentions his old pay (£3.95) right after the real answer (£4.45).

✏️ Paraphrase Training

In Section 1 the question paper uses short note language; the speaker uses full conversation. Train the swap.
Q1 — Type of work
Question note
Type of work: ______
What you hear
MAN: 'what sort of work is it — washing up?' WOMAN: 'It's answering the phone… And not waiting at table.'
Key paraphrase
'what sort of work' = Type of work; the man's guesses (washing up / waiting at table) are BOTH rejected — the answer is what the woman states
Your turn
Q4 — Pay
Question note
Pay: £______ an hour
What you hear
'We're offering £4.45 an hour.' — 'My last job was £3.95 an hour.'
Key paraphrase
'We're offering' marks the current job; the second figure describes his PAST job and is a distractor
Your turn
Q6 — Transport home
Question note
transport home when you work ______
What you hear
'because of the difficulties of getting public transport, if you're working after 11 o'clock we drive you home'
Key paraphrase
'we drive you home' = transport home; 'if you're working after 11 o'clock' = the time condition
Your turn
Q7–8 — Qualities required
Question note
Qualities required: a ______ / ability to ______
What you hear
'we want a clear voice… And you must be able to think quickly.'
Key paraphrase
'we want' → the noun phrase; 'you must be able to' = ability to + verb
Your turn
