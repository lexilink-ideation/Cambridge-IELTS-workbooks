Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 2 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Part 2 a speaker describes two history and nature walks — the Marsden Coastal Walk and the Melby Heritage Walk — and then gives a guided tour of Melby Coal Mine, explaining where each attraction (exhibition, bathhouse, mining tools, vehicle shed, ponies, education centre) is located. The last section is a map-labelling task.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 2 often has a map task. Before you listen, find the labelled features and the starting point on the map. Follow direction words carefully: north of, southwest corner, north-eastern boundary, via a covered walkway. "Not approached via a covered walkway" is a key distractor.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingBe careful of steep and rocky paths.
Audio meaning"there are no strenuous climbs"
Key paraphraseno strenuous climbs = the path is NOT steep (so this option is wrong)
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question wordingDon’t worry about getting lost.
Audio meaning"clear signposts all along … you don’t even need to take a map"
Key paraphraseclear signposts = you won’t get lost
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question wordingA tower stands on the site of an older structure.
Audio meaning"the tower … situated where there was once a … hunting lodge"
Key paraphrasewhere there was once = on the site of

Audio meaning"brings you back down to the starting point"
