📋 Quick Review

An astronomy lecture on the need for a Space Traffic Management system to control satellites — similar to Air Traffic Control for planes. The talk explains what such a system would do, the problems in developing one (crowding, no tracking, secrecy), and possible solutions. This is note completion — ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: Part 4 has clear signposts ('A Space Traffic Management system…', 'Problems…', 'Solutions…'). Use them to keep your place, and predict whether each gap needs an adjective (e.g. 'technical', 'cheap') or a noun (e.g. 'tracking', 'database').
