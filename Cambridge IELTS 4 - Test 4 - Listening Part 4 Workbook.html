📋 Quick Review

A lecture on sharks in Australia. First the speaker describes shark biology (size, weight, skeleton of cartilage, skin, how they swim and find food by smell). Then he explains beach-netting (meshing) — how it works, where it has spread, how many sharks are caught, and why the nets are sometimes less effective. Expect notes, multiple-choice and a two-answer question.

💡 Listening tip: A factual lecture packs in numbers and places. Predict each note's type (a weight, a body part, a place) and keep your pen on the next gap — the speaker rarely repeats a figure.
