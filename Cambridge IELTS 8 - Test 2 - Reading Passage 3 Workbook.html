📋 Passage Overview

This passage, 'The meaning and power of smell', explores how the sense of smell (olfaction) affects us physically, emotionally and socially. It discusses how smells trigger emotions and memories, help people bond and recognise family, why smell is undervalued despite being acute, why odours are so hard to describe or record, the unanswered scientific questions, and how smell is also a cultural phenomenon that helps define groups.

💡 Reading strategy tip: For heading matching and the 'what is the writer doing?' question, focus on the function of each paragraph, not just its topic. For ONE WORD ONLY sentence completion, the answer is a single word copied directly from the passage — check spelling and singular/plural.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"It is only when the faculty of smell is impaired for some reason that we begin to realise the essential role the sense of smell plays in our sense of well-being."
Main Clausewe begin to realise the essential role the sense of smell plays … (within an 'It is only … that …' cleft)
Subordinate / Relativewhen the faculty of smell is impaired for some reason (adverbial clause of time)
Grammar NoteCleft 'It is only when … that …' emphasises the condition; 'the role (that) smell plays' omits 'that'.
Simple MeaningWe notice how important smell is only when we lose it.
中文只有当嗅觉因某种原因受损时，我们才开始意识到嗅觉对我们幸福感所起的重要作用。
"Such associations can be powerful enough so that odours that we would generally label unpleasant become agreeable for particular individuals."
Main ClauseSuch associations can be powerful enough so that odours … become agreeable for particular individuals
Subordinate / Relativethat we would generally label unpleasant (relative clause describing 'odours')
Grammar Note'powerful enough so that …' = strong enough to cause a result; the 'that' clause defines which odours.
Simple MeaningStrong personal associations can make normally unpleasant smells pleasant for some people.
中文这种联想可以强烈到使某些人觉得原本被视为难闻的气味也变得宜人。
"In one well-known test, women and men were able to distinguish by smell alone clothing worn by their marriage partners from similar clothing worn by other people."
Main Clausewomen and men were able to distinguish … clothing worn by their marriage partners from similar clothing worn by other people
Subordinate / Relativeworn by their marriage partners / worn by other people (reduced relative clauses)
Grammar Note'distinguish X from Y'; 'worn by …' = which was worn by … (participle).
Simple MeaningIn a famous test, people could tell their partner's clothes from strangers' by smell alone.
中文在一项著名实验中，男女仅凭气味就能把配偶穿过的衣服与他人穿过的类似衣服区分开来。
"Smell is cultural, hence it is a social and historical phenomenon."
Main ClauseSmell is cultural … it is a social and historical phenomenon
Subordinate / Relativehence (conjunctive adverb showing consequence)
Grammar Note'hence' = therefore; links cause (cultural) to conclusion (social/historical).
Simple MeaningBecause smell is shaped by culture, it is also social and historical.
中文气味具有文化性，因此它也是一种社会和历史现象。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Author's Purpose — Ask what the writer is DOING in each paragraph (giving evidence, rejecting a belief, describing a limitation). Q35 tests this: Para C rejects a common belief that human smell is feeble.

Cause & Effect — Because odours cannot be named or recorded, olfactory research is difficult. Because smell carries cultural values, it can distinguish one culture from another.

Contrast — Emotional associations can make an 'unpleasant' odour agreeable and a 'fragrant' one disagreeable. Signal: while, however, therefore.

Definition / Classification — The passage separates the biological, psychological and cultural sides of smell — useful for matching each paragraph to its heading.
