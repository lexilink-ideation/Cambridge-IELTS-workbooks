📋 Passage Overview

This passage, 'The Effects of Light on Plant and Animal Species', explains how the length of daylight (the photoperiod) and light intensity control the behaviour and development of living things. It describes how plants use day length to time flowering, how animals use it to trigger breeding, migration and hibernation, and how artificial light can disrupt these natural cycles.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Biology passages define technical terms (photoperiod, phytochrome) and then give examples. Link each example back to the principle it illustrates — matching questions often test this link.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"Many plants use the length of the day, rather than temperature, to decide when to flower."
Main ClauseMany plants use the length of the day … to decide when to flower
Subordinate / Relativerather than temperature (contrast phrase); when to flower (embedded question)
Grammar Note'rather than' contrasts two cues; 'when to flower' is a noun phrase after 'decide'.
Simple MeaningMany plants time their flowering by day length, not by temperature.
中文许多植物依靠白昼的长度（而不是温度）来决定何时开花。
"Because day length changes in a regular way through the year, it is a reliable signal for timing life events."
Main Clauseit is a reliable signal for timing life events
Subordinate / RelativeBecause day length changes in a regular way through the year (cause clause)
Grammar Note'Because …' gives the reason day length is dependable; 'for timing' = 'to time'.
Simple MeaningDay length changes predictably each year, so it is a trustworthy timing signal.
中文由于白昼长度在一年中有规律地变化，它是安排生命活动时间的可靠信号。
"In animals, shortening days in autumn can trigger hibernation, while lengthening days in spring may start breeding."
Main Clauseshortening days in autumn can trigger hibernation … lengthening days in spring may start breeding
Subordinate / Relativewhile (contrast connector joining the two clauses)
Grammar Note'while' contrasts two seasonal effects; parallel structure (shortening/lengthening days).
Simple MeaningShorter autumn days can start hibernation, and longer spring days can start breeding.
中文在动物中，秋季变短的白昼会触发冬眠，而春季变长的白昼则可能启动繁殖。
"Artificial lighting at night, which confuses these natural signals, can disrupt migration, breeding and flowering."
Main ClauseArtificial lighting at night … can disrupt migration, breeding and flowering
Subordinate / Relativewhich confuses these natural signals (non-defining relative)
Grammar Note'which …' comments on artificial lighting; three parallel objects follow 'disrupt'.
Simple MeaningNight-time artificial light muddles nature's signals and can upset migration, breeding and flowering.
中文夜间的人造光会扰乱这些自然信号，从而干扰迁徙、繁殖和开花。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition + Example — Photoperiod = day length. Example: plants flower by day length; animals breed/hibernate by it. Match example to principle.

Cause & Effect — Shortening days → hibernation; lengthening days → breeding/flowering. Direction of change matters.

Contrast (cue used) — Plants rely on day length rather than temperature. Note which signal is emphasised.

Human impact — Artificial light confuses these signals and disrupts natural cycles. Cause → disruption.
