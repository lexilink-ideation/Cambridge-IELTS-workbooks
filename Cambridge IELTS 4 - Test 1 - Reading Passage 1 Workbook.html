📋 Passage Overview

This passage reports a study of children's ideas about tropical rainforests. Researchers gave secondary-school children a questionnaire with five open questions (what rainforests are, where they are, why they matter, what destroys them, and why they should be conserved). The article discusses the children's misconceptions and what this means for teaching.

💡 Reading tip: The questions mix True / False / Not Given with matching responses (A–P). For T/F/NG, decide whether the passage states, contradicts, or says nothing. 'Not Given' is not the same as 'False'.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

These misconceptions do not remain isolated but become incorporated into a multifaceted, but organised, conceptual framework, making it and the component ideas more robust but also accessible to modification.
Main ClauseThese misconceptions do not remain isolated but become incorporated into a … framework
Subordinate / Relativemaking it and the component ideas more robust but also accessible to modification (participle phrase of result)
Grammar Note'making…' is a present-participle clause showing the result; note the contrast pair 'robust but … accessible'.
Simple MeaningWrong ideas join a bigger network of ideas, which makes them stronger but also easier to change.
中文这些误解不会孤立存在，而是并入一个复杂但有条理的概念框架中，使其既更牢固，又更容易被修正。
It seems schools may not be providing an opportunity for children to re-express their ideas and so have them tested and refined by teachers and their peers.
Main ClauseIt seems schools may not be providing an opportunity
Subordinate / Relativefor children to re-express their ideas and so have them tested and refined (infinitive purpose clause)
Grammar Note'may not be providing' = tentative; 'have them tested' = causative have (get something done).
Simple MeaningSchools might not give children the chance to say their ideas again and get them checked and improved.
中文学校似乎没有为孩子提供重新表达想法、并让老师和同学检验和完善这些想法的机会。
While two fifths of the students provided the information that the rainforests provide oxygen, in some cases this response also embraced the misconception that rainforest destruction would reduce atmospheric oxygen.
Main Clausethis response also embraced the misconception
Subordinate / RelativeWhile two fifths of the students provided the information… (concession); that rainforest destruction would reduce atmospheric oxygen (noun clause)
Grammar Note'While' = although (concession); 'embraced' here = included/contained.
Simple MeaningTwo fifths said rainforests give oxygen, but some wrongly thought that destroying them would lower the oxygen in the air.
中文虽然五分之二的学生知道雨林提供氧气，但有些人也抱有误解，以为雨林被毁会减少大气中的氧气。
Pupils did not volunteer ideas that suggested that they appreciated the complexity of causes of rainforest destruction.
Main ClausePupils did not volunteer ideas
Subordinate / Relativethat suggested that they appreciated the complexity of causes (relative clause + noun clause)
Grammar Note'volunteer' as a verb = offer without being asked; two 'that' clauses nest inside each other.
Simple MeaningThe pupils did not offer ideas showing they understood how complex the causes of destruction are.
中文学生们没有主动提出能表明他们理解雨林破坏原因之复杂性的想法。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & effect: the study argues that media coverage causes children to form ideas 'independent of any formal tuition' — some correct, some mistaken.

Contrast: girls vs boys — more girls than boys saw rainforests as animal (and human) habitats. Watch comparative signals: 'More girls (70%) than boys (60%)'.

Concession → correction: 'Perhaps encouragingly, more than half… identified human activities' is balanced against misconceptions (acid rain, pollution, oxygen loss).

Statistics as evidence: percentages (43%, 64%, 59%, 6%) are used to rank how common each response was — the 6% for global warming is flagged as surprisingly low.
