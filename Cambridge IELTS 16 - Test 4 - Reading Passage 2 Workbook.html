Reading Passage 2

Cambridge IELTS 16 — Test 4 — Reading Passage 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Passage Overview

Changes in reading habits. A neuroscience writer argues that digital reading is quietly reshaping the 'reading brain' — encouraging skimming and weakening deep reading, critical analysis and empathy — but that we can still act. Question types: multiple choice (Q14–17), summary from a list (Q18–22) and Yes / No / Not Given (Q23–26).

💡 Reading tip: Yes/No/Not Given targets the writer's views. Note the writer's worried-but-hopeful stance: change is real, but 'use it or lose it' means we still have a choice.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Unbeknown to most of us, an invisible, game-changing transformation links everyone in this picture: the neuronal circuit that underlies the brain's ability to read is subtly, rapidly changing.
Main Clausean invisible, game-changing transformation links everyone in this picture
Subordinate / RelativeUnbeknown to most of us (comment); that underlies the brain's ability to read (relative)
Grammar NoteFronted comment phrase + a colon introducing the explanation.
Simple MeaningWithout realising it, we're all affected: the brain's reading network is changing fast.
中文我们大多数人并未察觉，一场无形而深刻的变化把画面中的每个人联系起来：支撑大脑阅读能力的神经回路正在悄然而迅速地改变。
We do not err as a society when we innovate, but when we ignore what we disrupt or diminish while innovating.
Main ClauseWe do not err … but …
Subordinate / Relativewhen we innovate (time); when we ignore what we disrupt or diminish while innovating (time)
Grammar Note'not X but Y' contrast; two parallel 'when' clauses.
Simple MeaningInnovating isn't the mistake; ignoring what it damages is.
中文作为社会，我们的错误不在于创新，而在于创新时忽视了自己破坏或削弱了什么。
When the reading brain skims like this, it reduces time allocated to deep reading processes.
Main Clauseit reduces time allocated to deep reading processes
Subordinate / RelativeWhen the reading brain skims like this (time); allocated to deep reading processes (reduced relative)
Grammar Note'When…' condition + reduced relative ('allocated' = 'that is allocated').
Simple MeaningSkimming leaves less time for deep reading.
中文当阅读大脑这样略读时，用于深度阅读的时间就减少了。
If we work to understand exactly what we will lose, alongside the extraordinary new capacities that the digital world has brought us, there is as much reason for excitement as caution.
Main Clausethere is as much reason for excitement as caution
Subordinate / RelativeIf we work to understand exactly what we will lose (conditional); that the digital world has brought us (relative)
Grammar NoteConditional + 'as much X as Y' balance structure.
Simple MeaningIf we grasp both the losses and the gains, we can be hopeful as well as careful.
中文如果我们努力弄清将会失去什么，同时看到数字世界带来的非凡新能力，那么既有理由期待，也有理由警惕。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Problem → contributing evidence: the problem is weaker deep reading; the evidence is Mangen's print-vs-screen study and Liu's 'skimming is the new norm'. Summary answers come straight from these studies.

Not a simple binary: the writer insists it's 'not print versus digital' but about how and why we read. Watch for this refusal of an either/or framing — a YNNG trap.

Hope in the mechanism: 'use it or lose it' implies choice — we can 'redress' the changes 'before they become entrenched'. The final YNNG item rewards spotting this optimistic claim.
