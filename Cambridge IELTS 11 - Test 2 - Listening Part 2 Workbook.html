📋 Quick Review

Jody welcomes two new staff members to the Amersham Theatre. She describes the refurbishment work, the facilities the theatre offers the public, the workshop programme, and finally the ground-floor plan so the new staff can find their way around.

三组 “选两个”多选题 + 一组平面图标注——最难的是分辨“已经做了”和“打算做”。

💡 Listening tip: Q11–16 are 'Choose TWO letters'. The trap is tense and time: 'we've replaced' (done) vs 'we're about to start / we're looking into / we're considering' (not done). Underline the tense in each option before you listen.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

On 'Choose TWO letters' the paraphrase is short but the tense does the work.
Q11/12 — a different type of seating
Option B
A different type of seating has been installed.
What you hear
'We've replaced them with larger seats with more legroom … seats that can easily be moved'
Key paraphrase
'we've replaced them with larger seats' = 'a different type of seating has been installed'
Your turn
Q11/12 — rooms with a new use
Option A
Some rooms now have a different use.
What you hear
'We've also turned a few storerooms over to other purposes, like using them for meetings'
Key paraphrase
'turned over to other purposes' = 'now have a different use'
Your turn
Q13/14 — backstage tours
Option B
backstage tours
What you hear
'organising backstage tours so people can be shown round the building … These are proving very popular'
Key paraphrase
present continuous + 'are proving popular' = it happens now
Your turn
Q15/16 — puppet-making
Option C
making puppets
What you hear
'A surprise success is the workshop on making puppets … we're now running them every month'
Key paraphrase
'now running them every month' = currently offered
Your turn
Q17 — the box office
Question
17 box office
What you hear
'although it's part of this building it's next door, with a separate entrance from the road'
Key paraphrase
'next door with a separate entrance from the road' places it at the edge of the plan → G

🧠 Mini Dictation

These chunks mix present perfect (done) with future/intention (not done). Hear the difference.
1.We're carrying out a major refurbishment at the moment.
2.We've had to postpone installing an elevator.
3.We've replaced them with larger seats with more legroom.
4.We're looking into the possibility of opening a cafe in due course.
5.Our lighting workshop has already started, with great success.
6.It's next door, with a separate entrance from the road.
7.Go across the foyer and through the double doors, turn right.
8.Turn right at the water cooler and right again at the end.
