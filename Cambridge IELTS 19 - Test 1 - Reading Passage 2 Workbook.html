📋 Passage Overview

The pirates of the ancient Mediterranean. This history passage explains that pirates raided Mediterranean trade routes for thousands of years before the famous Caribbean pirates. It covers why geography helped them, how ancient Egypt, Greece and Rome each dealt with piracy, and how the Roman general Pompey finally eliminated it. Question types: paragraph matching, multiple choice (choose TWO) and summary completion.

💡 Reading tip: Paragraph-matching (Q14–19) does not follow passage order. Turn each prompt into a keyword ('denial', 'campaign carried out', 'view pirates today') and scan for the paragraph that expresses that idea, not just the same words.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Only when the pirates directly threatened the interests of ancient Rome did the Roman Republic organise a massive fleet to eliminate piracy.
Main ClauseThe Roman Republic organised a massive fleet.
Subordinate / Relative'Only when the pirates directly threatened the interests of ancient Rome…' (fronted time clause).
Grammar NoteFronting 'Only when…' forces inversion: 'did the Roman Republic organise'.
Simple MeaningRome only built a big fleet once pirates started hurting Rome itself.
中文只有当海盗直接威胁到古罗马的利益时，罗马共和国才组建了庞大的舰队来消灭海盗。
Before the invention of ocean-going caravels in the 15th century, ships could not easily cross long distances over open water.
Main ClauseShips could not easily cross long distances over open water.
Subordinate / Relative'Before the invention of ocean-going caravels in the 15th century' (time phrase).
Grammar NoteA prepositional time phrase sets the historical limit.
Simple MeaningUntil caravels were invented, ships had to stay near the coast.
中文在15世纪远洋卡拉维尔帆船发明之前，船只无法轻易横渡开阔水域。
Interestingly, in his works the Iliad and the Odyssey, the ancient Greek writer Homer not only condones, but praises the lifestyle and actions of pirates.
Main ClauseHomer not only condones but praises the lifestyle of pirates.
Subordinate / Relative'in his works the Iliad and the Odyssey' (place phrase) + 'not only… but…' (correlative).
Grammar Note'not only… but (also)…' adds emphasis and escalation.
Simple MeaningIn his poems, Homer accepts and even admires the pirate way of life.
中文有趣的是，古希腊作家荷马在《伊利亚特》和《奥德赛》中不仅容忍，甚至赞美海盗的生活方式。
After cleansing one district of pirates, the fleet would join another in the next district, and the process continued until the entire Mediterranean was free of pirates.
Main ClauseThe fleet would join another in the next district.
Subordinate / Relative'After cleansing one district of pirates' (reduced time clause) + 'until the entire Mediterranean was free' (time clause).
Grammar Note'After + -ing' reduces 'After it had cleansed'; 'until' marks the end point.
Simple MeaningOnce one area was cleared, the fleet moved on, area by area, until all were clear.
中文清剿完一个区域后，舰队便会转往下一个区域，如此持续，直到整个地中海再无海盗。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & effect: geography (rugged, coastal land + hidden coves) → local men 'turned to piracy' and could 'avoid retaliation'. Map the chain from environment to behaviour.

Contrast between civilisations: Egypt's power was 'land-based'; the Greeks 'relied on the Mediterranean in almost all aspects of life'. This contrast explains their different attitudes to piracy.

Problem → solution: pirates kidnapped Roman dignitaries (problem) → a new law gave Pompey funds and he divided the sea into 13 districts (solution).

Concession: 'The Greeks' liberal approach… does not mean they always tolerated it, but attempts to curtail piracy were hampered…' — 'does not mean… but' signals a qualified point.
