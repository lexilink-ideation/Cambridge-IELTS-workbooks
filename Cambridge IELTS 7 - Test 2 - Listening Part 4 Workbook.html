📋 Quick Review

Section 4 is a single-speaker academic talk (a student summarising an article and research) on the topic of "handedness" — whether being left-handed, right-handed, or "mixed-handed" affects performance in different sports. The speaker (Anita) discusses the work of sports psychologist Peter Matthews, covering studies on musicians, apes, and three sports: hockey, tennis, and gymnastics.

This is typical IELTS Listening Part 4 content: a longer, uninterrupted monologue on an academic topic, tested with multiple-choice questions and table/note completion — with no break in the middle, so sustained concentration is essential.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Because Part 4 has no pause, use the time before the recording starts to preview ALL the questions (both the multiple-choice section and the table). Predict what type of word is missing in each blank (noun, adjective, number) so you can write quickly without losing your place.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / PhraseSimple Meaning中文Example SentenceIELTS Note
handednessthe tendency to use one hand more than the other利手（左撇子或右撇子）"My topic is handedness in sport."Core topic word — appears throughout the talk
lateralitythe preferential use of one side of the body over the other偏侧性"There are three main types of laterality: mixed, single, and cross."More technical synonym for handedness (includes feet/eyes too)
mixed-handedable to use both hands equally well混合利手的"Keyboard players had high levels of mixed-handedness."Contrast with "single-handed" (favouring one side)
cross-lateralitywhen a person's dominant hand and dominant eye are on opposite sides交叉利侧性"Cross-laterality is best for gymnastics because it aids balance."Opposite of "single laterality" (same-side dominance)
dominantthe stronger, preferred, or more frequently used side/hand/eye占优势的，主导的"The racket is invisible from the dominant eye during the swing."Common collocation: dominant hand / dominant eye
sheer volumea very large quantity, emphasised for effect巨大的数量"She was struck by the sheer volume of research he'd done.""Sheer" adds emphasis — common in academic talks
deployto use or position something for a purpose运用，部署"A hockey stick must be deployed in two directions."Formal verb, often used for tools/equipment/strategy
drawbacka disadvantage or negative aspect of something缺点，不利之处"It would be a drawback to favour only one side."Synonym for "disadvantage"
correctionan adjustment made to fix an error, especially in movement（动作）纠正，调整"Cross-lateral players can only make corrections too late."Key word in the tennis section — linked to timing
short-sighted(figurative) lacking foresight; not thinking about long-term consequences目光短浅的"Ignoring the science is rather short-sighted."Note: this is a figurative use, not about eyesight here

🔄 Paraphrase Training

Part 4 multiple-choice questions rely heavily on paraphrase. The speaker's actual words are usually more casual/detailed than the formal wording of the question options.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingAnita first felt the article was of value when she realised the impressive size of his research project.
Audio wording"...what I soon became struck by was the sheer volume of both observation and investigation he had done in many different sports."
Key paraphraseimpressive size of research = sheer volume of observation and investigation

Paraphrase Example 2
Question wordingAnita feels findings on handedness will help sportspeople as they plan tactics for each game.
Audio wording"...they can help them identify the most suitable strategies to use in a given game."
Key paraphraseplan tactics for each game = identify the most suitable strategies to use in a given game
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question wordingAnita feels most sports coaches underestimate what science has to offer sport.
Audio wording"...at present they are rather reluctant to make use of the insights scientists like Matthews can give, which I think is rather short-sighted."
Key paraphraseunderestimate what science offers = reluctant to make use of scientific insights
