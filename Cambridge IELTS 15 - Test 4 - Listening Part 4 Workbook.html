📋 Quick Review

A lecture on the rise of the department store. It explains how new wealth, technology and sources of power from the Industrial Revolution allowed mass-produced goods (especially textiles) to be sold in large new shops, and how stores attracted customers through newspaper advertising, bright lighting and grand display windows. Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY (Q31–40).

💡 Listening tip: Part 4 flows in a clear order — causes (wealth, technology, power) → goods (textiles, machines) → marketing (newspapers, advertising) → store features (lighting, windows). Use those stages to keep your place and predict a noun.
