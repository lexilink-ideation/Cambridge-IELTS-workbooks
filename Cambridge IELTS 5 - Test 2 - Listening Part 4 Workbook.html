Listening Part 4

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 2 — Listening Part 4

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

An academic lecturer gives a talk introducing Antarctica: its extreme geography, the international research stations built there, what people eat and how much energy they need, the ongoing scientific research (especially into climate change), and the job opportunities available on the continent.

This is a single-speaker lecture with a wide range of topics — geography, daily life, science, and careers — covered in a logical order.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 has no break and 10 questions in a row, so it is easy to lose your place. Use the sub-headings on the question paper (e.g. "Geography," "Food," "Research") to predict topic changes, and listen for numbers and superlatives ("the highest," "the coldest") — they are often exactly what's tested.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / Phrase Simple Meaning 中文 Example Sentence IELTS Note
continent one of the world's large land masses 大陆；洲 "Antarctica is the coldest continent." Compare: country, region
precipitation rain, snow, etc. falling from the sky 降水 "Very low precipitation makes it a desert." Formal/academic word for "rainfall"
desert an area that receives very little rainfall 沙漠 "Most of Antarctica is classed as desert." Not always hot — deserts can be icy
accommodation a place where people live or stay 住宿 "Stations include accommodation and a gym." Uncountable noun — no plural "accommodations" in British English
stilts / legs supports that raise a structure off the ground 支柱；支腿 "Buildings are raised on stilts." Solves the problem of snow build-up
sledge a vehicle for travelling over snow or ice 雪橇 "Supplies were carried 15km by sledge." American English: sled
kilocalorie a unit for measuring the energy in food 千卡（热量单位） "They need 5,000 kilocalories a day." Same as "calorie" in everyday speech
freeze-dry to preserve food by freezing then removing its water 冷冻干燥 "Field rations are freeze-dried." Listen for the hyphenated compound word
ice-cap a permanent thick layer of ice covering land 冰盖 "Scientists measure changes in the ice-cap." Related to sea-level research
ozone layer the atmospheric layer that blocks harmful UV rays 臭氧层 "They monitor the hole in the ozone layer." Two-word compound noun
greenhouse gas a gas that traps heat in the atmosphere 温室气体 "Ice bubbles reveal past greenhouse gas levels." Linked to climate change topics
qualified having the right skills or training for a job 有资质的；合格的 "There are openings for qualified staff." Paraphrase: skilled, trained

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Why Antarctica counts as desert
Question wording Why is most of Antarctica classified as desert?
Audio meaning "Despite all that ice, hardly any rain or snow actually falls there, so officially most of the continent counts as desert."
Key paraphrase "hardly any rain or snow falls" = extremely low precipitation
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Dealing with snow build-up
Question wording How are modern research stations designed to deal with snow?
Audio meaning "The buildings are set up on legs that can be lifted, so they don't get buried as the snow piles up around them."
Key paraphrase "legs that can be lifted" = stilts that can be raised
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3 — High calorie needs
Question wording Why do workers in Antarctica need so many calories?
Audio meaning "Because of the cold and all the physical labour, someone working there needs around five thousand kilocalories every day — far more than usual."
Key paraphrase "far more than usual" = much higher than a normal diet
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4 — Ice-bubble research
Question wording What does the ice-bubble research measure?
Audio meaning "By analysing the air trapped inside bubbles in the ice, scientists can track greenhouse gases and pollution created by human activity."
Key paraphrase "track greenhouse gases and pollution" = measures effects of human activity
Your practice
