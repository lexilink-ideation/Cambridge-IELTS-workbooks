Cambridge IELTS 17 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A talk on staff turnover in hotels: why staff leave, the impact on managers, mistakes to avoid, and a benefit one hotel noticed (Q11–14, multiple choice). Then the speaker matches six named hotels to the method each used to reduce turnover (Q15–20).

💡 Listening tip: The matching task has only three options (A/B/C) reused across six hotels, so each answer is heard more than once. Note the one clear method for each hotel — teamwork, incentives, or career opportunities.
