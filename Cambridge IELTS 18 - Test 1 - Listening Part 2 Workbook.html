📋 Quick Review

A single speaker gives a talk about becoming a volunteer for ACE, a voluntary organisation that supports people in the local area. She covers who can volunteer, the training offered, two things applicants should consider, and then matches helpful qualities to five areas of voluntary work. Expect multiple choice (Q11–13), choose TWO (Q14/15) and matching (Q16–20).

💡 Listening tip: In matching tasks, the options (A–G) stay on the page but the speaker paraphrases each one. Underline a keyword in every option first, then listen for its synonym — e.g. 'fitness' → 'walk for long periods', 'good memory' → 'not forget any important steps'.
