📋 Quick Review

Greg introduces a museum tour. First he describes six collections and says one distinctive thing about each (Q11–16, choose from a box). Then he gives practical directions to the facilities in the basement (Q17–20, plan labelling).

“从选项框里选”+ 地下室平面图。每个收藏只有一个特征是被提到的。

💡 Listening tip: With six items and seven options, each collection has exactly one matching comment. Turn the options into keywords before you listen: one person / media / public gifts / artists' gifts / most popular / largest / moved. Then just listen for that keyword.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

For box-matching, the paraphrase is usually one short phrase. Learn to hear it fast.
Q11 — most popular
Option E
includes the most popular exhibits in the museum
What you hear
'It's the gallery that attracts the largest number of visitors, so it's best to go in early'
Key paraphrase
'attracts the largest number of visitors' = 'the most popular exhibits'
Your turn
Q13 — relocated
Option G
has had some of its contents relocated
What you hear
'a number of the sculptures have been moved to other parts of the museum'
Key paraphrase
'moved to other parts' = 'relocated'; note the gallery itself is closed, which is not an option
Your turn
Q15 — public gifts
Option C
includes some items given by members of the public
What you hear
'many of them were discovered by ordinary people digging their gardens, and donated to the museum'
Key paraphrase
'ordinary people … donated' = 'given by members of the public'
Your turn
Q16 — one person
Option A
was given by one person
What you hear
'All our porcelain and glass was left to the museum by its founder, when he died in 1878'
Key paraphrase
'left to the museum by its founder' = 'given by one person'
Your turn
Q20 — the cloakroom
Question
20 cloakroom

Key paraphrase
'before you come to the corridor' means you have not entered the corridor yet

🧠 Mini Dictation

Museum-tour chunks. The direction phrases are the ones to get letter-perfect.
1.It's the gallery that attracts the largest number of visitors.
2.Several of the artists each donated one work.
3.A number of the sculptures have been moved to other parts of the museum.
4.Many of them were discovered by ordinary people digging their gardens.
5.In the terms of his will, we're not allowed to add anything to that collection.
6.Go straight ahead to the far side of the sitting area, then turn right into the corridor.
7.The cafe is immediately on the right.
8.It's through the last door before you come to the corridor.
