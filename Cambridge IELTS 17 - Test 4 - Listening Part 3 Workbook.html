📋 Quick Review

Thomas and Jeanne discuss Thomas's school sports and a trip to Kenya (Q21–24, 'choose TWO'), then comment on the development of six items of sporting equipment (Q25–30, matching each item to a comment A–H).

💡 Listening tip: 'Choose TWO' questions reward careful elimination. And in the equipment matching, each item has one distinctive historical fact — listen for that single detail (mass-produced material, caused injuries, had to be replaced…).
