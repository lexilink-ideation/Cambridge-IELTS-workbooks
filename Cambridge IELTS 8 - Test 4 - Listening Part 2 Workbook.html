Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

On the radio show Your City Today, councillor Graham Campbell explains a plan to improve the fast-growing suburb of Red Hill. He describes residents' concerns (traffic and safety), the decision to bury the old power lines and who pays, then walks through a map of the changes — trees, wider footpaths, a coloured road surface, a new sign, traffic lights, artwork and a children’s playground. The tasks are multiple-choice plus map labelling.

💡 Listening strategy tip: For the map-labelling task, first find the fixed landmarks (the supermarket, Days Road, the street names) and the compass. Then follow the councillor’s location language ('just to the right of the supermarket', 'on the corner of…', 'half way down…') to place each labelled feature.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ11: Community groups are mainly concerned about …
What you hear / read"mainly worried about traffic … the increasing speed of cars near schools … a lot of children walk to school."
Key paraphraseConcern for walking children = pedestrian safety → answer A.

Key paraphrase'moved underground' = buried → answer B.

Paraphrase Example 3
