Cambridge IELTS 19 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Bella and Don, plan a presentation on recycling footwear. They discuss whether the topic has enough depth, trainers in school, why people recycle shoes, a confusing statistic, examples of shoes rejected for recycling, and a failed reuse project. Expect multiple choice (Q21–24, Q29–30) and a matching task (Q25–28).

💡 Listening tip: In discussion sections, note where the two speakers agree and where they disagree — questions often test exactly that (e.g. Q22 asks what they disagree about). For the matching task, each item of footwear has a specific fault.
