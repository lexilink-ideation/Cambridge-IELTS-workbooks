📋 Quick Review

A guide welcomes visitors to Riverside Industrial Village, a museum. First he gives a short history of why industry began here (water, raw materials and fuel). Then he describes a plan of the site, pointing out each building. Expect fuels, dates/numbers, and building names to label on a map.

💡 Listening tip: For a plan-labelling task, keep track of the speaker's position words — to our right, in front of us, top left corner, across the yard. Follow the route he takes, not the numbers on the page.
