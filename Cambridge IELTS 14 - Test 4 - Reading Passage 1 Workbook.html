📋 Passage Overview

The secret of staying young. The ant Pheidole dentata shows almost no signs of aging: old workers perform as well as young ones and their brains stay sharp. The passage reports Ysabel Giraldo's research comparing ants at four ages, measuring their behaviour and brain condition, and explains why social ants are useful for studying human aging.

💡 Reading tip: For notes completion, one word only — match the note to a paraphrase in the text. For True/False/Not Given, 'Not Given' means the text simply does not say, even if it sounds plausible.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Old worker ants can do everything just as well as the youngsters, and their brains appear just as sharp.
Main ClauseOld worker ants can do everything just as well as the youngsters, and their brains appear just as sharp
Subordinate / Relativejust as well as the youngsters (comparison of equality)
Grammar NoteTwo clauses joined by 'and'; 'just as … as' shows equality between old and young ants.
Simple MeaningOld worker ants perform as well as young ones, and their brains seem equally sharp.
中文年老的工蚁做任何事都和年轻工蚁一样出色，它们的大脑看起来也同样敏锐。
But the vast majority of animals deteriorate with age just like people do.
Main Clausethe vast majority of animals deteriorate with age
Subordinate / Relativejust like people do (comparison clause)
Grammar Note'just like … do' compares animals with people; 'But' contrasts with the age-defying ants.
Simple MeaningMost animals get worse with age, as people do.
中文但绝大多数动物都会像人一样随年龄增长而衰退。
It's this social complexity that makes P. dentata useful for studying aging in people.
Main ClauseIt's this social complexity that makes P. dentata useful for studying aging in people
Subordinate / Relative(cleft structure: It's X that …)
Grammar NoteA cleft sentence ('It's … that …') emphasises 'this social complexity' as the reason.
Simple MeaningIt is their complex social life that makes these ants useful for studying human aging.
中文正是这种社会复杂性，使得齿切叶蚁对研究人类衰老很有价值。
By contrast, most animal studies of aging use mice, worms or fruit flies, which all lead much more isolated lives.
Main Clausemost animal studies of aging use mice, worms or fruit flies
Subordinate / Relativewhich all lead much more isolated lives (non-defining relative)
Grammar Note'By contrast' signals a difference; the relative clause adds why they differ from ants.
Simple MeaningIn contrast, most aging studies use mice, worms or flies, which live far more alone.
中文相比之下，大多数衰老研究使用的是小鼠、蠕虫或果蝇，而它们的生活都要孤立得多。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Puzzle → investigation. The passage opens with a puzzle (ants that don't seem to age) and then reports the research designed to test it. Track the shift from claim to method.

Comparison as evidence. Naked mole rats and isolated lab animals (mice, worms, flies) are compared with social ants to explain why ants are useful models for human aging.

Careful with 'Not Given'. Statements like 'the ants behaved as she predicted' are FALSE, while 'bees used different methods' is NOT GIVEN — check exactly what the text claims.
