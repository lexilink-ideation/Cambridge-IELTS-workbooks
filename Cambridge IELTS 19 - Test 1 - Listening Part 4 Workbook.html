📋 Quick Review

A student gives a presentation on the Céide Fields in north-west Ireland, one of the largest Neolithic sites in the world. The talk covers how the site was discovered under a bog, how objects were preserved, how the Neolithic farmers lived, and why farming there eventually declined. This is note completion — ONE WORD ONLY for each answer.

💡 Listening tip: Part 4 is a continuous monologue with no second speaker to slow it down. Use the headings on the question paper (Discovery, Neolithic farmers, Reasons for decline) to keep your place, and listen for cause-and-effect signals like because, led to, as a result.

Question note
Items are well preserved because of a lack of ___.
What you hear

Key paraphrase
deficiency of oxygen = a lack of oxygen → oxygen
