📋 Quick Review

A radio interview with Alice Bussell from the Dolphin Conservation Trust. She explains what the charity does, why oil exploration in eastern Scotland threatens dolphins, how she became interested, and then describes four dolphins in the 'Adopt a Dolphin' scheme.

这是一段“电台采访 + 慈善机构介绍”。最后一部分是四只海豚的配对题,信息密集且顺序打乱,必须记住每只海豚的“人设”。

💡 Listening tip: In Q16–20 the same letter can be used more than once — and here D (Samson) is the answer twice. Never assume each option is used only once unless the instructions say so. Build a quick profile for each dolphin as you listen: Moondancer = elegant, large fin; Echo = unseen, noisy; Kiwi = youngest, photogenic, noisy; Samson = energetic, newest.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Radio interviews are full of hedged and negative statements. The exam options are always positive and simple, so you must convert. 采访中的说法往往含蓄或否定,而选项总是简单肯定,必须转换。
Q11/12 — 'helps finance campaigns'
Option C
It helps finance campaigns for changes in fishing practices
Audio wording
'The charity uses its money to support campaigns — for example, for changes in fishing policy and so forth'
Key paraphrase
finance = uses its money to support; practices = policy
Trap
'employs its first full-time biologist' is only a hope ('hopes soon to be able to').
Your turn
Q13 — 'better known'
Option B
It made the work of the trust better known
Audio wording
'it has made our activities even more widely publicised and understood'
Key paraphrase
better known = more widely publicised and understood
Trap
'it's not meant an enormous amount of money' rules out A; 'In the long term it may not bring in extra members' rules out C.
Your turn
Q14 — 'noise'
Option A
noise
Audio wording
'although there'll be little pollution from oil, exploration creates a lot of underwater noise. It means the dolphins can't rest and socialise.'
Key paraphrase
the effect is stated straight after the cause
Trap
Oil leaks (B) and ships (C) are both mentioned in the same passage — but as things that are NOT the problem.
Your turn
Q16 — 'has not been seen'
Comment
It has not been seen this year.
Audio wording
'Echo is being rather elusive this year and hasn't yet been sighted by our observers'
Key paraphrase
not been seen = elusive / not been sighted
Trap
'we remain optimistic that he'll be out there soon' — do not let the optimism confuse you.
Your turn
Q20 — 'an unusual shape'
Comment
It has an unusual shape.
Audio wording
'Moondancer's easy to find because she has a particularly large fin on her back'
Key paraphrase
unusual shape = a particularly large fin
Trap
Kiwi is also 'easy to pick out' — but by noise, not shape.
Your turn
