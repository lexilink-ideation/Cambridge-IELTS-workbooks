Cambridge IELTS 16 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

📋 Quick Review

A father phones Sarah at Children's Engineering Workshops to ask about holiday classes for his two daughters — Lydia (aged 4, the Tiny Engineers group) and Carly (aged 7, the Junior Engineers group). Sarah describes the activities, then gives the day, place and cost. Note completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: The notes are in order, and each gap needs a single 'thing', a day or a place. Predict the word type before each gap and listen for a spelled-out name (Fradstone) — spelling counts.

The notes paraphrase Sarah's words. Train the swap between the note and what you hear.
