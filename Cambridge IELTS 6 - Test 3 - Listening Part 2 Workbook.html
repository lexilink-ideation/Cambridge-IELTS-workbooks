Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 3 — Listening Part 2

Rosewood House & Gardens · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A tour guide welcomes a group of visitors to Rosewood House and Gardens, once home to the famous writer Sebastian George. The guide first tells the history of the house — when George bought it and what his life was like at the time — then gives a guided tour of the gardens using a map, and finally describes an optional riverside walk beyond the formal grounds.

This is a classic Part 2 monologue: one speaker giving information to a group, mixing multiple-choice history questions with a map-labelling exercise and sentence completion.

💡 Part 2 strategy: For the map questions (Q14–17), trace the route the guide describes — "follow the path," "round the first corner," "to your left" — and mark each location as it's mentioned, in order. For the multiple-choice questions (Q11–13), listen for the guide's exact reasoning, not just a matching keyword.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 6  |  🎧 Test: 3, Section 2  |  ❓ Questions: 11–20

📝 Task types: Multiple choice (Q11–13) · Map labelling (Q14–17) · Sentence completion (Q18–20)

🗣️ Setting: A guided tour of Rosewood House, once the home of writer Sebastian George

👥 Speaker: A single tour guide addressing visitors

Task breakdown

Q11–13: The history of the house — George's first sighting of it, his personal life, and his feelings about it.

Q14–17: A map of the gardens — matching Pear Alley, Mulberry Garden, the shop, and the tea room to lettered locations A–J.

Q18–20: The River Walk beyond the gardens — a field, a footbridge, and a viewpoint.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In Part 2, the guide's exact phrasing rarely matches the question wording. Study the paraphrase pattern in each card, then practise your own.
Q11 — George's First Reaction to the House
Question wordingWhen George first saw Rosewood House, he… (choose A, B or C)
Exact script line
GUIDE: …the owners let it out to a tenant because George was too slow making up his mind to buy it.
Key paraphrase"too slow making up his mind" = "was unsure whether to buy it" → Answer C
