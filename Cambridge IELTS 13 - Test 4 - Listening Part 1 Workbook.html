Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 13 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Martha Clines phones Alex, who completed a traineeship at JPNW in 2014, because she is applying for the same programme. He describes his own training, the diploma options, the other trainees, the benefits of the scheme, and finishes with three pieces of interview advice.

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. The last three notes begin with Don't wear…, Don't be…, Make sure you… — so Q8 needs a clothing noun, Q9 an adjective and Q10 a verb. Predicting word class before you listen is the whole battle here.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The last three answers are hidden inside positive sentences that the note turns into negatives. Practise the flip.
Q2 — the missing qualification
Note wording
Alex didn't have a qualification from school in ___
What you hear
'I didn't do well in my exams at school and I was really worried because I failed Maths'
Key paraphrase
failed = no qualification in → Maths
Your turn
Q3 — the better diploma
Note wording
Alex thinks he should have done the diploma in ___ skills
What you hear
'I took the one on IT skills but I wish I'd done that one instead' (business)
Key paraphrase
'I wish I'd done that one' refers to Martha's choice → business, not IT
Your turn
Q8 — clothing advice
Note wording
Don't wear ___
What you hear
'Nothing too casual — like jeans, for example.'
Key paraphrase
'too casual, like jeans' → the specific item is jeans
Your turn
Q9 — timing advice
Note wording
Don't be ___
What you hear
'arrive in plenty of time. They hate people who are late.'
Key paraphrase
arrive in plenty of time = don't be late
Your turn
