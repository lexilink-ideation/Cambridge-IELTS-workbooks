📋 Quick Review

A museum representative gives a short talk to teachers about bringing school groups to the Dinosaur Museum. The speaker explains opening hours, where groups are met, how long a visit takes, what students may and may not bring inside, and which activities are available after the tour. It is a one-speaker (monologue) information talk.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 2 there is only one speaker, so you can't rely on a question-and-answer rhythm. Use the headings and question order to keep your place, and listen for signposting words like 'firstly', 'as far as … goes', 'finally'. Watch for 'do' vs 'don't' — the speaker often says what students may NOT bring.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ13: School groups are met by tour guides in the …
What you hear / read"We ask you to remain with your group in the car park. One or more of the tour guides will welcome you there."
Key paraphrase'met by guides' = guides 'welcome you' — location is the car park.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptQ14: The visit includes ___ minutes for the guided tour.
What you hear / read"15 minutes to get on and off the coach, 45 minutes for the guided tour and 30 minutes for after-tour activities."
Key paraphraseFilter the three numbers — only the tour figure (45) is asked for.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptQ16–18: Which THREE things can students have with them?
What you hear / read"handouts with questions and quizzes … they'll need to bring something to write with … we do allow students to take photographs."
Key paraphraseworksheets + pens + cameras → C, F, G.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4
Question / promptQ19–20: Which TWO activities can students do after the tour AT PRESENT?
What you hear / read"screenings of short documentaries … the activity room … is closed at the moment … an IT centre where students have access to … dinosaur games."
Key paraphrase'at present' rules out the closed activity room → watch films (B) + computer games (E).
