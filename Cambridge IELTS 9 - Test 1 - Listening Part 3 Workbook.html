Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A teacher interviews two international students, Spiros (Greek, marketing) and Hiroko (Japanese, engineering), about how useful their English for Academic Purposes course was. They compare their experiences of presentations, tutorials and reading. All ten questions are multiple choice, A/B/C.

💡 Listening tip: In Part 3 the danger is attribution — who said what. Draw two columns (Spiros / Hiroko) on your question paper before the recording starts. Also listen for concession signals: 'although…', 'that's true, but…', 'yes, but psychologically speaking…'. The real answer usually comes AFTER the 'but'.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Multiple-choice options are formal summaries; the speakers use natural spoken English. Bridge the gap.
Q21 — Why Spiros felt happy
Option B
his style was good
What you hear
'in relation to some of the other people in my group, I did quite a good job because my overall style was quite professional'
Key paraphrase
'my overall style was quite professional' = his style was good
Your turn
Q23 — Hiroko's feeling afterwards
Option B
dissatisfied
What you hear
'I didn't feel any real sense of satisfaction. I didn't feel positive about the experience at all.'
Key paraphrase
'no sense of satisfaction' + 'not positive at all' = dissatisfied (NOT 'embarrassed' — she never mentions shame)
Your turn
Q26 — Why tutorials feel better now
Option B
she is making more of a contribution
What you hear
'I've been trying to speak up more and I just jump in… then they actually asked for my opinion'
Key paraphrase
'speak up more / jump in' = making more of a contribution
Your turn
Q28 — Spiros's reading skills
Option B
it still takes him a long time to read
What you hear
'My reading speed is still quite slow, though I'm much better at dealing with vocabulary than I used to be.'
Key paraphrase
'reading speed is still quite slow' = still takes a long time; 'though' cancels option C
Your turn
