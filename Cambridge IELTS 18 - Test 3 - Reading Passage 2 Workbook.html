📋 Passage Overview

The steam car. This passage tells the story of the Doble brothers and their attempts to perfect the steam car in the early 20th century — the rise of the internal combustion engine, the acclaimed Model B, commercial orders for the Model C, disappointed customers, and the remarkable but costly Model E. Question types: matching headings, multiple choice and summary completion.

💡 Reading tip: For matching headings, read the whole paragraph and find its main idea before you choose. Headings often paraphrase a turning point — 'commercial orders', 'a disappointing outcome', 'a period in cold conditions'.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Steam had already enjoyed a long and successful career in the railways, so it was only natural that the technology evolved into a miniaturized version which was separate from the trains.
Main ClauseIt was only natural that the technology evolved into a miniaturized version.
Subordinate / Relative'Steam had already enjoyed a long and successful career in the railways' (reason) + 'which was separate from the trains' (relative).
Grammar Note'so' links cause and effect; 'it was natural that…' is an extraposed subject.
Simple MeaningSince steam already worked well on railways, it naturally shrank into a car-sized version.
中文蒸汽动力在铁路上早已取得长期成功，因此这种技术自然而然演变成与火车分离的微缩版本。
At first, these noisy gasoline cars were unpopular because they were more complicated to operate and they had difficult hand-crank starters, which were known to break arms when the engines backfired.
Main ClauseThese noisy gasoline cars were unpopular.
Subordinate / Relative'because they were more complicated to operate and they had difficult hand-crank starters' (reason) + 'which were known to break arms when the engines backfired' (relative + time clause).
Grammar Note'because' gives two reasons; the relative adds a vivid detail.
Simple MeaningEarly petrol cars were disliked: hard to use, with crank starters that could break your arm.
中文起初，这些嘈杂的汽油车并不受欢迎，因为它们操作更复杂，而且有难用的手摇起动器——发动机回火时甚至会打断手臂。
By the following April, the General Engineering Company had received 5,390 deposits for Doble Detroits, which were scheduled for delivery in early 1918.
Main ClauseThe company had received 5,390 deposits for Doble Detroits.
Subordinate / Relative'which were scheduled for delivery in early 1918' (relative clause).
Grammar NotePast perfect 'had received' sets the earlier time; the relative adds the delivery plan.
Simple MeaningBy that April the company had 5,390 orders for cars meant to arrive in early 1918.
中文到次年四月，通用工程公司已收到5,390份Doble Detroit汽车的订金，这些车计划于1918年初交付。
For his whole career, until his death in 1961, Abner Doble remained adamant that steam-powered automobiles were at least equal to gasoline cars, if not superior.
Main ClauseAbner Doble remained adamant that steam-powered automobiles were at least equal to gasoline cars.
Subordinate / Relative'until his death in 1961' (time phrase) + 'if not superior' (elliptical addition).
Grammar Note'at least equal…, if not superior' upgrades the claim.
Simple MeaningAll his life Doble firmly believed steam cars were as good as — or better than — petrol cars.
中文直到1961年去世，阿布纳·多布尔一生都坚信蒸汽动力汽车至少与汽油车不相上下，甚至更优越。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology: the passage moves through the models in order (first car → Model B → Model C → Model E), which is how the headings (Q14–20) track the story.

Contrast: 'in contrast to clattering gas engines' and 'Its success was short-lived' — the writer repeatedly weighs steam against petrol (Q21).

Cause & effect: the electric starter (1912) made petrol cars easy to start, so steam was phased out — the turning point behind heading viii (Q15).

Evaluation: the final paragraph judges the Model E — costly and inconsistent, yet clean enough to pass modern laws — supporting heading iv, 'a possible solution to the issues of today' (Q20).
