📋 Quick Review

Section 3 is an interview between a radio/TV interviewer and Dr Martin Merrywhether, a guest from the Antarctic Centre in Christchurch, New Zealand. They discuss why the Centre is located in Christchurch, its two main roles (scientific base and information centre), what Antarctica itself is like, and finally the history and achievements of the Antarctic Treaty.

This is typical IELTS Listening Part 3 content: an academic-style discussion between two (or more) speakers, testing your ability to follow an interview and pick out specific facts, reasons, and opinions.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 3 interviews, the interviewer's questions act as signposts — each new question usually introduces a new group of answers. Notice reason-giving language ("because", "the result of", "since") for multiple-choice questions about causes, and listen for exact dates/numbers for table completion.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

Section 3 multiple-choice questions almost always paraphrase the interview's actual words. Study these examples carefully.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingThe Antarctic Centre was established in Christchurch because... (Christchurch is geographically well positioned)
Audio wording"New Zealand is the second closest country to Antarctica and Christchurch is often used on Antarctic expeditions."
Key paraphrasegeographically well positioned = second closest country / used on expeditions
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question wordingThe purpose of the Visitors' Centre is to show people what Antarctica is like.
Audio wording"...the visitors' centre aims to recreate the atmosphere of Antarctica."
Key paraphraseshow people what X is like = recreate the atmosphere of X
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question wordingAntarctica is very cold because of the shape of the continent.
Audio wording"Antarctica is a land mass shaped like a dome, with the result that the winds blow down the slopes at speeds of up to 150 km an hour."
Key paraphraseshape of the continent = land mass shaped like a dome
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4
Question wordingDr Merrywhether thinks Antarctica was part of another continent because there is geological evidence of this.
Audio wording"...because fossils and rocks have been discovered in Antarctica which are the same as those found in places such as Africa and Australia."
Key paraphrasegeological evidence = fossils and rocks discovered that match other continents
