📋 Passage Overview

Plant 'thermometer' triggers springtime growth. Cambridge scientists found that phytochrome molecules, used to sense light by day, act as thermometers at night — helping plants read seasonal temperature and possibly helping breed climate-resilient crops. Question types: True / False / Not Given (Q27–32), matching information (Q33–37) and sentence completion (Q38–40).

💡 Reading tip: Science passages pack the key mechanism into one or two paragraphs. Once you understand 'dark reversion' (slow when cold, fast when warm), several answers become obvious.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Researchers have revealed that molecules called phytochromes — used by plants to detect light during the day — actually change their function in darkness to become cellular temperature gauges.
Main ClauseResearchers have revealed that molecules… change their function in darkness
Subordinate / Relativethat molecules called phytochromes change their function… (noun clause); used by plants to detect light during the day (reduced relative)
Grammar NoteReported discovery + an inserted reduced relative (dashes) adding the day-time role.
Simple MeaningScientists found that phytochromes, which sense light by day, become thermometers in the dark.
中文研究者发现，被植物用于白天感光的光敏色素分子，在黑暗中会改变功能，成为细胞温度计。
The lower the temperature, the slower the rate at which phytochromes revert to inactivity, so the molecules spend more time in their active, growth-suppressing state.
Main Clausethe molecules spend more time in their active, growth-suppressing state
Subordinate / RelativeThe lower the temperature, the slower the rate… (correlative comparison); at which phytochromes revert to inactivity (relative)
Grammar Note'The + comparative…, the + comparative…' links two variables; 'so' gives the result.
Simple MeaningWhen it's colder, the molecules stay active longer, holding back growth.
中文温度越低，光敏色素回复失活的速度越慢，分子因此更长时间处于抑制生长的活跃状态。
If a plant finds itself in shade, phytochromes are quickly inactivated, enabling it to grow faster to find sunlight again.
Main Clausephytochromes are quickly inactivated
Subordinate / RelativeIf a plant finds itself in shade (conditional); enabling it to grow faster to find sunlight again (result participle)
Grammar NoteConditional + 'enabling… to' result phrase.
Simple MeaningIn shade, phytochromes switch off, so the plant grows faster toward the light.
中文若植物处于阴影中，光敏色素会迅速失活，使其更快生长以重新找到阳光。
The work was done in a model system, using a mustard plant called Arabidopsis, but Wigge says the phytochrome genes necessary for temperature sensing are found in crop plants as well.
Main ClauseThe work was done in a model system
Subordinate / Relativeusing a mustard plant called Arabidopsis (participle); but… the phytochrome genes… are found in crop plants as well (contrast clause)
Grammar NoteParticiple phrase names the plant; 'but' extends the finding to crops.
Simple MeaningThey studied a mustard plant, but the same genes exist in crops too.
中文研究是在以拟南芥（一种芥属植物）为模型的系统中完成的，但维格表示，感温所需的光敏色素基因在作物中同样存在。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Mechanism (day vs night): by day, light rapidly switches phytochromes (fast, <1 second); by night, they change slowly ('dark reversion') at a rate set by temperature. TFNG Q32 tests this day/night difference.

Cause → effect (season): cold night → slow reversion → more growth suppression → slower winter growth; warm night → fast reversion → growth resumes. This chain drives the whole passage.

Tradition meets science: the old rhyme 'oak before ash… ash before oak…' is explained by oak relying on temperature and ash on day length. Matching answers reward spotting where folklore is scientifically supported.
