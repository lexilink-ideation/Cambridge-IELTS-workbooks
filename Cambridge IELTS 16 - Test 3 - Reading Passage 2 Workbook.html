📋 Passage Overview

Climate change reveals ancient artefacts in Norway's glaciers. As mountain ice melts, it exposes objects preserved for thousands of years, giving archaeologists a race against time and new insights into ancient trade and hunting. Question types: matching information to sections (Q14–19), summary completion (Q20–22) and choose TWO (Q23–26).

💡 Reading tip: For 'which section contains…', scan for the specific idea (e.g. 'physical difficulty', 'future discoveries') rather than reading every section fully — the detail is usually in just one place.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

As the ice has vanished, it has been giving up the treasures it has preserved in cold storage for the last 6,000 years.
Main Clauseit has been giving up the treasures
Subordinate / RelativeAs the ice has vanished (time/cause); it has preserved in cold storage… (relative)
Grammar Note'As' clause + present perfect continuous shows an ongoing process.
Simple MeaningAs the ice melts, it releases objects it kept frozen for 6,000 years.
中文随着冰层消融，它释放出保存于寒冷环境中长达6000年的珍宝。
This is because unless they're protected from the microorganisms that cause decay, they tend not to last long.
Main Clausethey tend not to last long
Subordinate / RelativeThis is because… (reason); unless they're protected from the microorganisms (conditional); that cause decay (relative)
Grammar Note'unless' = 'if not'; nested relative defines the microorganisms.
Simple MeaningWithout protection from decay-causing microbes, these materials rot quickly.
中文这是因为，除非受到保护、免于导致腐烂的微生物侵害，否则它们通常撑不了多久。
With climate change shrinking ice cover around the world, glacial archaeologists need to race the clock to find newly revealed artefacts, preserve them, and study them.
Main Clauseglacial archaeologists need to race the clock
Subordinate / RelativeWith climate change shrinking ice cover… (absolute phrase); to find… preserve… and study them (purpose)
Grammar Note'With + noun + -ing' sets the situation; three parallel infinitives give the goal.
Simple MeaningAs ice shrinks, archaeologists must hurry to find, save and study the finds.
中文随着气候变化使全球冰盖缩小，冰川考古学家必须争分夺秒地寻找、保存并研究新暴露的文物。
The people who used these mountains left things behind, and it is those objects that are of interest to archaeologists.
Main Clauseit is those objects that are of interest to archaeologists
Subordinate / RelativeThe people who used these mountains left things behind (clause 1); that are of interest… (cleft relative)
Grammar NoteCleft 'it is… that…' emphasises the objects.
Simple MeaningAncient mountain users dropped things, and those items interest archaeologists.
中文使用这些山地的人留下了物品，正是这些物品引起了考古学家的兴趣。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause → effect (preservation): extreme cold blocks decay → organic artefacts survive; once thawed → they decay fast. This pairing underlies both the summary and the 'race the clock' idea.

Why hunters came: reindeer gathered in the mountains in summer to escape biting insects on lower ground → hunters followed → they dropped equipment. Matching-section answers often hinge on this reasoning.

Trade context (Viking Age): growing demand for goods meant hunters benefited, and Vikings used routes other than ships. The 'choose TWO' items test these specific claims.
