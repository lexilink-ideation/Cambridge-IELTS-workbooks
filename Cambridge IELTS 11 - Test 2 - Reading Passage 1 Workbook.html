📋 Passage Overview

'Raising the Mary Rose' tells how a sixteenth-century English warship, sunk in the Solent in 1545, was rediscovered and finally lifted from the seabed in 1982. The passage is largely a chronology (1545 → 1836 → 1840 → 1965 → 1967 → 1971 → 1979 → 1982) followed by a detailed three-stage description of the lifting operation.

题型:T/F/NG Q1–4 + 事件配年份 Q5–8 + 示意图标注 Q9–13。

💡 Reading tip: For date-matching (Q5–8), do not read the whole passage — scan for the numbers. Every date in the List of Dates appears in the text, and the sentence containing it holds the answer.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Accounts of what happened to the ship vary: while witnesses agree that she was not hit by the French, some maintain that she was outdated, overladen and sailing too low in the water, others that she was mishandled by undisciplined crew.
Main ClauseAccounts of what happened to the ship vary
Subordinate / Relativewhile witnesses agree that she was not hit by the French (concession); 'some maintain that…, others (maintain) that…' = two contrasting reports with ellipsis
Grammar NoteColon introduces the explanation; 'others that…' leaves out the repeated verb 'maintain'.
Simple MeaningPeople disagree about why she sank: some blame the ship's condition, others the crew.
中文关于这艘船遭遇的说法不一:目击者一致认为她并未被法军击中,但有人坚称她已过时、超载、吃水过低,另一些人则认为是缺乏纪律的船员操作不当。
The hull (the body of the ship) acted as a trap for the sand and mud carried by Solent currents.
Main ClauseThe hull acted as a trap for the sand and mud
Subordinate / Relative'(the body of the ship)' = bracketed definition; 'carried by Solent currents' = reduced relative ('that was carried')
Grammar NoteBrackets give a definition; a past participle reduces a passive relative clause.
Simple MeaningThe ship's body caught the sand and mud that the currents brought.
中文船体(即船的主体)起到了捕集索伦特海峡水流所携带泥沙的作用。
As a result, the starboard side filled rapidly, leaving the exposed port side to be eroded by marine organisms and mechanical degradation.
Main Clausethe starboard side filled rapidly
Subordinate / Relative'As a result' = result connector; 'leaving the exposed port side to be eroded…' = participle clause of consequence
Grammar Note'leaving + object + to be + past participle' expresses an unintended consequence.
Simple MeaningSo the right side filled with mud quickly, while the exposed left side was worn away.
中文结果,右舷迅速被填满,而暴露在外的左舷则被海洋生物和机械作用侵蚀。
The problem of the hull being sucked back downwards into the mud was overcome by using 12 hydraulic jacks.
Main ClauseThe problem … was overcome by using 12 hydraulic jacks
Subordinate / Relative'of the hull being sucked back downwards into the mud' = gerund phrase with its own subject ('the hull')
Grammar Note'the problem of + noun + being + past participle' — a compressed passive gerund. Very common in technical writing.
Simple MeaningTwelve hydraulic jacks stopped the hull from being pulled back down into the mud.
中文船体被重新吸回泥中的问题,通过使用12个液压千斤顶得以解决。
It was only when the hull was hanging freely from the lifting frame, clear of the seabed and the suction effect of the surrounding mud, that the salvage operation progressed to the second stage.
Main Clausethe salvage operation progressed to the second stage
Subordinate / Relative'It was only when … that …' = cleft structure for emphasis; 'clear of the seabed and the suction effect…' = adjective phrase describing the hull
Grammar NoteCleft sentence ('It was only when X that Y') puts strong emphasis on the condition.
Simple MeaningStage two began only after the hull hung free, away from the seabed and the mud's suction.
中文只有当船体从起吊框架上自由悬挂、脱离海床和周围泥浆的吸力时,打捞作业才进入第二阶段。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology. The passage is organised strictly by time. Each paragraph opens with a time marker: 'On 19 July 1545', 'During the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries', 'Then, on 16 June 1836', 'But in 1965', 'In 1967'. Use these as signposts.

Cause and effect. The ship sank on her starboard side → the hull trapped mud → the starboard side filled and was protected → the port side was exposed and eroded → nearly all the starboard half survived. Q3 tests one link in this chain.

Problem → solution (technical). Problem: the hull would be sucked back into the mud. Solution: 12 hydraulic jacks. Problem: the hull was an open shell. Solution: lift in three distinct stages. Diagram labels come from these solutions.

Contrast between 'agreed' and 'disputed'. 'Accounts vary … witnesses agree … some maintain … others … What is undisputed, however, is that…' Q1 depends entirely on recognising this contrast.

Process order. Stage 1: attach to lifting frame with bolts and wires, raise with jacks. Stage 2: crane lifts the frame; hull transferred into the lifting cradle via stabbing guides; air bags cushion it. Stage 3: lift the whole structure into the air.
