📋 Quick Review

A man is carrying out a transport survey and interviews a woman, Sadie Jones, about how she travels around the city. He collects her personal details, then asks about her bus journey today, her use of a car, and why she does not cycle. Expect a postcode, a date, a spelled street name and several everyday nouns. It is note completion: ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER.

💡 Listening tip: Section 1 always has personal details — practise catching letters, numbers and dates fast. Postcodes mix letters and numbers (DW30 7YZ); a date needs the day and month; and a spelled name (C-L-A-X-B-Y) must be written exactly as spelt.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The question note is short; the speaker says the same idea in a longer, everyday way.
Reason for the trip (Q3)
Question note
Reason for trip: shopping and visit to the ___
What you hear
'I did some shopping but the main reason I came here was to go to the dentist'
Key paraphrase
'go to the dentist' → the noun needed is dentist
Your turn
Why she takes the bus (Q4)
Question note
Travelled by bus because cost of ___ too high
What you hear
'I stopped driving in ages ago because parking was so difficult to find and it costs so much'
Key paraphrase
'costs so much' = cost too high; the noun is parking
Your turn
