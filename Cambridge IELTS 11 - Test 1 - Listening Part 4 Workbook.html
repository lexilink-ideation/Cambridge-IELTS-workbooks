📋 Quick Review

A student lecture on ocean biodiversity. The speaker explains what 'biodiversity hotspots' are, then reviews four pieces of research — Boris Worm (2005), Lisa Ballance (2007), the Census of Marine Life, and the Global Marine Species Assessment — and ends with recommendations for protecting ocean species.

Part 4 是学术讲座,笔记按“研究者 + 年份”分块,顺序非常清楚。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY — no numbers, no two-word answers. The notes follow the lecture exactly, and each researcher's name is a signpost. When you hear a name, look at the next gap.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 4 notes compress long sentences into short phrases. Practise expanding the note back into the lecture sentence.
Q31 — conservation
Question note
important for locating targets for ___
What you hear
'they allow us to locate key areas for focusing efforts at conservation'
Key paraphrase
'key areas for focusing efforts at X' = 'targets for X' → conservation
Your turn
Q32 — food
Question note
were not always rich in ___
What you hear
'the main thing he'd expected to find was that they had very high concentrations of food, but to his surprise that was only true for four of the hotspots'
Key paraphrase
'only true for four of the five' = 'not always' → food
Your turn
Q34 — oxygen
Question note
had sufficient ___ in the water
What you hear
'the water needed to have enough oxygen in it'
Key paraphrase
'enough' = 'sufficient' → oxygen
Your turn
Q37 — decline
Question note
rate of ___
What you hear
'they calculate the rate at which the decline of the species is happening'
Key paraphrase
the note simply shortens the clause → decline
Your turn
Q40 — consumption
Question note
catch fish only for the purpose of ___
What you hear
'if these commercial fishing boats used equipment which was more selective, so that only the fish wanted for consumption were caught'
Key paraphrase
'only the fish wanted for consumption' = 'only for the purpose of consumption'
Your turn
