📋 Quick Review

This passage is about the saiga, a strange-looking antelope of the Central Asian steppes. It describes the saiga’s remarkable adaptations, the many threats it faces (poaching, habitat loss, disease, climate change), and the recent conservation efforts — led by the Altyn Dala Conservation Initiative — that have helped the population recover.

💡 Reading strategy tip: For note completion, copy the exact word from the passage (singular/plural must match). For True/False/Not Given, use FALSE only when the text clearly says the opposite, and NOT GIVEN when a detail (like "more than other regions") is simply never stated.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"One such adaptation is its bizarre bulbous nose, which enables the animal to survive the extreme seasonal temperature swings of the region."
Main ClauseOne such adaptation is its bizarre bulbous nose
Subordinate / Relative Clausewhich enables the animal to survive the extreme seasonal temperature swings (relative clause)
Grammar Note"which enables … to survive" adds information about the nose. "enable somebody to do" = make it possible.
Simple MeaningIts odd swollen nose lets the saiga survive the region’s extreme changes in temperature.
中文其中一项适应是它奇特的球状鼻子，使这种动物能够熬过该地区极端的季节性温差。
"In the ten years following the break-up of the former Soviet Union in 1991, over 95% of the global population was lost — one of the fastest examples of species loss ever recorded for a mammal."
Main Clauseover 95% of the global population was lost
Subordinate / Relative ClauseIn the ten years following the break-up of the former Soviet Union in 1991 (time phrase) + appositive "one of the fastest examples …"
Grammar Note"following …" = after. The dash introduces an appositive that comments on the whole event. Passive "was lost".
Simple MeaningIn the ten years after the USSR collapsed in 1991, more than 95% of all saiga died — one of the fastest mammal losses ever.
中文在1991年苏联解体后的十年里，全球95%以上的种群消失——这是有记录以来哺乳动物中最快的物种损失之一。
"Poaching reached epidemic levels after misguided conservationists tried to relieve the pressure on threatened African rhinos by actively encouraging the use of saiga horns in traditional medicine as an alternative to those of rhinos."
Main ClausePoaching reached epidemic levels
Subordinate / Relative Clauseafter misguided conservationists tried to relieve the pressure … by … encouraging the use of saiga horns …
Grammar Note"after …" gives the cause/time. "by …ing" states the method. "as an alternative to" = instead of.
Simple MeaningPoaching exploded after well-meaning but mistaken conservationists promoted saiga horn as a substitute for rhino horn.
中文在一些好心却误入歧途的保护者为减轻濒危非洲犀牛的压力、积极鼓励用赛加羚羊角替代犀牛角入药之后，偷猎达到了泛滥的程度。
"In 2017, 60% of the Mongolian saiga population — a subspecies found nowhere else in the world — was killed by a virus that spilled over from livestock."
Main Clause60% of the Mongolian saiga population … was killed by a virus
Subordinate / Relative Clausea subspecies found nowhere else in the world (appositive) + that spilled over from livestock (relative)
Grammar NoteThe dashes enclose an appositive describing the population. "that spilled over from" = which passed across from.
Simple MeaningIn 2017 a virus that jumped from farm animals killed 60% of Mongolia’s saiga, a subspecies found nowhere else.
中文2017年，一种从牲畜传播过来的病毒杀死了60%的蒙古赛加羚羊——这是世界上别处再无的一个亚种。
"But thanks to the intervention of the Altyn Dala Conservation Initiative, the most recent episodes in the ongoing story of the saiga have been relatively uplifting."
Main Clausethe most recent episodes … have been relatively uplifting
Subordinate / Relative Clausethanks to the intervention of the Altyn Dala Conservation Initiative (reason phrase)
Grammar Note"thanks to" = because of (positive). "the ongoing story" describes something still continuing.
Simple MeaningBecause of the Altyn Dala project’s action, the latest news about the saiga has been fairly encouraging.
中文但由于阿尔滕达拉保护倡议的介入，赛加羚羊这一持续故事的最新篇章相对令人振奋。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Track how threats and recovery are argued in the passage.

Cause & Effect

Each threat is a cause with an effect: poaching → population crash; barriers → herds trapped and starve; a bacterium/virus → mass mortality; warming → streams dry up. Follow each chain.

Contrast

The passage swings between decline and recovery: "numbers steadily recovered … But the respite was only temporary". Signal words: but, despite, although, however.

Chronology

Dates anchor the story: 19th c. (hunters) → 1991 (collapse begins) → 2000 (all-time low 21,000) → 2015/2017 (mass mortality) → recent surveys (1.9 million). Use dates to locate answers fast.

Writer’s Tone

The ending is cautiously hopeful — "critically endangered, but the direction of travel is positive." Note the balance for attitude questions.
