📋 Quick Review

A lecture on nanotechnology — science and engineering at the scale of atoms and molecules. The first section discusses public fears and how the technology should be safeguarded; the second section lists possible uses in transport, technology, the environment and health and medicine.

这是一篇科技类讲座。前3题是选择题(涉及观点与态度),后7题是笔记填空(ONE WORD ONLY),按四个小标题依次出现。

💡 Listening tip: Q33 asks for the speaker's opinion. Listen for first-person opinion markers: 'But as far as I'm concerned there's a need to plough ahead…' — everything before that is other people's views. Q34–40 are ONE WORD ONLY and all are common nouns or adjectives.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

This lecture states its ideas twice — once in technical language, once in everyday language. The exam notes usually take the everyday version. 讲座常把同一观点说两遍,笔记通常取通俗版。
Q31 — 'misunderstood by the public'
Option C
it is misunderstood by the public
Audio wording
'society often gets the wrong idea about its capabilities. Numerous science-fiction books and movies have raised people's fears…'
Key paraphrase
misunderstood = gets the wrong idea; the public = society
Trap
'take over the world' (option A) and 'monitor everything you do' (option B) are both said — but as examples of the misunderstanding, not as real problems.
Your turn
Q33 — 'ought to be continued'
Option C
ought to be continued
Audio wording
'But as far as I'm concerned there's a need to plough ahead with the discoveries and applications of nanotechnology.'
Key paraphrase
ought to be continued = there's a need to plough ahead
Trap
'unethical' appears in the very next sentence — but the speaker says scientists want to guard against unethical uses.
Your turn
Q35 — 'space travel'
Note wording
___ travel will be made available to the masses
Audio wording
'dramatically reduce the cost of travelling into space making it more accessible to ordinary people'
Key paraphrase
available to the masses = accessible to ordinary people
Trap
'opening up a totally new holiday destination' comes next and tempts you to write 'holiday'.
Your turn
Q37 — 'solar energy'
Note wording
___ energy will become more affordable
Audio wording
'The cost of solar cells will be drastically reduced so harnessing this energy will be far more economical'
Key paraphrase
more affordable = far more economical; the gap word comes from 'solar cells'
Trap
'revolutionise the way that we generate power' comes first — 'power' does not fit the gap.
Your turn
Q40 — 'medical tests'
Note wording
Analysis of medical ___ will be speeded up
Audio wording
'tiny biosensors are developed to analyse tests in minutes rather than days'
Key paraphrase
speeded up = in minutes rather than days
Trap
'How we detect disease will change' comes first — 'disease' does not fit 'Analysis of medical ___'.
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Write out each chunk. Science-lecture vocabulary is very useful for Writing Task 2 technology topics. 科技类词汇写作也常用。
1.Nano means tiny, so it's science and engineering on the scale of atoms and molecules.
2.Society often gets the wrong idea about its capabilities.
3.As far as I'm concerned, there's a need to plough ahead with the discoveries and applications.
4.These could be virtually unbreakable, lighter and much more pliable.
5.Those same improved capabilities will dramatically reduce the cost of travelling into space.
6.The cost of solar cells will be drastically reduced.
7.Nanotechnology will allow us to remove oil and other contaminants from the water.
8.Tiny biosensors are developed to analyse tests in minutes rather than days.
