Cambridge IELTS 16 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Jake and Amy, plan a report on city bike-sharing schemes. They agree the most important benefits (Q21&22) and what makes schemes successful (Q23&24), then give opinions on schemes in six cities (Q25–30, matching).

💡 Listening tip: For the city-matching task, listen for the speakers' shared opinion, not one person's aside. Words like 'surprising', 'shame', 'not enough spent' point to the matching letter.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Options reword the discussion. Match the shared opinion, not the words.
A key benefit (Q22)
Option C
improving air quality
What you hear
'the impact of reduced emissions on air pollution has a more significant effect on people's health'
Key paraphrase
'reduced emissions / less air pollution' = improving air quality
Your turn
New York (Q29)
Option B
they think it should be cheaper
What you hear
'it was more than ten times the price of most other schemes… more than a taxi'
Key paraphrase
far too expensive = it should be cheaper
Your turn
Sydney (Q30)
Option A
they agree it has been disappointing
What you hear
'I can't quite work out why it hasn't been an instant success… It's a shame really'
Key paraphrase
'a shame / not a success' = disappointing
Your turn
