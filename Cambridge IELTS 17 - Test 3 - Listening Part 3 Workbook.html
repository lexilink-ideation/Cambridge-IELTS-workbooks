📋 Quick Review

Holly discusses her work-placement at Orion Stadium with her tutor Dr Green (Q21–24, multiple choice), then the two agree on the most important aspect of six events-management skills (Q25–30, matching skills to aspects A–H).

💡 Listening tip: In the matching task, the two speakers must agree. Listen past the first suggestion to the point they both settle on — that agreed idea is the answer, not the idea one of them rejects.
