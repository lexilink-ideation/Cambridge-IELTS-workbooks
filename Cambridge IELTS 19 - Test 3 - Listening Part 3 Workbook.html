Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 19 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two trainee teachers, Clare and Jake, discuss a science experiment Clare must design for her Year 12 class. They consider a diet study, switch to using mice, and then plan the procedure step by step. Expect multiple choice (Q21–25) and a flowchart-completion task (Q26–30).

💡 Listening tip: For the flowchart, follow the steps in order — the answers come as the two students describe the method. Listen for the reason behind each step (why mark the mice, why a weighing chamber) to pick the right word from the box.
