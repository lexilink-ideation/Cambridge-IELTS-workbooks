📋 Quick Review

A visitor, Louise Taylor (the woman), goes to a police station to report a theft to an officer (the man). She gives her personal details, describes what was stolen from her backpack, and explains where and when it may have happened. Expect personal information (nationality, address), a sum of money, a date, and a long reference number.

💡 Listening tip: This is form/note completion, so answers come in the order you hear them. Predict the word type before each gap — is it a place, a number, or a date? The instruction is ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER, so never write two words.
