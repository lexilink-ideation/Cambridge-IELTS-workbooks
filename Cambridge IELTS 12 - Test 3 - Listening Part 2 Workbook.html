📋 Quick Review

A training-day talk at BC Travel about specialised activity holidays. The speaker first reviews which age groups are booking more (Q11–12), why activity holidays are popular (Q13–14), then describes painting, cooking and photography holidays (Q15–17), and finishes with a table of fitness holidays in Ireland/Italy, Greece and Morocco (Q18–20).

💡 Listening tip: The age-group section is a classic trend trap. Every group is mentioned, but you need the two that are increasing. Mark each group as ↑ ↓ or → as you listen: over-65s ↑, 31–42 →, 16–30 ↓ (biggest but falling), 55–64 ↑, 43–54 → (steady).

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Trend language and ranking language are the two skills tested here. Drill both.
Q11/12 — the rising groups
Option wording
over 65 years / 55–64 years
What you hear
'the over-65s are… increasing steadily year on year'; 'a noticeable growth in the number of holidays taken by the 55 to 64-year-olds'
Key paraphrase
increasing = growth. Contrast: 'shows no sign of rising', 'the biggest drop', 'bookings are steady' all mean NOT increasing
Your turn
Q13/14 — the cultural reason
Option wording
Clients learn about a different culture
What you hear
'they do often take place outside the main tourist centres, which gives an opportunity for clients to find out more about the local people and customs'
Key paraphrase
learn about a different culture = find out more about the local people and customs → C
Your turn
Q15 — expanding painting holidays
Option wording
by employing more teachers
What you hear
'five additional ones will be joining us this year so that we can offer a greater number of classes'
Key paraphrase
employing more teachers = five additional artists joining. Locations (four centres) and levels ('all abilities') are already in place
Your turn
Q16 — why cooking holidays are unusual
Option wording
They have an international focus
What you hear
'a lot of agents offer holidays where clients cook recipes related to one particular country… but we focus on dishes from a great many different ones'
Key paraphrase
'a lot of agents… but we' = this is the point of difference → B
Your turn
