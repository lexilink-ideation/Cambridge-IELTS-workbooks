📋 Passage Overview

The return of the huarango. In the arid Ica Valley of southern Peru, the huarango tree was once central to life: its deep roots reach water, its pods were part of people's diet, and it protects the soil against erosion and stops land turning to desert. Ecologist Oliver Whaley is working with local families to bring the tree back. Question types: note completion (Q1–5), table completion (Q6–8) and TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN (Q9–13).

💡 Reading tip: For 'ONE WORD ONLY' / 'NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS', copy the exact word(s). For T/F/NG, keep the statement and passage side by side: FALSE = a clear contradiction; NOT GIVEN = the passage never says (e.g. a future plan that is only guessed at).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Farmers can increase their income from the huarangos by turning their products into foodstuffs, such as syrup and flour made from the beans.
Main ClauseFarmers can increase their income from the huarangos
Subordinate / Relative'by turning their products into foodstuffs' = means phrase; 'such as syrup and flour made from the beans' = example phrase
Grammar NoteMain clause + a 'by + -ing' means phrase + an example phrase.
Simple MeaningFarmers can earn more from huarangos by making foods like syrup and flour from the beans.
中文农民可以通过把牧豆树的产品加工成食品(如用豆子制成的糖浆和面粉)来增加收入。
But even if Whaley can convince the local people to fall in love with the huarango again, there is a further obstacle.
Main Clausethere is a further obstacle
Subordinate / Relative'even if Whaley can convince the local people to fall in love with the huarango again' = concession clause
Grammar NoteConcession ('even if…') + main clause 'there is a further obstacle'.
Simple MeaningEven if Whaley gets locals to value the huarango again, another problem remains.
中文但即使惠利能说服当地人重新爱上牧豆树,仍然存在另一个障碍。
Its deep roots, which can extend as far as 80 metres into the soil, allow it to access water that other plants cannot reach.
Main ClauseIts deep roots … allow it to access water
Subordinate / Relative'which can extend as far as 80 metres into the soil' = non-defining relative clause; 'that other plants cannot reach' = relative clause defining 'water'
Grammar NoteSubject + a non-defining relative clause + verb 'allow it to…' + a defining relative clause.
Simple MeaningIts roots, reaching up to 80 metres down, let it get water that other plants can't reach.
中文它的深根可深入土壤达80米,使其能获取其他植物无法触及的水分。
If we can manage to bring back the huarango to the most fragile desert system on Earth, then that's a real message of hope for lots of places.
Main Clausethen that's a real message of hope for lots of places
Subordinate / Relative'If we can manage to bring back the huarango to the most fragile desert system on Earth' = conditional clause
Grammar NoteFirst conditional: 'If … can …, then that's …'.
Simple MeaningIf we can restore the huarango in Earth's most fragile desert, that gives real hope for many other places.
中文如果我们能把牧豆树带回地球上最脆弱的沙漠生态系统,那对许多地方来说都是真正的希望讯息。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause and effect. Deep roots → reach water → the tree survives drought and protects soil → prevents erosion and desertification. Following this chain places the note answers (Q1–5).

Problem → solution. The problem is a lost tree and a fragile desert; the solution is persuading farmers to replant and profit from huarango foods. T/F/NG statements test details of that plan (Q9–13).

Fact vs speculation. Statements about future plans (e.g. going to Africa) are often NOT GIVEN if the writer only hopes or hints. Distinguish what Whaley has actually done from what is merely possible.
