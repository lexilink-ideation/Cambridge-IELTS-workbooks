📋 Quick Review

A supervisor, Kaeden, shows a new employee, Charlotte, around a supermarket on her first day. He explains where to leave her things, what to do at HR, his phone number, and her tasks in the bakery, sushi and meat/fish sections. Expect a spelled-out name, a phone number, and a table of responsibilities (ONE WORD ONLY).

💡 Listening tip: Phone numbers and spelled names are common Section 1 answers. Write digits as you hear them and remember 'double six' = 66. For the table, predict whether each gap is a colour, a material or an object before the speakers reach it.
