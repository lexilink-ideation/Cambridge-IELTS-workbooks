Cambridge IELTS 10 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A student, John Wishart, visits his professor to get help with his entry for the Global Design Competition. He has designed a dishwasher called the Rockpool that cleans with liquid carbon dioxide instead of water and detergent. In the second half the professor advises him on what to do before the final presentation.

典型的“学生 + 导师”讨论。前半段是产品原理(多选题),后半段是建议(填空题)。

💡 Listening tip: In Q21–25 the three options are often all mentioned — the difference is whether they are affirmed, rejected, or belong to a different year/stage. Q21 is a perfect example: 'save energy' was last year's focus. Q26–30 are ONE WORD ONLY.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 3 multiple choice is really a paraphrase test. The correct option is a rewording of one clause in the conversation; the wrong options reword something that is rejected, or something from a different time. 正确选项是对话中某一句的改写。
Q21 — the competition rules
Option C
develop a new use for current technology
Audio wording
'We have to adopt an innovative approach to existing technology, using it in a way that hasn't been thought of before'
Key paraphrase
new use = innovative approach / hasn't been thought of before; current = existing
Trap
Option A ('energy-efficient design') = 'that was the focus in last year's competition'.
Your turn
Q22 — why a dishwasher
Option A
make dishwashers more appealing
Audio wording
'they're all pretty boring and almost identical … people will be prepared to pay a little extra for something that looks different'
Key paraphrase
more appealing = looks different / not boring
Trap
Option C 'more economical' matches 'pay a little extra' only if you misread — he says people will pay more.
Your turn
Q23 — the stone
Option B
to switch it on
Audio wording
'Instead of pushing a button, you turn the stone' … 'a novel way of starting the dishwasher'
Key paraphrase
switch on = start; turn the stone replaces pushing a button
Trap
'Is that just for decoration?' is the professor's guess, immediately corrected by 'Actually it does have a function.'
Your turn
Q24 — the holding chamber
Option A
changes back to a gas
Audio wording
'the liquid is depressurised and so it reverts to a gas'
Key paraphrase
changes back to = reverts to
Trap
'to allow them to dry' comes just before — but drying is the purpose, not what the CO₂ does.
Your turn
Q25 — the end of the process
Option C
is collected ready to be re-used
Audio wording
'The carbon dioxide is sent back to the cylinder and can be used again and again'
Key paraphrase
collected = sent back to the cylinder; re-used = used again and again
Trap
The oil and grease go to the waste system — that is option B's idea, but it is not the CO₂.
Your turn
