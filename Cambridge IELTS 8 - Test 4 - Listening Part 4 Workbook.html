📋 Quick Review

A researcher gives a lecture on Australian Aboriginal rock paintings. She describes three painting styles — Dynamic, Yam and Modern — and how each depicts human figures, size, and different creatures. She then explains her project on the Rainbow Serpent image and how environmental change at the end of the ice age may have inspired it. The tasks are matching features to styles, and notes completion.

💡 Listening strategy tip: For the matching task, build a quick grid of the three styles (Dynamic = old, moving stick figures, miniature; Yam = curvy, plants & sea creatures; Modern = x-ray/bones, figures with parts missing). Then match each feature. For notes, keep to NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptQ31: figures revealing bones …
What you hear / read"the Modern style, which is known as ‘x-ray’ because it makes a feature of the internal skeleton."
Key paraphraseBones/skeleton = x-ray = Modern → answer C.

Key paraphraseMiniature = smaller than life size = Dynamic → answer A.
