📋 Passage Overview

Materials to take us beyond concrete. Concrete is cheap and versatile but a huge source of carbon dioxide because it is used on such a vast scale. The passage reviews alternatives — treated timber (cross-laminated wood) and cement substitutes such as fly ash and iron-ore slag — and the experts' doubts about each. Question types: section matching, summary completion and matching statements to people.

💡 Reading tip: For 'match the statement to the person', locate each named expert and read their quotation. A letter may be used more than once — Q10 and Q13 are both Chris Cheeseman.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

If concrete is the only answer to the construction of new cities, then carbon emissions will soar, aggravating global warming.
Main ClauseCarbon emissions will soar.
Subordinate / Relative'If concrete is the only answer to the construction of new cities' (conditional) + 'aggravating global warming' (participial result).
Grammar Note'If…, then…' sets a condition; '-ing' shows the consequence.
Simple MeaningIf cities can only be built with concrete, CO₂ will rise sharply and worsen warming.
中文如果建造新城市只能依靠混凝土，那么碳排放将激增，加剧全球变暖。
Concrete and steel have similar thermal expansion properties, so steel can be used to reinforce concrete, making it far stronger and more flexible as a building material than it could be on its own.
Main ClauseSteel can be used to reinforce concrete.
Subordinate / Relative'Concrete and steel have similar thermal expansion properties' (reason) + 'making it far stronger and more flexible…' (participial result).
Grammar Note'so' links cause and effect; the comparative 'than it could be on its own' completes the idea.
Simple MeaningBecause concrete and steel expand alike, steel can strengthen concrete beyond what it manages alone.
中文混凝土与钢材的热膨胀性质相近，因此可用钢材加固混凝土，使其作为建筑材料远比单独使用时更坚固、更有韧性。
Of course, using timber in a building also locks away the carbon that it absorbed as it grew.
Main ClauseUsing timber also locks away the carbon.
Subordinate / Relative'that it absorbed as it grew' (relative clause with a time clause inside).
Grammar NoteThe relative modifies 'carbon'; 'as it grew' sets the time.
Simple MeaningWood in a building keeps stored the carbon the tree took in while growing.
中文当然，在建筑中使用木材还能封存树木在生长时吸收的碳。
Only when a wider range of construction projects has been proven in practice will it be possible to see wood as a real alternative to concrete in constructing tall buildings.
Main ClauseIt will be possible to see wood as a real alternative to concrete.
Subordinate / Relative'Only when a wider range of construction projects has been proven in practice' (fronted time clause causing inversion).
Grammar Note'Only when…' at the front forces inversion: 'will it be possible'.
Simple MeaningWood can only be seen as a true concrete alternative once many more projects prove it works.
中文只有当更广泛的建筑项目在实践中得到验证，木材才有可能被视为建造高楼时混凝土的真正替代品。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Concession: 'Concrete is not a high-carbon product… but it is the scale on which it is used that makes it high carbon.' The writer separates the material from the problem of scale (Q13).

Problem & alternatives: the essay states the problem (concrete's emissions) then reviews alternatives (timber, fly ash, slag), each with a drawback.

Cause & effect: wood 'expands as it absorbs moisture', is 'susceptible to pests' and fire — causes of why building in wood 'is not straightforward' (Q6).

Attribution: each caution is tied to a named expert (Cheeseman, Mannström, Surgenor, Preston & Lehne) — exactly what Q9–13 test.
