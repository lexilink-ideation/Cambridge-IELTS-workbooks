Cambridge IELTS 14 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Janet Parker, a human resources manager, welcomes a group of new apprentices and gives them advice and company policies for their six-month scheme. This is a single-speaker talk. Expect multiple-choice questions about advice, and questions on policies (internet, holidays, overtime, dress code, meals).

💡 Listening tip: In multiple-choice, all three options are usually mentioned — listen for the one the speaker recommends or confirms. Watch for 'distractor' words that sound right but are rejected ('don't assume…', 'this doesn't mean…').

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Multiple-choice answers are hidden behind paraphrase and 'distractors'. Train the swap.
When unsure
Question idea
What should apprentices do if they are unsure?
What you hear
'The important thing is to check with someone if you're not sure what to do.'
Key paraphrase
'if you're not sure' = when unsure; the advice is to ask/check
Your turn
Flexible working (distractor)
Question idea
Is flexible working guaranteed after probation?
What you hear
'don't assume you'll automatically be permitted to do this'
Key paraphrase
'don't assume' signals NO — it is not automatic; it depends
