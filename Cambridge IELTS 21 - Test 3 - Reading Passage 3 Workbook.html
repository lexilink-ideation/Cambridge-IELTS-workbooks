📋 Quick Review

This passage argues that the story of human evolution is being rethought. The writer makes two big points: many "advanced" behaviours (architecture, art) go back much further than thought — even to extinct hominins like Homo naledi — and that old patriarchal assumptions about gender in prehistory (like "Man the Hunter") are being overturned.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 3 uses MCQ about the writer’s purpose, plus YES/NO/NOT GIVEN about the writer’s views. For "what is the writer doing?" questions, ask whether the paragraph explains, argues, corrects or gives examples. For Y/N/NG, match the writer’s opinion exactly.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"One is the growing evidence that many supposedly ‘advanced’ behaviours, such as architecture and art, can be traced much further back in time than we thought, often to hominin species that existed before modern humans."
Main ClauseOne is the growing evidence that …
Subordinate / Relative Clausethat many … behaviours … can be traced much further back … than we thought (noun clause) + "that existed before modern humans" (relative)
Grammar Note"the evidence that …" defines the evidence. "than we thought" is a comparison. "that existed …" describes the hominin species.
Simple MeaningOne point is that "advanced" behaviours like building and art go back far earlier than we believed — often to pre-human species.
中文其一是越来越多的证据表明，许多所谓"先进"的行为（如建筑和艺术）可以追溯到比我们以为的更久远的年代，往往属于早于现代人的古人类物种。
"This would be unsurprising if they weren’t 476,000 years old."
Main ClauseThis would be unsurprising
Subordinate / Relative Clauseif they weren’t 476,000 years old (second conditional)
Grammar NoteSecond conditional (unreal present): "would be … if … weren’t". It means the age IS surprising.
Simple MeaningThis would be no surprise — except that the logs are 476,000 years old, which makes it astonishing.
中文如果它们不是有47.6万年的历史，这本不足为奇。
"However, it had quite a small brain, typical of older hominins — and was therefore, according to palaeoanthropological dogma, incapable of complex behaviours."
Main Clauseit had quite a small brain … and was therefore … incapable of complex behaviours
Subordinate / Relative Clausetypical of older hominins (appositive) + according to palaeoanthropological dogma (source phrase)
Grammar Note"typical of …" describes the brain. "therefore … according to dogma" reports a belief the writer later questions.
Simple MeaningH. naledi had a small brain like older hominins, so — by traditional belief — it "couldn’t" do complex things.
中文然而，它的脑相当小，与更古老的古人类相似——因此，按照古人类学的教条，它无法进行复杂行为。
"Palaeoanthropologists got fixated on brain size because it was what they could see: if what you have is skeletons, then all you know about brains are their shapes and sizes."
Main ClausePalaeoanthropologists got fixated on brain size
Subordinate / Relative Clausebecause it was what they could see (reason) + "if what you have is skeletons, then …" (conditional)
Grammar Note"because …" gives the reason. The colon introduces an explanation with an "if … then …" conditional.
Simple MeaningScientists focused on brain size simply because, with only skeletons, size and shape are all they can measure.
中文古人类学家执着于脑的大小，因为那是他们能看到的：如果你拥有的只是骨骼，那么你对大脑所知的就只有其形状和大小。
"However, a meta-analysis published in June 2023 compiled data on several dozen foraging societies and found women hunted in 80 per cent of them."
Main Clausea meta-analysis … compiled data … and found women hunted in 80 per cent of them
Subordinate / Relative Clausepublished in June 2023 (reduced relative describing the meta-analysis)
Grammar Note"published …" = which was published. Two verbs "compiled" and "found" share the subject.
Simple MeaningA 2023 review of dozens of hunter-gatherer societies found that women hunted in 80% of them.
中文然而，2023年6月发表的一项元分析汇编了数十个觅食社会的数据，发现在其中80%的社会里女性都参与狩猎。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

This is an argument text — follow the writer’s reasoning and stance.

Claim & Evidence

The writer states two claims and supports each with finds: interlocking 476,000-year-old logs (early architecture), Neanderthal cave art, H. naledi etchings; and the "Man the Hunter" demolition (women hunted in 80% of societies).

Concession & Rebuttal

The writer concedes that the H. naledi claims are controversial and "more evidence is needed", but rebuts the small-brain objection by pointing to the brain’s "internal wiring".

Analogy

Mobile phones and the first birds are analogies: big advances usually start small and improve incrementally, not in a sudden "explosion". Recognise analogies used to make a point.

Writer’s Stance

The writer is a cautious reformer: open to new ideas, sceptical of old "dogma" and "preconceptions", and clear that patriarchy and inequality are "choices", not inevitable.
