📋 Quick Review

A doctor, Carl Rogers (the man), takes details from a new patient, Julie Garcia (the woman), at the Total Health Clinic. He checks her personal information, then asks about the knee pain that brought her in, her exercise habits and her medical history. Expect a phone number, a date, a name to spell, and body/health words.

💡 Listening tip: When a name is spelled out, write the letters exactly (Cawley = C-A-W-L-E-Y). For dates, catch both the day and the month — and watch out for the speaker correcting the year.
