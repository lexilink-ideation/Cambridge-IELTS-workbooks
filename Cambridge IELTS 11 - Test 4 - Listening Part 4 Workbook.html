📋 Quick Review

A lecture on using soil to reduce carbon dioxide in the atmosphere. It covers Rattan Lal's claim, how carbon moves between plants and soil, how agriculture released that carbon, regenerative agriculture, and two current studies — one in California and one in Australia.

环境科学讲座。笔记分五块,每块开头都有清晰的小标题。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY. Two gaps need adjectives ('soil that is ___', 'soil that was very ___', 'remains fertile and ___') and the rest need nouns. Decide the word class from the grammar around the gap before the recording starts.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Notice how each note compresses a long spoken clause into three or four words.
Q31 — dry soil
Question note
Erosion is more likely in soil that is ___
What you hear
'But erosion can occur in dry soil, which is a likely effect if it contains inadequate amounts of carbon'
Key paraphrase
'erosion can occur in dry soil' = 'more likely in soil that is dry'
Your turn
Q34 — roots
Question note
some CO₂ moves from the ___ to the soil
What you hear
'some transfer from the roots to fungi and soil microbes, which store the carbon in the soil'
Key paraphrase
'transfer from the roots to … soil microbes' = 'moves from the roots to the soil'
Your turn
Q36 — variety
Question note
increasing the ___ of plants that are grown
What you hear
'keeping fields planted all year round, and increasing the variety of plants being grown'
Key paraphrase
almost word for word — but you must hear it inside a list
Your turn
Q38 — gardens
Question note
compost made from waste from agriculture and ___
What you hear
'both agricultural, including manure and cornstalks, and waste produced in gardens, such as leaves, branches, and lawn trimmings'
Key paraphrase
'waste produced in gardens' = 'waste from gardens' → gardens
Your turn
Q40 — payment
Question note
giving farmers ___ for carbon storage, as well as their produce
What you hear
'farmers should receive payment not just for the corn or beef they produce but also for the carbon they can store in their soil'
Key paraphrase
'not just … but also' matches 'as well as' → payment
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Science-lecture chunks. Get the linking words and the noun endings right.
1.Carbon-rich soil is dark, crumbly and fertile, and retains some water.
2.Soils so devoid of organic matter that the ground had become extremely hard.
3.Plants absorb carbon dioxide and transform it into sugars.
4.Some transfer from the roots to fungi and soil microbes.
5.This aims to boost the fertility of soil and keep it moist.
6.Keeping fields planted all year round, and increasing the variety of plants being grown.
7.Cultivating grasses that stay green all year round.
8.Farmers should receive payment for the carbon they can store in their soil.
