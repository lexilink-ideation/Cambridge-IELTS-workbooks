📋 Passage Overview

Urban farming. In Paris, engineer Pascal Hardy has built the world's largest urban rooftop farm using aeroponic (soil-free) growing towers. The passage weighs the method's benefits — less water, fewer pesticides, food grown and sold locally — against its limits (root vegetables and fruit trees are impossible). Question types: sentence completion, table completion and True/False/Not Given.

💡 Reading tip: For completion tasks, obey the word limit exactly ('ONE WORD ONLY' vs 'NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER'). For TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN, remember NOT GIVEN means the text neither confirms nor denies the claim — don't use your own knowledge (Q9, Q13).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Produce grown using this soil-free method — which relies solely on a small quantity of water, enriched with organic nutrients, pumped around a closed circuit of pipes, towers and trays — is produced up here, and sold locally, just down there.
Main ClauseProduce grown using this soil-free method is produced up here and sold locally.
Subordinate / Relative'which relies solely on a small quantity of water…' (relative clause) + 'enriched with… pumped around…' (reduced relatives).
Grammar NoteDashes insert a long description between subject and verb; participles ('enriched', 'pumped') reduce 'which is…'.
Simple MeaningThe food is grown on the roof with just a little nutrient-rich water and sold right below.
中文用这种无土方法种植的农产品——它只需少量富含有机养分、在管道、种植塔和托盘间循环的水——在屋顶上种植，就在楼下就地出售。
You can select crop varieties for their flavour, not their resistance to the transport and storage chain, and you can pick them when they're really at their best.
Main ClauseYou can select crop varieties for their flavour and pick them at their best.
Subordinate / Relative'not their resistance to the transport and storage chain' (contrast phrase).
Grammar Note'for X, not Y' contrasts the real reason with a rejected one.
Simple MeaningBecause the food doesn't travel, growers can choose tasty types and harvest them when perfectly ripe.
中文你可以按风味而非耐运输、耐储存来选择品种，并在最佳状态时采摘。
The equipment weighs little, can be installed on almost any flat surface and is cheap to buy: roughly €100 to €150 per square metre.
Main ClauseThe equipment weighs little, can be installed on almost any flat surface and is cheap to buy.
Subordinate / Relative'roughly €100 to €150 per square metre' (appositive giving the price).
Grammar NoteThree verbs share one subject; a colon introduces the figure that explains 'cheap'.
Simple MeaningThe kit is light, fits on any flat roof and is inexpensive — about €100–150 per m².
中文这套设备很轻，几乎可以安装在任何平面上，而且便宜——每平方米约100到150欧元。
There are limits to what farmers can grow this way, of course, and much of the produce is suited to the summer months.
Main ClauseThere are limits to what farmers can grow this way.
Subordinate / Relative'what farmers can grow this way' (noun clause as object of 'to').
Grammar Note'much of the produce is suited to…' adds a second, linked limitation.
Simple MeaningYou can't grow everything this way, and most crops only work in summer.
中文当然，这种方式能种的作物有限，而且大部分农产品只适合夏季。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Contrast: Hardy lists what he dislikes about intensive farming (pesticides, greenhouse gases, 2,000 km of transport) to set up the advantages of aeroponics. The reasons he gives become the table answers (Q4–7).

Cause & effect: 'the water… is recycled, so the method uses 90% less water.' Recycling (cause) → huge water saving (effect).

Concession: 'We're not ever, obviously, going to feed the whole city this way… But if enough unused space can be developed…' He admits a limit, then states a realistic target (5–10%).

Example signalling: 'Strawberries are being grown in disused shipping containers, mushrooms in underground carparks.' Concrete examples support the claim that urban farming is booming.
