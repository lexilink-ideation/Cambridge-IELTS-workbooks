📋 Passage Overview

Adapted from a book's introduction, this passage sets the historical context of the Little Ice Age (≈1300–1850). It explains how humans have always adapted to climate change, how scientists reconstruct past climate from tree rings and ice cores, and how the cooling reshaped Europe — driving changes in fishing and farming — before the Modern Warm Period and the first human-caused global warming after 1850.

💡 Reading strategy tip: This text is organised by time periods, so build a quick mental timeline (Medieval Warm → Little Ice Age → Modern Warm). That timeline makes the heading-matching and classification questions much faster. For the word-list summary, check grammar and meaning fit before choosing.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"We tend to think of climate — as opposed to weather — as something unchanging, yet humanity has been at the mercy of climate change for its entire existence."
Main ClauseWe tend to think of climate … as something unchanging … yet humanity has been at the mercy of climate change
Subordinate / Relativeas opposed to weather (inserted contrast) / yet … (contrast conjunction)
Grammar NoteDashes insert a contrast; 'yet' signals the opposite is true.
Simple MeaningWe assume climate never changes, but in fact it has always shaped human life.
中文我们往往认为气候（与天气不同）是不变的，然而人类在其整个历史中一直受气候变化的支配。
"The Little Ice Age was far from a deep freeze, however; rather an irregular seesaw of rapid climatic shifts, few lasting more than a quarter-century."
Main ClauseThe Little Ice Age was far from a deep freeze … rather an irregular seesaw of rapid climatic shifts
Subordinate / Relativefew lasting more than a quarter-century (reduced relative: few of which lasted…)
Grammar Note'far from' = not at all; 'rather' corrects to the true description.
Simple MeaningThe Little Ice Age was not a steady deep freeze but constant back-and-forth changes, each short.
中文然而，小冰期远非持续的严寒，而是快速气候起伏的不规则摆动，鲜有持续超过二十五年的。
"A gradual agricultural revolution in northern Europe stemmed from concerns over food supplies at a time of rising populations."
Main ClauseA gradual agricultural revolution … stemmed from concerns over food supplies
Subordinate / Relativeat a time of rising populations (prepositional phrase of time)
Grammar Note'stemmed from' = was caused by; the time phrase gives the background.
Simple MeaningBecause populations were growing and food was a worry, farming slowly transformed.
中文在人口增长的时期，出于对食物供应的担忧，北欧逐渐发生了一场农业革命。
"The unprecedented land clearance released vast quantities of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, triggering for the first time humanly caused global warming."
Main ClauseThe unprecedented land clearance released vast quantities of carbon dioxide
Subordinate / Relativetriggering … humanly caused global warming (participle clause showing result)
Grammar Note'-ing' participle 'triggering' shows the consequence of the clearance.
Simple MeaningClearing so much land released huge amounts of CO₂, causing the first human-made warming.
中文空前规模的土地开垦向大气释放了大量二氧化碳，首次引发了人为的全球变暖。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology / Periods — The text is built on three periods: Medieval Warm (≈900–1200), Little Ice Age (≈1300–1850), Modern Warm (1850–). Sorting events by period answers Q23–26.

Cause & Effect — Colder, stormier seas forced fishing fleets further offshore; food worries stemmed an agricultural revolution; land clearance triggered human-caused warming.

Contrast — The Little Ice Age was NOT a steady 'deep freeze' but an 'irregular seesaw of rapid climatic shifts'. Signal: however, rather than, far from.

Evidence — Because systematic records are recent, scientists use 'proxy records' — tree rings and ice cores — plus a few written accounts.
