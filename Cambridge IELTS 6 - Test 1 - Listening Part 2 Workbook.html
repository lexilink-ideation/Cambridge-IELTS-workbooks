📋 Quick Review

A radio presenter interviews Lynne Rawley, the Public Relations Officer of the Rivenden City Theatre, about its reopening after a three-year redevelopment. Lynne describes the physical changes made to different parts of the theatre (box office, seats, lifts, dressing rooms), then gives details about the first play showing after reopening — dates, times, ticket availability, and prices.

This is a classic IELTS Listening Part 2 monologue/interview — one main speaker giving detailed factual information, tested with a matching task and a table/note completion task.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In the matching task (Q11–16), listen for words that describe change — "used to be", "now", "instead of", "have been converted into". The change described tells you which letter (A–G) to choose.

Key paraphrase"its own access" = a separate entrance
