Cambridge IELTS 4 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Mike and Sue, plan an assignment: choosing two science experiments to do with eight-year-old children. They go through five experiments — a balloon hovercraft, measuring with paperclips, growing crystals, a spinning colour wheel and a home-made record player — noting the equipment, purpose and a problem with each. Expect a table and letter matching.

💡 Listening tip: When speakers evaluate options, the problem is signalled by a criticism — 'a bit risky', 'too tame', 'we'd have to wait a fortnight', 'a bit babyish', 'too ambitious'. Match the criticism to the label in the box.
