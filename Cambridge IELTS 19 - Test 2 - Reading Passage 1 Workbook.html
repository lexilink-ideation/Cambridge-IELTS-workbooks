📋 Passage Overview

The Industrial Revolution in Britain. This passage traces how Britain changed from a rural society into an industrial one: the development of steam power, the demand for coal, the mechanised textile and iron industries, new communications (the telegraph), rapid urbanisation, and the Luddite resistance. Question types: note completion and True/False/Not Given.

💡 Reading tip: For the notes (Q1–7), the answers appear in passage order and must be ONE WORD from the text. Match each note to its section (Steam power, Textiles, Iron, Communications, Urbanisation) before you scan.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

In Britain, it was a period when a largely rural, agrarian society was transformed into an industrialised, urban one.
Main ClauseIt was a period.
Subordinate / Relative'when a largely rural, agrarian society was transformed into an industrialised, urban one' (relative clause of time).
Grammar Note'when' introduces a relative clause describing 'a period'; 'one' replaces 'society'.
Simple MeaningDuring this time, a farming society became an industrial, city-based one.
中文在英国，这是一个把以农村和农业为主的社会转变为工业化、城市化社会的时期。
Watt later worked with the English manufacturer Matthew Boulton to invent a new steam engine driven by both the forward and backward strokes of the piston.
Main ClauseWatt worked with Matthew Boulton to invent a new steam engine.
Subordinate / Relative'driven by both the forward and backward strokes of the piston' (reduced relative describing the engine).
Grammar Note'driven by…' reduces 'which was driven by…'.
Simple MeaningWatt and Boulton built an engine powered by the piston moving both ways.
中文瓦特后来与英国制造商马修·博尔顿合作，发明了一种由活塞前后两个冲程共同驱动的新型蒸汽机。
Many had spent years learning their craft, and they feared that unskilled machine operators were robbing them of their livelihood.
Main ClauseThey feared that unskilled machine operators were robbing them of their livelihood.
Subordinate / Relative'that unskilled machine operators were robbing them…' (noun clause, object of 'feared').
Grammar Note'rob sb of sth' = take sth away from sb.
Simple MeaningSkilled workers worried that machine operators were taking away their jobs.
中文许多人花了多年学习手艺，他们担心不熟练的机器操作工正夺走他们的生计。
The unrest finally reached its peak in April 1812, when a few Luddites were shot during an attack on a mill near Huddersfield.
Main ClauseThe unrest reached its peak in April 1812.
Subordinate / Relative'when a few Luddites were shot during an attack on a mill near Huddersfield' (relative clause of time).
Grammar Note'when' adds detail about the time; passive 'were shot'.
Simple MeaningThe trouble was worst in April 1812, when some Luddites were shot attacking a mill.
中文动乱在1812年4月达到顶峰，当时几名卢德分子在袭击哈德斯菲尔德附近的一家工厂时被枪击。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & effect: steam power + new machines (cause) → mass production in factories (effect). Trace this chain through textiles and iron.

Contrast (before/after): 'prior to the Industrial Revolution, the British textile business was a true "cottage industry"' vs the later 'mechanised textile factories'. This before/after supports Q3–4.

Problem → response: Luddites broke machines (problem) → the government made machine-breaking 'punishable by death' (response).

Concession: 'Although industrialisation increased… economic output… many poor people continued to struggle.' A positive is qualified by a negative — key for Q11.
