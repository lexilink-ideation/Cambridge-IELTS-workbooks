📋 Quick Review

A lecture on an experimental underground house built into a disused stone quarry. The speaker covers the owners' aims, the choice of site, the two-storey design with its glass south-facing wall and photovoltaic tiles, the energy-efficient features, and the environmental cost of the concrete used. Question types: multiple choice (Q31–32) and note completion (Q33–40, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER).

💡 Listening tip: The notes use three headings — Design, Special features, Environmental issues — and the talk follows them in order. Listen especially for the turn at 'It's true that the actual construction of the house was harmful…' — that 'However' paragraph contains the last two answers and reverses the positive tone.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Academic sentences compress into one-word notes. Practise the reduction both ways.
Q31 — The owners
Option B
were interested in environmental issues
What you hear
'Their chief aim was to create somewhere that was as environmentally-friendly as possible'
Key paraphrase
'chief aim… environmentally-friendly' = interested in environmental issues → B
Your turn
Q34 — The foam layer
Note line
A layer of foam was used to improve the ______ of the building
What you hear
'the walls had a layer of foam around them too, to increase the insulation'
Key paraphrase
'increase' = 'improve' → insulation
Your turn
Q36 — Electricity surplus
Note line
the house may produce more ______ than it needs
What you hear
'the house may even generate an electricity surplus, and the owners will be able to sell some to the national grid'
Key paraphrase
'a surplus' = more than it needs → electricity
Your turn
Q40 — The environmental debt
Note line
will have paid its 'environmental debt' within ______
What you hear
'once the initial debt has been cleared - and it's been calculated that this will only take fifteen years'
Key paraphrase
'will only take fifteen years' = within 15 years (NOT the 70 tons figure)
Your turn
