📋 Quick Review

Leon tells Shannon, who has just moved into a new flat, where to buy food in the Kite Place area by the harbour. He describes a fish market, an organic shop, a supermarket minibus, and recommends things to buy for a curry and dessert. Expect place directions, a spelled-out word (samphire), and food items (ONE WORD ONLY in the table).

💡 Listening tip: Section 1 mixes notes and a table. Predict the answer type for each gap — a place feature, a time, a colour, or a food. When a word is spelled out ('S-A-M-P-H-I-R-E'), write the letters and check the spelling at the end.
