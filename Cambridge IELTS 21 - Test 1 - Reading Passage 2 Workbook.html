📋 Quick Review

This passage explains why noise harms health and why silence can help. It covers the body’s automatic reaction to sudden noise, evidence from the WHO and European agencies, ways cities are reducing noise, and research on flotation tanks and quiet environments by scientists such as Feinstein, Ben-Soussan and Pfeifer.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 2 often mixes “which section” matching with summary completion and MCQ. For matching-information questions, read the statement first, then scan for the paraphrased idea — the section letters are not in the order of the questions.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"To help ensure loud or unexpected noises get the attention they deserve, our internal chemistry alters in response to them."
Main Clauseour internal chemistry alters in response to them
Subordinate / Relative ClauseTo help ensure loud or unexpected noises get the attention they deserve (infinitive of purpose)
Grammar Note"To help ensure …" tells us WHY the change happens. "they deserve" is a reduced relative describing "the attention".
Simple MeaningSo that we notice loud or surprising sounds, our body chemistry changes automatically.
中文为了确保我们注意到响亮或意外的声音，我们体内的化学反应会随之改变。
"When we are exposed to too much noise over the long term, however, those responses can lead to a multitude of health issues, from sleep disturbance to even cardiovascular disease."
Main Clausethose responses can lead to a multitude of health issues
Subordinate / Relative ClauseWhen we are exposed to too much noise over the long term
Grammar Note"When"-clause sets the condition. "however" contrasts with the earlier positive point. "from … to …" gives a range.
Simple MeaningBut if we hear too much noise for a long time, those same reactions can cause many health problems, from poor sleep to heart disease.
中文然而，如果长期暴露于过多噪音，这些反应会引发多种健康问题，从睡眠障碍到心血管疾病。
"While some people experience altered perception in the tanks, involving subtle humming sounds and visual effects, these effects are benign and do not detract from the benefits of the experience."
Main Clausethese effects are benign and do not detract from the benefits
Subordinate / Relative ClauseWhile some people experience altered perception … (concession) + "involving …" participle
Grammar Note"While" = although. "involving …" is a participle phrase describing the altered perception.
Simple MeaningAlthough some people sense strange sounds or images in the tank, these are harmless and do not reduce the benefits.
中文虽然有些人在舱内会有知觉改变，出现轻微嗡嗡声和视觉效果，但这些无害，也不会削弱体验的益处。
"Not everyone will benefit from silence to the same extent, but Ben-Soussan says one characteristic is key: the person must need to want to engage in the experience."
Main ClauseNot everyone will benefit from silence to the same extent
Subordinate / Relative Clausethe person must need to want to engage in the experience (explained after the colon)
Grammar NoteThe colon introduces an explanation of "one characteristic". "but" contrasts equal benefit vs. individual differences.
Simple MeaningPeople get different amounts of benefit from silence; the key is that they must genuinely want to take part.
中文并非人人都能从静默中获益相同，但本-苏珊说关键在于：这个人必须真心愿意投入其中。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Track how the writer links noise, health and silence.

Cause & Effect

Loud noise → blood pressure rises, muscles tense, glands release hormones (fight or flight). Over the long term → sleep disturbance and cardiovascular disease. Signal: lead to, in response to.

Problem & Solution

Problem: excessive city noise (WHO, 1-in-5 exposed). Solutions: quieter buses, noise-reducing road coatings, greenery, bans on leaf blowers, fines for modified vehicles. Learn to pair problems with fixes.

Comparison

Pfeifer found people felt more relaxed in an outdoor garden than in a completely silent room. Comparison signals: more … than, compared with. Useful for MCQ and YES/NO.

Qualification (limits)

The writer repeatedly qualifies claims: “Not everyone will benefit …”, “some people may not benefit …”. These hedges are important for NOT GIVEN vs FALSE decisions.
