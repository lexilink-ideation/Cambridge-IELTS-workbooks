📋 Passage Overview

Why we need to protect polar bears. The passage argues that polar bears — uniquely adapted to Arctic cold — could teach humans about heart disease and osteoporosis, and that they are more intelligent and emotional than people assume. Question types: True / False / Not Given (Q1–7) and summary completion (Q8–13).

💡 Reading tip: For True/False/Not Given, decide first whether the passage says the same (True), says the opposite (False), or simply doesn't say (Not Given). Don't use outside knowledge about polar bears.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Humans with comparable levels of adipose tissue would be considered obese and would be likely to suffer from diabetes and heart disease. Yet the polar bear experiences no such consequences.
Main Clausethe polar bear experiences no such consequences
Subordinate / RelativeHumans with comparable levels… would be considered obese (conditional 'would')
Grammar NoteContrast with 'Yet'; hypothetical 'would' describes humans, real present for the bear.
Simple MeaningThe same amount of fat would make a human ill, but it doesn't harm the bear.
中文同样多的脂肪会让人生病，但对北极熊却没有害处。
They discovered that pregnant bears were able to increase the density of their bones before they started to build their dens.
Main ClauseThey discovered [that…]
Subordinate / Relativethat pregnant bears were able to increase the density of their bones (noun clause); before they started to build their dens (time clause)
Grammar NoteReported discovery + a 'before' time clause showing sequence.
Simple MeaningBefore making their dens, pregnant bears made their bones denser.
中文在筑巢之前，怀孕的母熊能够增加骨密度。
We tend to want to protect animals we think are intelligent and possess emotions, such as elephants and primates.
Main ClauseWe tend to want to protect animals
Subordinate / Relative[that] we think are intelligent and possess emotions (relative clause)
Grammar NoteReduced relative clause (omitted 'that'); 'such as' introduces examples.
Simple MeaningPeople usually protect animals they see as clever and feeling, like elephants.
中文我们往往想保护那些我们认为聪明、有情感的动物，比如大象。
This is even more astonishing since polar bears are known to actively hunt humans in the wild.
Main ClauseThis is even more astonishing
Subordinate / Relativesince polar bears are known to actively hunt humans in the wild (reason clause)
Grammar Note'since' = 'because'; passive 'are known to'.
Simple MeaningIt's surprising because wild polar bears usually hunt humans.
中文这更令人惊讶，因为野生北极熊会主动捕猎人类。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Cause & effect (medical value): the APoB gene reduces bad cholesterol → so polar bears may be a model for understanding human heart disease. Notice how a biological cause leads to a research consequence.

Paradox / contrast: the mother bear fasts for six months and depletes her reserves, yet her bones stay dense. IELTS often tests whether you can hold a 'surprising but true' contrast in mind.

Assumption vs evidence: people assume bears are 'stupid and violent', but anecdotes (GoGo's tool use, stacking games) suggest problem-solving and creativity. Look for the 'and yet' that flips the assumption.
