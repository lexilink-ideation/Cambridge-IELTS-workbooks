📋 Quick Review

This passage criticises "AI solutionism" — the belief that machine learning can solve every problem. It argues that politicians overestimate AI, that the public sector lacks the data and skills to use it, that AI faces security risks, and it gives real failures (IBM Watson for Oncology and sentencing algorithms) to show that AI is not the answer to everything.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 3 mixes MCQ, summary-from-a-word-list, and YES/NO/NOT GIVEN about the writer’s claims. Y/N/NG is about the writer’s opinion: choose NO only if the writer clearly disagrees, and NOT GIVEN if the writer gives no opinion on that exact point.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"While it is undeniable that AI has opened up a wealth of promising opportunities, it has also led to the emergence of a mindset that can be best described as ‘AI solutionism’."
Main Clauseit has also led to the emergence of a mindset … ‘AI solutionism’
Subordinate / Relative ClauseWhile it is undeniable that AI has opened up a wealth of promising opportunities (concession)
Grammar Note"While … undeniable that" concedes a point before the "but"-idea. "that can be best described as" is a relative clause.
Simple MeaningYes, AI has created many opportunities — but it has also produced the "AI solutionism" mindset.
中文虽然不可否认人工智能带来了大量有前景的机会，但它也催生了一种最好称之为"人工智能万能论"的心态。
"This is the philosophy that, given enough data, machine learning algorithms can solve all of humanity’s problems."
Main ClauseThis is the philosophy
Subordinate / Relative Clausethat … machine learning algorithms can solve all of humanity’s problems (noun clause) + "given enough data" (conditional phrase)
Grammar Note"that …" defines the philosophy. "given enough data" = if there is enough data (reduced conditional).
Simple MeaningIt is the belief that, with enough data, AI can solve every human problem.
中文这是一种理念：只要有足够的数据，机器学习算法就能解决人类的一切问题。
"But what many politicians do not understand is that simply adding a neural network to a problem will not automatically mean that you’ll find a solution."
Main Clausewhat many politicians do not understand is that …
Subordinate / Relative Clausethat simply adding a neural network … will not automatically mean that you’ll find a solution
Grammar NoteA "what"-clause is the subject. Two nested "that"-clauses report the point. "simply adding …" is a gerund subject inside.
Simple MeaningMany politicians don’t realise that just adding a neural network won’t automatically solve a problem.
中文但许多政客不明白：仅仅给一个问题加上神经网络，并不自动意味着你就能找到解决方案。
"This means that a malicious AI can target another AI to make it behave in a certain way, such as forcing it to make wrong predictions."
Main Clausea malicious AI can target another AI
Subordinate / Relative Clauseto make it behave in a certain way … (infinitive of purpose) + "such as forcing it to make wrong predictions" (example)
Grammar Note"to make it behave" states purpose. "such as …ing" introduces an example.
Simple MeaningA harmful AI can attack another AI to control how it acts — for example, making it predict wrongly.
中文这意味着恶意的人工智能可以针对另一个人工智能，使其以特定方式行事，例如迫使它做出错误预测。
"Using AI simply for the sake of AI may not always be productive or useful, and not every issue is best addressed by applying machine intelligence to it."
Main ClauseUsing AI simply for the sake of AI may not always be productive or useful
Subordinate / Relative Clauseand not every issue is best addressed by applying machine intelligence to it (coordinated clause)
Grammar NoteGerund subject "Using AI …". "for the sake of" = only for the purpose of. Passive "is best addressed".
Simple MeaningUsing AI just to use AI is not always helpful, and not every problem is best solved with AI.
中文仅仅为了使用人工智能而使用它，未必总是有成效或有用；并非每个问题都最适合用机器智能来解决。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

This is an argument text — follow the writer’s reasoning and stance.

Claim & Counter-claim

The writer sets a popular claim (AI will solve everything) against a counter-claim (it won’t). Signal words: But, in fact, however, still. The whole passage argues the counter-claim.

Cause & Effect

AI solutionism → disregards safety principles + sets unrealistic expectations → jeopardises the value of AI. Poor data + few skilled staff → the public sector can’t use AI well.

Example → Conclusion

Two failures (IBM Watson for Oncology; US sentencing algorithms) lead to the conclusion: "there is no AI solution for everything." Separate the examples from the general point.

Writer’s Stance

The writer is sceptical of hype and supportive of critics like Russell and Brooks. For YES/NO/NOT GIVEN, agreeing with a critic = the writer’s view.
