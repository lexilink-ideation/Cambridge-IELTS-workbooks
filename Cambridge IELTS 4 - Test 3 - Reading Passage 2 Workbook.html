📋 Passage Overview

Volcanoes — earth-shattering news explains how volcanic activity (vulcanism) has shaped the planet — making continents, the atmosphere and the oceans' water. It describes the earth's structure and tectonic plates, the different types of eruption (slow granite, fast horizontal sheets, explosive), and why volcanoes are so unpredictable.

💡 Reading tip: 'Choose the heading for each section' rewards reading the first and last sentences of each section for its main idea. Do the ones you are sure of first, then use elimination for the rest.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

A violent eruption can blow the top few kilometres off a mountain, scatter fine ash practically over the globe and hurl rock fragments into the stratosphere to darken the skies a continent away.
Main ClauseA violent eruption can blow the top … off a mountain, scatter fine ash … and hurl rock fragments into the stratosphere
Subordinate / Relativeto darken the skies a continent away (infinitive of result)
Grammar Notethree parallel verbs (blow / scatter / hurl) share one subject; 'to darken' shows result.
Simple MeaningA big eruption can remove a mountain's top, spread ash worldwide, and throw rock high enough to darken skies far away.
中文一次剧烈的喷发能把山顶数公里炸掉，把细灰几乎撒遍全球，并将岩石碎块抛入平流层，使一个大陆之外的天空变暗。
Even though the mantle rocks are solid by overlying pressure, they can still slowly 'flow' like thick treacle.
Main Clausethey can still slowly 'flow' like thick treacle
Subordinate / RelativeEven though the mantle rocks are solid by overlying pressure (concession)
Grammar Note'Even though' concedes a fact; 'like thick treacle' is a simile.
Simple MeaningAlthough pressure keeps the mantle solid, it can still creep like thick syrup.
中文尽管上覆压力使地幔岩石保持固态，它们仍能像浓稠的糖浆一样缓慢地'流动'。
During quiet periods, volcanoes cap themselves with their own lava by forming a powerful cone from the molten rocks slopping over the rim of the crater.
Main Clausevolcanoes cap themselves with their own lava
Subordinate / Relativeby forming a powerful cone from the molten rocks slopping over the rim (means, gerund); slopping over the rim (participle)
Grammar Note'by + gerund' shows how; 'slopping…' is a reduced relative describing the rocks.
Simple MeaningWhen quiet, volcanoes seal themselves by building a cone from lava spilling over the crater edge.
中文在平静期，火山会用溢出火山口边缘的熔岩堆成坚固的锥体，从而把自己封住。
The eruption was so fierce that dust thrown into the stratosphere darkened the skies, cancelling the following summer in Europe and North America.
Main ClauseThe eruption was so fierce that dust … darkened the skies
Subordinate / Relativeso … that … (result); cancelling the following summer (participle of result)
Grammar Note'so + adjective … that …' shows degree/result; 'cancelling…' adds a further consequence.
Simple MeaningThe eruption was so violent that its dust darkened the sky and wiped out the next summer in Europe and North America.
中文这次喷发如此猛烈，以致抛入平流层的尘埃使天空变暗，取消了欧洲和北美随后的那个夏天。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Whole → parts: Section A claims volcanoes shaped the planet; later sections give the mechanism (structure, plates, eruption types).

Analogy: the soft-boiled egg (yolk/white/shell) models the earth's molten core, mantle and crust.

Classification: Section C sorts eruptions into slow (granite), faster (horizontal sheets) and explosive types.

Cause of unpredictability: 'geological time is not like human time' — quiet volcanoes can seal themselves for centuries (Pinatubo, 600 years) then blow.
