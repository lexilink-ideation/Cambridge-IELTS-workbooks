📋 Quick Review

This is a Section 4 academic lecture from a Hospitality and Tourism module. The lecturer discusses the luxury end of the hotel market: research showing that even expensive hotels can feel "empty and dispiriting" because they overlook guests' need to feel at home. The lecture then covers a new trend — luxury serviced apartments — before finishing with the psychology behind why people choose traditional holiday hotels (escape, being pampered, small treats).

Section 4 is always a single, longer academic monologue with no pause between question groups, so you must keep pace with the speaker throughout.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Section 4 lectures often follow a clear structure: an opening scenario/problem, research findings, then a new example or contrasting idea. Use structure words ("however," "but back to the main point," "finally") to predict when the topic — and the next group of answers — is about to shift.
