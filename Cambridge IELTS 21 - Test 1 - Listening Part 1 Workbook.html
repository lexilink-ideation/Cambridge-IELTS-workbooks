Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Part 1 you hear a phone conversation between a man and a woman who works at Oyster Bay Sailing Club. The man is a complete beginner and wants to find out about sailing courses. The woman explains the Taster Day and the Level 1 course, telling him about prices, what is included, what to bring, and general club facilities.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 1 two people talk about everyday, practical information. Answers come in the order you hear them. Listen carefully for numbers, prices, and things you must bring — and watch for a price or number that is corrected or changed.
