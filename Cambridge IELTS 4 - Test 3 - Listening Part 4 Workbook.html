📋 Quick Review

A Students' Union representative presents students' opinions on the design of a new Union building to a committee. He explains how opinion was gathered (a questionnaire of ~2,000 students), compares three possible sites, lists the facilities requested, and reports on the drama theatre debate and security measures. Expect notes, a table and letter-choice questions.

💡 Listening tip: For a site-comparison table, track the reason attached to each site — 'unpopular because of…', 'close to…', 'the most popular because of…'. Answers are the reasons, not just the place names.
