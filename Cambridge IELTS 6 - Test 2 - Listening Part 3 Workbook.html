Dissertation Tutorial Record · Sandy Gibbons · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A tutor and a student, Sandy Gibbons, discuss Sandy's dissertation in Education. The tutor reviews three previous targets Sandy agreed to work on — progress made and further actions needed — then sets three new targets for the next period, including specific improvements to Chapter One and preparation for Chapter Two.

This is a classic Part 3 academic discussion: two educated speakers, topic-specific vocabulary, and both tables must be completed from a single flowing conversation. The challenge is tracking two parallel structures (work completed vs. further action) while keeping up with the dialogue.

💡 Part 3 strategy: Study both tables before the recording. The first table (Q21–25) reviews past work; the second (Q26–30) plans future work. Answers are delivered in column order within each row — expect the tutor to give the target, Sandy to report what she did, then the tutor to suggest the next step. Watch for concession language: "Well, I … but I'm afraid I didn't manage to…" signals a partial result.
