Cambridge IELTS 15 — Test 2 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A caller asks for information about a local arts festival. First a table of daily events (a concert, a ballet, a comedy play, a dance) is completed, then notes about the festival's workshops and outdoor activities — making things, swimming, and a guided woodland walk. Expect names to spell, dates, and everyday words.

💡 Listening tip: Note completion, ONE WORD ONLY, answers in order. Use the table headings (date · type of event · details) and the note headings (Workshops · Outdoor activities) to know exactly where you are, and predict a noun for each gap.
