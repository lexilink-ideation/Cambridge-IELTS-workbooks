Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

This Part 2 is a podcast about an annual business exhibition. The host reads feedback from an exhibitor, explains the discounts on offer, and then introduces the keynote speakers — describing each one’s background and what they will talk about.

💡 Listening strategy tip: For the speaker-matching task, listen for the ONE thing each keynote speaker will focus on (e.g. helping people find work, or the need for flexibility). The description of their past is often just a distractor — match the topic of their talk.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question wordingit helped the company become better known
Audio meaning"that also effectively raised our corporate profile"
Key paraphraseraised our corporate profile = became better known

Audio meaning"known for her down-to-earth attitude"
Key paraphrasedown-to-earth = practical and sensible

Audio meaning"the need for flexibility to cope with new directions"
