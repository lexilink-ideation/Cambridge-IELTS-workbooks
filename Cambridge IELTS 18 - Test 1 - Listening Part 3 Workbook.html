Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 18 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two first-year students, Hugo and Chantal, discuss a talk they heard on jobs in fashion design. They compare their reactions to the speaker's message, their school careers advice, and the speaker's account of her own first job. Expect multiple choice (Q21–26) and two choose TWO tasks (Q27/28 and Q29/30).

💡 Listening tip: In Part 3 opinions matter. Watch for agreement vs disagreement signals — 'that's right', 'exactly' (agree) versus 'I'm not going to accept that', 'we'll just have to differ' (disagree). Q25 tests where they disagree.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The MCQ options are formal; the speakers use casual language. Match the meaning, not the words.
The speaker's message (Q23)
Option (A)
unfair to them at times
What you hear
'It was a bit harsh, though! We know it's a tough industry.'
Key paraphrase
'a bit harsh' = unfair at times → A
Your turn
A first-job mistake (Q27/28)
Option (B)
paying too much attention to how she looked
What you hear
'I'm always considering my own clothes… you should be focusing on your client'
Key paraphrase
considering her own clothes = too much attention to appearance → B
Your turn
