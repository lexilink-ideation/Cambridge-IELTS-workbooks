📋 Quick Review

A lecture on ecology and conservation about the spirit bear — a rare white bear that belongs to the black bear family and lives on a few islands off British Columbia. The talk is organised under four headings: general facts, habitat, threats and going forward.

这是一篇动物保护类讲座。四个小标题就是路标,跟着它们走,答案一定按顺序出现。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY for all ten answers, and every answer is a fairly common noun (gene, power, strangers, erosion, islands, roads, fishing, reproduction, method, expansion). If you write two words you lose the mark — e.g. Q34 is erosion, not 'soil erosion'.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Section 4 note completion is nearly always a compressed rewrite of a full spoken sentence. Practise expanding the notes into sentences, then compare with the audio. 把笔记还原成完整句子,再和录音对照。
Q31 — 'Its colour comes from'
Note wording
Its colour comes from an uncommon ___
Audio wording
'One in ten black bears is born with a white coat, which is the result of a special gene'
Key paraphrase
comes from = is the result of; uncommon = special / rare
Trap
'coat' is the colour itself, not the cause.
Your turn
Q33 — 'protect the bear from'
Note wording
They protect the bear from ___
Audio wording
'they do not speak of seeing it to anyone else. It is their way of protecting it when strangers visit the area'
Key paraphrase
protect from strangers = do not tell visitors about it
Trap
The audio never says the word 'from'; you must infer the danger is the strangers.
Your turn
Q36 — 'construction of ___'
Note wording
deforestation and construction of ___ by logging companies
Audio wording
'logging companies have stripped the land by cutting down trees. In addition, they have built roads'
Key paraphrase
deforestation = cutting down trees; construction of roads = built roads
Trap
'hibernation sites' comes immediately after and tempts you.
Your turn
Q37 — 'Unrestricted ___'
Note wording
Unrestricted ___ is affecting the salmon supply
Audio wording
'the number of salmon in those streams is declining because there is no legal limit on fishing'
Key paraphrase
unrestricted = no legal limit on
Trap
'logging of the trees along the streams' is a different threat in the same sentence group.
Your turn
Q39 — 'must improve their ___ of logging'
Note wording
Logging companies must improve their ___ of logging
Audio wording
'The government is now requiring logging companies to adopt a better logging method'
Key paraphrase
must improve = are required to adopt a better…
Trap
Word order changes: 'logging method' → 'method of logging'.
Your turn
