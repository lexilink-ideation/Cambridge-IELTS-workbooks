📋 Quick Review

This is a book review of Peter Bellerby’s The Globemakers. It tells how a simple wish to give his father a globe led Bellerby on a decade-long, obstacle-filled quest to revive the lost craft of bespoke globemaking, weaving in stories of great globemakers of the past such as Behaim and Coronelli.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 3 uses summary completion from a word list, YES/NO/NOT GIVEN, and MCQ about the writer’s meaning. Distinguish what the book reports from the reviewer’s opinion, and read a whole paragraph before answering a "what point is made?" question.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"What seemed simple enough to start with triggered an almost obsessive, decade-long journey, marked by a series of obstacles that would have deterred anyone less determined."
Main ClauseWhat seemed simple enough … triggered an almost obsessive, decade-long journey
Subordinate / Relative Clausemarked by a series of obstacles that would have deterred anyone less determined (reduced relative + relative)
Grammar Note"What seemed simple" is a noun clause subject. "marked by …" = which was marked by. "would have deterred" is a third-conditional-style hypothetical.
Simple MeaningSomething that looked easy at first became a ten-year obsession full of obstacles that would have stopped a less determined person.
中文起初看似简单的事，却引发了一段近乎痴迷、长达十年的旅程，其间的种种障碍足以让意志不那么坚定的人望而却步。
"After all, contrary to stubbornly held popular views of our ancestors’ geographical ignorance, we have known that the world is spherical since at least the 6th century BCE."
Main Clausewe have known that the world is spherical since at least the 6th century BCE
Subordinate / Relative Clausecontrary to stubbornly held popular views of our ancestors’ geographical ignorance (contrast phrase)
Grammar Note"contrary to …" flags a correction of a popular myth. Present perfect "have known … since" shows duration.
Simple MeaningDespite the common belief that our ancestors were geographically ignorant, people have known the Earth is round since at least 600 BCE.
中文毕竟，与人们顽固持有的、认为祖先地理无知的看法相反，我们至少从公元前6世纪起就知道地球是球形的。
"Having decided to compromise and opt for two half-spherical pieces that could be fitted together, he was unable to discover anyone capable of casting moulds with sufficient accuracy."
Main Clausehe was unable to discover anyone capable of casting moulds with sufficient accuracy
Subordinate / Relative ClauseHaving decided to compromise and opt for two half-spherical pieces that could be fitted together (participle clause)
Grammar Note"Having decided …" is a perfect participle showing an earlier action. "capable of casting" = able to cast.
Simple MeaningAfter choosing to make two half-spheres that join together, he couldn’t find anyone able to cast the moulds accurately enough.
中文在决定折中、选择可拼合的两个半球之后，他却找不到任何人能够足够精确地铸造模具。
"Bellerby clearly found a kindred spirit in Martin Behaim, the Nuremberg entrepreneur who in 1492 created the Erdapfel, the world’s oldest surviving globe."
Main ClauseBellerby clearly found a kindred spirit in Martin Behaim
Subordinate / Relative Clausethe Nuremberg entrepreneur who in 1492 created the Erdapfel … (appositive + relative)
Grammar Note"the Nuremberg entrepreneur who …" renames and describes Behaim. "the world’s oldest surviving globe" is a further appositive.
Simple MeaningBellerby felt a strong connection with Martin Behaim, who made the oldest surviving globe in 1492.
中文贝勒比显然在马丁·贝海姆身上找到了志同道合者——这位纽伦堡企业家于1492年制作了现存最古老的地球仪"地球苹果"。
"But it is more than enough to stir up admiration for the craftsmanship of the great mapmakers of the past and the obsessive determination of a modern successor who revived their almost moribund art."
Main Clauseit is more than enough to stir up admiration
Subordinate / Relative Clausefor the craftsmanship … and the obsessive determination of a modern successor who revived their almost moribund art
Grammar Note"more than enough to …" = easily able to. "who revived …" describes the modern successor (Bellerby).
Simple MeaningThe book easily inspires admiration for the old mapmakers’ skill and for Bellerby, who revived their nearly dead craft.
中文但它足以激起人们对昔日伟大制图师技艺的钦佩，以及对这位复兴了他们几近消亡技艺的现代传人执着精神的赞叹。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

This is a review — separate story, history and opinion.

Contrast & Correction

The reviewer corrects a myth: "contrary to … popular views of our ancestors’ geographical ignorance" — people have known the Earth is round since 600 BCE. Watch for such corrections.

Cause & Effect

A simple wish (a birthday gift) → no good globe available → Bellerby makes his own → a decade of technical problems → founding a bespoke company. Follow the chain of consequences.

Example / Historical Interlude

Behaim (1492 Erdapfel) and Coronelli (giant globes for Louis XIV) are historical examples woven in to enrich the story and mirror Bellerby’s ambition.

Writer’s Stance

The review is admiring: the book "stirs up admiration" for past craftsmanship and Bellerby’s "obsessive determination", while also being a "lament" for fading traditions.
