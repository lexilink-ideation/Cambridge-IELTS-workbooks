📋 Quick Review

A lecture on the early history of weather forecasting. It moves through time: ancient weather gods and rites, early Babylonian and Chinese observations, the scientific approach of Aristotle, weather proverbs, the invention of instruments (thermometer, barometer), and the role of the telegraph. This is a note-completion task: ONE WORD ONLY.

💡 Listening tip: This lecture is strongly chronological. Use the dates (650 BC, 340 BC, 1592, 1648, mid-19th century) as anchors, and predict whether each gap needs a person, an instrument or a phenomenon.
