Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 7 — Test 3 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Section 1 conversation, a university student visits the Student Job Centre to ask about part-time work. A job centre agent checks she is a registered full-time student, then goes through several part-time positions (reception, cleaning, library work) before finding one that fits her timetable — an Office Assistant role at the English Language Centre.

The agent then fills in an application form with the student's personal details (name, address, room number, skills) and arranges an interview time. This is a classic "form/notes completion" Section 1 task, so most answers are short factual details: names, places, numbers and times.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Section 1 is always a everyday conversation between two speakers, usually about a transaction, booking or application. Answers come in the exact order you hear them. Before you listen, underline the key nouns in each gap (e.g. "Position available", "Room No.") — these tell you what kind of word (a job title, a place, a number) you are listening for.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Job type
Question wordingType of job required
Audio meaning"I'm looking for a job — a part-time job."
Key paraphrase"type of job required" = "looking for... a part-time job"
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2 — Location
Question wordingWhere (is the position)
Audio meaning"a job working at the reception desk at the Sports Centre"
Key paraphrasethe location is embedded after "at the reception desk at..."
Your practice

Question wordingDuties
Audio meaning"you'll be required to deal with student enquiries and answer the phone"
