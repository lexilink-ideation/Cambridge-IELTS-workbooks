📋 Quick Review

Sara Lim, a student, phones a homestay adviser (Lynda) because she must move out of her aunt's flat. Lynda fills in an accommodation request form (age, time in Australia, address, course, move-in date), asks about her room and housemate preferences, and explains the rent, deposit and payment arrangements. Expect a form, multiple-choice and sentences.

💡 Listening tip: When a form is filled in, listen for the total that combines two figures — 'a year in Adelaide and six months in Sydney' becomes 1½ years. Do the small maths as you listen.
