📋 Quick Review

A speaker gives a talk about Minster Park — its history and its recent transformation. First there are multiple-choice questions about how the park began, a statue, its wartime use and the start of the works (Q11–14); then you label a map of the park (Q15–20), placing the statue, sculptures, playground, maze, tennis courts and fitness area.

💡 Listening tip: For map labelling, orient yourself first (find the gates, the river, North). Follow the speaker's directions — 'past', 'opposite', 'on your left', 'in the corner' — and place each feature step by step. Don't jump ahead.
