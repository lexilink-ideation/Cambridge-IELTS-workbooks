Cambridge IELTS 7 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Planning a Geography Presentation · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two university students, Julie and Trevor, meet to plan a class presentation about a group of islands off the coast of West Africa. They negotiate the timing, decide which topics to include (and which to leave out), and then divide up the job of finding maps, statistics and visual aids for their talk.

This is a typical Part 3 academic discussion: two (or more) speakers debating and negotiating, with a mix of short-answer, classification (A/B/C), and table completion questions. Listen for moments of disagreement and correction — they often signal the real answer.

💡 Part 3 strategy: When two speakers discuss a list of topics, don't assume the first person's suggestion is the final decision. Listen to how the other speaker responds — "no," "not this time," "that goes somewhere else" — because the final agreement is always the answer.

Task breakdown

Q21–22: Timing details of the presentation and whether it will be assessed.

Q23–26: Which topics will definitely, possibly, or not be included.

Q27–30: Where each visual aid or set of statistics will come from.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

IELTS Listening questions never use the exact same words as the audio. Study each contrast below, note the key paraphrase, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — Whether the Talk Will Be Assessed
Question wordingThe presentation will not be ______
Audio meaningTrevor asks if this presentation counts toward their grade. Julie confirms it does not, because it is their first one this term.
Key paraphrase"is this one going to be assessed?" / "No, not this time round" = it will not be assessed
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — The Economy Topic
Question wordingEconomy — A / B / C?
Audio meaningJulie raises the idea of covering the economy, but Trevor says their supervisor told them not to go into that level of detail — the topic gets deferred, not included.
Key paraphrase"Dr White said we shouldn't go into that sort of detail" = they will not include this topic
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — Role of English Language
Question wordingRole of English Language — A / B / C?
Audio meaningTrevor assumes this topic belongs in their presentation, but Julie corrects him — it actually belongs to a different event entirely (the Language Policy Seminar).
Key paraphrase"that goes in the Language Policy Seminar" = not included in this presentation
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — Source of the Islands Map
Question wordingMap of the islands — Where from? ______
Audio meaningTrevor worries the islands map will be difficult to find, but Julie remembers she already has a suitable one in a booklet she showed him previously.
Key paraphrase"there's a very clear map...in that tourist brochure I showed you" = the source is a tourist brochure
