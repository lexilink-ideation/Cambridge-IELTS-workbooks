📋 Quick Review

In this Part 3, two students, Mia and Leo, plan a joint presentation on houses of the future. They decide to focus on intergenerational living, then discuss specific ideas — roof gardens, shared working spaces, moveable internal walls, smart health mirrors, bike sheds with charging points, car-free areas, and communal vegetable plots.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 3 planning discussions, listen for decisions and reservations. When a speaker says "let’s go with that" they have chosen; when they say "I’m not so sure" or "but what if…", they are raising a doubt. The answer is often the point they finally agree on.
