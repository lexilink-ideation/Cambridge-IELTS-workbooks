Reading Passage 1

Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 1 — Reading Passage 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

This passage tells the story of Gwendoline and Margaret Davies, two wealthy Welsh sisters who, between 1908 and 1924, built one of Britain’s largest collections of French Impressionist paintings. It describes their background, how their collecting developed, the effect of the First World War, and the cultural legacy they left to the National Museum Wales.

💡 Reading strategy tip: Passage 1 is usually the most straightforward. For note/summary completion, copy the exact word from the text. For True/False/Not Given, choose FALSE only if the text contradicts the statement, and NOT GIVEN if the text simply says nothing about it.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"In 1907, when Gwendoline came into her inheritance (Margaret would follow in 1909), the sisters were said to be the wealthiest unmarried women in Britain."
Main Clausethe sisters were said to be the wealthiest unmarried women in Britain
Subordinate / Relative Clausewhen Gwendoline came into her inheritance — plus the bracketed aside "(Margaret would follow in 1909)"
Grammar NoteA "when" time-clause + a parenthetical remark inside brackets. "were said to be" = people said they were.
Simple MeaningWhen Gwendoline got her inheritance in 1907 (and Margaret in 1909), the two sisters were probably the richest single women in Britain.
中文1907年格温多琳继承遗产时（玛格丽特于1909年继承），据说姐妹俩是英国最富有的未婚女性。
"While it was long assumed that these men were largely responsible for the nature of the sisters’ collection, it has recently been accepted that Gwendoline and Margaret retained a far more active role in the process."
Main Clauseit has recently been accepted that … retained a far more active role
Subordinate / Relative ClauseWhile it was long assumed that these men were largely responsible …
Grammar Note"While" here means "although" (contrast). Two "that"-clauses report beliefs. Passive: "it was assumed / it has been accepted".
Simple MeaningPeople used to think the male advisers shaped the collection, but now we know the sisters themselves made the key choices.
中文虽然长期以来人们认为这些男性主导了收藏，但近来人们认可姐妹俩其实发挥了更主动的作用。
"By the early 1920s, Gwendoline felt increasingly uncomfortable buying art works when faced with the poverty and social upheaval created by the First World War."
Main ClauseGwendoline felt increasingly uncomfortable buying art works
Subordinate / Relative Clausewhen faced with the poverty and social upheaval created by the First World War
Grammar Note"created by …" is a reduced relative clause (= which had been created by). "when faced with" = when she was faced with.
Simple MeaningIn the early 1920s the poverty and disruption caused by the war made Gwendoline feel bad about spending money on art.
中文到20世纪20年代初，面对一战造成的贫困和社会动荡，格温多琳越来越不愿购买艺术品。
"The sisters collected French Impressionist paintings at a time when such art was routinely ignored by individuals and institutions alike."
Main ClauseThe sisters collected French Impressionist paintings
Subordinate / Relative Clauseat a time when such art was routinely ignored by individuals and institutions alike
Grammar Note"when" introduces a relative clause describing "a time". "alike" = both, equally. Passive "was ignored".
Simple MeaningThey collected this art during a period when both ordinary people and museums usually took no notice of it.
中文姐妹俩收藏法国印象派画作时，个人与机构普遍都对这类艺术视而不见。
"Although they relied on a trusted circle of advisers, they made frequent trips to London and Paris, and also regularly had paintings sent to their home for consideration."
Main Clausethey made frequent trips to London and Paris, and … had paintings sent to their home
Subordinate / Relative ClauseAlthough they relied on a trusted circle of advisers
Grammar Note"Although" = concession. "had paintings sent" = causative have (they arranged for paintings to be sent).
Simple MeaningEven though they trusted their advisers, the sisters still travelled a lot themselves and had art brought home to look at.
中文尽管她们依靠一群可信的顾问，但仍常去伦敦和巴黎，也经常让人把画送到家中供她们考虑。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Academic passages are built from logical relationships. Learn to spot the signals.

Cause & Effect

Their religious upbringing gave them a sense of social responsibility, so they chose to use their inheritance for charity. Signal words: gave them, so, chose to. Cause = upbringing; effect = philanthropic choices.

Contrast

“While the sisters were wealthy in relative terms, their income was nothing compared to American collectors.” The contrast (while / compared to) explains why they could not buy expensive Old Masters at first.

Chronology (time order)

The passage moves through dates: 1907–09 (inheritance) → 1908 (first purchases) → 1912 (first Impressionists) → 1914–18 (war) → early 1920s (Gwendoline stops) → 1951 & 1963 (donations). Tracking dates helps you locate answers fast.

Author’s Attitude

The writer calls the sisters “unlikely pioneer collectors” and praises their “lasting and meaningful cultural legacy”. The tone is admiring / positive, useful for YES/NO or attitude questions.
