📋 Passage Overview

'Flying tortoises' — how conservationists used a helicopter to return 300 captive-bred giant tortoises to the slopes of Sierra Negra on the Galapagos island of Isabela. The passage moves from how tortoises colonised the islands, through centuries of exploitation, to the breeding centre, the transport problem, the plan, the operation itself and a closing image. Question types: matching headings (Q1–7) and note completion (Q8–13).

💡 Reading tip: Seven paragraphs, eight headings — one is never used. Work out each paragraph's function first (background / problem / solution / action / image), then match. Headings 'iv Planning a bigger idea' and 'vi Carrying out a carefully prepared operation' are a plan/action pair, so they must go to consecutive paragraphs.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

As these ancestral tortoises settled on the individual islands, the different populations adapted to their unique environments, giving rise to at least 14 different subspecies.
Main Clausethe different populations adapted to their unique environments
Subordinate / Relative'As these ancestral tortoises settled on the individual islands' = time clause; 'giving rise to at least 14 different subspecies' = result participle clause.
Grammar Note'As' = while/when; the '-ing' clause at the end shows the consequence, not a simultaneous action.
Simple MeaningWhen the first tortoises spread to separate islands, each group adapted differently, and at least 14 subspecies developed.
中文当这些祖先陆龟在各个岛屿上定居后,不同的种群适应了各自独特的环境,由此产生了至少14个不同的亚种。
Relatively immobile and capable of surviving for months without food or water, the tortoises were taken on board these ships to act as food supplies during long ocean passages.
Main Clausethe tortoises were taken on board these ships
Subordinate / Relative'Relatively immobile and capable of surviving for months without food or water' = fronted adjective phrases (= 'Because they were…'); 'to act as food supplies' = infinitive of purpose.
Grammar NoteTwo adjective phrases moved to the front to give the reason; the main clause is passive.
Simple MeaningBecause they moved slowly and could live for months without eating or drinking, sailors took tortoises aboard as food.
中文由于行动相对缓慢,又能数月不吃不喝而存活,这些陆龟被带上船,在漫长的航程中充当食物。
Captive-bred tortoises can't be reintroduced into the wild until they're at least five years old and weigh at least 4.5 kilograms, at which point their size and weight — and their hardened shells — are sufficient to protect them from predators.
Main ClauseCaptive-bred tortoises can't be reintroduced into the wild
Subordinate / Relative'until they're at least five years old and weigh at least 4.5 kilograms' = time clause; 'at which point their size and weight… are sufficient to protect them' = non-defining relative clause referring to that moment.
Grammar Note'not … until' = only after; 'at which point' refers back to a whole time expression, not a single noun.
Simple MeaningTortoises bred in captivity can only be released once they are five years old and 4.5 kg, because by then they are big enough to survive predators.
中文人工繁育的陆龟必须等到至少五岁、体重至少4.5公斤时才能放归野外;到那时,它们的体型、体重以及硬化的甲壳才足以抵御捕食者。
But in November 2010, the environmentalist and Galapagos National Park liaison officer Godfrey Merlin, a visiting private motor yacht captain and a helicopter pilot gathered around a table in a small cafe in Puerto Ayora on the island of Santa Cruz to work out more ambitious reintroduction.
Main ClauseGodfrey Merlin, a … captain and a helicopter pilot gathered around a table
Subordinate / Relative'the environmentalist and Galapagos National Park liaison officer' = long pre-modifier naming Merlin's roles; 'to work out more ambitious reintroduction' = infinitive of purpose.
Grammar NoteA three-part compound subject buried inside heavy modification. Find the verb ('gathered') first, then read backwards.
Simple MeaningIn November 2010 three men — a park officer, a yacht captain and a helicopter pilot — met in a café to plan a bigger reintroduction.
中文但在2010年11月,环保人士兼加拉帕戈斯国家公园联络官戈弗雷·梅林、一位来访的私人游艇船长和一名直升机飞行员,在圣克鲁斯岛阿约拉港的一家小咖啡馆里围桌而坐,共同筹划一次更为宏大的放归行动。
Originally an air ambulance, the yacht's helicopter has a rear double door and a large internal space that's well suited for cargo, so a custom crate was designed to hold up to 33 tortoises with a total weight of about 150 kilograms.
Main Clausethe yacht's helicopter has a rear double door and a large internal space / a custom crate was designed
Subordinate / Relative'Originally an air ambulance' = fronted appositive; 'that's well suited for cargo' = defining relative clause; 'so' joins the result clause.
Grammar NoteAppositive at the front + relative clause + result clause with 'so'.
Simple MeaningThe helicopter had once been an air ambulance, so it had wide rear doors and space for cargo; a special crate was built to carry 33 tortoises weighing about 150 kg.
中文这架游艇上的直升机原本是空中救护机,尾部有双开门和适合装货的大空间,因此专门设计了一个货箱,可装载多达33只、总重约150公斤的陆龟。
Eventually, one tiny tortoise came across a fully grown giant who had been lumbering around the island for around a hundred years.
Main Clauseone tiny tortoise came across a fully grown giant
Subordinate / Relative'who had been lumbering around the island for around a hundred years' = defining relative clause with past perfect continuous.
Grammar NotePast perfect continuous ('had been lumbering') shows an action continuing right up to the moment of meeting.
Simple MeaningIn the end a very small tortoise met a huge adult that had been walking the island for about a century.
中文最终,一只小陆龟遇到了一只已在岛上缓慢踱步约一百年的成年巨龟。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology drives the structure. A (5 million years ago → colonisation) → B (17th century → 1790s → 20th century → settlers) → C (1989 breeding centre) → D (the size/age window) → E (November 2010 planning) → F (the operation) → G (release). Once you place a paragraph in time, its heading almost chooses itself.

Cause and effect. No predators → giant size and long life. Whaling ships need food → tortoises taken → population collapse. Successful captive breeding → overpopulation problem. Each arrow is stated explicitly, so scan for 'due to', 'saw this… grow', 'proved to be… and it eventually had to deal with'.

Problem framed as a narrow window. Paragraph D is unusual: it contains no events at all, only a constraint. Tortoises must be old enough to survive but not so old they cannot be carried. That is precisely heading i, 'The importance of getting the timing right'.

Plan then execution. Paragraph E is people meeting to decide ('to work out more ambitious reintroduction'); paragraph F is the operation happening ('a custom crate was designed', 'volunteers worked around the clock', 'wardens cleared landing sites'). Plan → action is one of the most common heading pairs in IELTS.

Closing image as argument. The last two sentences are not information but symbolism: 'The two stood side by side, a powerful symbol of the regeneration of an ancient species.' A paragraph that ends on an image rather than a fact is signalling its own heading — here, 'Young meets old'.
