Cambridge IELTS 14 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

📋 Quick Review

Joe is doing teaching practice and has been put in charge of a school marching band. He talks it over with Lizzie: the band's size and events, what he has done to motivate them (a drummer's speech, a film, a parade), and several difficult band members he is finding hard to manage. Expect questions about the band and a matching task on the members.

💡 Listening tip: When several people are described, note a keyword for each (the 'unreliable' one, the 'bossy' one, the one with 'breathing' trouble). Match on the described problem, not the instrument.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Match on the described problem, not the instrument. Train the swap.
The flautist
Question idea
What is the problem with the flautist?
What you hear
'she gives me a very plausible reason … but to be honest, I don't believe her'
Key paraphrase
'plausible … don't believe her' = she is unreliable / makes excuses
Your turn
The trombonist
Question idea
Why is the trombonist a problem?
What you hear
'he has breathing difficulties … doesn't have enough breath for the trombone'
Key paraphrase
the issue is physical: breathing, so he can't play all the notes
Your turn
