Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 8 — Test 1 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two friends, George and Nina, talk about a brochure George found in the library for a summer music festival of mostly Spanish music. They discuss which concerts to attend and then fill in a booking form — giving an address, postcode, phone number, and choosing tickets, prices and dates. This is a typical Part 1 everyday conversation with a form to complete.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 1 answers come in order. Personal details (address, postcode, phone) are usually spelt out or read digit by digit, so write letters and numbers exactly as you hear them. Watch the word-limit instruction: here it is NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER.

📚 Vocabulary Review

Word / PhraseSimple Meaning中文Example SentenceIELTS Note
brochurea small booklet with information宣传册"He found a brochure from a summer festival."Often confused with 'book' — a distractor in Q Example.
festivala series of special events/performances音乐节；节日"They booked tickets for the music festival."Topic word; spelling: festi-val.
refreshmentslight food and drinks茶点；饮料"The ticket price includes free refreshments in the garden."Paraphrase of 'drinks' — key for Q6.
postcodecode of letters and numbers for an address邮政编码"Our new postcode is WS6 2YH."Spelt out letter+number; write exactly (Q4).
discountmoney taken off the normal price折扣"Students get a 50% discount on everything."'50% discount' = 'half price' (Q10).
senior citizenan older / retired person老年人"Children, students and senior citizens pay less."Listed with children & students as a group.
bargainsomething cheap; good value便宜货；划算"It sounds like a bargain to me!"Signals a good deal; positive attitude.
keenvery interested / enthusiastic热衷的"I'm feeling quite keen now."'keen on' + noun/-ing = want to do.
persuademake someone want to do something说服"Something on the form might persuade me."Signals a change of decision.
instrumentalmusic played by instruments (no singing)器乐的"The instrumental group Guitarrini plays first."Contrast with 'singer' / 'vocal'.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS the question words are almost never the words you hear or read. Recognising paraphrase is the key skill. Study each example, then write your own sentence.
Paraphrase Example 1
Question / promptIn the lobby of the library George saw …
What you hear / read"They had a video with all the highlights of the festival at a stand in the lobby."
Key paraphrase'a video about the festival' = a video with highlights → answer C, not 'a display of instruments'.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 2
Question / promptGeorge wants to sit at the back so they can …
What you hear / read"If you sit at the back you can actually hear the whole thing better."
Key paraphrase'hear clearly' = hear the whole thing better → answer B.
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 3
Question / promptThe ticket price of £6 includes …
What you hear / read"Singer, ticket £6.00 includes drinks in the garden."
Key paraphrase'free refreshments' = drinks (in the garden).
Your practice
Paraphrase Example 4
Question / promptReduced price for certain groups …
What you hear / read"Children, Students and Senior Citizens get a 50% discount on everything."
Key paraphrase'50% discount' = half price for these groups → answer 50%.
