📋 Passage Overview

The importance of children's play. The passage argues that play is not trivial but a key part of children's development. It uses the example of six-year-old Alice, cites Dr David Whitebread of Cambridge, and reports research showing that playful activities (with dolls and with Lego) help children write better and support creativity, self-control and problem-solving.

💡 Reading tip: For notes/summary completion, the answers appear in order and are usually a single noun. Match the note's meaning to a paraphrase in the text, then copy the exact word.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Although she isn't aware of it, this fantasy is helping her take her first steps towards her capacity for creativity.
Main Clausethis fantasy is helping her take her first steps towards her capacity for creativity
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough she isn't aware of it (concession clause)
Grammar Note'Although' introduces a contrast: the child does not realise the benefit that is nonetheless happening.
Simple MeaningEven though the child doesn't know it, pretending helps her start to become creative.
中文尽管她自己没有意识到，这种幻想正帮助她迈出培养创造力的第一步。
Play in all its rich variety is one of the highest achievements of the human species.
Main ClausePlay … is one of the highest achievements of the human species
Subordinate / Relativein all its rich variety (added noun phrase)
Grammar NoteThe inserted phrase adds detail without changing the core statement.
Simple MeaningPlay, in all its many forms, is one of humanity's greatest achievements.
中文游戏及其丰富多样的形式，是人类最伟大的成就之一。
Children wrote longer and better-structured stories when they first played with dolls representing characters in the story.
Main ClauseChildren wrote longer and better-structured stories
Subordinate / Relativewhen they first played with dolls representing characters (time clause + reduced relative)
Grammar Note'when…' gives the condition; 'representing…' is a reduced relative describing the dolls.
Simple MeaningIf children played with dolls of the characters first, they wrote longer, better stories.
中文当孩子们先用代表故事人物的玩偶玩耍时，他们写出的故事更长、结构更好。
Somehow the importance of play has been lost in recent decades; it's regarded as something trivial.
Main Clausethe importance of play has been lost in recent decades
Subordinate / Relativeit's regarded as something trivial (added independent clause)
Grammar NotePresent perfect ('has been lost') links a past change to the present situation.
Simple MeaningIn recent years people have forgotten how important play is and think it unimportant.
中文不知为何，近几十年来游戏的重要性被忽视了；它被视为无关紧要的东西。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Example → general claim. The writer opens with a concrete example (Alice building a kingdom, playing schools, playing a board game) and then generalises to a broad claim about creativity, emotion-regulation and rule-following. Spotting this 'specific-to-general' move helps you predict the topic sentence.

Contrast with 'work'. Watch the contrast the writer builds between play and 'work'. Words like trivial, negative and contrasts with signal the view the writer is arguing against.

Research as evidence. Findings (dolls study, Lego study) are used as evidence. In True/False/Not Given, check exactly what the research showed — e.g. the Lego result was that children always had ideas, so 'had problems thinking up ideas' is FALSE (Q12).
