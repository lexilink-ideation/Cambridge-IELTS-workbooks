Cambridge IELTS 17 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

📋 Quick Review

A single speaker, tour guide Lou Miller, introduces a boat trip round the Tasmanian coast. She covers safety and seating, the boats' new colour, free lunchboxes and litter rules (Q11–14, multiple choice), then a lighthouse, the sea creatures you might see, and the caves (Q15–20, 'choose TWO' questions).

💡 Listening tip: For 'choose TWO letters', the two right options can appear far apart. Keep all five options in view and cross off wrong ones as you hear them contradicted or simply not mentioned.
