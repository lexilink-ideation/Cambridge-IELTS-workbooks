Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Brian Ardley, a prospective part-time student, visits a college tutor to ask about enrolling on a management course. They discuss what he should prepare beforehand, then Brian asks about several college facilities — the refectory, nursery, careers advice, fitness centre, library, and computer access — before the tutor introduces him to the college's Business Centre, a training resource with books, videos, and hireable equipment.

This is a classic IELTS Listening Part 3 conversation — two speakers (a student and a tutor) discussing an academic topic, tested with multiple choice and table/summary completion.

💡 Listening strategy tip: In Part 3, questions often follow a "topic-by-topic" structure (refectory → nursery → careers → fitness → library → computers → Business Centre). If you lose track of one answer, jump ahead to the next topic heading rather than falling behind.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In IELTS Listening, the audio never uses the exact words in the question. Recognising paraphrases is the key skill. Study each example below, then try your own.
Paraphrase Example 1 — Attending a Class
Question wordingWhat is Brian going to do before the course starts? → attend a class
Audio meaning"would it be possible to sit in on a teaching session?"
Key paraphrase"sit in on a teaching session" = attend a class
