📋 Passage Overview

This passage, 'Early Childhood Education', discusses New Zealand's national early-childhood curriculum, Te Whāriki, and the research behind investing in the early years. It reports findings that good early education brings long-term social and economic benefits, describes a major longitudinal study, and considers questions of funding, quality and access to childcare and pre-school programmes.

💡 Reading strategy tip: This passage has lettered sections A–F. For 'which section contains…' questions, do NOT read straight through — scan each section's topic sentence and match the specific information.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"Research suggests that the quality of early-childhood education has a lasting effect on children’s later achievement."
Main ClauseResearch suggests that the quality of early-childhood education has a lasting effect
Subordinate / Relativeon children's later achievement (prepositional phrase); that … (noun clause reporting the finding)
Grammar Note'suggests that' reports a claim cautiously; 'lasting effect on' is a fixed collocation.
Simple MeaningStudies say good early education keeps helping children do well later.
中文研究表明，幼儿教育的质量对儿童日后的成就有持久影响。
"Children who attended high-quality programmes were more likely to finish school and less likely to need welfare support."
Main ClauseChildren … were more likely to finish school and less likely to need welfare support
Subordinate / Relativewho attended high-quality programmes (defining relative clause)
Grammar NoteThe relative clause defines which children; 'more/less likely to' compares outcomes.
Simple MeaningKids in good programmes tended to finish school and need less welfare.
中文参加过高质量项目的儿童更有可能完成学业，也更少需要福利救助。
"Although such programmes are expensive to run, economists argue that the long-term savings outweigh the costs."
Main Clauseeconomists argue that the long-term savings outweigh the costs
Subordinate / RelativeAlthough such programmes are expensive to run (concessive clause)
Grammar Note'Although' concedes the cost; 'outweigh' = are greater/more important than.
Simple MeaningThese programmes cost a lot, but experts say the future savings are bigger.
中文尽管此类项目运营成本高昂，但经济学家认为长期的节省超过了成本。
"Te Whāriki takes a holistic approach, treating a child’s social, emotional and cognitive growth as interconnected."
Main ClauseTe Whāriki takes a holistic approach
Subordinate / Relativetreating a child's social, emotional and cognitive growth as interconnected (participle phrase)
Grammar NoteThe -ing participle explains what the 'holistic approach' means; 'as interconnected' = as linked.
Simple MeaningTe Whāriki sees a child's feelings, social skills and thinking as all connected.
中文《Te Whāriki》采取整体方法，将儿童的社会、情感和认知发展视为相互关联的。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Claim + Evidence — 'Early education has lasting effects' is supported by longitudinal study results (finishing school, less welfare). Evidence backs the claim.

Cost–Benefit — Programmes are expensive, but long-term savings outweigh costs. Concession ('although') then the writer's point.

Comparison — Disadvantaged children benefit MOST; effects are compared across groups. Watch 'more … than', 'most'.

Definition — Te Whāriki is holistic: social + emotional + cognitive growth seen as one. Definitions are common question targets.
