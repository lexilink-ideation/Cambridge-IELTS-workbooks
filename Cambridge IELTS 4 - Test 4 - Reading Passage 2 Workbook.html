📋 Passage Overview

The Nature and Aims of Archaeology explains what archaeology is — part discovery, part scientific analysis, part imagination — and how it relates to anthropology and history. Anthropology divides into physical, cultural and archaeology; archaeology studies past societies through their material culture and works, like a scientist, from evidence to conclusions.

💡 Reading tip: Yes / No / Not Given follows the order of the text. Underline the qualifying words in each statement ('must', 'more demanding', 'realistic') and check whether the writer actually makes that claim.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Archaeology is partly the discovery of the treasures of the past, partly the careful work of the scientific analyst, partly the exercise of the creative imagination.
Main ClauseArchaeology is partly … , partly … , partly …
Subordinate / Relative(three parallel complements after 'is')
Grammar Noterepeated 'partly + noun phrase' balances three aspects of the field.
Simple MeaningArchaeology is part treasure-hunting, part careful analysis, and part creative imagination.
中文考古学一部分是对过去珍宝的发现，一部分是科学分析者的细致工作，一部分是创造性想象力的运用。
Whereas cultural anthropologists will often base their conclusions on the experience of living within contemporary communities, archaeologists study past societies primarily through their material remains.
Main Clausearchaeologists study past societies primarily through their material remains
Subordinate / RelativeWhereas cultural anthropologists will often base their conclusions on… (contrast clause)
Grammar Note'Whereas' sets up a contrast between two disciplines.
Simple MeaningCultural anthropologists learn from living communities, but archaeologists study the past mainly through its physical remains.
中文文化人类学家常常依据在当代社群中生活的经验得出结论，而考古学家主要通过物质遗存来研究过去的社会。
To archaeology, which studies all cultures and periods, the distinction between history and pre-history is a convenient dividing line that in no way lessens the importance of the useful information contained in oral histories.
Main Clausethe distinction between history and pre-history is a convenient dividing line
Subordinate / Relativewhich studies all cultures and periods (non-defining relative); that in no way lessens the importance… (relative clause)
Grammar Notetwo relative clauses; 'in no way' is an emphatic negative.
Simple MeaningFor archaeology, the history/pre-history line is just convenient and doesn't reduce the value of oral histories.
中文对研究一切文化和时期的考古学来说，历史与史前史之间的区分只是一条方便的分界线，它丝毫不减损口述历史中有用信息的重要性。
In this respect, the practice of the archaeologist is rather like that of the scientist, who collects data, conducts experiments, formulates a hypothesis, tests the hypothesis against more data, and then devises a model.
Main Clausethe practice of the archaeologist is rather like that of the scientist
Subordinate / Relativewho collects data, conducts experiments, formulates a hypothesis… (relative clause with a list)
Grammar Note'that of' avoids repeating 'the practice'; the relative clause lists the scientist's steps.
Simple MeaningIn this way an archaeologist works like a scientist — gathering data, testing a hypothesis, and building a model.
中文在这一点上，考古学家的做法与科学家颇为相似：科学家收集数据、进行实验、提出假设、用更多数据检验假设，然后建立一个模型。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition by parts: the opening 'partly… partly… partly…' defines archaeology as discovery + analysis + imagination.

Classification: anthropology 'breaks down into' physical, cultural anthropology and archaeology — a hierarchy of fields.

Contrast: archaeology vs history — for 99% of the human story, material culture is the 'only significant source'.

Analogy: the archaeologist works 'rather like a scientist' — from data and hypotheses to a model of the past.
