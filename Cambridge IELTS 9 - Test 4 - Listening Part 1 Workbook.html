Listening Part 1

Cambridge IELTS 9 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A man who has just moved to the area asks a receptionist where his family can register with a doctor. She describes three health centres and their doctors, explains which services are free at the Shore Lane Centre, and lists four talks for patients. Question types: table completion (Q1–4, ONE WORD ONLY), choose TWO letters (Q5–6) and a second table (Q7–10).

💡 Listening tip: Q5–6 is the hard part. The speaker runs through six services, mixing free ones and paid ones in a single flow. Write F or £ beside each service as you hear it rather than trying to remember. The two free ones (vaccinations for trips abroad, fitness check-ups for a new employer) are separated by three paid ones — do not stop listening after the first.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The table uses short headings; the speaker uses long conversational sentences. Bridge the two.
Q1 — Dr Green's advantage
Table cell
especially good with ______
What you hear
'We always recommend her for babies, because she's very good with them'
Key paraphrase
'very good with them' where 'them' = babies → babies
Your turn
Q3 — Dr Fuller's advantage
Table cell
offers ______ appointments
What you hear
'it's particularly good if you're busy during the day, because they also do appointments in the evening'
Key paraphrase
'do appointments in the evening' = offers evening appointments (Saturday closing is a distractor)
Your turn
Q5–6 — Free services
Question
Which TWO are offered free of charge?
What you hear
'you won't have to pay for this' … 'you can get a free fitness check-up there'
Key paraphrase
two different phrasings of 'free' — 'won't have to pay' and 'free'; everything else uses 'paying basis' or 'pay for'
Your turn
Q9 — The time
Table cell
9th March at ______
What you hear
'Oh, it's a late afternoon talk, at four thirty, and it'll be in Room 6'
Key paraphrase
'four thirty' → 4.30 (the room is already printed, so don't write it)
Your turn
