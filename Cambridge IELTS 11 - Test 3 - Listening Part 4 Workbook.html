📋 Quick Review

A lecture on ethnography in business — research that explores human cultures, now used by companies to understand customers. The speaker gives five examples (kitchen equipment, cell phones in Uganda, computer companies, hospitals, airlines) and then lists the principles of this kind of research.

Part 4 商业研究方法讲座:例子部分靠“行业名”定位,原则部分靠“项目符号”定位。

💡 Listening tip: ONE WORD ONLY. Each example begins with an industry name (kitchen equipment / cell phones / computer companies / hospitals / airlines) printed in your notes — these are perfect anchors. If you miss one gap, jump forward to the next industry name.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Part 4 rewards recognising when a long clause has been compressed into a short note.
Q32 — the numbers
Question note
cooks could not easily see the ___ in measuring cups
What you hear
'although the measuring cups had numbers inside them, the cooks couldn't see these easily'
Key paraphrase
the note simply reverses the clause order → numbers
Your turn
Q33 — the time
Question note
These customers wanted to check the ___ used
What you hear
'they were eager to know how much time they'd spent on the call so far'
Key paraphrase
'eager to know how much time' = 'wanted to check the time' → time / minutes
Your turn
Q35 — patients
Question note
Nurses needed to access information about ___
What you hear
'the nurses needed to access the computer records of their patients no matter where they were'
Key paraphrase
'computer records of their patients' = 'information about patients'
Your turn
Q38 — comfortable
Question note
The participants must feel ___ about taking part
What you hear
'they have to be comfortable talking about themselves and being watched as they go about their activities'
Key paraphrase
the adjective is said directly, but inside a long clause → comfortable
Your turn
Q40 — analysis
Question note
A lot of time is needed for the ___ of the data
What you hear
'70 to 80 per cent of their time is spent not on the collecting of data but on its analysis'
Key paraphrase
'not on X but on Y' — the answer is always Y → analysis
Your turn

🧠 Mini Dictation

Academic lecture chunks. Notice the 'not X but Y' patterns.
1.Ethnography is a type of research aimed at exploring the way human cultures work.
2.Valuable information about their attitudes towards existing products.
3.The cooks had to check and recheck the contents.
4.They were eager to know how much time they'd spent on the call so far.
5.The nurses needed to access the computer records of their patients.
6.The researcher has to be completely open-minded.
7.It's the participant rather than the researchers who decides what direction the interview will follow.
8.Not on the collecting of data but on its analysis.
