Cambridge IELTS 17 — Test 2 — Listening Part 3

📋 Quick Review

Two students discuss their reviews of The Emporium's production of Romeo and Juliet. They agree on what a review must include (Q21–22), give an opinion on each aspect of the production (Q23–27, matching), and answer questions on the play's relevance and appeal (Q28–30, multiple choice).

💡 Listening tip: For opinion-matching, watch for agreement/disagreement words: 'I thought so too', 'both of us', 'we couldn't decide'. The letters (A–G) describe whether they agreed, disagreed, or were disappointed — not the aspect itself.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The opinion letters reword what the speakers feel. Match the attitude.
The set (Q23)
Option D
they agree this was a major strength
What you hear
'the set was easily the best thing — we both loved it'
Key paraphrase
'best thing / both loved' = agree it was a major strength
Your turn
Costume design (Q25)
Option A
both expected this to be more traditional
What you hear
'I thought the costumes would be historical, but they were modern'
Key paraphrase
expected historical = expected more traditional
Your turn

Option C
