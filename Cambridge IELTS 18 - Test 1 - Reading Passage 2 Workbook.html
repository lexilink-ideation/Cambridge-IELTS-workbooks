📋 Passage Overview

Forest management in Pennsylvania, USA. The passage explains how markets for low-use ('junk') wood as bioenergy can encourage healthier, more sustainable forests. It describes several cutting approaches — TSI Cut, Salvage Cut and Shelterwood Cut — and stresses that some low-use wood should be left behind. Question types: paragraph matching, matching purposes to cuts and sentence completion.

💡 Reading tip: For 'which paragraph contains…' questions, read the whole option first, then scan for the idea, not one keyword. The answers are not in order, and a letter may be used more than once (Q14 and Q18 are both paragraph B).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

A large, straight black cherry tree has high value as timber to be cut into logs or made into furniture, but for a landowner more interested in wildlife habitat, the real value of that stem may be the food it provides to animals.
Main ClauseA tree has high value as timber, but its real value may be the food it provides.
Subordinate / Relative'to be cut into logs or made into furniture' (infinitive of purpose) + 'it provides to animals' (contact relative).
Grammar Note'but for a landowner more interested in…' shifts viewpoint; the value depends on the goal.
Simple MeaningThe same tree is 'valuable' as furniture wood to one person and as animal food to another.
中文一棵高大笔直的黑樱桃树作为可锯成原木或制成家具的木材价值很高，但对更关注野生动物栖息地的地主而言，它真正的价值也许是为动物提供的食物。
These trees have lower economic value for traditional timber markets, compete for growth with higher-value trees, shade out desirable regeneration and decrease the health of a stand, leaving it more vulnerable to poor weather and disease.
Main ClauseThese trees have lower economic value, compete, shade out regeneration and decrease the health of a stand.
Subordinate / Relative'leaving it more vulnerable to poor weather and disease' (participial result clause).
Grammar NoteFour verbs share the subject 'these trees'; '-ing' shows the result.
Simple MeaningJunk trees are worth less and harm the forest, making it weaker against weather and disease.
中文这些树在传统木材市场经济价值较低，会与高价值树争夺生长空间，遮蔽理想的更新，并降低林分的健康，使其更易受恶劣天气和病害影响。
A Shelterwood Cut, which only takes place in a mature forest that has already been thinned several times, involves removing all the mature trees when other seedlings have become established.
Main ClauseA Shelterwood Cut involves removing all the mature trees.
Subordinate / Relative'which only takes place in a mature forest' + 'that has already been thinned several times' (nested relatives) + 'when other seedlings have become established' (time clause).
Grammar NoteA relative inside a relative; 'when' sets the timing of the cut.
Simple MeaningIn an already-thinned mature forest, once seedlings are growing, all the old trees are removed.
中文择伐更新（Shelterwood Cut）只在已多次疏伐的成熟林中进行，指在其他幼苗已扎根后，移除所有成熟树木。
The tops of trees that have been cut down should be left on the site so that their nutrients cycle back into the soil.
Main ClauseThe tops of trees should be left on the site.
Subordinate / Relative'that have been cut down' (relative) + 'so that their nutrients cycle back into the soil' (purpose clause).
Grammar Note'so that' introduces purpose; passive 'have been cut down'.
Simple MeaningLeave the tree-tops where they fall so the goodness returns to the soil.
中文被砍伐树木的树梢应留在原地，以便其养分循环回到土壤中。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Definition: The writer defines each cut in turn (TSI, Salvage, Shelterwood). Matching a purpose to a cut (Q19–21) means reading the definition, not just the name.

Cause & effect: 'high-grading… has left a legacy of low-use wood.' A past practice (cause) explains today's junk-tree problem (effect) — the key to Q14 and Q18.

Contrast / concession: 'However, it is important to remember that some retention of… low-use wood is very important.' The passage turns from removing junk wood to keeping some of it.

Exemplification: 'many species like hawthorn provide food for wildlife.' The example proves that 'not all small trees are low-use' — and gives the Q25 answer.
