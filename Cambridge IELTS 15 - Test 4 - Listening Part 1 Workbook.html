Cambridge IELTS 15 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A rail company representative interviews a passenger, Sophie Bird, for a Customer Satisfaction Survey. She gives her details and journey information (occupation, reason for travel, station, ticket type, cost, where bought) and then rates her satisfaction with the journey and the station facilities. Expect a place name to spell, a price, and everyday travel vocabulary.

💡 Listening tip: Form completion, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. Follow the form fields top to bottom, predict the answer type (a job? a place? a price?), and listen carefully for the price format (e.g. £23.70).
