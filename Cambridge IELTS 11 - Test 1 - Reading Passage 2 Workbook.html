📋 Passage Overview

The Falkirk Wheel in Scotland is the world's first and only rotating boat lift, opened in 2002. The passage explains why it was built (the two canals were 35 m apart in height), how it was designed and assembled, and — in detail — how a boat is actually lifted, using Archimedes' principle of displacement.

题型:TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN Q14–19 + 流程图标注 Q20–26(ONE WORD ONLY)。

💡 Reading tip: The diagram questions (Q20–26) all come from two paragraphs near the end. Find the paragraph that describes the lifting process step by step, then read it slowly in order — the labels follow the same order as the text.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

The major challenge of the project lay in the fact that the Forth & Clyde Canal is situated 35 metres below the level of the Union Canal.
Main ClauseThe major challenge of the project lay in the fact
Subordinate / Relativethat the Forth & Clyde Canal is situated 35 metres below the level of the Union Canal (noun clause in apposition to 'the fact')
Grammar Note'the fact that…' introduces an appositive noun clause — a very common academic pattern.
Simple MeaningThe hardest part was that one canal is 35 metres lower than the other.
中文该项目的主要挑战在于,福斯-克莱德运河比联合运河低35米。
Historically, the two canals had been joined near the town of Falkirk by a sequence of 11 locks — enclosed sections of canal in which the water level could be raised or lowered — that stepped down across a distance of 1.5 km.
Main Clausethe two canals had been joined … by a sequence of 11 locks
Subordinate / Relative'enclosed sections of canal in which the water level could be raised or lowered' = appositive definition; 'that stepped down across a distance of 1.5 km' = relative clause describing the locks
Grammar NotePast perfect passive + a dash-bracketed definition + a relative clause. The dashes give you a free glossary.
Simple MeaningIn the past, 11 locks — small sections where the water goes up or down — linked the canals over 1.5 km.
中文历史上,两条运河曾在福尔柯克镇附近由11座船闸相连——船闸是可以升降水位的封闭河段——它们在1.5公里的距离上逐级下降。
These gondolas always weigh the same, whether or not they are carrying boats.
Main ClauseThese gondolas always weigh the same
Subordinate / Relativewhether or not they are carrying boats (concessive clause of alternative condition)
Grammar Note'whether or not' = 'it does not matter if'. Directly tests Q18 (FALSE).
Simple MeaningThe gondolas weigh the same with or without a boat inside.
中文无论是否载船,这些吊舱的重量始终相同。
So when a boat enters a gondola, the amount of water leaving the gondola weighs exactly the same as the boat.
Main Clausethe amount of water leaving the gondola weighs exactly the same as the boat
Subordinate / Relativewhen a boat enters a gondola (time clause); 'leaving the gondola' = reduced relative ('that leaves')
Grammar NoteTime clause + reduced relative + comparative 'the same as'.
Simple MeaningWhen a boat comes in, exactly its own weight of water flows out.
中文因此当一艘船进入吊舱时,流出吊舱的水的重量恰好与该船相同。
The Wheel could not be constructed to elevate boats over the full 35-metre difference between the two canals, owing to the presence of the historically important Antonine Wall, which was built by the Romans in the second century AD.
Main ClauseThe Wheel could not be constructed to elevate boats over the full 35-metre difference
Subordinate / Relativeowing to the presence of the … Antonine Wall (reason phrase); which was built by the Romans in the second century AD (non-defining relative clause)
Grammar Note'owing to' = 'because of' + noun; the non-defining relative adds extra information after a comma.
Simple MeaningThe Wheel could not lift boats the whole 35 metres because the ancient Antonine Wall was in the way.
中文由于存在具有重要历史价值的安东尼长城(由罗马人于公元2世纪修建),该轮无法把船只提升到两条运河间35米的全部高差。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology. The passage moves in time: 11 locks dismantled in 1933 → project launched 1994 → design chosen → assembled in Derbyshire → dismantled summer 2001 → opened 2002. Time markers make Q14–Q16 easy to locate.

Process description. The last three paragraphs are a step-by-step process: gates raised → water pumped out → clamp removed → motors rotate the axle → arms and gondolas turn → cogs keep gondolas level → boat exits onto the aqueduct → locks finish the climb. Diagram labels always follow process order.

Cause and effect. Displacement → gondolas always weigh the same → the Wheel stays balanced → very little power is needed (1.5 kWh, 'roughly the same as boiling eight small domestic kettles').

Comparison for the reader. The writer explains scale by comparison: 'like one giant toy building set', 'the same as boiling eight small kettles'. These comparisons are never the answer, but they help you keep reading fast.
