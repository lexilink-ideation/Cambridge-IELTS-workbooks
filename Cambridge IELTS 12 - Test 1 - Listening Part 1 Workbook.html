📋 Quick Review

A visitor phones a tourist centre employee to ask about family excursions. The employee describes three options — a lake cruise on an old steamship, a farm visit, and a cycling trip on the Back Road — and finishes with the total price. Expect everyday travel vocabulary, one spelled-out name, and a final number.

💡 Listening tip: This is note completion — ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER. The answers come in order under three headings (Cruise / Farm visit / Cycling trips), so use the headings as signposts. The price at the end is corrected mid-sentence — always write the last figure you hear.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The notes never copy the speaker word-for-word. Train the swap between the printed note and the audio.
Q1 — what surrounds the lake
Question note
Can take photos of the ___ that surround the lake
What you hear
'don't forget to take pictures of the mountains. They're all around you'
Key paraphrase
take photos = take pictures; that surround the lake = they're all around you → mountains
Your turn
Q3 — where visitors can walk
Question note
Visitors can walk in the farm's ___ by the lake
What you hear
'some very beautiful gardens along the side of the lake which also belong to the farm'
Key paraphrase
by the lake = along the side of the lake; the farm's = belong to the farm → gardens
Your turn
Q6 — who the trail suits
Question note
Only suitable for cyclists who have some ___
What you hear
'Quite challenging in places… It needs someone who's got a bit more experience.'
Key paraphrase
who have some ___ = who's got a bit more ___ → experience
Your turn
Q9 — what the area lacks
Question note
There are no ___ or accommodation in the area
What you hear
'you won't see any shops round there, or anywhere to stay'
Key paraphrase
no … = you won't see any…; accommodation = anywhere to stay → shops
Your turn
Q10 — the final price
Question note
Total cost for whole family: $ ___
What you hear
'that's $214 altogether. Oh, wait a minute… Then I'm afraid it's $267'
Key paraphrase
'Oh, wait a minute' / 'I'm afraid' = a correction is coming → write 267
Your turn
