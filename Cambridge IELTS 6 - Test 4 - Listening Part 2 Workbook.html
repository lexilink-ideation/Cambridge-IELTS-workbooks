Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Staff Orientation for a Travel Exhibition · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Jenny Stewart, the Staff Manager at an exhibition centre, gives an orientation talk to 200 newly recruited temporary staff ahead of the International Travel Exhibition, which is expected to attract over 10,000 visitors a day. She explains how staff are split into four colour-coded teams, then outlines the plan for the rest of the orientation morning.

This is a typical Part 2 monologue: one speaker giving organised information, often matched to a diagram, list, or set of categories (here, the four colours).

💡 Part 2 strategy: When a talk lists categories (like the four coloured teams), listen for the exact order they're introduced and write notes next to each colour as you hear it — the categories are rarely repeated, so you get one chance per detail. Watch too for a schedule of times (9:30, 10:30, 11:00…) — mark each activity next to its time.

Unit at a glance

📅 Book: Cambridge IELTS 6  |  🎧 Test: 4, Section 2  |  ❓ Questions: 11–20

📝 Task type: Note completion / matching (which team does which job)

🗣️ Setting: An orientation talk for new exhibition staff

👥 Speaker: Jenny Stewart (Staff Manager) — monologue

Task breakdown

Q11–13: Which colour team performs which role — phones/entrance, hospitality, car park duties.

Q14–15: What two other staff members (Anne Smith, Peter Chen) will cover in their talks.

Q16–20: The rest of the morning's schedule — where the coffee break is, the video room, lunch, and meeting team leaders.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In monologues like this one, the speaker often describes a duty in different words than the question uses. Study each contrast, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — Red Team's Entrance Duty
Question wordingWho stamps tickets and gives out leaflets?
Audio meaningJenny explains that as well as answering phone enquiries, the red team also checks visitors' tickets at the gate and gives out the exhibition's promotional handouts.
Key paraphrase"checking tickets at the gate" = stamping entrance tickets; "promotional handouts" = publicity leaflets
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — Location of the Coffee Break
Question wordingWhere is the coffee break held?
Audio meaningJenny tells staff they'll have a short break down in the staff dining area, which is on the building's lowest level.
Key paraphrase"staff dining area on the lowest level" = the canteen, ground floor
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — Requirement Before Pay
Question wordingWhat must staff do before they are paid?
Audio meaningJenny explains that Anne will give out some paperwork relating to tax, and it needs to be completed and handed back before anyone's first wage can go through.
Key paraphrase"paperwork relating to tax" = a tax form; "before your first wage can go through" = before pay is processed
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — Finishing Time
Question wordingWhat time does the orientation finish?
Audio meaningJenny says that once staff have met their team leaders after lunch, everyone should be free to go by mid-afternoon.
Key paraphrase"free to go by mid-afternoon" = the day finishes by 3:00pm
