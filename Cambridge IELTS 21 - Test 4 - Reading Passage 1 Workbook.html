📋 Quick Review

This passage describes the water hyacinth, an invasive plant that has spread across Lake Victoria in Kenya, blocking fishing routes and harming livelihoods. It then explains a surprising benefit: the plant can be turned into biogas, offering a cleaner alternative to firewood and charcoal for cooking in villages such as Dunga.

💡 Reading strategy tip: For True/False/Not Given, decide FALSE only when the text clearly says the opposite. For flow-chart and note completion, use the exact words from the passage and obey the word limit (NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS / ONE WORD ONLY).

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

"There, with no natural predators and perfect temperature conditions, the plant began spreading in the open water, blocking fishing routes and providing a new habitat for disease-carrying mosquitoes."
Main Clausethe plant began spreading in the open water
Subordinate / Relative Clausewith no natural predators and perfect temperature conditions (reason) + "blocking … and providing …" (participles of result)
Grammar Note"with no … conditions" gives the reason. The two "-ing" participles show the results of the spreading.
Simple MeaningBecause nothing ate it and the temperature suited it, the plant spread fast, blocking boats and creating a home for mosquitoes.
中文在那里，由于没有天敌且温度条件理想，这种植物开始在开阔水域蔓延，堵塞渔道，并为携带疾病的蚊子提供了新的栖息地。
"About three out of four families in Kenya depend on wood or charcoal to cook their daily meals, and the rate is even higher in rural areas."
Main ClauseAbout three out of four families in Kenya depend on wood or charcoal
Subordinate / Relative Clauseand the rate is even higher in rural areas (coordinated clause)
Grammar Note"three out of four" = 75%. "to cook" is an infinitive of purpose. "even higher" is a comparative.
Simple MeaningAround 75% of Kenyan families cook with wood or charcoal, and the figure is higher still in the countryside.
中文肯尼亚约四分之三的家庭依靠木柴或木炭做日常饭菜，而在农村地区这一比例更高。
"But buried in those decaying waxy leaves was a renewable energy gold mine."
Main Clausea renewable energy gold mine was buried in those decaying waxy leaves
Subordinate / Relative Clause(inverted sentence for emphasis)
Grammar NoteInversion: the sentence starts with the place phrase "buried in …", putting the verb before the subject for emphasis.
Simple MeaningHidden inside those rotting leaves was a valuable source of renewable energy.
中文但在那些腐烂的蜡质叶片中，埋藏着一座可再生能源的"金矿"。
"The digesters work a bit like a stomach: the mixture goes in one end, and over the next 20 to 30 days, it goes through a fermentation process and breaks down, giving off gas that comes out the other end."
Main ClauseThe digesters work a bit like a stomach
Subordinate / Relative Clausethe mixture goes in one end … and … breaks down, giving off gas that comes out the other end (explanation after the colon)
Grammar NoteThe colon introduces an explanation. "giving off gas" is a participle; "that comes out" is a relative clause.
Simple MeaningThe digesters act like a stomach: the mixture enters, ferments over 20–30 days, breaks down and releases gas at the other end.
中文这些消化器有点像胃：混合物从一端进入，在接下来的20到30天里发酵分解，释放出气体从另一端排出。
"But unless the price of the machines drops, it’s pretty clear that most communities will never be able to afford any, since they sell for about $750."
Main Clauseit’s pretty clear that most communities will never be able to afford any
Subordinate / Relative Clauseunless the price of the machines drops (condition) + since they sell for about $750 (reason)
Grammar Note"unless" = if … not. "since" = because. The main clause uses "it’s clear that …".
Simple MeaningUnless the machines get cheaper, most communities won’t afford one, because they cost about $750.
中文但除非机器价格下降，否则很明显大多数社区将永远买不起，因为每台售价约750美元。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Follow the problem–benefit structure of the passage.

Problem & Solution

Problem: the water hyacinth blocks fishing and firewood smoke harms health. Solution: turn the weed (plus cow dung) into clean biogas. Signal: "a two-for-one solution to the dual menaces".

Cause & Effect

Solid fuels → indoor pollution → ~14,300 deaths a year. Biogas → less smoke → fewer respiratory diseases and more time to earn money. Trace each chain.

Chronology

Dates track the story: early 1900s (arrival) → 1980s (Lake Victoria + biofuel research) → 2014 (Nigeria) → 2018 (Dunga digesters). Use dates to locate answers.

Evaluation

The writer weighs benefits against a drawback: biogas helps health and income, BUT the machines cost ~$750, so affordability is "a challenge worth addressing".
