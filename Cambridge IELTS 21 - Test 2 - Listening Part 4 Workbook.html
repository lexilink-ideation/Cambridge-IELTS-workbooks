📋 Quick Review

This Part 4 is a student presentation on the cruise ship industry. It explains how cruise ships contribute to overtourism (with examples from Barcelona, Bruges and Dubrovnik), the industry’s image problem with younger travellers, and the solutions cruise lines are using to become more sustainable and appealing.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 note-completion needs ONE word, usually a noun. Use the headings to predict the topic of each gap. Watch for the exact word the speaker stresses — e.g. cities respond by imposing a tax, controlling the timing of entries.

Question wordingcities cannot manage the number of passengers
Audio meaning"unable to cope with thousands of cruise ship passengers"
Key paraphrasecope with = manage / deal with
