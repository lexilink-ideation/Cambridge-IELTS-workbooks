Cambridge IELTS 14 — Test 4 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Jake Stevens, a holiday rep at a hotel, describes the excursions available to guests (dolphin watching, a forest walk, a cycle trip, a craft tour, an observatory visit and horse riding) and two evening events ('Musical Favourites' and a 'Castle Feast'). Expect matching and detail questions about days, prices and features.

💡 Listening tip: Build a quick grid in your head: excursion → day → price → key feature. Distractors are common (e.g. 'no places left for this afternoon'), so listen for the confirmed detail.

Question idea
Which trip costs nothing?

'the best thing about this trip is that it's completely free'
Key paraphrase
'completely free' = no charge — that's the craft tour
