Cambridge IELTS 4 — Test 4 — Listening Part 1

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Lisa and Tony organise a goodbye party for John. They agree on the venue, who to invite, when to send invitations, how to collect money for a present and how much, which presents to price, and what to ask guests to bring. Expect note-completion (≤3 words / a number) covering names, dates, amounts and items.

💡 Listening tip: Two friends planning an event will 'think aloud' and change their minds. Write the final decision, not the first suggestion — e.g. a date is proposed, rejected because of exams, then fixed.
