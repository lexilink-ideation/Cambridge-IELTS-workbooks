Cambridge IELTS 12 — Test 1 — Listening Part 2

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Joy Parkins, a restaurant manager, gives a briefing to new kitchen assistants. She covers what people like about the job, appearance and safety rules, why today will be busy, equipment restrictions, what makes the work stressful, and the responsibilities of four named staff. One speaker only, so the signposting words do all the work.

💡 Listening tip: Sections with multiple choice + a matching task punish 'word spotting'. Every wrong option is usually mentioned in the talk — but negated ('none of you have unsuitable footwear'), reversed, or attached to the wrong person. Listen for the whole idea, not the keyword.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

In multiple choice, the correct option is a summary of several sentences. Practise compressing.
Q11 — what people like
Option wording
the variety of work
What you hear
'they have so many different things to do, which means they never get bored'
Key paraphrase
variety = so many different things; the promotion option is only a 'might', so it is not what most people say
Your turn
Q13 — why the day is busy
Option wording
the restaurant is almost fully booked
What you hear
'we don't have any tables free for this evening, and only a few for lunch'
Key paraphrase
almost fully booked = no tables free / only a few left. The head chef is back, so B is contradicted
Your turn
Q17 — Joy Parkins
Option wording
timetables
What you hear
'I decide who does what during the day and how long they work for'
Key paraphrase
timetables = who works when and for how long
Your turn
Q20 — Mike Smith
Option wording
food stocks
What you hear
'takes care of all the stores of perishables… if we're getting low in flour or sugar'
Key paraphrase
food stocks = stores of perishables; 'getting low' = running out
Your turn
