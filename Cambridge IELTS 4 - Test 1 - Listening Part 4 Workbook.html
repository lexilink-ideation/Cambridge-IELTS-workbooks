📋 Quick Review

A lecture on the urban landscape: how vegetation (trees) affects a city's climate and how trees can be used to plan more comfortable cities. The speaker compares the large-scale and local effects of trees, then contrasts trees and buildings for temperature, wind and noise. Expect note-completion (two words max).

💡 Listening tip: A Section 4 lecture is a monologue with clear signposts — 'Two areas of focus…', 'On the local scale…', 'Comparing trees and buildings…'. Use the headings in the notes to know where you are.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

Match the lecturer's explanation to the note gap.
Temperature control
Note wording
Trees regulate temperature by losing water through their leaves.
What you hear
'it evaporates water through its leaves… trees remain cooler than buildings because they sweat'
Key paraphrase
evaporate through leaves = sweat = regulate temperature
Your turn
Building surfaces
Note wording
A building surface can become far hotter than a tree.
What you hear
'the temperature of a building surface can easily be twenty degrees more than our temperature'
Key paraphrase
twenty degrees more = much hotter
Your turn
