📋 Passage Overview

Attitudes towards Artificial Intelligence. AI often forecasts better than humans, yet people distrust it — the Watson-for-Oncology case, why we trust human judgement more, how films polarise views, and how experience, transparency and user control can build trust. Question types: matching headings (Q27–32), multiple choice (Q33–35) and Yes / No / Not Given (Q36–40).

💡 Reading tip: For matching headings, capture each section's overall job (problem, reason, solution) rather than a single detail. Several sections here move from 'distrust' toward 'building trust'.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Yet for all these technological advances, we still seem to deeply lack confidence in AI predictions, and prefer to trust human experts, even if these experts are wrong.
Main Clausewe still seem to deeply lack confidence in AI predictions
Subordinate / Relativefor all these technological advances (concession); even if these experts are wrong (concession)
Grammar NoteTwo concessive phrases ('for all…', 'even if…') frame the main claim.
Simple MeaningDespite AI's advances, we trust human experts more, even when they're wrong.
中文尽管技术不断进步，我们对AI的预测仍深感不信任，宁愿相信人类专家，即使他们判断有误。
And the machine wouldn't be able to explain why its treatment was plausible, because its machine-learning algorithms were simply too complex to be fully understood by humans.
Main Clausethe machine wouldn't be able to explain why its treatment was plausible
Subordinate / Relativewhy its treatment was plausible (embedded question); because its algorithms were… too complex to be fully understood (reason)
Grammar NoteEmbedded question + 'because' reason with 'too… to' structure.
Simple MeaningWatson couldn't justify its advice because its methods were too complex to understand.
中文这台机器无法解释其治疗方案为何合理，因为它的机器学习算法过于复杂，人类无法完全理解。
It was found that, regardless of whether the film depicted AI in a positive or negative light, simply watching a cinematic vision of our technological future polarised the participants' attitudes.
Main Clausesimply watching a cinematic vision… polarised the participants' attitudes
Subordinate / RelativeIt was found that… (reporting); regardless of whether the film depicted AI in a positive or negative light (concession)
Grammar Note'regardless of whether…' concedes both possibilities before the result.
Simple MeaningWhether the film was pro- or anti-AI, watching it pushed people's views to extremes.
中文研究发现，无论影片将AI描绘成正面还是负面，仅仅观看这种对科技未来的影像描绘就会使参与者的态度趋于两极。
We don't need to understand the intricate inner workings of AI systems, but if people are given a degree of responsibility for how they are implemented, they will be more willing to accept AI into their lives.
Main Clausethey will be more willing to accept AI into their lives
Subordinate / RelativeWe don't need to understand the intricate inner workings… (contrast clause); if people are given a degree of responsibility… (conditional)
Grammar NoteContrast ('but') + conditional giving the route to acceptance.
Simple MeaningPeople needn't grasp the details; a say in how AI is used makes them accept it.
中文我们无需理解AI系统复杂的内部运作，但只要让人们对其实施方式有一定的掌控权，他们就更愿意接受AI进入生活。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Paradox: AI forecasts better than humans, yet we trust it less. Every section builds from this tension, so heading choices track the move from distrust to solutions.

Reasons for distrust: Watson couldn't explain itself (Section B); AI is unfamiliar and its process is hard to grasp (Section C); failures get disproportionate media attention. Multiple-choice items target these reasons.

Solutions → trust: experience with AI, transparency about algorithms (Section E), and giving users some control (Section F). The final YNNG items reward spotting that user-modifiable AI wins more approval.
