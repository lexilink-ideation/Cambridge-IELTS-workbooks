📋 Quick Review

A radio arts review (Jane Hemmington on 'Focus on the Arts') previews this year's Summer Festival, focusing on circuses and outdoor shows. She compares Circus Romano (in a tent), Circus Electrica (Studio Theatre) and a water-puppet troupe, noting each show's highlights and audience. Expect multiple-choice and a table.

💡 Listening tip: Reviews give opinions with signals — 'the best part is…', 'it's really for adult tastes', 'a must for young children'. The recommended audience often hides in a phrase like 'leave them at home'.
