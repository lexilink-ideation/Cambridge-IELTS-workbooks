📋 Passage Overview

Archaeologists discover evidence of prehistoric island settlers. Dr Ceri Shipton's team excavated caves on the Indonesian island of Obi, finding axes, bones, obsidian and beads that show its ancient people hunted, foraged and travelled between islands. The passage tracks how climate change shaped their tools and way of life over thousands of years. Question types: True/False/Not Given and note completion.

💡 Reading tip: This passage is organised by time (18,000 → 1,000 years ago). Build a quick timeline as you read so TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN statements about dates and changes are easy to check.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

The research team's discoveries suggest that the prehistoric people who lived on Obi were adept on both land and sea, hunting in the dense rainforest, foraging on the seashore, and possibly even voyaging between islands.
Main ClauseThe discoveries suggest that the people were adept on both land and sea.
Subordinate / Relative'who lived on Obi' (relative clause) + three parallel -ing phrases (hunting, foraging, voyaging).
Grammar NoteParallel present participles list what the people did.
Simple MeaningThe finds show Obi's ancient people could hunt, forage and travel by sea.
中文研究团队的发现表明，居住在奥比岛上的史前人类水陆兼能：在密林中狩猎、在海滨采集，甚至可能在岛屿之间航行。
While stone takes about twice as long to grind into an axe compared to shell, the harder material keeps its sharp edge for longer.
Main ClauseThe harder material keeps its sharp edge for longer.
Subordinate / Relative'While stone takes about twice as long… compared to shell' (concession clause).
Grammar Note'While' concedes a drawback before stating the advantage.
Simple MeaningStone axes take longer to make than shell ones, but they stay sharp longer.
中文虽然把石头磨成斧子所需时间约为贝壳的两倍，但这种更硬的材料能更久地保持锋利。
Shipton's team's excavation of the shelters at the Kelo site unearthed a volcanic glass substance called obsidian, which must have been brought over from another island, as there is no known source on Obi.
Main ClauseThe excavation unearthed a volcanic glass substance called obsidian.
Subordinate / Relative'which must have been brought over from another island' (relative clause) + 'as there is no known source on Obi' (reason clause).
Grammar Note'must have been' = a confident deduction about the past; 'as' = because.
Simple MeaningThey found obsidian, which had to come from elsewhere, since Obi has none.
中文希普顿团队在凯洛遗址的发掘中出土了一种名为黑曜石的火山玻璃物质，它一定是从别的岛屿带来的，因为奥比岛上并无已知的黑曜石来源。
Whatever the reason for the departure, there is no evidence for use of the Kelo shelters after this time, until about 1,000 years ago, when they were re-occupied by people who owned pottery.
Main ClauseThere is no evidence for use of the Kelo shelters after this time.
Subordinate / Relative'Whatever the reason for the departure' (concession) + 'when they were re-occupied by people who owned pottery' (relative clause).
Grammar Note'Whatever…' concedes any possible cause; nested relative clauses add detail.
Simple MeaningFor whatever reason people left, the caves stayed empty until about 1,000 years ago.
中文无论离开的原因是什么，此后再无使用凯洛庇护所的证据，直到约1000年前，它们被拥有陶器的人重新占用。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Chronology: the passage runs from ~18,000 years ago (earliest occupation) through 11,700 (climate warms, stone axes) to ~8,000 (abandonment) and ~1,000 (re-occupation with pottery). A timeline answers most questions.

Cause & effect: warmer, wetter climate → thicker jungle → need for durable stone axes to clear forest. Trace the environment-to-tool chain (supports Q5).

Evidence & inference: obsidian and beads 'not found naturally on Obi' → the islanders 'routinely travelled to other islands'. The finds are evidence for a conclusion.

Speculation signals: 'Perhaps… Perhaps…' shows the writer offering possible, unconfirmed reasons for the abandonment — important for NOT GIVEN judgements.
