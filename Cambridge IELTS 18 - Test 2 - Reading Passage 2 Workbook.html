📋 Passage Overview

Living with artificial intelligence. The writer argues that powerful AI must be reliably aligned with human values — but this raises hard problems. If machines become our moral 'guides', we may sacrifice some of our own freedom and autonomy. The essay uses the NHS as a real example. Question types: multiple choice, Yes/No/Not Given and summary completion (from a list).

💡 Reading tip: This passage tests the writer's opinion, so YES/NO/NOT GIVEN turns on what the writer claims, not on facts. Read each paragraph's purpose — several MCQs (Q15, Q19) ask what the writer is 'doing' or 'suggesting', not just what is stated.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

But the more powerful AI becomes, the more important it will be to specify its goals with great care.
Main ClauseIt will be more important to specify its goals with great care.
Subordinate / Relative'the more powerful AI becomes' (correlative comparative clause).
Grammar Note'the more…, the more…' links two rising quantities.
Simple MeaningAs AI grows stronger, defining its goals carefully matters more.
中文人工智能越强大，就越需要极其谨慎地设定它的目标。
So we need to create powerful AI machines that are 'human-friendly' — that have goals reliably aligned with our own values.
Main ClauseWe need to create powerful AI machines that are 'human-friendly'.
Subordinate / Relative'that are human-friendly' + 'that have goals reliably aligned with our own values' (two relative clauses).
Grammar NoteA dash introduces the second relative as a definition of the first.
Simple MeaningWe must build strong AI whose goals truly match our values.
中文因此，我们需要打造"对人类友好"的强大人工智能——即目标能可靠地与我们价值观一致的机器。
Machines who are better than us at sticking to the moral high ground may be expected to discourage some of the lapses we presently take for granted.
Main ClauseMachines may be expected to discourage some of the lapses.
Subordinate / Relative'who are better than us at sticking to the moral high ground' (relative) + 'we presently take for granted' (contact relative).
Grammar NotePassive 'may be expected to'; the second relative omits 'that'.
Simple MeaningMore ethical machines might stop us doing wrong things we now accept.
中文在坚守道德高地方面胜过我们的机器，可能会阻止一些我们如今习以为常的过失。
Since we'd want to ensure that people are treated equally and that policies are fair, the goals of AI would need to be specified correctly.
Main ClauseThe goals of AI would need to be specified correctly.
Subordinate / Relative'Since we'd want to ensure that people are treated equally and that policies are fair' (reason clause with two 'that' clauses).
Grammar Note'Since' gives the reason; two 'that'-clauses share 'ensure'.
Simple MeaningTo keep things fair and equal, AI's goals must be set correctly.
中文由于我们希望确保人人受到平等对待、政策公平，人工智能的目标就必须被正确设定。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Analogy: the King Midas story illustrates that badly specified goals can go wrong — a concrete example supporting an abstract warning (Q16).

Concession: 'Loss of freedom to behave badly isn't always a bad thing… But are we ready for ethical silicon police?' The writer admits a benefit before raising a worry.

Problem structure: the essay names 'two big problems' — the 'getting started' problem and the 'destination' problem — and develops each in turn.

Opinion signalling: 'we want', 'we need', 'few of us are likely to welcome' mark the writer's stance — vital for YES/NO/NOT GIVEN (Q20–23).
