Cambridge IELTS 6 — Test 4 — Listening Part 3

Joining the Education Library · Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

A student who has just transferred to the School of Education visits the library to find out how to join. A librarian explains that the library has two sites — Fordham and Castle Road — describes what each one holds, sets out the borrowing rules, and tells the student about three study-skills workshops running this term.

This is a typical Part 3 conversation: an academic discussion between two (sometimes three) speakers, often comparing and contrasting options — here, the two library sites and their different collections.

💡 Part 3 strategy: Listen for comparison language ("whereas," "unlike," "on the other hand") to catch which fact belongs to which site. Also listen for exceptions to a general rule (e.g. "you can renew any time — except overdue books") — IELTS often tests the exception, not the rule.

🔄 Paraphrase Training

In academic conversations, speakers often describe collections and rules using different wording than the question. Study each contrast, then practise.
Paraphrase 1 — What Castle Road Specialises In
Question wordingCastle Road holds material on: ______ and ______
Audio meaningThe librarian says that if the student wants material on secondary teaching, or is interested in the social side of education, Castle Road is the site to use.
Key paraphrase"the social side of education" = sociology of education; "secondary teaching" = textbooks/resources for most secondary subjects
Your practice
Paraphrase 2 — The Overdue Book Rule
Question wordingWhat happens if a book becomes overdue?
Audio meaningThe librarian explains that once a book is overdue, it can't be renewed over the phone or online — it must have been renewed on or before its due date.
Key paraphrase"can't be renewed over the phone" = cannot be renewed by phone/email once overdue
Your practice
Paraphrase 3 — This Term's Workshop
Question wordingWhat is this term's second workshop about?
Audio meaningThe librarian mentions that last term's session was "Internet for Beginners," but this term's workshop is about tracking down research material on the web.
Key paraphrase"tracking down research material on the web" = finding research materials online
Your practice
Paraphrase 4 — What the Dissertation Workshop Covers
Question wordingWhat does the dissertation workshop focus on?
Audio meaningThe librarian clarifies that the workshop is not about how to actually write the dissertation itself — it's more about the conventions, like putting together a bibliography and referencing sources properly.
Key paraphrase"not about how to write it" = the workshop covers academic writing conventions (bibliography, citation), not the content itself
