Cambridge IELTS 21 — Test 1 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

In this Part 3, two students, Phil and Lucy, discuss a lecture on ocean biodiversity and then evaluate a list of resources (an article, a documentary, a podcast, a book) for their seminar. They share opinions about how useful, balanced or up-to-date each resource is.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 3 is an academic discussion between 2–3 people. For matching-opinion tasks, the answer is the speakers’ attitude, not the topic. Listen for evaluative language: "showing its age", "stating the obvious", "above my head", "one-sided". These paraphrase the options in the box.
