📋 Passage Overview

Why zoos are good. Scientist David Hone argues that good zoos can give animals a quality of life as high as, or higher than, in the wild, and that zoos serve key purposes: conservation, education and research. The passage presents his case and weighs common objections to keeping animals in captivity.

💡 Reading tip: This is an argument with a clear point of view. For True/False/Not Given, separate what the writer states from what merely sounds reasonable. For paragraph matching, scan for the specific claim.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

In my view, it is perfectly possible for many species of animals living in zoos to have a quality of life as high as, or higher than, in the wild.
Main Clauseit is perfectly possible for many species … to have a quality of life as high as, or higher than, in the wild
Subordinate / Relativeliving in zoos (reduced relative); as high as, or higher than (comparison)
Grammar Note'It is possible for X to …' + a comparison expressing equal-or-greater quality.
Simple MeaningI think many zoo animals can live as well as, or better than, wild ones.
中文在我看来，许多生活在动物园里的物种，完全可能拥有与野外相当、甚至更高的生活质量。
They do not suffer from the threat or stress of predators, or the irritation and pain of parasites or injuries.
Main ClauseThey do not suffer from the threat or stress of predators, or the irritation and pain of parasites or injuries
Subordinate / Relative(compound object with paired nouns)
Grammar NoteA single verb ('suffer from') takes a long list of negatives joined by 'or'.
Simple MeaningThey avoid the danger of predators and the pain of parasites or injury.
中文它们不必遭受捕食者带来的威胁和压力，也不必忍受寄生虫或伤病带来的痛苦。
To view a life that is 'free' as one that is automatically 'good' is, I think, an error.
Main ClauseTo view a life … as one that is automatically 'good' is … an error
Subordinate / Relativethat is 'free' / that is automatically 'good' (defining relatives); I think (inserted comment)
Grammar NoteAn infinitive phrase is the subject; 'is an error' is the judgement.
Simple MeaningIt's a mistake to assume a 'free' life is automatically 'good'.
中文我认为，把“自由”的生活自动等同于“美好”的生活，是一种错误。
The average captive animal will have a greater life expectancy compared with its wild counterpart.
Main ClauseThe average captive animal will have a greater life expectancy
Subordinate / Relativecompared with its wild counterpart (comparison phrase)
Grammar NoteA comparison using 'greater … compared with' contrasts captive and wild animals.
Simple MeaningOn average, a captive animal lives longer than a wild one.
中文与野生同类相比，圈养动物的平均预期寿命更长。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Claim → reasons. Hone claims zoo life can be as good as wild life, then lists reasons (diet, safety, no predators/parasites) and the key purposes of zoos (conservation, education, research).

Answering objections. The writer anticipates the view that 'free = good' and calls it an error. Recognising the counter-argument helps with Yes/No and True/False items.

Comparisons. Repeated comparisons (captive vs wild, life expectancy) are the backbone. For paragraph matching, scan for the specific comparison each prompt names.
