📋 Quick Review

This Part 4 is an academic lecture on the sources of rubber. The speaker explains why rubber is an essential natural resource, why the supply of natural rubber is limited, the new threats it faces, the limits of synthetic rubber, and a surprising alternative source — the dandelion.

💡 Listening strategy tip: Part 4 is a single academic monologue with note-completion. Answers come in order and are usually ONE word. Use the headings and bullet layout to predict the type of word (noun, adjective, verb) before you listen, and listen for the idea being paraphrased.
