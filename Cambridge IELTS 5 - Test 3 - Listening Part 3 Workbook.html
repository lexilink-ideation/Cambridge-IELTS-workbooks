Listening Part 3

Cambridge IELTS 5 — Test 3 — Listening Part 3

Practice Workbook

📋 Quick Review

Two students, Annie and Ben, discuss their course and how it is assessed. They talk about deadlines and dates, problems with the library (too few copies, not enough computers), the course outline, teaching standards, group discussions, handouts, written work and student support. Expect date completion and multiple-choice.

💡 Listening tip: In Section 3 discussions, opinions can change. Track WHO says WHAT and note words of agreement ('Yes, exactly') or disagreement ('Actually, I think…') — these decide the answer.
