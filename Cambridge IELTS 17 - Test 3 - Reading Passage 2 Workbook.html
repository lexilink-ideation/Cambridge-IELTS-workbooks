📋 Passage Overview

Palm oil. The world's most-used vegetable oil is prized by manufacturers but blamed for deforestation and species loss. The passage presents a balanced, 'nuanced' picture: palm oil's high yield and carbon storage can be ecological benefits, it supports millions of livelihoods, it is regulated by the RSPO, and the bird's nest fern may help restore biodiversity. Question types: section matching (Q14–20), 'choose TWO' (Q21–22) and sentence completion (Q23–26).

💡 Reading tip: Sentence completion says 'NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS' — count your words and copy them exactly. For section matching, look for the specific idea (figures, an organisation, a species), not a keyword.

🔍 Complex Sentence Analysis

Primarily because its unique properties – such as remaining solid at room temperature – make it an ideal ingredient for long-term preservation.
Main Clauseits unique properties … make it an ideal ingredient for long-term preservation
Subordinate / Relativesuch as remaining solid at room temperature (example phrase inside dashes)
Grammar NoteThe dashes insert an example; 'make + it + adjective phrase' is a causative structure.
Simple MeaningIts special features, like staying solid at room temperature, make it great for preserving food.
中文主要是因为它独特的性质——比如在室温下保持固态——使它成为长期保存的理想成分。
There are countless news stories of deforestation, habitat destruction and dwindling species populations, all as a direct result of land clearing to establish oil palm tree monoculture.
Main ClauseThere are countless news stories of deforestation, habitat destruction and dwindling species populations
Subordinate / Relativeall as a direct result of land clearing to establish oil palm tree monoculture (reason phrase)
Grammar Note'all as a direct result of…' attributes the three problems to one cause.
Simple MeaningClearing land for palm-only plantations directly causes deforestation, lost habitat and fewer species.
中文有无数关于森林砍伐、栖息地破坏和物种数量减少的新闻,全都是为建立油棕单一种植而清理土地的直接后果。
If ten times more palm oil can be produced from a patch of land than any competing oil, then ten times more land would need to be cleared to produce the same volume of oil from that competitor.
Main Clausethen ten times more land would need to be cleared…
Subordinate / RelativeIf ten times more palm oil can be produced from a patch of land than any competing oil (conditional clause)
Grammar NoteSecond conditional 'If + present, would + base' arguing a hypothetical comparison.
Simple MeaningBecause palm oil yields ten times more per hectare, using another oil would need ten times more land.
中文如果同一块土地产出的棕榈油是其他竞争油类的十倍,那么用那种竞争油生产同样多的油就需要清理十倍的土地。
Only once these requirements are fully satisfied is the oil allowed to be sold as certified sustainable palm oil (CSPO).
Main Clauseis the oil allowed to be sold as certified sustainable palm oil
Subordinate / RelativeOnly once these requirements are fully satisfied (time/condition clause triggering inversion)
Grammar NoteFronted 'Only once…' causes subject–auxiliary inversion ('is the oil allowed').
Simple MeaningThe oil can only be sold as CSPO after all these requirements are met.
中文只有在完全满足这些要求之后,该油才被允许作为认证可持续棕榈油(CSPO)出售。

🧠 Reading Logic Training

Balanced argument. The passage deliberately presents both sides: environmental harm vs economic and even ecological benefits. Recognising this 'nuanced' structure explains why answers to Q14 (advantages) and Q20 (harmed creatures) sit in different sections.

Comparison of yields. Section F compares palm oil's yield with soybean, rapeseed and sunflower — the core of its 'less land' argument. Look for the multiplier ('four to ten times more').

Definition + criteria. Section G defines the RSPO and lists its certification criteria (no virgin-forest clearing, transparency, carbon-stock assessment). These criteria supply Q21–22 and Q25.
