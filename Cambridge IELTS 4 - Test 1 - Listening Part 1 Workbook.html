📋 Quick Review

A student (the man) asks a social-programme adviser (the woman) about the trips the school organises. She explains how often they run, what places they visit, the cost, how to book, and then lists the weekend trips with their dates, number of seats and optional extra visits. Expect place names (some spelt out), dates, and numbers of seats.

💡 Listening tip: In a Section 1 table the rows follow the order you hear them. Predict the answer type for each gap — a date, a number of seats, or a place name — before the speakers reach it.

✏️ Paraphrase Training

The question note and the speaker's words rarely match — train the swap.
Special trips
Question wording
Special trips are organised for groups of a certain size.
What you hear
'we do offer to arrange special trips if there are more than twelve people'
Key paraphrase
arrange = organise; more than twelve = the group size
